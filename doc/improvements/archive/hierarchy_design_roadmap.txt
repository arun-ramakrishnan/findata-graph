# Hierarchy Design Roadmap — enriching the notes ↔ DB ↔ graph knowledge model

Source: forward-looking design review (2026-07-30) of the whole capture → store
→ graph stack, prompted by "look at the structure of notes captured via
markdown_parse, storage in findata/{Companies,Sectors}, SQLite, DuckDB etc and
think about what design changes/improvements can enhance the richness of this
hierarchy — are there more sources we can use, more things we can put in
markdowns?"

Sibling to the four other files here (sqlite_improvs / duckdb_improvs /
graph_improvs / findata_corpus_audit) but DIFFERENT IN KIND: those audit what
EXISTS (bugs, gaps, coverage holes in the current state, every claim verified
live); this proposes what to ADD (design directions that would make the
knowledge model RICHER). Items here are PROPOSED, not verified-against-a-bug —
they are candidates prioritised by leverage, each with the concrete schema or
pipeline change it implies.

Methodology: a full structural map of the pipeline was rebuilt first — the
8-stage procedure (doc/procedures/markdown_parse.md), the parse_newsletter.py
orchestrator (Stages 0-3 + 5-6 automated; Stage 4 worklist emitted; Stage 8 =
extract_relations), capture_newsletter_images.py (Stage 0), and
extract_relations.py (Stage 8) — then cross-checked against the live schema
(SQLite memory/research.db; DuckDB memory/graph.duckdb / fin_graph). The edge-
mix diagnostic in §THE FRAMING FINDING is the live SELECT, not an estimate.
file:line references point at current source.

Environment as of this review:
  - 1031 company + 42 sector + 78 sub_sector + 9 super_sector = 1160 entities
  - 3634 graph_edges (see §THE FRAMING FINDING for the type mix)
  - 8 graph_analytics metrics, ALL topology-only (no financial signals)
  - DuckDB fin_graph: defined + catalogued but INERT under a plain connection
    (duckpgq not autoloaded) — see D13
  - markdown_parse procedure version 8.3; extract_relations.py 1867 lines

How to read the tiers: TIER 1 needs NO new data source (the signal is already
in the 75 newsletter editions on disk); TIER 2 adds entity types/dimensions
that are also latent in existing notes; TIER 3 requires new external sources;
TIER 4 is storage/engine hygiene that unblocks the rest.

================================================================================
THE FRAMING FINDING — the graph is 95% scaffolding, 5% signal
================================================================================

This single observation frames every proposal below. The live edge
distribution (SELECT edge_type, count(*) FROM graph_edges GROUP BY 1):

  | edge_type        | count | category          |
  | ---              | ---   | ---               |
  | co_mentioned_in  | 1329  | co-occurrence     |
  | part_of          | 1031  | taxonomy/struct   |
  | has_company      | 1031  | taxonomy/struct   |
  | belongs_to       | 120   | hierarchy/struct  |
  | subsidiary_of    | 58    | SEMANTIC          |
  | acquired         | 25    | SEMANTIC          |
  | jv_with          | 25    | SEMANTIC          |
  | competes_with    | 7     | SEMANTIC          |
  | same_group       | 4     | SEMANTIC          |
  | supplier_to      | 3     | SEMANTIC          |
  | customer_of      | 1     | SEMANTIC          |

So ~3511 of 3634 edges are STRUCTURE + CO-OCCURRENCE. The genuinely commercial
relationships — who competes with whom, who supplies whom, who is a customer —
total ELEVEN (7+3+1, plus same_group's 4). The richest part of a financial
knowledge graph is its thinnest part. Every TIER 1/2 proposal is aimed at
rebalancing this. (Note: findata_corpus_audit.txt H1 already measured the
typed-edge gap at "~10% of available signal" and closed the *extraction* side
of it; the proposals here address the *modelling* side — capturing signal the
current two-anchor, company-only design structurally cannot hold.)

TIER 1 — HIGH-LEVERAGE (no new sources required; signal already in 75 editions)
================================================================================

D1. Structured metrics layer — promote concall NUMBERS out of prose [DEFERRED]
    doc/procedures/markdown_parse.md:379-390 (concall taxonomy: Guidance /
    Margins / Capex / Segment color / Strategic / Risk — a WRITING convention
    today, never captured as DATA); 286 ## The Chatter blocks across the corpus.
    Every edition carries concrete quantitative signals — "FY27 revenue 10-12%",
    "EBIT margin 16%", "₹40,000 cr Kusha order book FY29", "working capital 140
    vs 176 days", "AI now 70% of retail applications" — that today live ONLY as
    bullets inside ## The Chatter prose and are therefore unqueryable.
    Proposed table (SQLite, source of truth; mirror to DuckDB like graph_edges):
        company_metrics(
          entity          TEXT FK->entities(name),
          metric          TEXT,          -- revenue_growth, ebit_margin, order_book, ...
          value           REAL,
          unit            TEXT,          -- pct, inr_cr, days, multiple, ratio
          period          TEXT,          -- FY27, Q1FY26, FY29
          direction       TEXT,          -- guidance | actual | target
          as_of_edition   TEXT,          -- the newsletter edition that sourced it
          confidence      TEXT,          -- high | medium (from the quote)
          source_quote    TEXT,          -- the verbatim audit trail
          captured_at     DATETIME
        )
    Unlocks: guidance tracking (did they hit it next quarter?), management-
    accuracy backtesting (L&T FY27 10-12% reiterated vs actuals), sector
    aggregation (avg margin guidance across Engineering_Capital_Goods),
    deviation alerts (a metric shifting >X between editions). This is a PURE
    EXTRACTION WIN over editions already on disk and converts 75 editions of
    qualitative commentary into a quantitative time-series — the temporal layer
    the system currently lacks entirely.
    DEFERRED 2026-07-30: the headline value of a metrics layer is TRACKING a
    number over time against itself — guidance-vs-actual backtesting, quarter-
    on-quarter deviation, management-accuracy scoring. That requires a REGULAR
    cadence of guidance/earnings data (e.g. D8 concall transcripts, recurring
    quarterly filings). Today the corpus is one-shot newsletter commentary with
    no recurring per-company guidance series, so D1 would collapse to isolated
    snapshots with nothing to track against. Un-defer WHEN a recurring source
    lands (D8 transcripts, or a quarterly-earnings ingest). The schema above is
    kept as the design; only the build is parked. Note D7 (events) does NOT
    depend on D1 and remains in scope — events give every company a timeline
    from existing dated prose (acquired/jv/board changes) without needing a
    recurring metric series.
    Leverage: HIGHEST (when un-deferred). Prerequisite beyond D3: a recurring
    guidance/earnings source. See D7 (events) — the independent, still-in-scope
    temporal proposal.

D2. Thicken the semantic edges — sector-targetable relations + edge weights [DEFERRED]
    extract_relations.py two-anchor design (design principle 1, lines 30-34):
    BOTH endpoints must resolve to a COMPANY entity. This is deliberately strict
    to avoid false positives — KEEP it for company<->company. The original
    proposal (below) suggested letting supplier_to / customer_of / competes_with
    target a SECTOR node (42 exist) and populating graph_edges.weight. DEFERRED
    after measuring the sidecar against the live DB (2026-07-30): the salvage is
    much smaller than implied and one sub-claim was wrong. Findings:
      - findata/_pending_relations.txt has 199 supplier_to/competes_with drops.
        Classification: ~130 are OCR garble / generic nouns ("ikea significantly
        over competition", "approximately 3", "that", "major oems") correctly
        rejected; ~34 would match a sector name.
      - Of those 34: ~19 are SAME-sector supplier (a pharma co supplying "pharma")
        — weak; ~15 are CROSS-sector supplier — genuine salvage; but ~15 of the
        competes_with drops are the "international/global/foreign X manufacturers"
        pattern, which would assert competes_with -> the International sector
        node. That node holds 24 REAL globally-listed companies, so the edge would
        read "competes with Vodafone/Apple/etc." — actively wrong. These are
        "competes with unnamed foreign peers", not "competes with the sector".
        The two-anchor design exists precisely to avoid this.
      - So the realistic salvage is ~15 cross-sector supplier_to/customer_of
        edges, NOT the broad rescue implied. competes_with targeting a sector is
        a false-positive source and should NOT be done.
      - CORRECTION to the original weight claim: PageRank sums to ~1.0 BY
        DEFINITION (stationary distribution), so weighted PageRank STILL sums to
        1.0 and CANNOT fix the failing test_graph_algorithms.py:329 guard
        (0.9 < sum < 1.1; the 0.888 drift is a dangling-node/damping artifact on
        a sparse disconnected subgraph, a pre-existing test-design issue, not a
        missing-weights problem). The only credible weight source is co-mention
        frequency, and that's on co_mentioned_in — the weak-signal edge type.
    Narrow scope if revisited (PROPOSED-NARROW, not the original): let ONLY
    supplier_to / customer_of (never competes_with) target a sector node, ONLY
    for CROSS-sector relationships, with an explicit international|global|foreign
    exclusion. That is a defensible ~15-edge salvage. Edge weights are not worth
    pursuing until richer sources (D8/D9) supply non-unit signals.
    Leverage: LOW-MEDIUM (was HIGH). Folded into the "thicken semantic edges"
    direction; real gain waits on D8/D9 sourced edges.

TIER 2 — MEDIUM-LEVERAGE (new entity types / dimensions, also latent in notes)

D4. Theme nodes — the cross-sector dimension analysts actually reason in [DONE]
    Hierarchy today is super_sector(9) -> sector(42) -> sub_sector(78) ->
    company(1031): clean, GICS-aligned, strictly hierarchical. But analysts
    think orthogonally: "China+1 beneficiaries", "PLI scheme plays",
    "premiumization", "defense indigenization", "data-center/AI infra" CUT ACROSS
    sectors (China+1 = Electronics + EMS + Pharma API + Textiles). Today these
    exist only as free-text investment_theme tags on a handful of sector notes.
    Proposed: a theme ENTITY TYPE + exposed_to edges (company -> theme, with an
    optional exposure weight). Promote the verbal clustering already present in
    the ## Newsletter synthesis blocks into first-class queryable nodes. Unlocks
    "show me all PLI beneficiaries" — impossible today.
    Leverage: MEDIUM-HIGH. New node kind + one edge type; capture is LLM-assisted
    over existing synthesis prose (same shape as D1's extractor).

    RESOLUTION 2026-07-30 (scope: curated enum + deterministic keyword match;
    directed exposed_to edge). The seed-source decision: NOT tag-promotion
    (only 32 tagged entities, 23/34 singletons) and NOT LLM extraction (every
    bolded-lead synthesis sentence is unique editorial, not a canonical label),
    but a CURATED CANONICAL_THEMES set (8 themes: China_Plus_One, PLI_Scheme,
    Premiumization, EV_Transition, Data_Center_Infrastructure, Renewable_Energy,
    Make_In_India, Defense_Indigenization) + a deterministic keyword-matching
    extractor (helpers/graph/derive_themes.py). Aliases are NARROW by design for
    precision (PLI matches "pli scheme"/"pli benefit", NOT bare "pli" which is
    boilerplate in 42/42 sector notes); the bare-pli guard is a pinned test.
    Result: 8 theme entities + 358 exposed_to edges. CROSS-SECTOR VALUE PROVEN:
    PLI_Scheme's 22 members span 9 sectors (Automotive, Diagnostics, EMS,
    Engineering_Capital_Goods, Packaging, Renewables, Technology,
    Telecommunications, Textiles) — the query that was impossible before.
    Graph layer: v_theme projection + e_exposed_to CTAS (dedicated, like
    belongs_to, because the company->theme endpoint is mixed-kind); _SCHEMA_VERSION
    bumped 6->7; theme_members() query wrapper + /api/graph/neighbors/<theme>
    branch. Integrity check extended (exposed_to allowlist + endpoint-type check +
    theme fileless exemption, mirroring sub_sector). make qa green: 455 tests,
    integrity 100%, validation 100%, snapshot OK (entities 1168, relations 3992).
    STILL DEFERRED: per-theme Markdown notes (theme-members index via the
    sync_sector_wikilinks pattern); exposure weights/buckets; LLM-assisted
    extraction over synthesis prose for higher recall.

D5. Product / brand nodes — structured asset relationships below companies [PROPOSED]
    Companies own portfolios (HUL -> Dove/Knorr/Surf; Tata Capital -> Yog Loans).
    acquired edges already SMUGGLE brands into JSON (properties.brand:"Dulux").
    Proposed: a brand/product ENTITY TYPE + owns_brand edge. Turns "Tata Capital
    acquired Yog Loans" into a structured asset relationship rather than a prose
    footnote, and models divestments/spinoffs later. findata_corpus_audit M3
    ("No product/segment model, 460+ notes with ## Product Portfolio") deferred
    this; D5 is the narrower, higher-signal cut (brands/M&A targets, not full
    segment revenue).
    Leverage: MEDIUM.

D6. Person nodes — management + quote attribution [DEFERRED]
    Management names appear in every note (## Management, 286 notes) and every
    quote attribution ("— Rajiv Sabharwal, CEO"). They are not entities.
    findata_corpus_audit M2 deferred this. Proposed: a person ENTITY TYPE +
    manages / on_board_of edges. Captures executive movement (a CEO change at an
    NBFC is material) and lets you trace people across companies. Attribution
    lines are already present, just unlinked.
    Leverage: MEDIUM. Source lines are structured enough ("- <Name>, <Title>")
    for deterministic extraction; the person<->company edge needs a role +
    date range (ties to D7).

D7. Event nodes — timestamped happenings, the temporal backbone [DONE]
    Acquisitions, JVs, guidance changes, management changes, earnings are
    TIMESTAMPED HAPPENINGS. Today ONLY acquired has valid_from. Proposed: a
    first-class events concept (entity, event_type, date, magnitude, source)
    giving every company a TIMELINE — the natural way to read financial history
    and the foundation D1's metrics WOULD attach to (D1 itself is DEFERRED; D7
    stands on its own — events from existing dated prose like acquired/jv/board
    changes need no recurring source). Also lets temporal extraction (today
    artificially limited to 'acquired' at extract_relations.py:320) generalise:
    an event row can carry a date regardless of edge type, so the ~80% false-
    positive risk is confined to the date column of a typed event, not spread
    across all edges.
    Leverage: MEDIUM-HIGH. D6 (people) wants to hang off dated events; D7 is a
    useful spine independent of D1.

    RESOLUTION 2026-07-31 (scope: a dedicated events TABLE, not graph vertices;
    4 event types via 2 extraction arms). The model decision: a typed TABLE
    (entity, event_type, event_date, period, date_precision, magnitude,
    counterparty, source_quote, as_of_edition, source_ref, properties) — the
    roadmap BODY's "event row / date column" language, not the "node" in the
    title. A timeline is SELECT ... ORDER BY event_date; events are instances
    (many per company), naturally tabular. Crucially this means NO graph-layer
    changes (no v_event, no edge, no _SCHEMA_VERSION bump, no DROP-tuple /
    fileless-exemption pitfalls D4 hit) — the events table is SQLite-only, FK->
    entities(name) ON DELETE CASCADE, never mirrored to DuckDB.
    Four event types (CANONICAL_EVENT_TYPES): acquisition, jv, guidance,
    management_change (earnings deferred to D8 — no reliable date source).
    Two extraction arms (helpers/graph/derive_events.py):
      * ARM 1 (promote): lifts graph_edges acquired->acquisition / jv_with->jv
        into event rows. Near-zero new FP risk — the counterparty + valid_from
        were already resolved by extract_relations.py + backfill_valid_from.py.
      * ARM 2 (extract): NEW prose extraction for guidance + management_change,
        both NARROW by design to confine the roadmap's ~80%-FP warning:
          - guidance requires THREE signals (fiscal token + metric + forward
            projection) so a historical actual like "FY2025: delivered ₹275"
            does NOT trigger (the D7 analogue of D4's bare-PLI guard).
          - management_change requires change-verb + executive title, and
            rejects "Appointed Actuary/Auditor" role-attribution false positives.
        Date parsing reuses the proven _extract_year_from_context helper.
    Idempotency: DELETE derived rows (source_ref LIKE 'derive:events:%') then
    re-insert; hand-seeded manual:/migration: rows preserved. Integrity check
    extended (check_events: unknown_type + orphaned + bad_properties, gated).
    API: GET /api/events/<name> returns the date-ordered timeline (dated first
    oldest->newest, undated last; optional ?event_type= filter).
    Result: 259 events (25 acquisition, 25 jv, 208 guidance, 1 management_change)
    across 174 companies; 49 dated / 210 undated. VALUE PROVEN: Larsen_and_
    Toubro's timeline shows its FY27 guidance (10-12% order inflow) + the LTM
    acquisition + an energy-margin event — the timeline that didn't exist before.
    The thin management_change count (1) reflects the corpus: most management
    prose carries only role+person, no change verb (D6 person-node extraction
    would capture the broader set). make qa green: 479 tests, integrity 100%,
    validation 100%, snapshot OK.
    STILL DEFERRED: earnings (needs D8 concall transcripts); person-linking of
    management_change events (the D6 follow-up that properties.person feeds);
    relative-date resolution ("last quarter") against an edition->year map;
    event->event graph edges (only if a query pattern needs traversal THROUGH
    events).

TIER 3 — EXPANSION (new external data sources)
================================================================================

D8. Earnings concall TRANSCRIPTS (primary source) [PROPOSED]
    The newsletters are DERIVATIVE of concall transcripts. Ingesting transcripts
    directly captures guidance BEFORE it is editorialised and is the cleanest
    source for D7 (events). Aggregated/historical transcripts are available.
    NOTE: D8 is also the recurring-cadence source whose absence is the reason
    D1 is DEFERRED — landing D8 (a quarterly transcript ingest) is the trigger
    that un-defers D1, since it supplies the guidance/earnings series a metrics
    layer needs to track against. Highest-value new source on its own merits
    (events, D6 board changes, D9 filing cross-checks) AND the key to D1.
    Leverage: HIGH (gated on a new ingest adapter).

D9. BSE/NSE corporate FILINGS (regulatory, structured) [PROPOSED]
    These are the GROUND TRUTH that acquired / subsidiary_of / jv_with edges
    currently GUESS at from prose. A filing-sourced edge beats a prose-extracted
    one. Also feeds D6 (board changes) and D7 (events) at source quality.
    Leverage: HIGH for edge precision.

D10. Annual reports via the EXISTING PDF pipeline [PROPOSED]
    The repo has helpers/pdf/ (capture_newsletter_images) and a pdf skill
    installed but ZERO integration for annual-report PDFs. Annual reports are
    sitting ducks for the structured-data layer (segment revenue, capex plans,
    risk factors). Reuses tooling already present.
    Leverage: MEDIUM-HIGH.

D11. Screener.in / Trendlyne — normalized 10-year financials + ratios [PROPOSED]
    Directly feeds a financial time-series. Prompts a re-examination of the
    strip_valuation_ratios.py decision: P/E, P/B, RoE are currently THROWN AWAY
    deliberately (a whole analytical dimension). Storing them WITH A FRESHNESS
    DATE may be better than dropping them. helpers/maintenance/strip_valuation_
    ratios.py is the touchpoint.
    Leverage: MEDIUM.

D12. Index constituents — Nifty/Sensex membership [PROPOSED]
    index_membership was DROPPED as a column (graph_design.txt C2/L2). Membership
    in Nifty 50/500 is high-signal for flows. Proposed: an index ENTITY TYPE +
    index_member edge (company -> index). Clean, bounded, regulatorily defined.
    Leverage: MEDIUM.

D13. fin_graph is INERT under a plain DuckDB connection [EVALUATED NOT-ADOPTED]
