# Interglobe Aviation

## Company Overview
InterGlobe Aviation Limited (IndiGo), incorporated in 2004 and headquartered in Gurugram, is India's largest airline and the country's dominant low-cost carrier (LCC). It provides passenger and cargo air transport under the IndiGo brand along with allied services (ground handling, in-flight sales, aircraft/engine leasing, charter flights, pilot training via its cadet program). As of March 2025, its fleet stood at 434 aircraft, serving business, leisure, student and family travellers across domestic and international routes.

## Key Insights (from Edition #24, Mar 2026)
- IndiGo's weak headline Q3 FY26 profitability was driven by exceptional items (~₹15.5 bn) — largely one-off provisions and disruption-related costs; stripping these out, profits jump sharply, showing the core business is far stronger than reported numbers suggest.
- The December operational disruption caused a temporary shift of customer bookings to other airlines, but the company expects a quick return to normal market dynamics.
- Capital deployment continues via the GIFT City entity (~$820 mn investment) to acquire aviation assets, partly used to prepay loans on 12 leased aircraft to gain ownership — signalling balance-sheet optimisation alongside fleet growth.
- New labour-code costs were treated as an exceptional item initially but will become a recurring expense within employee benefits in future quarters, a structural cost watch-point.
