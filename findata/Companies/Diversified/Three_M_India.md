# 3M India

## Company Overview

3M India is a diversified technology company that develops science-based solutions across Safety & Industrial, Transportation & Electronics, Health Care, and Consumer segments. It operates as a subsidiary of the global 3M Company, leveraging localised manufacturing and R&D to serve the Indian market. Brands include Command, Littmann, Nexcare, Post-it, Scotch-Brite, and Scotch.

- **Incorporated:** 1987 | **HQ:** Bengaluru, India
- **Parent:** 3M Company (USA)
- **MD:** Aseem Joshi | **CFO:** Nikhil Arora
- **Segments:** Safety & Industrial; Transportation & Electronics; Health Care; Consumer
