# Sodexo

Sodexo S.A. is a French multinational corporation and global leader in food services and facilities management. Headquartered near Paris, it employs over 426,000 people, serves 80 million consumers daily across 43 countries, and acts as a major driver of corporate operations worldwide.

- **Sector:** International — Food Services & Facilities Management

- **Geography:** France (global operations, 43 countries)
- **Business model:** B2B / B2B2C (corporate, healthcare, education, venues)
- **Segments:** Food services, Facilities management, Sodexo Live! (venues)
