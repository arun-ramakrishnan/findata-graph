# Kering Beauté

## Company Overview
Kering Beauté is the beauty division of Kering SA (EPA:KER), anchored by the House of Creed. On 2026-03-31 L'Oréal completed its acquisition of Kering Beauté for ~EUR 4bn, including Creed and fifty-year exclusive beauty/fragrance licences for Bottega Veneta and Balenciaga (announced 2025-10-19). The division ceased to be a Kering asset on completion; the entity is retained as the acquisition target of record.
