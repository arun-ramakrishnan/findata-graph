# SpaceX

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX) develops reusable launch vehicles, satellite communications infrastructure (Starlink), and space transportation systems. Founded by Elon Musk and headquartered in Hawthorne, California, it is privately held.

- **Sector:** International — Aerospace & Space Technology
- **Listing:** Private (unlisted)
- **Geography:** United States (global services via Starlink)
- **Business model:** B2B / B2G (launch services, satellite broadband, defense)
- **Key businesses:** Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, Starship, Starlink (broadband + Mobile)

## The Chatter — Global Chatter: Micron, SpaceX, Palantir & More

**Space-based AI compute and Starlink Mobile are the next growth waves:** President & COO Gwynne Shotwell framed SpaceX's roadmap around satellite capacity, vertical integration, and orbital data centers.
- Starlink growth is **capacity-constrained, not demand-constrained** — more demand than can be fulfilled in major markets.
- **Starlink Mobile** TAM exceeds home broadband — "more than half the global population has a cell phone."
- Starlink missions double as reliability test-bed for Falcon 9; AI satellites will play the same role for Starship.
- Deepening vertical integration: in-house propellants, software, launch sites; significant capex ahead.
- **Orbital AI inference thesis** — solar power 6× stronger in orbit, radiative cooling free; surplus compute to be sold to third parties.
- Signalled openness to M&A — historically not an acquirer, but "M&A is in the future, especially in the AI world."

> "But the most efficient place to put inference compute is on orbit. It's always sunny in space. You get six times the amount of power out of a solar cell in space as you do here on Earth. And cooling is free because space is actually quite cold."
> — Gwynne Shotwell, President & COO, SpaceX
