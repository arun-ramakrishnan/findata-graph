# Crocs

## Company Overview
Crocs, Inc. is a global footwear company that designs, develops, markets, and distributes casual lifestyle footwear and accessories under its own brands, Crocs and HEYDUDE. The company is known for its comfortable, innovative footwear, including clogs and sandals, made with proprietary foam technologies like Croslite, sold through wholesale and direct-to-consumer channels.
