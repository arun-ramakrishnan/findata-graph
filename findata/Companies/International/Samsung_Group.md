# Samsung Group

Samsung Group is a South Korean multinational **chaebol** (conglomerate) headquartered in Seoul, founded in 1938. It is the parent group of numerous affiliated companies across electronics, semiconductors, life insurance, heavy industries, and trading — most notably **Samsung Electronics** (the flagship, already a separate tracked entity in this KB). The group is privately held via a circular-shareholding structure; there is no single "Samsung Group" listed ticker.
