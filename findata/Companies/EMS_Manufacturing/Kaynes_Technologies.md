**Customs duty cut on display/camera-module components** — a direct input-cost reduction for Kaynes' automotive and industrial EMS franchises; management expects better profitability, lower tax-locked working capital and improved cash conversion.

**Semiconductor + display diversification** — Kaynes has publicly entered both businesses and is receiving RFQs; order book is expanding and new products are under development for future revenue streams.

**Automotive localisation tailwind** — global Tier-1/Tier-2 OEMs are actively pushing supply chains to manufacture in India; management expects India's component availability, manufacturing depth and technical knowledge to "improve significantly" over the next 3–5 years.

**Memory is only 2–3% of BOM** — global memory price spikes are largely pass-through and don't materially compress margins. Supply-chain disruption is also an opportunity for India as a preferred manufacturing base.

**Working capital inflection from Q3** — backlog is being cleared, new pipelines are coming in, and WC is expected to improve by next quarter, supporting better earnings quality.
