# Quadrant Future Tek

Quadrant Future Tek is a research-oriented company developing advanced Train Control and Signalling Systems for Indian Railways' KAVACH project, alongside a specialty cable manufacturing facility with an Electron Beam (E-Beam) Irradiation Centre.
