# Dixon Technologies

## Company Overview
Dixon Technologies (India) Limited is a leading Indian electronics manufacturing services (EMS) company that designs and manufactures mobile phones, consumer electronics, home appliances, lighting products, wearables, refrigerators, and telecom/IT hardware for leading global and domestic brands. The company provides end-to-end OEM solutions including global sourcing, manufacturing, quality testing, packaging, and logistics, positioning itself as a comprehensive partner in electronics innovation.

## The Chatter — Dixon Motherson Biocon

**Revenue doubling without equity dilution:** Management targets ₹1 lakh crore revenue (2× current ₹50,000 crore) funded entirely through internal accruals and working capital efficiency, with meaningful free cash flow expected from FY27.

**Margin expansion via backward integration & ODM mix:** Three drivers — component manufacturing (camera modules via QTech, display JV with HKC operational by Q4), higher-margin ODM business in washing machines/lighting/refrigerators, and operating leverage as volumes scale.

**Margin recovery timeline:** Expects margins to begin recovering from FY27 with significant improvement through FY28–FY29; evaluating precision component acquisitions (20%+ margins) and high-end specialty EMS opportunities.

**Export-led, component-led strategy:** Partnerships and JVs to contribute meaningfully; exports rising across verticals; backward integration in displays, camera modules, SSDs, power supplies, mechanical components as growth drivers.

**Market share resilience amid industry contraction:** Despite 10–15% smartphone market contraction from memory price surge (30–35% price increase in low/mid range), Dixon expects to maintain or grow volumes by gaining share.
