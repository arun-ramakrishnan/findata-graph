# Astra Microwave Products

## Company Overview
Astra Microwave Products Limited (AMPL) is a specialized Indian company focusing on designing, developing, and manufacturing RF (Radio Frequency) and microwave sub-systems for critical sectors including defense, space, meteorology, and telecommunications. With a strong R&D background and in-house engineering capabilities, AMPL has established itself as a key player in India's strategic electronics and defense manufacturing ecosystem.
