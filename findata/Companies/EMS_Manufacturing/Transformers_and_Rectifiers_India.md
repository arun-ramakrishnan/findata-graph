# Transformers and Rectifiers (India)

Transformers & Rectifiers (India) Limited, established in 1994, is a leading manufacturer of a wide range of transformers in India. With state-of-the-art infrastructure across three plants in Ahmedabad, the company produces high-quality power, distribution, furnace, and specialty transformers for both domestic and international markets.

## Business Overview

A diversified and high-quality customer base across power, renewables, metals, infrastructure, and heavy engineering anchors long-term demand. Relationships with marquee public and private sector clients reinforce execution credibility and repeat order visibility.

## The Chatter — HDFC, Groww, Yes Bank, Havells & More

**Backward integration on track:** Site preparation progressing and long-lead plant/machinery orders placed; in-house component capability will reduce external dependency and improve supply-chain resilience.

**HVDC oligopoly entry:** Only four global HVDC players (Hitachi Energy, Siemens, GE, TBEA); with TBEA unable to compete in other tenders, the company is the sole Indian entrant — positioning for significant share and pricing power.

**Disciplined order-book cap:** Deliberately limiting the order book to 18-24 months to avoid being locked into low prices if raw-material costs rise; declining incremental orders while booked for the next two years.

**Capacity nearly doubling:** Changodar facility live from Q2 and Moraiya expansion next, taking capacity from 40,000 MVA to 75,000 MVA — the primary future revenue-growth driver.

**Cleaned-up order book:** Older, less profitable contracts largely executed; the current ~₹5,000 cr book has no margin or cost pass-through issues, and internal component production may evolve into third-party (CTC) sales to peers.

> "We are deliberately not taking orders currently because, as we previously mentioned, we want to limit our exposure to no more than 18 to 24 months. We want to be very selective in which orders we take in terms of delivery and pricing. As long as we are booked for the next 24 months, we do not want to take any further orders."
> — Satyen Mamtora, Managing Director and CEO
