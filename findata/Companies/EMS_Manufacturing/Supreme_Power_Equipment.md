# Supreme Power Equipment

## Company Overview

Supreme power equipment (SPEL) is engaged in manufacture and supply of oil filled power and distribution transformers of ratings up to 25 MVA / 110KV class, focused on quality and service.

## Key Insights (The Chatter #50)

- Deprioritizing windmill transformer orders due to low margins despite repeat business
- Order book jumped from ₹75–100 crores last year to ₹300–340 crores, with another ₹100–150 crores yet to be finalized
- Government contracts carry price-variation clause (PVC) with no ceiling; private contracts get PVC for delivery beyond 2–3 months
- Anticipates faster government order releases and no payment disruptions ahead of Tamil Nadu elections
- Larger power transformers (3–4 month cycle vs 2 months) push working capital to 20–25% of revenue as it targets ₹300 crores next year
