# TechEra Engineering (India)

Incorporated in 2018, Techera Engineering (India) Limited designs, manufactures and supplies precise tooling and components for the aerospace and defense industries.

## Key Insights from The Chatter (Edition #40)

### Private Jet Manufacturing MOU

**Strategic Partnership:** TechEra is set to sign an MOU with India's first private jet manufacturer to supply aircraft tooling, marking a significant entry into a new, high-value segment.

> "Now the time comes to tell you guys that we are in discussion with one of the first India's private jet manufacturing companies. We will be signing the MOU with them soon that will be manufacturing the entire tooling set for the full aircraft."
> — Nimesh Rameshchandra Desai, Managing Director, Founder & Promoter

**Multi-Year Engagement:** This private jet tooling project is expected to generate substantial revenue and growth for the company for at least two years, starting this quarter.

> "This is what the big thing coming up in little bit in this quarter and next year is going to be the boom because we have to support them till the first aircraft fly and then entire two years we will keep supporting them by keep the tool alive and in the working condition so that they keep manufacturing the aircraft and flying."
> — Nimesh Rameshchandra Desai, Managing Director, Founder & Promoter

### Indian Air Force Direct Supply

**Strategic Advantage:** The company has secured a direct supply channel with the Indian Air Force, eliminating intermediaries and promising a significant strategic advantage and improved margins.

> "I got a call from our direct Indian air force at around 10:30... directly we'll be supplying the Indian air force henceforth there is no middleman which we used to do right now..."
> — Nimesh Rameshchandra Desai, Managing Director, Founder & Promoter

### L&T Bullet Components Order

**Major Order in Finalization:** TechEra is close to finalizing a substantial order for bullet-related components from L&T via an intermediary, encompassing full manufacturing and assembly.

> "the third one though we are in another discussion and I think order will be finalized me in a couple of days maybe in a week... Actually the customer is L&T but we will be getting this order from another middle company but the entire part manufacturing assembly proving everything will be done in our company."
> — Nimesh Rameshchandra Desai, Managing Director, Founder & Promoter

**Continuous Demand:** The L&T related order for bullet components is anticipated to be in "huge quantity" due to continuous demand, indicating significant revenue generation.

> "function reliability. It's a big kind of bullet. ground supported equipment holding. So it's a huge quantity because lot of firing happening. So that is the reason that it is in quantity requirement."
> — Nimesh Rameshchandra Desai, Managing Director, Founder & Promoter

### Competitive Advantage

**Expertise Over Equipment:** The company's competitive advantage stems from its decade of specialized operations, capability and expertise in complex machinery, which cannot be easily replicated by competitors merely acquiring equipment.

> "It's a different thing to have a machine and capability to use that machine. If you have 10 12 15 Crores you can buy the machine. But we have been in the field for 10 years all that not have that capability to work."
> — Nimesh Rameshchandra Desai, Managing Director, Founder & Promoter

### Growth Projection

**Leap Steps Expected:** The Managing Director projects significant growth and "leap steps" for TechEra in the next two to three years, driven by favorable industry conditions and strategic initiatives.

> "I want to make you understand what we are doing where the company is heading toward and tiny tiny steps are being taken, or the right time comes now to take a leap steps in coming I think two three years the situation is very good for us right now so rest assured from my side as a promoter and as someone who has built this company from the beginning thank you."
> — Nimesh Rameshchandra Desai, Managing Director, Founder & Promoter
