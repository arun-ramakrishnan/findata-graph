## The Chatter — Edition #46 (13 Feb 2026): Tailwinds building

> Q3 FY26 earnings-call coverage. Source: Zerodha "The Chatter", Edition #46, 13 Feb 2026. [Original](findata/The_Chatter/Tailwinds_building.md)

### Key Signals (Q3 FY26)
- **Data centers = massive power demand**: Hyperscale in India already ~1 GW; US projects (e.g., Fermi in Texas) talking 11 GW.
  > "Some of the data centers in India that we are talking about hyperscale is already 1 gigawatt... We are also looking at some projects like Fermi in US where they're talking about 11 gigawatt in Texas." — Bharanidharan Pandyan, Joint Managing Director
- **City-scale power**: 10–11 GW ≈ feeding a city like Pune 24/7.
  > "When you talk about 10 or 11 gigawatt, this is like a city of Pune that needs to be fed to a data center 24/7."
- **Multi-product opportunity**: Not just HVDC — instrument transformers, wave traps, transformers; a full power ecosystem.
  > "There is going to be lot of high power equipments that's being built, not just HVDC. It will be instrument transformers, wave traps, transformers. It's just a power ecosystem."
- **India projects still 2+ years out**: Optimistic but no money on the ground yet.
  > "Do I see anything happening in the next two years? I don't think so, because there has to be still some commitments like even the money is still not seen on the ground yet."
- **Already executing hyperscale internationally**: Microsoft hyperscale data center in Finland.
  > "In the US, we see a lot of hyperscale data centers coming up... We just executed a Microsoft hyperscale data center in Finland." — Bharanidharan Pandyan, Joint Managing Director
