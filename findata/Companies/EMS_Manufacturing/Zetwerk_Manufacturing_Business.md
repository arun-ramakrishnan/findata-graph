Zetwerk is a Bengaluru-based, tech-led manufacturing company that builds products such as transformers, solar modules and precision components, using its own plants (26 in-house) and a network of ~7,000 partner factories coordinated by in-house software. Founded in 2018, it opened its ₹5,012 crore IPO (fresh issue ₹2,830 crore) on October 15, 2026. **Stub note** — market_cap to be firmed up post-listing.
