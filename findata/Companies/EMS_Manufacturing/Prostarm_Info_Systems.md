# Prostarm Info Systems

Prostarm Info Systems Limited (formerly Prostar Micronova Power Systems; renamed 2017; incorporated 2008) designs, manufactures, and services energy storage and power-conditioning equipment — UPS systems, solar hybrid inverters, lift inverters, lithium-ion battery packs, isolation transformers, and SCVS. Headquartered in Navi Mumbai.
