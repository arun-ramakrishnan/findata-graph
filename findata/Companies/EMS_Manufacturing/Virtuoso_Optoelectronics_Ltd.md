# Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited

Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited is a manufacturing company specializing in high volume, large-scale production of consumer & industrial products. With a diverse product portfolio, they aim to improve lives through innovative products. Their solutions cater to small to medium-sized companies seeking performance optimization.

## Business Structure

Virtuoso Optoelectronics operates through three entities:

### Main Company (VOEPL)
Manufactures air conditioners, AC components, washing machines, EMS solutions, and refrigeration products like water dispensers and deep freezers.

### Virtuoso Compressor Limited (VCPL)
Produces compressors through this subsidiary where VOEPL holds 65% stake (set to increase to 90%).

### Virtuoso Polymers Private Limited (VPPL)
Wholly-owned subsidiary that provides plastic and EF components.
