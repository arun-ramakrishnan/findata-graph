# DP Abhushan Ltd

DP Abhushan Ltd was incorporated in year of 2017. The company is in business activity of Trading Of Jewellery Of Gold, Silver And Other Precious Or Base Metal; Manufacture Of Jewellery Of Gold, Silver And Other Precious Of Base Metal.

## Industry Context

India's domestic jewellery market is on a strong growth trajectory, expected to nearly triple from $50 billion in FY20 to $140 billion by FY28. Gold dominates the fine jewellery segment, making up around 85% of market, while non-gold categories like diamond and silver are growing even faster at 18% CAGR.
