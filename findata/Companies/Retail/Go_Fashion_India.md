# Go Fashion (India)

## Overview

Go Fashion (India) Limited is an Indian women's fashion company focused on casual and ethnic wear. The company operates through a B2C model, specializing in women's clothing and apparel, targeting the growing fashion retail market in India. Go Fashion focuses on designing, manufacturing, and retailing women's casual and ethnic wear, with emphasis on quality, affordability, and contemporary designs that appeal to modern Indian women.
