# IKEA

## Company Overview

IKEA is a global furniture and home-furnishings retailer (franchisor: Inter IKEA Systems B.V.; largest franchisee: Ingka Group), headquartered in the Netherlands with Swedish roots. It is the world's largest furniture retailer, known for ready-to-assemble furniture, kitchen appliances, and home accessories at accessible price points. IKEA operates stores and e-commerce in 60+ countries, with a growing presence in India.
