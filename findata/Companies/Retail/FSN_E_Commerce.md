# FSN E-Commerce (Nykaa)

## Overview

FSN E-Commerce Ventures Ltd. is the Indian parent company of Nykaa, a major consumer technology platform for beauty, personal care, and fashion products. The company operates primarily through Nykaa platform (online) and physical retail stores, offering a wide range of products including its own private-label brands like Nykaa Cosmetics and Kay Beauty. Nykaa has established itself as a leader in India's beauty and fashion e-commerce market with strong brand recognition and customer loyalty.
