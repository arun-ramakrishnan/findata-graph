# FSN E-Commerce (Nykaa)

## Overview

FSN E-Commerce Ventures Ltd. is the Indian parent company of Nykaa, a major consumer technology platform for beauty, personal care, and fashion products. The company operates primarily through Nykaa platform (online) and physical retail stores, offering a wide range of products including its own private-label brands like Nykaa Cosmetics and Kay Beauty. Nykaa has established itself as a leader in India's beauty and fashion e-commerce market with strong brand recognition and customer loyalty.

## The Chatter (Reliance, PayTM, Nykaa & More)

**Fashion vertical nearing EBITDA break-even:** Q3 fashion EBITDA margin improved to -2.0% (from -5.4% a year ago); 9-month losses at -3.7% of revenue — a meaningful reduction in the cash burn traditionally associated with newer segments.

**Brand partnerships (full-stack D2C operations):** Expanding the model to run full digital and physical operations for major global brands — Nike (D2C India business), Kiehl's (entire India business — D2C, stores, e-commerce), and Foot Locker. Creates stickier relationships with international partners and diversifies revenue.

**Beauty expansion — male demographic:** New specialty store formats are attracting a previously under-served male demographic — 45%+ of business from these stores is men's fragrance.

**Hyperlocal / quick-commerce:** All retail stores now enabled with hyperlocal delivery capabilities; Nykaa Now live in all seven Tier 1 cities. Strategy prioritizes order frequency over basket size for quick delivery.

**Ad platform (MarTech):** Modernized advertising platform to target consumers across the full funnel (not just top-of-funnel) — a structural shift toward high-margin ad revenue that enhances the margin profile.

> "Historically, it used to be all top-of-funnel advertising opportunities, but we've built out the technology... to enable brands to do advertising across the funnel... that's more structural in nature."
> — Anchit Nayar, Executive Director and CEO, Beauty

Gross margin improvements driven by advertising revenue and increasing scale of private labels (owned brands). Potential trade deals / lower import tariffs could make international premium brands more affordable, benefiting Nykaa as a major importer/distributor.

## The Chatter — Marico DLF BSE

- **TAM expansion to 100M online shoppers:** Online fashion has reached 55–65M serious buyers; rising income levels are expected to lift that to ~100M within 5 years, giving Nykaa a 65–100M relevant TAM runway.
- **Nykaa Now scaling to 25+ cities:** The 60-minute quick-commerce service expanded from 3 cities/limited assortment (Q1 FY26) to 13 cities/1,000+ brands; targeting 25+ cities by end-FY27 — offering the widest beauty assortment among quick-commerce peers (speed + choice as the differentiator).
- **Quick-commerce opening new categories:** Nykaa Now is generating incremental demand for low-ASP personal-care items (face washes, cleansers, bath gels) that historically weren't core to Nykaa's luxury-focused platform — a new frequent-purchase stream.
- **Beauty dual-engine: penetration + premiumization:** Platform visits ~half a billion in Q1 FY27 (+22% YoY); 20M+ annual unique transacting customers; average order values up ~5% (premiumization more visible among existing customers).

> "We believe that somewhere between 65 million and 100 million will be the relevant TAM for Nykaa… given the way income levels are shifting, that number itself will be about 100 million in the next 5 years."
> — Falguni Nayar, MD & CEO
