**Sector:** Retail | **Market Cap:** Small Cap | **Geography:** India (International Operations)**

## Overview
Vaibhav Global Ltd is an Indian retailer specializing in jewelry and lifestyle products with significant international presence. Founded in 1980 by Sunil Agrawal, the company is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and serves markets across United States, United Kingdom, Germany, and Austria.
