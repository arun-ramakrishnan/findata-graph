# Metro Brands

## Company Overview
Metro Brands Limited, founded in 1955 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a leading Indian footwear specialty retailer offering footwear for men, women, and kids under its own brands (Metro, Mochi, Walkway, daVinchi) and third-party brands (Crocs, Foot Locker, Clarks, FILA, Vans, etc.), plus accessories and footcare. It sells through stores, distributors, and online channels.
