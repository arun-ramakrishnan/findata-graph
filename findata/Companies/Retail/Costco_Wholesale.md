# Costco Wholesale

## Company Overview

Costco is a leading global membership-based retailer (founded 1983) operating warehouse clubs across the US, Canada, and internationally, known for high-volume, low-margin sales of a limited SKU selection including Kirkland Signature private label.

- Committed to passing any tariff refunds back to members via lower prices — "first to lower prices, last to raise them"

- Slight membership growth slowdown attributed to absence of large new-market openings; long-term rate still ~5%
