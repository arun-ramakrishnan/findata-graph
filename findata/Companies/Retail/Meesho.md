# Meesho

## Company Overview
Meesho (Fashnear Technologies — filed for IPO; HQ Bengaluru) is a leading Indian e-commerce marketplace focused on value/low-ticket-size commerce for tier-2+ consumers, with a strong ~90%+ of its order book from third-tier-and-below cities. Operates a low-commission, asset-light model with logistics and payments bundled via providers.

## The Chatter — Meesho, Marico, Bajaj & More

**Inflation as a tailwind for value commerce:** High-inflation environments historically favour value-focused players as tighter budgets push consumers toward affordable options — a structural tailwind for Meesho, even if absolute spend softens.

**Meesho Mall aimed at 'mass India':** Meesho Mall is being scaled for affordable/value brands and value packs (not just premium), letting popular mass brands reach online customers they previously could not — a big strategic focus.

**Contribution margin is the real trajectory metric:** Management argues contribution margin — not revenue per order — best reflects business health, since a rising prepaid mix lowers revenue/order but keeps contribution margin stable as lower service costs are passed to consumers.

**CAC reduction via tech/AI:** Customer-acquisition cost has fallen on the back of historical tech investment and AI features like the Vani voice agent, which lower the friction between app install and first order despite naturally higher CACs in deeper India.

**Prepaid, not BNPL, drives COD share down:** BNPL remains early-stage; the main force shrinking cash-on-delivery is direct prepaid growth, supported by lower prepaid costs and discounts, with BNPL expected to contribute more meaningfully later.

> "Typically, if you look at times like these... value-focused companies actually tend to gain share when inflation gets high because budgets are tighter for people across the board. That potentially is a tailwind."
> — Vidit Aatrey, Chairman, Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer

## The Chatter — Infosys Adani Power

**25% 5-year CAGR guidance:** Meesho guides ~25% revenue CAGR over the next 5 years — higher in early years, gradually moderating.

**Q2 optical dip from Diwali shift:** Diwali moving from September (last year) to November (this year) shifts the annual festive sale into Q3; combined Q2+Q3 growth should still track the usual trajectory — a timing artefact, not a slowdown.

**Cost per delivered order still falling:** Valmo's logistics share held ~50%; despite Q1 fuel-price hikes and minimum-wage increases, cost per delivered order actually fell ~₹1 QoQ on operating efficiency.

**Localised supply chains for low-AOV SKUs:** Building local supply chains to make sub-₹20–30 items (fresh grocery, ultra-low-priced apparel) viable — these can't be shipped competitively on the national platform.

**Platform stickiness:** For >50% of GMV-contributing sellers, Meesho is a primary source of income.

> "We have a long-term growth guidance of a 25% CAGR over the next 5 years. This is obviously higher in the initial years and may be lower in the latter years. That is the baseline we work with."
