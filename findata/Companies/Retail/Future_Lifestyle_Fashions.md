# Future Lifestyle Fashions Limited

## Company Overview
Future Lifestyle Fashions Limited, incorporated in 2012 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a distressed fashion retailer caught in the Future Group bankruptcy. It operated Central and Brand Factory store formats and marketed proprietary and international brands including Lee Cooper, Champion, Indigo Nation, Scullers, Celio, and Cover Story. The company has approximately 3,841 employees.
