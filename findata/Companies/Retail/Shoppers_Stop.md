# Shoppers Stop Limited

## Company Overview
Shoppers Stop Limited, founded in 1991 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a leading department store chain. The company offers apparel (men/women/kids), beauty and cosmetics, fragrances, personal care, home decor, kitchen and dining, accessories, watches, and luggage. It operates Shoppers Stop and HomeStop stores plus a mobile app and website, distributes international beauty brands, and runs the Intune format.
