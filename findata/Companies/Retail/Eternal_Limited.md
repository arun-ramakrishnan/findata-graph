- 1P (First Party) model implementation - company owns inventory and acts as retailer
- Moving most business to own inventory model
- Better control over fulfillment and margins through inventory ownership
- Reduction in administrative and licensing burden for both company and brands

### Competitive Landscape

- Recognition that competitive intensity continues to grow
- Strategic positioning to maintain market leadership amid growing competition

### Bolt Service Performance

- Less than 6 months vintage showing strong initial performance
- Built purposefully and thoughtfully as a distinct category
- Positioning as unique category not just in India but globally

### Marketing and Growth Strategy
- Ad-spend intensity expected to remain high near term
- New user acquisition costs remain attractive
- Continued investment in marketing to power growth
- Targeting new consumers in market at reasonable marketing costs
