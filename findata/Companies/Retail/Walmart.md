# Walmart Inc

## Overview

Walmart Inc is the world's largest retailer by revenue, operating a multinational retail corporation that sells a wide variety of products through its physical stores and e-commerce platforms. The company operates as a discount retailer with a strategy of providing low prices (everyday low prices - EDLP), with a business model combining physical retail stores and online sales. Walmart serves millions of customers globally through its extensive retail network of Walmart stores, Sam's Club warehouse chain, and various e-commerce platforms.
