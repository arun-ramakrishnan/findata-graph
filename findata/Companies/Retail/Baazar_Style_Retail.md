# Baazar Style Retail

## Overview

Baazar Style Retail Limited is an Indian public company specializing in retailing a variety of apparels and consumer products through retail stores under Brand/TI names of Style Baazar and Express Baazar. The company operates through a B2C model, focusing on value fashion and apparel retailing with emphasis on affordability and accessibility. Baazar Style Retail operates in the value fashion segment, catering to price-sensitive customers with affordable clothing and apparel options.
