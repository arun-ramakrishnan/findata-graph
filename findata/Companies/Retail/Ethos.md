# Ethos Limited

## Company Overview
Ethos Limited, founded in 2003 and headquartered in Gurugram, is India's luxury watch boutique chain. It offers luxury and premium watches, pre-owned watches, jewellery, watch accessories, and luggage, alongside repair services (movement overhauling, battery replacement, polishing). The business operates an omnichannel model spanning online and physical boutiques.
