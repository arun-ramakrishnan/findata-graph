# Bata India

## Company Overview
Bata India Limited, incorporated in 1931 and headquartered in Gurugram, is one of India's largest footwear retailers, operating over 1,400 stores nationwide. The company manufactures and sells footwear and accessories across an extensive brand portfolio serving value, mid-market, and premium consumer segments. It is a subsidiary of Bata (BN) B.V.
