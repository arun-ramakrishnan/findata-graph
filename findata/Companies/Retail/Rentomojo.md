# Rentomojo

## Company Overview
Rentomojo is India's leading digital platform for furniture, appliances and electronics on monthly subscription — an asset-heavy rental model managed by proprietary demand-prediction algorithms and a growing network of offline experience centres. IPO-track: management references its RHP and DRHP disclosures on the concall (not yet listed). Key metrics: >100% EBITDA-to-operating-cash-flow conversion for three straight years, normalised RoCE >26%, 83–84% occupancy, and ~₹408 cr of unrecognised contracted revenue.

## The Chatter — PNB, Central Bank of India, Rentomojo & More

**Structural demand thesis — mobility economics:** urbanisation + workforce churn (~20% attrition, job changes every ~2 years) + the joint-to-nuclear/solo family shift + average tenancy of ~17 months (Redseer) makes renting the rational default: every move compounds relocation costs, breakages, repairs and disposal headaches.

**"Purchase displacement" as the north-star metric:** FY26 saw ~1 mn items ordered = ~₹1,100–1,150 cr of retail purchases displaced; Q1 FY27 alone ~3.3 lakh items = ~₹400 cr. Management: items ordered is the single most important metric — "the rest of the aspects flow through the P&L from that one variable."

**Earnings quality is the pitch:** operating cash flow +49.6% YoY (~37% CAGR), >100% EBITDA→CFO conversion for 3 years; FY26 generated ~₹173 cr operating cash flow covering ~₹80 cr growth capex — self-funded growth with normalised RoCE >26% including Q1 FY27. Normalised PAT margin rose 15% → 17% (+230 bps) on finance-cost optimisation (finance cost 7.59% → 5.8% of revenue while revenue grew ~51%).

**Asset economics:** the 2017 cohort generated ~5x its cost in revenue over its lifecycle, with ~56% of products still revenue-generating — payback period is far shorter than useful life, the engine of the high return on capital.

**Supply-side moat is algorithmic:** inventory addition runs on demand + churn + repair-rate prediction (200–300 SKUs, 1,000+ consumables, technician/carpenter attrition forecasts) — a barrier smaller competitors without the historical data cannot replicate. Occupancy deliberately held at 83–84%: "not 90%, not 65–70%" — the balanced point between stockouts and idle inventory.

**Tier-2 expansion is demand-pulled:** Indore and Lucknow growing at "very strong exponential rates" (discovered during COVID reverse migration); network presence itself compounds the network effect. Acquisition is largely organic — the paid-search funnel is still small, so "somebody [who] is the largest player... would have the biggest network effect to leverage."

**Inflation pass-through without demand shock:** a 10–15% rental increase staggered over an 18–20-month tenancy barely moves the conversion funnel — subscription pricing spreads the pain. Dixon partnership supplies refrigerators and washing machines directly, with similar contract-manufacturing relationships being built.

**Revenue visibility:** ~₹554 cr of revenue contracted in the quarter, ~₹408 cr of it unrecognised — the subscription backlog that traditional retail lacks.
