# Redtape

## Company Overview
Redtape (NSE: REDTAPE) is an Indian footwear and apparel brand, originally the international label of Mirza International, demerged and listed separately in 2023. It sells men's/women's footwear, apparel, and accessories through a mix of owned retail, multi-brand outlets, and e-commerce.
