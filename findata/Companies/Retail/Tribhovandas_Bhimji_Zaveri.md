## Efficient Sourcing: Gold Metal Loan

### Innovative Financing Model
- **Gold Metal Loan**: 10 kg gold from bank on lease on day 0
- **Contract Period**: 180 days
- **Bank Guarantee**: 110% of gold leased
- **Daily Repayment**: Based on actual sales of gold jewellery
- **Interest Cost Savings**: Significantly lower than cash credit

### Process Benefits
- **Lower Financing Cost**: Gold metal loan at lower interest rates
- **Daily Inventory Turns**: 2-3 times, enabling early lease closure
- **No Commodity Risk**: No gain/loss on gold price changes
- **Faster Replenishment**: Take new 1 kg on lease each day

### Risk Management
- **Gold Price Volatility**: Marked to market daily, requiring bank guarantee top-up
- **Contract Risk**: If unable to sell within 180 days, must convert balance to purchase
- **Drawing Power**: Bank guarantee based on company's drawing power
