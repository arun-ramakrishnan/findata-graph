# Spencers Retail Limited

## Company Overview

Spencers Retail is a multi-format retailer offering a wide range of food, non-food, and general merchandise products. The company operates hypermarkets, supermarkets, and other retail formats across India, focusing on providing quality products and shopping experience to customers. Spencers Retail has established itself as a significant player in India's retail sector with a strong brand presence.
