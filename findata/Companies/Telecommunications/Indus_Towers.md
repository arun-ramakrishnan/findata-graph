# Indus Towers

One of the world's largest telecom tower companies, providing passive
infrastructure services to mobile network operators across India — an
extensive shared infrastructure network of over 270,000 macro towers
supporting 4G/5G network expansion. Tenancy-ratio-led business model;
expanding internationally into Africa.

## The Chatter — SEBI, L&T, Cipla, SBI Life Insurance & More

**Tenancy ratio up for the first time in several quarters:** 1.62 → 1.63 on strong co-location additions in Q2 — customer network expansion plus a pickup in rollouts by a major customer; healthy order-book visibility.

**5G densification is the structural catalyst:** total data volume +30% YoY (Q1), usage per subscriber +18%; 5G usage +79% YoY, now 44% of total data consumption (from 32%) — deployment focus shifting to network densification.

**Africa entry at lower capex/tower:** rollouts commenced for the anchor tenant, ramping in H2; engagements initiated beyond the anchor to validate the tenancy-led model — "we strongly believe we have the right to win."

**Diesel usage down 11% — structural, not seasonal:** ~49,000 solar sites deployed by end-Sep, AI-driven fuel operations planning, and the lead-acid → lithium-ion transition reaching meaningful scale.

**Macro towers back in favour:** telcos are prioritising 5G capacity and multi-tenant capability over leaner single-purpose sites; Indus is adapting rollout strategy to match.

> "We are happy to share that we recorded strong growth in co-location additions in Q2. This has translated into an improvement in our tenancy ratio from 1.62 to 1.63, following a period of stability."
> — Prachur Sah, Managing Director and CEO
