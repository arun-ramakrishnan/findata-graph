# Vodafone Idea

## Company Overview

Vodafone Idea (Vi) is an Indian telecommunications company formed through a partnership between the Aditya Birla Group and Vodafone Group, providing voice and data services across 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G platforms. The company serves individual consumers and businesses with mobile services, digital solutions, IoT, and entertainment content under the "Vi" brand.

## Key Insights (The Push and Pull)

- Won a 10-year contract with GE Power Infrastructure for 5 million smart meters, described by CEO Akshaya Moondra as one of India's largest smart-metering wins and a landmark IoT achievement.
- Expanded 5G to 22 cities across 13 circles post-Mumbai launch in March 2025, with further expansion to all 17 priority circles planned by September 2025.
- Raised 4G coverage to 84%, backed by ₹24.4 billion in Q1 capex to support high-speed network expansion.

## Key Insights (Opportunity Between The Lines)

- Holds the highest spectrum per subscriber (excluding mmWave) among peers, providing a strong base to migrate 4G users to 5G.
- Urban teledensity remains high while rural teledensity is low, representing an untapped growth market for Vi.

## Key Insights (Moving Pieces)

- The government ruled out increasing its equity stake beyond 49%, as that would convert Vi into a PSU; future funding is left to the company and its investors.
