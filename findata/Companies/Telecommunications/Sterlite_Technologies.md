# Sterlite Technologies

## Company Overview

Sterlite Technologies Limited (STL) designs and manufactures optical fibres, optical fibre and specialty cables, and optical connectivity products. The company also provides multiverse and access network distribution solutions, and operates the Neox communication platform. Founded in 1988, headquartered in Pune.
