# Tejas Networks

## Overview

Tejas Networks Limited is an Indian telecommunications equipment company specializing in designing, developing, and selling high-performance products for telecommunications service providers, internet service providers, utility companies, defense companies, and government entities. The company follows a B2B business model, serving telecom operators, ISPs, utilities, defense organizations, and government agencies with advanced telecommunications equipment and solutions. Tejas Networks focuses on providing innovative and cost-effective telecommunications solutions to enhance network infrastructure and connectivity.
