# Bharti Hexacom

## Company Overview
Bharti Hexacom Limited (incorporated 1995; HQ New Delhi/Jaipur; formerly Hexacom India Ltd) is a subsidiary of Bharti Airtel Limited, providing mobile (2G/4G/5G), fixed-line, broadband, and FWA services under the Airtel brand in the Rajasthan and North-East telecom circles. It also offers Airtel Black (converged mobility, Wi-Fi, digital TV, OTT) and telemedicine services.
