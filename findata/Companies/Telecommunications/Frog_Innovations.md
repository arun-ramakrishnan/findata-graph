# Frog Innovations

## Overview

Frog Cellsat Limited (Frog Innovations) is a telecom equipment manufacturer specializing in products for telecom towers and wireless communication infrastructure. The company follows a B2B business model, serving telecom operators, infrastructure providers, and large commercial facilities. Frog Innovations produces RF repeaters, optical DAS (Distributed Antenna Systems) systems, and provides in-building coverage planning services. The company holds ISO 9001:2015 certification and offers price-competitive, field-proven, and reliable RF enhancement solutions. Frog Innovations is the only Indian company with proprietary DAS technology and has received approval for the Design-led manufacturing PLI scheme.
