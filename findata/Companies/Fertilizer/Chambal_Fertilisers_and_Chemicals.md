# Chambal Fertilisers and Chemicals

## Company Overview
Chambal Fertilisers and Chemicals Limited, incorporated in 1985 and headquartered in New Delhi, is a major Indian fertiliser and agri-inputs company. It produces and sells urea, complex fertilisers (DAP, NPK, MOP), crop protection chemicals, biologicals, specialty nutrients, and technical ammonium nitrate (TAN) under the Uttam brand. The company has a significant position as a fertiliser supplier across Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, and Haryana. It operates through segments: Own Manufactured Fertilisers; Complex Fertilisers; Crop Protection Chemicals and Specialty Nutrients; and Others.

## Key Insights (from Edition #24, Mar 2026)
- Fertiliser demand is structurally supported by food security focus, irrigation expansion and the shift toward balanced nutrition; growth beyond urea is increasingly coming from specialty nutrients, crop protection and biologics, while TAN offers a high-margin industrial growth lever linked to infrastructure and mining.
- India is the world's second-largest fertiliser consumer and third-largest producer; the market is projected to grow ~6% CAGR to ~USD 63 bn by 2030, with complex fertilisers and specialty nutrients expanding faster than traditional urea.
- TAN demand is driven by mining, infrastructure and explosives with a persistent domestic supply deficit; structurally this remains a tight, high-margin segment with strong volume visibility.
- Crop protection chemicals (CPC) and specialty nutrients offer the key margin expansion lever given India's very low agrochemical intensity (~0.6 kg/ha vs. global 4–10 kg/ha), with premiumisation (biologicals, WSF, micronutrients) and export competitiveness driving growth.
- Subsidy receivables remain a core working-capital swing factor — cash flow visibility continues to be policy-dependent rather than purely operational.
