# Rail Vikas Nigam (Rail Vikas Nigam Limited)

## Company Overview
Rail Vikas Nigam Limited, incorporated in 2003 and headquartered in New Delhi, India, engages in rail infrastructure works in India and internationally. The company undertakes rail project development and implementation, providing financial resources mobilization services for various railway projects including new lines, doubling, gauge conversion, railway electrification, workshops, bridge work, mega and monumental bridges, metro and metropolitan transport projects, hilly railway projects, construction of cable stayed bridges, institutional buildings, track renewal, training institutes, railway and other safety works, traffic facilities, and road over bridges.
