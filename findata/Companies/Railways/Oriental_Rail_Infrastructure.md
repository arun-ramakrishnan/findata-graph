# Oriental Rail Infrastructure

## Company Overview
Oriental Rail Infrastructure Limited, incorporated in 1991 (formerly Oriental Veneer Products Limited, renamed March 2022) and based in Mumbai, manufactures rolling stock components, railway coach interiors and freight wagons for Indian Railways and private logistics operators. Products include compreg wood sheets (electrical insulation), silicon foam blocks and shuttering plates for seats/berths, acosonic boards (architectural/thermal/noise barriers), lavatory doors, phenolic resins, densified thermal bonded blocks, and artificial leather/rexine under the ORVIN brand. The company also trades in timber woods and ferrous/non-ferrous metals, and manufactures heavy engineering equipment (wagons, bogies, couplers, draft gears). It is strategically transitioning into high-technology smart wagons and recurring-revenue wagon leasing.

## Key Insights (from Edition #23, Mar 2026)
- Moving into wagon leasing shifts the business model toward stable annuity revenues, reducing the impact of volatile manufacturing tender cycles and providing predictable long-term cash flows and higher-margin growth.
- Strategic collaboration with United Wagon Company and its design arm VNICT to develop next-generation 25-ton axle load gondola wagons tailored for Indian conditions (technology/design transfer, Make in India aligned); advanced-stage discussions underway, with customised wagon designs to be exclusively manufactured and marketed by Oriental.
- A record ₹2.9 lakh crore railway budget for FY27 signals a multi-year commitment to modernization, providing long-term demand visibility and supporting confident capacity expansion.
- Freight segment is the revenue backbone (~65% of railway revenue): 11,700+ freight trains daily, ~4,17,000 wagons, ₹1.75L cr FY25 revenue. National Rail Plan 2030 targets fleet doubling from ~3,00,000 to ~6,00,000 wagons (~1,17,000 already under execution); the freight wagon market is expected to nearly double to ₹25,000-30,000 cr by 2031.
- Passenger segment: 13,500+ passenger trains daily, ~715 cr passengers in FY25 (up 67 cr YoY, approaching pre-COVID); FY25 passenger revenue ₹94,927 cr; ~1,000 new trains planned over next 5 years; 144+ Vande Bharat trains operational (400+ targeted by 2030) with AC coach conversion accelerating.
- Strategic benefits to Oriental: multi-year visibility in freight wagon procurement (corridor expansion, logistics growth), smart wagon opportunity (telematics, KAVACH integration), wagon-leasing recurring revenue via private sector freight demand, replacement/upgrade demand from large installed wagon base, and passenger premiumisation (modular seating/berth demand).
- Enablers: 2,052 km East-West Dedicated Freight Corridor and ₹5.25 lakh crore PPP-led logistics investments by 2031 will significantly enhance freight capacity and network efficiency.
