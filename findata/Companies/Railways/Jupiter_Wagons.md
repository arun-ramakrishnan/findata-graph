# Jupiter Wagons

## Company Overview
Jupiter Wagons Limited (incorporated 1979; HQ Kolkata, West Bengal) manufactures railway freight wagons, passenger/metro coaches, wagon components, and commercial vehicle load bodies for Indian Railways, private wagon aggregators, CV OEMs, and defense/logistics customers. It also produces ISO marine containers, cold-chain solutions, and has diversified into battery storage for railway applications.
