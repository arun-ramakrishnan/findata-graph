# Titagarh Rail Systems | Small Cap | Engineering & Capital Goods | **TITAGARH**

Titagarh Wagons Limited is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India. The Company is mainly engaged in manufacturing and selling of Freight Wagons, Passenger Coaches, Steel Castings, Specialised Equipments & Bridges, Shi Heavy Earthmoving and Mining Equipments, etc. The Company caters to both domestic and export market.

## Business Operations

### Core Product Categories
- **Freight Wagons**: Manufacturing and sales of freight wagons
- **Passenger Coaches**: Railway passenger transportation solutions
- **Steel Castings**: Industrial steel components
- **Specialized Equipment**: Custom equipment for specific applications
- **Heavy Earthmoving Equipment**: Mining and construction machinery
- **Bridges**: Infrastructure construction components

### Market Segments
- **Domestic Market**: Indian railway infrastructure projects
- **Export Market**: International railway equipment sales
- **Government Contracts**: Public sector transportation projects
