# Railtel Corporation Of India

## Company Overview

RailTel Corporation of India is a major neutral telecom infrastructure provider (Miniratna PSU under Indian Railways), offering high-capacity DWDM and MPLS networks to meet the critical communication needs of Indian Railways and other clients. It also runs data centres in Gurugram and Secunderabad and provides telecom, ICT, cloud, and signalling services, plus turnkey projects (Wi-Fi, BharatNet, smart cities, KAVACH).

- **Incorporated:** 2000 | **HQ:** New Delhi, India
- **Type:** Miniratna PSU (Indian Railways)
- **Chairman & MD:** Sanjai Kumar | **CFO:** V. Rama Manohara Rao
- **Segments:** Telecom Services; Project Work Services
