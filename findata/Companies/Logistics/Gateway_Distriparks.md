# Gateway Distriparks

## Company Overview
Gateway Distriparks Limited (GDL), incorporated in 2005 and headquartered in New Delhi, is an integrated inter-modal logistics services provider in India. It offers general and bonded warehousing, rail and road transportation, container handling, temperature-controlled warehousing, and value-added services, owning and operating rail rakes and a fleet of trailers. (Formerly known as GatewayRail Freight Limited; the rail freight business was demerged in a prior restructuring.)
