# Container Corporation of India

## Company Overview

Container Corporation of India Limited (CONCOR) is a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Railways, specializing in container handling, rail transportation, warehousing, and multimodal logistics solutions. The company is the dominant player in India's containerized freight and trade logistics.
