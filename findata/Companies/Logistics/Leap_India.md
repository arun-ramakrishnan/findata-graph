## The Chatter — Milky Mist, Bluestone, Leap India, Avanti & More
**Pooling value proposition:** Palletisation lifted a global beverage client's truck throughput from 50–60/day to 300+/day — the productivity case behind stickiness; no customer lost since inception.
**Index-linked pricing:** 5–9% annual price increases tied to the German wood price index — a built-in inflation hedge; per-pallet yield rose from ₹1.45 to ₹1.54 (+5–6% YoY).
**Capex discipline:** Crate costs nearly doubled (₹650 → ₹1,000–1,200), so Q1 asset investment was cut to ₹76 cr (vs ₹110 cr LY); growth is coming from faster asset retrieval and pooling efficiency rather than new buys, protecting ROCE.
**Volume vs price:** Movement Hire pallets +8% QoQ (711K → 766K) against +21% revenue growth — textile-sector softness dragged volumes while pricing carried the mix.
**Structural unlock + M&A:** ~1,000 truck sizes fragment Indian road logistics; Leap is lobbying (with OEMs) for ≤10 standard sizes, which could cut supply-chain costs by 5–6% of GDP. Board-disclosed acquisition targets in India and abroad, with clarity expected in Q2–Q3 FY27.
