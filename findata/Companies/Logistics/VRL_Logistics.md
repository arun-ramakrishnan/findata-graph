**On-role driver model:** VRL keeps drivers as direct employees rather than contractors — a strategic moat in a skilled-driver-short market that supports premium pricing and >21% EBITDA margins.

**Targeted fuel cost pass-through:** Rising diesel costs are passed on via selective route-level rate tweaks rather than blanket hikes, protecting margins without losing price-sensitive volume.

**Owned hub & warehouse strategy:** Heavy capex (~₹300-350 cr annually: ₹100-150 cr vehicles, ₹200+ cr land/buildings) on owned hubs cuts long-term rental costs and stabilizes operations.

**Pivot back to expansion:** After a year of pruning non-performing locations, VRL targets ~100 net new branches and ~500 additional owned trucks (400 to be added by December) to drive FY27 volume growth.

**DFC threat dismissed:** Management argues the Dedicated Freight Corridor is irrelevant to VRL's fragmented parcel/LTL commodity mix, which does not directly compete with full-truckload railway services.
