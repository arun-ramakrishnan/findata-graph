# CMS Info Systems Limited

## Company Overview

CMS Info Systems Limited (incorporated 2008; headquartered in Mumbai) is India's largest cash management and ATM outsourcing company. It serves banks (including SBI, HDFC, ICICI and others) and retail clients, managing ~36,000 ATMs under management. The company went public via IPO in December 2021.

It operates through three segments: **Cash Management Services**, **Managed Services**, and **Cards**.

## Product Portfolio / Business Segments

- **Cash Management Services:** Cash-in-transit for banks; ATM cash management (cash processing, cassette management, ATM replenishment, cash evacuation for note accepting/recycling, day-end reporting, reconciliation); retail cash management (cash pickup/deposit logistics, Cash-X electronic, retail cash vault solutions).
- **Managed Services:** Banking automation (ATM manufacturing and maintenance, currency recyclers, self-service kiosks); brown-label ATM deployment and managed services for banks; ATM multi-vendor software and automated ATM security software; remote monitoring technology.
- **Cards:** Card personalization software, card fulfillment, and trading in card services.
