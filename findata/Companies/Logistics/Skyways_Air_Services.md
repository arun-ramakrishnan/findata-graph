# Skyways Air Services

## Company Overview

Skyways Air Services is an Indian air freight forwarder providing integrated logistics across air, ocean and express cargo, leveraging a technology-driven platform and an international footprint (Middle East, Vietnam) to serve pharma, textiles, automotive and other sectors. It runs an asset-light model — no owned aircraft or ships — with full fuel-cost pass-through contractual structures.

## The Chatter — Maruti Hy-Tech Symbiotec

**National air-cargo tailwind:** The Ministry of Civil Aviation targets ~10 million metric tonnes of air cargo within five years (from 3.96 million MT last year); Skyways has outgrown market growth for the last 10 years and expects a larger share of the expanded market as new airports and aircraft arrive.

**ASAP digital booking platform:** Launching a platform to combine the traditional network's strengths with tech-enabled visibility and scalability, aimed especially at tier-2/tier-3 customers with limited access to competitive pricing and carrier capacity.

**Read revenue growth as volume first:** ~23–25% of the recent revenue increase came through volumes; the rest is per-unit logistics cost (fuel index) — pricing fluctuates with fuel markets, so volume growth is the truest health measure.

**Full fuel pass-through:** Fuel index changes are passed through to customers in air, ocean and trucking, insulating margins from energy-price spikes (yields rise and fall with the index).

**Middle East + Vietnam build-out:** Saudi Arabia's 5–6 year infrastructure expansion, the UAE as the Middle East gateway, and a strong existing Vietnam presence are expected to add substantial value over the next 2–3 years; asset-light ownership lets the company pivot between trade lanes quickly when disruption hits.
