# Gati

## Overview

Gati Limited is an express delivery and logistics company operating as part of Allcargo group. The company provides express delivery services and logistics solutions primarily in India, serving B2B customers across multiple industry segments.

## Business Segments

### Express Delivery Services
- **Surface Express**: Surface transportation for time-sensitive shipments
- **Air Express**: Air cargo services for urgent deliveries
- **Rail Express**: Railway-based express services for cost-effective transport
- **Multi-modal Solutions**: Integrated multi-modal transportation solutions

### Logistics Solutions
- **Supply Chain Management**: End-to-end supply chain solutions
- **Warehousing**: Storage and warehousing facilities across India
- **Distribution**: Last-mile distribution services
- **Freight Forwarding**: Freight forwarding and consolidation services

### Specialized Services
- **Cold Chain**: Temperature-controlled logistics for perishable goods
- **Project Logistics**: Specialized logistics for project cargo
- **E-commerce Logistics**: Last-mile delivery for e-commerce customers
- **Industrial Logistics**: Specialized solutions for industrial sectors
