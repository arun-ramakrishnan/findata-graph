# ABS Marine Services

ABS Marine Services Limited, formerly known as ABS Marine Services Private Limited, has been operating since 1992. Over years, the company has become a leading player in ship management, ship owning, marine services, and port services demonstrating significant growth and expertise in maritime industry.

## Business Segments
- **Ship Management**: End-to-end technical & crew management for 36+ vessels
- **Ship Owning**: Owns 9 ships including DP-2 OSVs, AHTS, Harbour Crafts
- **Marine Services**: Vessel chartering, inspections, dry docking, SPM operations
- **Port Services**: Mooring, patrolling, firefighting, oil spill response

## Growth Drivers
- **Maritime Sector Growth**: India handles ~95% of trade by volume through ports
- **Offshore Energy**: Vital contributor to global oil and gas supply (~30% output)
- **Vision 2047**: Government-backed expansion with 1,000 ships target
- **Infrastructure Development**: 6 new mega ports planned under Sagarmala

## Financial Highlights
- **Hybrid Model**: Mix of asset-light and asset-backed strategies
- **Long-term Contracts**: PSU and government charters ensuring revenue stability
- **Diversified Revenue Streams**: Multiple services across vessel lifecycle
