# Afcom Holdings

## Company Overview

Afcom Holdings specializes in operating cargo flights to ASEAN countries with a focus on Singapore, offering airport-to-airport cargo transportation and supply chain solutions to a wide range of customers.

## Key Insights (Points & Figures #22)

- Diverse cargo mix: odd-dimensional cargo (29%), general cargo (28%), dangerous goods (26%), other goods (12%), hazardous materials (5%)
- Strong 2026 operational growth: trips rose from 372 (Q1) to 502 (Q3); charge weight from ~5,080 to ~6,143 tons; revenue/kg and yield improved
- Positions as standalone cargo airline focused on 24-hour delivery, last-mile connectivity, track & trace, bonded trucking, and chartering
- Partnerships with DHL, DB Schenker, Turkish Airlines, Etihad, Kuehne+Nagel, DSV, Virgin Atlantic strengthening global cargo network
- Focus on fast, flexible logistics solutions for specialized freight capabilities
