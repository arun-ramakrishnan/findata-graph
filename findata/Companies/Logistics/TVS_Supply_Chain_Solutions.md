## The Chatter — MazDock Mphasis Redington

**Headwinds are commercially accretive:** Rising fuel costs and container/route delays are passed through contractually, while Fortune 500 clients building parallel supply-chain hubs and inventory buffers in India are driving network volumes up.

**Cost pass-through protects margins:** The majority of contracts carry cost-pass-through mechanisms; the freight-forwarding business delivers a 4% EBITDA margin under this structure.

**FY27: profit growing faster than revenue:** ₹500 crore of new annualised revenue added over the last two quarters on a robust pipeline and very low churn; FY27 guided at mid-to-high-teen consolidated revenue growth with EBITDA expanding significantly faster; 20–25% conversion implies ₹2,000+ crore of new annualised revenue this year; 4% PBT margin target is mix/cost/pricing-driven and independent of freight-rate volatility.
