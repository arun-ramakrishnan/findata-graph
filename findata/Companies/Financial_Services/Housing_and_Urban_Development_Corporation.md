# Housing and Urban Development Corporation

## Company Overview
Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited (HUDCO), incorporated in 1970 and headquartered in New Delhi, is a government-owned corporation under the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. It provides term loans and financing for housing and urban infrastructure projects — serving state government agencies, housing boards, urban local bodies, and implementing agencies — and offers research, training, and consultancy services.
