# 360 ONE WAM

## Company Overview
360 ONE WAM (formerly IIFL Wealth Management) is a leading Indian wealth and asset management firm specialising in the ultra-high-net-worth (UHNW) segment. The company provides integrated advisory, distribution, and alternative investment solutions through a full-stack platform spanning wealth management, asset management (AIF, PMS, mutual funds), and private credit.
