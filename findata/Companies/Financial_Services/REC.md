## Company Overview

REC Limited (formerly Rural Electrification Corporation) is a leading infrastructure finance company providing financial assistance for power sector projects including generation, transmission, and distribution. The company serves central/state government power utilities as well as private sector power utilities.

**Founded:** 1969 | **Headquarters:** Gurugram, India

### Key Business Segments
- **Long-term Loans:** Financial assistance for power generation projects
- **Transmission Financing:** Funding for power transmission infrastructure  
- **Distribution Finance:** Support for power distribution networks
- **Renewable Energy Projects:** Financing for solar, wind, and other renewable projects
- **Policy Implementation:** Nodal agency for government schemes like Surya Ghar Yojana
