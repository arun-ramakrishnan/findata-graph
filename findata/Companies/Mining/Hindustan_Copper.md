# Hindustan Copper

## Company Overview
Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL; incorporated 1967; HQ Kolkata) is India's only vertically integrated primary copper producer, spanning exploration, mining, beneficiation, smelting, and refining. Its flagship is the Malanjkhand copper project (Madhya Pradesh); it sells continuous-cast copper rods, cathodes, concentrates, copper sulfate, sulphuric acid, and by-products.

## Key Insights
- India holds just ~2.16 mn tonnes of copper reserves (~0.2% of global); FY25 mine production ~25.24 Kt (~0.12% of world output) — a tiny but strategic domestic base.
- Per-capita copper consumption in India is only ~0.6 kg vs the world average of 3.2 kg (Germany 13.6 kg, China 8.4 kg), implying large structural demand runway as India industrializes and urbanizes.
- Ambitious expansion roadmap: mine capacity to scale from 3.47 mn tonnes (FY25) to ~12.2 mn tonnes by FY31 (~4x) via capex and Mine Developer-Operator (MDO) models.
- Demand drivers: Make in India, smart cities, renewables, metro/rail, and EV adoption; HCL is the sole domestic miner while Hindalco, Vedanta, Adani, and JSW operate as import-reliant custom smelters. *(Source: Points & Figures — Opportunity Between The Lines)*
