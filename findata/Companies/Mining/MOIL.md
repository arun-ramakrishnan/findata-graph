# MOIL

## Company Overview
MOIL Limited, founded in 1896 (formerly Manganese Ore India Limited, renamed 2010) and headquartered in Nagpur, is a Schedule 'A' Miniratna Category-I central public sector company and India's largest producer of manganese ore. It operates 10 mines across Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh, manufacturing high/medium/blast-furnace/dioxide-grade manganese ores, electrolytic manganese dioxide (EMD) and high-carbon ferro manganese, plus wind and solar power generation assets (4.8 MW Nagda Hills, 15.2 MW Ratedi Hills, 5 MW Maharashtra, 5.5 MW MP). MOIL fulfils about half of India's dioxide ore requirement.

## Key Insights (from Edition #23, Mar 2026)
- ~95-96% of manganese demand is metallurgical (steel & alloys), with ferro and silico manganese forming the bulk of consumption — making the sector a direct proxy on the steel cycle and infrastructure demand.
- India's manganese ore production is targeted to scale from ~12-18 lakh tonnes historically to 35 lakh tonnes by FY30, implying a strong ramp-up trajectory reflecting capacity expansion and rising domestic demand visibility (driven by steel/alloy consumption).
- India remains structurally import-dependent (~5.5 MT average imports over last 5 years) despite steady domestic production growth; MOIL dominates domestic supply (~45-53% share of India's production), but the gap between consumption (~avg 3.1 MT domestic production) and demand highlights a persistent supply deficit.
- Global manganese supply is highly concentrated — South Africa (~46%) and Gabon (~19%) dominate world production while India contributes only ~5%; this concentration creates supply-side risks and pricing-power cycles globally.
- Reserves: manganese reserves in India are concentrated in Odisha (~34%), Karnataka (~25%) and MP/Maharashtra (~12% each); MOIL has significantly expanded its reserve base — total reserves + resources grew from 90.00 MT (Apr 2020) to 121.97 MT (Apr 2025).
