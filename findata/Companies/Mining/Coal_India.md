# Coal India

## Overview

Coal India Limited (CIL) is the largest coal mining company in the world, operating through a B2B model with the Government of India as its primary customer. The company plays a critical role in India's energy security, supplying coal to thermal power plants, steel plants, and other industries.

### Coal Mining Operations
- **Underground Mining**: Underground coal mining operations across multiple coalfields
- **Opencast Mining**: Surface mining operations with higher productivity
- **Washery Operations**: Coal beneficiation and washing facilities for quality improvement
- **Coal Preparation**: Crushing, sizing, and blending of coal for customer requirements

### Regional Operations
- **Eastern Coalfields**: Operations in Jharkhand, West Bengal, Odisha, and Chhattisgarh
- **Central Coalfields**: Operations in Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh
- **Western Coalfields**: Operations in Maharashtra
- **Northern Coalfields**: Operations in Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh
- **Mahanadi Coalfields**: Operations in Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh

### Subsidiaries
- **Neyveli Lignite**: Lignite mining for thermal power plants in Tamil Nadu
- **CMPDIL**: Coal and mineral exploration in India and abroad
- **ECL Mines**: Coal operations through Eastern Coalfields
- **BCCL**: Coal operations through Bharat Coking Coal

### Industry Standing
- **Market Cap**: Mega-cap company with strategic importance
- **Business Model**: B2B with Government of India as primary customer
- **Strategic Importance**: Critical for India's energy security
- **Market Leadership**: World's largest coal mining company
