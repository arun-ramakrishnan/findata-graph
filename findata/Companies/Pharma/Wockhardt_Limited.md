# Wockhardt Limited

**Sector:** Healthcare

## Company Overview

Wockhardt Limited is a global pharmaceutical and biotechnology organisation, providing affordable, high-quality medicines for a healthier world. It is India's leading research-based global healthcare enterprise with relevance in the fields of Healthcare, Biotechnology and a chain of advanced Super Speciality Hospitals. The company's businesses include manufacture and marketing of pharmaceutical and bio-pharmaceutical formulations, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and vaccines.
