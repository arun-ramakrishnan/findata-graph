## The Chatter — Bajaj Finserv Zydus BEL

**Saroglitazar clinical validation in two liver indications:** New Phase 3 PBC analysis presented at ACG 2026 showed consistent biochemical response vs placebo across US and non-US subgroups (in inadequate responders/intolerant to UDCA); the Evidence-IV global Phase 2b in MASH met its primary endpoint — steatohepatitis resolution without fibrosis worsening at 52 weeks.

**US mix shift toward specialty is compounding:** Specialty products hit 16% of US revenues, up ~550 bps sequentially; Rolvedon keeps gaining, and USFDA accepted the BLA for the Pembrolizumab biosimilar — a major biologics milestone alongside the upcoming Saroglitazar launch.

**US Saroglitazar launch economics:** March launch targeted (GDUFA date November; no December year-end launch), with ~$100M/year of commercialisation expense as the sales field force and front-end teams are hired.

**India to outgrow IPM by 200-400 bps:** Management guides market growth of 10-11% and consistent 200-400 bps outperformance; the last two quarters' momentum is expected to continue.

**Transdermal patch capacity is the binding constraint:** Demand exceeds served market by ~50% and no competitor can add capacity quickly; Zydus could double its transdermal capacity within about a year — a significant unlock for the segment.

**R&D concentrated on complexity:** R&D guided at 8-8.5% of sales and still growing; only 10-12 of 30 filed products are conventional generics — the rest are first-to-file, complex generics, biologics, vaccines, specialty and NCEs. Net debt fell from ₹5,900 cr to ₹5,200 cr while management stays comfortable up to 1x net debt/EBITDA to fund the capex cycle.

> "Notably, specialty products accounted for 16% of US revenues during the quarter, up approximately 550 basis points sequentially, underscoring the pace and success of our strategic transformation."
> — Dr. Sharvil Patel, Managing Director
