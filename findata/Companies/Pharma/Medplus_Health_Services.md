# Medplus Health Services

## Company Overview
MedPlus Health Services Limited, founded in 2006 and headquartered in Hyderabad, is a leading omnichannel healthcare retailer with a network of ~5,000 pharmacy stores, an online pharmacy, path labs, and optical services. It offers genuine medicines, wellness and FMCG products, leveraging technology-driven supply-chain efficiencies, and is expanding private-label non-pharma categories.
