# Ipca Laboratories Limited

## Company Overview
Ipca Laboratories Limited, incorporated in 1949 and headquartered in Mumbai, is an integrated pharmaceutical company manufacturing formulations and active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs). Its API portfolio spans anti-hypertensive, anti-malarial, DMARD, and anthelmintic therapies, while formulations cover 30+ therapeutic segments including allergy, anti-cancer, anti-arthritic, anti-epileptic, cardiology, diabetes, dermatology, gastroenterology, neurology, NSAIDs, nutraceuticals, psychiatry, rheumatology, and urology. The company exports worldwide.
