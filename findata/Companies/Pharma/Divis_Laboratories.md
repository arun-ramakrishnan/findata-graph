# Divis Laboratories

## Company Overview

Divis Laboratories Limited is a leading Indian pharmaceutical company specializing in Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), intermediates, and nutraceutical ingredients. The company serves global innovator and generic pharmaceutical companies with a strong B2B focus on complex chemistry and regulatory compliance.

## The Chatter — ONGC, Divi's, LG, Pinelabs & More

**Geopolitical supply pressure, no stoppages:** Multiple suppliers invoked force majeure (Middle East conflict); solvents and intermediates tightened, but Divi's had no production stoppages and secured material month-by-month for the next 3 months.

**Demand healthy, pricing the challenge:** Generics see slight pricing pressure, but no volume/supply losses with any customer; growth in Q4 came from existing portfolio, not from the 2026-27 patent-expiry opportunities still pending customer launches.

**Long-term contracts cushion input inflation:** Most generic APIs are backed by long-term contracts with raw-material variability clauses, reviewed quarterly.

**Peptides — strong positioning:** Several 3,000-liter SPPS reactors ("by far in India nobody has") underpin an ambition to be among the largest global peptide players; commercial traction already in iodine-based contrast media with top innovators.

**Margins below 32–38% historic peaks:** Generic pricing pressure plus higher material costs are the two drags; management targets a return to ~38% subject to market conditions. Unit 3 (Kakinada) is absorbing pre-chemistry/backward integration to free up GMP capacity at Units 1 & 2.

> "Most of our APIs on the generic segment are backed by long-term contracts, which have variability clauses, which protects us from such situations."
> — Dr. Kiran S. Divi, CEO

## The Chatter — Indian Oil Muthoot Maruti

**Peptides = strategic investment axis:** Capacity expansion across solid-phase (SPPS) and liquid-phase peptide synthesis continues; qualification/validation of multiple peptide fragments advancing over coming quarters alongside deeper process/analytical capability for complex chemistries.

> "Peptides remain a strategic area of investment for the company... we continue to strengthen the process development, analytical, and manufacturing capabilities required for increasingly complex peptide chemistries."
> — Dr. Kiran S Divi, Whole-time Director & CEO

**Unique backward integration in peptides:** Divi's builds its own peptide building blocks from basic raw materials — protected amino acids, di-/tri-peptides, fragments — the only player with the complete chain, a cost/reliability edge for global innovators.

**Unit 3 enabling back-integration:** New Unit 3 takes over early-stage chemical operations, freeing GMP capacity at Units 1 & 2 and improving network flexibility / capacity utilisation.

**Solvent inflation pressing margins; customer pricing in motion:** Solvent costs stayed elevated through much of the period; West Asia geopolitics adding supply-chain uncertainty. Engaging customers on commercial mechanisms to recover the input inflation.

**3-month rolling inventory buffer:** Since March, operating on a rolling 3-month raw-material inventory — no production losses or shipment stoppages in recent months despite global trade disruption.

**Iodine contrast-media — multi-year contracts:** Long-term supply deals with two customers (multi-year) being finalised; commercialisation with one already underway, the second in the next few months.
