# Divis Laboratories

## Company Overview

Divis Laboratories Limited is a leading Indian pharmaceutical company specializing in Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), intermediates, and nutraceutical ingredients. The company serves global innovator and generic pharmaceutical companies with a strong B2B focus on complex chemistry and regulatory compliance.
