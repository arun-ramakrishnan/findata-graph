# J B Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals

## Overview

J B Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Limited is an Indian pharmaceutical company engaged in the manufacturing and marketing of pharmaceutical formulations, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), and specialty chemicals. The company operates in both domestic and international markets, focusing on chronic and acute therapeutic segments. With a strong focus on research and development, J B Chemicals serves various pharmaceutical market segments including cardiovascular, anti-infective, gastro-intestinal, and respiratory therapeutic areas.
