# Aarti Drugs

*Source: Patterns_Beneath_the_Surface.md*

--------------------------------------------------

Aarti Drugs manufactures and sells Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), Pharma Intermediates, and Specialty Chemicals, and also produces Formulations through its subsidiary, Pinnacle Life Science.
## [Presentation]
Aarti Drugs is among the world's largest producers of several key APIs, includin Nimesulide, Ketoconazole, Tinidazole, and Metronidazole Benzoate. With 14 manufacturing facilities, over 80 finished products, and a monthly capacity of 5,1 MT, the company exports to 100+ countries, contributing $\sim 35 \%$ of its revenue fro exports.

The company operates across APIs, formulations, and specialty chemicals, supported by strong backward integration. It manufactures 50+ API molecules across major therapeutic areas, runs WHO-GMP and USFDA-approved formulation plants, and produces specialty chemicals like benzene and chlorosulphonic derivatives, ensuring a vertically integrated and diversified business model.
