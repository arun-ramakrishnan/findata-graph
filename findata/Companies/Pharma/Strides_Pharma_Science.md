## Company Overview

Strides Pharma Science Limited (incorporated 1990; formerly Strides Shasun, renamed in 2018; headquartered in Bengaluru) is a large-cap global pharmaceutical company engaged in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of generic formulations and branded generics. The company has a global footprint spanning Africa, Australia, North America, Europe, and Asia.
