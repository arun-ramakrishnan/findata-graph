# Sanofi India Limited

## Company Overview
Sanofi India Limited, incorporated in 1956 (formerly Aventis Pharma, renamed May 2012) and headquartered in Mumbai, is a subsidiary of Hoechst GmbH. The company manufactures and trades in pharmaceutical products across diabetes, cardiology, anti-infectives, and CNS therapies. Key brands include Lantus, Toujeo, Clexane, Amaryl, Cardace, Combiflam, DePura, Allegra, Buscogast, Dulcoflex, and Enterogermina.
