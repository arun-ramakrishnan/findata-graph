# Concord Biotech Limited

## Company Overview
Concord Biotech Limited, incorporated in 1984 and headquartered in Ahmedabad, is a biopharma company specializing in fermentation-based APIs. It serves global customers across therapeutic areas including immunosuppressants, oncology, anti-bacterials, anti-fungals, and statins, and also provides CDMO services. The company listed on the exchanges via IPO in August 2023.
