## Company Overview

Lupin Limited is India's 12th largest generic pharmaceutical company globally and largest in the US by prescriptions dispensed. The company operates across complex generics, branded drugs, and biosimilars with a strong presence in respiratory, diabetes, and cardiovascular therapies.

### Business Transformation

**Respiratory Franchise Success**
- Respiratory franchise has become one-third of US revenue
- Represents fundamental business model transformation
- MDI (Metered Dose Inhaler) and DPI (Dry Powder Inhaler) business in US
- Material contributor to business in US and globally
- Differentiates organization from competitors

**Strategic Government Partnership**
- Direct engagement with US National Security Council (White House)
- Government has identified 9 essential drugs
- Exploring partnership dialogue between India and US
- Indicates strategic partner status, not just commercial supplier relationship
- Builds on existing strong US market position

**Margin Performance and Adjustments**
- Reported EBITDA margin: 23.2%
- Core business margins actually ~30% when adjusting for investments
- Adjusted EBITDA margin would be close to 26%
- Adjacencies started in recent times still making losses
- Loss-making adjacencies represent additional 3.7% margin drag
- Management provides transparent margin analysis

**US Business Guidance**
- Management provided concrete guidance: USD 250M+ quarterly US run rate
- Acknowledges competitive pressures in US market
- Conservative guidance relative to internal expectations
- Expects some products to decline
- Tolvaptan and continued growth in Spiriva to offset declines
- Maintains focus on respiratory franchise expansion
