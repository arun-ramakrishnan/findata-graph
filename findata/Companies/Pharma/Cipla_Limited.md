## Company Overview

Cipla Limited is one of India's leading pharmaceutical companies with a global presence across respiratory, HIV/AIDS, anti-infectives, and other therapeutic areas. The company operates in India, North America, South Africa, and EMEU markets with a strong focus on complex generics and respiratory products.

## Recent Updates (The Chatter - Inflection Watch Edition #12)

### Market Dynamics

**Indian Pharma Market Deceleration**
- Overall market growth rate reduced from 10-12% to 8-10%
- Due to end of patent cliff cycle with fewer new product introductions
- Products that went off patent: dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, sacubitril/valsartan
- Completion of "NI season" of expiries reducing growth by 1-1.5%
- Lower inflation reducing pricing adjustments by additional 1-1.5%
- Market fundamentally shifting from double-digit to high single-digit growth

**EBITDA Margin Performance**
- Management transparently guides for 150 basis point EBITDA margin compression in FY26
- Compression primarily due to Revlimid exclusivity loss
- Exceeded guidance range in current year
- 150 basis points above guided range demonstrates strong execution
- Maintaining transparency on margin pressure from key product exclusivity

**Regulatory Excellence**
- Exceptional regulatory success with VAI classification across all facilities
- US FDA audited 5 Indian manufacturing facilities:
  - Patalganga
  - Kurkumbh
  - Goa
  - Virgonagar
  - Medispray
- All inspections classified as VAI (Voluntary Action Indicated)
- Reflects dedication to quality, compliance and operational excellence
- Significantly de-risks business from regulatory delays

**Capital Allocation Strategy**
- Disciplined approach focusing on high-return opportunities
- India as primary growth market with good return on capital
- Investment in people and capex expansion
- Looking at small to large M&A opportunities
- M&A can be in form of companies, product portfolios, etc.
- US portfolio additions through complex generic ANAs/NDAs
- Milestone upfront payments for assets with full commercialization rights

## The Chatter — Titan, Dixon, JSW Steel, Cipla & More

**India franchise scale:** Domestic business crossed the ₹12,500 crore revenue threshold, reinforcing India as Cipla's largest franchise and validating resilience of the home-market engine.

**US inhaler inflection:** First AB-rated generic Ventolin HFA approval from the US facility marks Cipla's first commercial MDI product manufactured in the US — de-risks the supply chain and proves complex-generic capability outside India.

**Biosimilars runway:** Management frames biosimilars as a ~$200 billion opportunity with ~100 biologics expected to lose exclusivity over the decade; plans to build 6–8 internal assets supplemented by inorganic opportunities.

**Capital allocation shift:** R&D (respiratory, peptides, differentiated products) is the #1 cash deployment; the elevated capex cycle of the last 3 years is expected to taper after another year as sufficient manufacturing capacity is now in place.
