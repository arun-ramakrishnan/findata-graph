# Glenmark Pharmaceuticals

## Company Overview
Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Limited (incorporated 1977; HQ Mumbai) is an Indian multinational drugmaker serving India, North America, Europe, and emerging markets. It focuses on branded & generic formulations across dermatology, respiratory, oncology, and cardiology, plus new chemical/biological entities (NCEs/NBEs) via its innovation subsidiary IGI.
