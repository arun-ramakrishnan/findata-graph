# AstraZeneca Pharma India Limited

## Company Overview

AstraZeneca Pharma India Limited (incorporated 1979, headquartered in Bengaluru) is a large-cap biopharmaceutical company and a subsidiary of AstraZeneca Pharmaceuticals AB (Sweden), which holds majority ownership. The company manufactures, markets, and trades innovative pharmaceutical products in India and internationally, with approximately 600 employees.

AstraZeneca Pharma India operates across critical therapeutic areas including cardiovascular, renal, metabolic, immunological, oncology, rare diseases, and respiratory. The company collaborates with partners such as Leica Biosystems and Daiichi Sankyo.
