# NATCO Pharma Limited

## Company Overview

NATCO Pharma Limited (incorporated 1981, headquartered in Hyderabad) is a large-cap pharmaceutical company engaged in the research, development, manufacturing, and marketing of finished dosage formulations, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), and intermediates in India and internationally. The Nannapaneni family is the promoter with majority ownership.

The company also provides agro chemicals and biological pest management solutions and contract manufacturing services.
