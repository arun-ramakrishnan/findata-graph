# Laurus Labs

## Company Overview
Laurus Labs Limited (incorporated 2005; HQ Hyderabad) is a global pharmaceutical company offering active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), generic finished dosage forms (FDF), and contract research/manufacturing (CDMO) services. It has a strong presence in anti-retroviral (ARV), Hepatitis C, oncology, cardio-metabolic, and ophthalmology therapies, with ~7,000 employees.

## The Chatter — RBI Tata Steel

**CDMO + utilisation drove the beat:** Q1 profit jumped on significant CDMO revenue growth plus higher plant utilisation and stronger API performance — operational leverage doing the work as the mix shifts away from legacy anti-retrovirals.

**Margin profile stepped up:** Gross margin expanded from 59% to ~63% YoY and EBITDA by ~7 pts to near 32%, reflecting a move up the value chain into higher-priced products.

**Guidance — structural, not cyclical:** Mgmt confident of holding >60% gross margin and >30% EBITDA margin for the full year, signalling the margin gains are durable rather than a one-off.

**ARV base stabilising in absolute terms:** Anti-retroviral revenue guided to ₹2,600–2,800 cr for the next several years — share of mix shrinks as other divisions (oncology, biologics, ADCs) grow, reducing therapy-area concentration.

**₹2,000 cr capex, internally funded:** FY26 investment spans small-molecule APIs, drug product, bio and ADCs; mostly funded from internal accruals, keeping net debt/EBITDA comfortable even with modest incremental borrowing.

**Low US generic exposure (~5%):** Shielded from US trade-policy/tariff risk given how small the FDF-to-US contribution is.
