# Symbiotec Pharmalab

## Company Overview

Symbiotec Pharmalab is a research-led pharmaceutical company specialising in steroid and hormone active pharmaceutical ingredients. It is vertically integrating into complex injectables (a proprietary dual-chamber drug-device platform) and biotechnology-based CDMO/biomanufacturing services for Western biotech partners, with fermentation capacity run under FDA and EU GMP inspection for years.

## The Chatter — Maruti Hy-Tech Symbiotec

**CDMO for the 'valley of death':** Positions itself as the manufacturing partner for Western synthetic-biology companies whose lab discoveries stall at commercial scale — pairing regulatory experience (FDA/EU GMP fermentation), process know-how and in-house engineered plants so partners avoid years of development time and capital.

**Forward integration into complex injectables:** A proprietary ready-to-use/ready-to-dispense dual-chamber vial-and-bag platform integrates drug and diluent in one sealed device — fewer preparation steps, less contamination and dosing risk — a higher-value finished-dose move up the chain from APIs.

**Capacity is the binding constraint:** Signed agreements and term sheets imply the need to build ~4x current biomanufacturing capacity across Phase 0 (existing), Phase 1 and Phase 2; a large part of existing capacity is already booked.

**Products ready, lines aren't:** 5–6 injectable products are developed and enter validation in coming quarters; management worries about capacity, not products, and may need a second line very soon after launch.

**Capex ₹200–250 cr/year:** Investment of roughly ₹200–250 crore annually for the next 2–3 years, funded from operating cash flows, with willingness to accelerate if more long-term customer contracts land.

> "Based on the visibility we already have, we believe that we will continue to invest approximately Rs.200-250 crores each year. If more opportunities come our way, we will have to be more innovative in determining how to fund those very large and optimistic capex requirements."
> — Anil Satwani, Chairman & MD
