# Pfizer Limited (India)

## Company Overview
Pfizer Limited (India), incorporated in 1950 and headquartered in Mumbai, is Pfizer's Indian subsidiary and a drug manufacturer operating in the general pharmaceuticals segment. Its therapeutic span covers neuro/CNS, hormones, cardiovascular, haemophilia, anti-infectives, gynecology, pain, orthopedic, oncology, urology, vaccines, respiratory, and vitamins.
