# Fortis Healthcare

## Company Overview
Fortis Healthcare Limited is one of India's leading private healthcare providers, operating a network of multi-specialty hospitals, diagnostics centers, and healthcare facilities through its subsidiaries, associates, and joint ventures. The company is led by Chairman and Managing Director Abhay Soi.
