## The Chatter — HUL Fortis Shiprocket

**Delhi HC forensic audit — four-part scope laid out:** (1) reconstruction of the complex evolution of erstwhile promoter entity FHHPL (2011–2018), which held the Fortis shares; (2) examination of IHH/TK's acquisition of the controlling stake in FHL — approvals, filings, and use of the investment amounts toward the RHT Health Trust (Singapore) healthcare-asset acquisition; (3) any role of FHL and its officers/KMPs in processing the erstwhile promoters' unencumbered share dissipation; (4) the role of all 13 banks and financial institutions in the dissipation of those assets (Dr. Ashutosh Raghuvanshi, MD & CEO).

**IHH commitment unchanged:** The majority owner reiterates the intent to take its stake above 50% and stands "fully committed" to infusing growth capex within legal limits — signalling the platform's strategic value survives the legal overhang (Dilip Kadambi, IHH Group CFO).

**Company distances itself from legacy transfers:** Management argues FHL played no role in the historical promoter-to-promoter share transfers — shares moved through the registrar at a time when no compliance-officer clearance rule existed, and the company was unaware of the transactions.

> "The company has no role in share transfers. We are a publicly listed company. Shares are transferred between the parties through the registrar, and the company plays no role... The company was not really aware that these kinds of transactions were taking place."
> — Dr. Ashutosh Raghuvanshi, MD & CEO, Fortis Healthcare
