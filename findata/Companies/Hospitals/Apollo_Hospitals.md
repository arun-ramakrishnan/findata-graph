# Apollo Hospitals

## Company Overview

Apollo Hospitals Enterprise Limited is India's largest integrated healthcare provider, established in 1983 by Prathap C Reddy. It operates a network of hospitals, pharmacies, and diagnostic centers offering comprehensive medical services ranging from primary care to advanced tertiary treatments across Asia.

- **ARPP +11% (case-mix shift):** Increase in average revenue per inpatient despite stable occupancy indicates deliberate shift toward more complex clinical cases and specialized surgeries — strengthens competitive moat vs smaller regional players.

- **Greenfield Tier-1 expansion (asset-light → land-grab):** Massive greenfield expansion in Tier-1 cities signals pivot from asset-light to aggressive land-grab dominance.
