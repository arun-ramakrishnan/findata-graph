# Aster DM Healthcare

## Company Overview
Aster DM Healthcare Limited, founded in 1987 and headquartered in Bengaluru, is a major integrated healthcare provider operating a network of hospitals, clinics, in-house and retail pharmacies, and optical outlets across India. Following the demerger/sale of its GCC business, Aster is now an India-focused pure-play and is executing a large-scale merger with Quality Care India (QCIL) to significantly expand bed capacity and specialty reach. It operates through Hospitals, Clinics, Wholesale Pharmacies, and Others segments, with strong clusters in Karnataka/Maharashtra and Andhra/Telangana.
