# Titan Company Limited

**TITAN.NS | NSE: ₹4,231.80 | Market Cap: ₹37,272 crores | P/E: 90.09**

## Company Overview

Titan Company Limited, India's leading lifestyle conglomerate, is a Tata Group company that has transformed from a watch manufacturer to a multi-category lifestyle brand. With a market dominance in watches (60%+ market share) and jewelry (leading organized player), Titan operates through trusted brands like Tanishq, Taneira, CaratLane, Mia, and Zoya.

### Key Details
- **Founded:** 1984 (as Titan Watches, a joint venture between Tata Group and Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation)
- **Headquarters:** Bangalore, India
- **Revenue:** ₹40,000+ crores annually
- **Employee Count:** 10,000+
- **Retail Presence:** 2,500+ stores across 350+ cities
- **Export Markets:** 30+ countries

### Lab Grown Diamond Launch
- Titan launched its Lab Grown Diamond brand – BeYon
- Anticipates continued price erosion and potential commoditization in the LGD market due to technological nature and increasing competition
- "We know that this is a category which, because it is man-made and a product of technology, prices can only go down. With many players having entered it—close to 500 stores currently and about 100 odd players or more and counting—we always felt that they could get commoditized."

### Consumer Awareness & Education
- Consumers are aware but confused about LGDs, partly due to competitors misrepresenting them as natural diamond alternatives and offering inappropriate exchange policies
- "What we have recognized is that the customer, thanks to the media narrative, is now far aware of this category but also a little confused. Many players who have jumped in have looked at the low-hanging fruit of trying to pitch it as an alternate to natural diamonds offering all kinds of exchange policies, even though this category is perhaps not suited for that."

### Market Strategy
- Titan believes the Indian market is unique, allowing both natural and lab-grown diamonds to coexist and independently contribute to overall diamond adoption
- "India is an "and" market where both could coexist. There is an opportunity for both to play independent of each other in driving adoption of diamonds as a whole."
- Plans to initially focus on understanding BeYon's unit economics in a few more stores before pursuing aggressive expansion beyond top cities

### Brand Positioning
- BeYon will be a comprehensive brand with online and offline presence, focusing on fashion-forward, experimental indulgence to attract new customers
- "BeYon really plays in being fashion-forward, experimental, and plays to the indulgence aspect of diamonds. The idea is to bring consumers early into category and bring them more often, then they can cross over to Mia, CaratLane, and then Tanishq."

### Pricing & Sourcing
- Titan is pricing its LGDs competitively at Rs 23k-25k per carat, below market average
- Will offer exchange value only for the gold component, not the LGD itself
- Currently plans to rely on external, high-quality partners for LGD sourcing
- Will primarily use 14 and 18 karat gold, with some silver

### Value Proposition
- Beyond's value proposition centers on empowering consumers, especially younger women, to make independent and indulgent diamond purchases without external approval
- "To exemplify, during our pilots, a young girl working in a hotel bought a product out of sheer indulgence without checking back with her home. That is the young persona we are looking for. Another woman said when she buys from her husband's money, she buys natural, but when she buys from her own savings, she picks a lab-grown because she does not need to seek permission. This freedom is at the heart of Beyond value proposition."

- Supply chain disruptions in precious metals
