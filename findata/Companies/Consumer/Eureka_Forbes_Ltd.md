# Eureka Forbes Ltd

## Company Overview
Eureka Forbes Limited, founded in 1982 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a consumer durables company focused on health and hygiene products — Aquaguard water purifiers, Forbes vacuum cleaners, and air purifiers. It serves both homes and institutions across India (with a growing international presence) and is known for its strong brand legacy and wide service network, backed by Advent International (operating as a subsidiary of Lunolux Limited).
