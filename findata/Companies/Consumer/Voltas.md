# Voltas

## Company Overview

Voltas is India's leading air conditioning company and a major provider of electromechanical projects and engineering services. A part of the Tata Group, it operates across cooling products (room ACs, commercial refrigeration, appliances), home appliances via a joint venture, and large infrastructure / electromechanical (HVAC, water treatment, mining & construction equipment) projects.

- **Founded:** 1954 | **Headquarters:** Mumbai, India
- **Group:** Tata Group
- **Segments:** Unitary Cooling Products; Electro-Mechanical Projects & Services; Engineering Products & Services
- **Geography:** India, Middle East, Africa, and international
