## The Chatter — TCS IFB Gulf Oil

**New MD's back-to-basics agenda:** First call under Sandeep Joseph Abraham — tighten processes, fix cost structures, manage dealer network, plug trade-scheme leakages; focus on ~10,500 high-volume outlets.

**Margin pressure persists into FY27:** April–May cumulative commodity + forex negative impact ~₹49 cr; partly offset by ₹29 cr cost actions and Q1 price hikes.

**Front-loader share understated:** 23% share, but IFB doesn't compete in the 12 kg segment (13% of market); ex-12kg, share would be ~25.5–26%. Launches planned in 13 kg and 14 kg later this year.

**Brand leverage for category extension:** IFB umbrella brand and established outlet relationships from washing machines lower the entry barrier for ACs and refrigerators.

**Engineering division growth plan:** 20–25% revenue growth targeted over 2–3 years; new streams — EV battery packs, motorcycle chains, brake discs (mandated by new legislation). FY27 new-order target raised to ₹350 cr (vs ₹153 cr actual in FY26).
