# Kalyan Jewellers

## Company Overview
Kalyan Jewellers India Limited, founded in 1993 and headquartered in Thrissur, is a leading gold and precious-stone jewellery manufacturer and retailer. It designs, makes, and retails gold, diamond, silver, platinum and gemstone jewellery through large-format showrooms across India and the Middle East, alongside the "My Kalyan" grassroots store network for smaller towns and the CANDERE online platform (candere.com). It caters to wedding, regional, aspirational and studded-jewellery segments through in-house brands (MUDHRA, NIMAH, ANOKHI, RANG, TEJASVI, ZIAH, LAYA, GLO, CANDERE, VEDHA, APOORVA, HERA, MUHURAT).

## Key Insights (from Edition #24, Mar 2026)
- India's jewellery demand remains wedding-led (~60%) and disproportionately rural (~60%), with the South commanding the largest regional share (~40%) — growth is anchored in non-metro, tradition-led markets rather than discretionary urban spending; India is the 2nd-largest gold market globally.
- Kalyan is building a multi-brand, multi-product portfolio (wedding Muhurat, aspirational Mudhra/Rang, regional Aishwaryam, studded Nimah/Tejasvi/Ziah/Hera) to target diverse price points and occasions and widen its addressable market.
- Expansion is increasingly driven by an asset-light FOCO (franchise-owned, company-operated) model — enabling rapid showroom scaling (especially via Candere and new formats) while bringing down debt and lifting ROCE/ROE, making growth more capital-efficient.
- Rising share of organised retail in jewellery continues to structurally benefit large, trusted chains like Kalyan; the recyclability of gold limits inventory obsolescence risk.
