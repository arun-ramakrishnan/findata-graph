# PC Jeweller Limited

## Company Overview
PC Jeweller Limited, incorporated in 2005 and headquartered in New Delhi, manufactures and sells gold, diamond, silver jewelry, and precious stones. Its product range includes rings, earrings, pendants, gold chains, bracelets, bangles, nose pins, necklaces, mangalsutra, and men's jewelry. Products are sold through retail showrooms, franchisee showrooms, and online, with additional export operations.
