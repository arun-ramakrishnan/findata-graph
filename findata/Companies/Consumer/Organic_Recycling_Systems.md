# Organic Recycling Systems

## Company Overview
Organic Recycling Systems Limited (incorporated 2008, HQ Navi Mumbai) is an environmental-engineering company providing waste-management and valorization solutions in India. It develops, constructs, and maintains waste-to-energy projects for the municipal solid waste (MSW) sector, and offers EPC services, project management consultancy, commissioning/recommissioning, consulting, laboratory services, and proprietary equipment (anaerobic digesters, segregation lines, Sanjeevak carbonisation systems). Clients include project developers, municipal corporations, corporate institutions, and bulk generators.
