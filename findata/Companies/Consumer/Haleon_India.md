# Haleon India


## Overview
Haleon India Private Limited is the Indian subsidiary of Haleon plc, a global consumer healthcare company. The company focuses on over-the-counter (OTC) healthcare products, vitamins, minerals, and supplements (VMS), and other consumer health solutions.

## Key Business Areas
- Over-the-counter (OTC) healthcare products
- Vitamins, minerals, and supplements (VMS)
- Consumer health solutions
- Pain management products
- Respiratory health products
- Oral health products
