## The Chatter — Solar Ind PhonePe

**Gold-price volatility deferring, not destroying, demand:** August was "extremely positive and robust"; September slowed as consumers waited for the price trend to settle — enquiries and new-design interest continue, and footfalls are expected to resume once direction clarity sets in (MD & CEO Suvankar Sen). Festive (Navratri/Dhanteras) buying starts firming from end-September, and a heavy wedding calendar (Nov–Mar) extends the runway.

**Old-gold exchange is now half the market:** Exchange share of transactions jumped from 25–30% to 45–50% — "a new reality"; recycled gold adds 6–7 days of processing before it re-enters manufacturing, requiring pre-planning. Senco actively promotes household-gold recycling to keep sales moving at high price points.

**Lightweight is the name of the game:** Demand skews to lighter, budget-fitting pieces — titanium jewellery launches, everyday wear — while traditional wedding/festive pieces remain a must-stock. The Sennes lab-grown-diamond/lifestyle line is growing 40–50% YoY off a small base with quarterly collection drops.

**Mass-market core:** Premium (Designia) is only 5–7% of sales; 80–85% sits in the ₹10–20k to ₹4–5 lakh band, 7–10% above ₹5 lakh. The Flexi Advance Gold festive scheme locks in whichever rate is lower — booking date or purchase date — to beat purchase hesitation.

**Duty pain accepted, for now:** High gold duties push the parallel economy against organised retail — a real competitive drag — but management accepts the macro logic (war, crude, current-account pressure) and expects relief only after global conditions ease.

> "This is a new reality. Initially it was 25–30%; now it has gone from 25–30% to 45–50%."
> — Suvankar Sen, Managing Director & CEO, Senco Gold & Diamonds
