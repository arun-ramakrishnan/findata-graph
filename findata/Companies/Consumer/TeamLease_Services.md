# TeamLease Services

## Company Overview
TeamLease Services Limited, incorporated in 2000 and headquartered in Bengaluru, is a leading HR-services provider offering temporary staffing, permanent recruitment, compliance consultancy, IT/ITES-BPO services, vocational and higher education, apprenticeships, and HR technology platforms. Operating on an asset-light model, it serves financial services, consumer, industrial, telecom, technology, healthcare, retail, and logistics industries.
