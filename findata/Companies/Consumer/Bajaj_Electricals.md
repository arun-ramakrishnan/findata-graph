# Bajaj Electricals

## Company Overview

Bajaj Electricals Limited (NSE/BSE: BAJAJELEC) is a Bajaj Group consumer-electricals company headquartered in Mumbai, manufacturing and marketing consumer appliances (fans, lighting, small kitchen appliances, water heaters, air coolers, morphy richards) and EPC (engineering, procurement & construction) for power transmission and distribution.

## Demerger of Bajel Projects

In 2022, Bajaj Electricals **demerged its power transmission & distribution (T&D) business** — towers, monopoles, and related infrastructure — into a separate listed entity, **Bajel Projects (NSE: BAJEL)**. Bajel Projects is therefore a former subsidiary / spin-out of Bajaj Electricals, retaining the Bajaj Group's T&D engineering capability while Bajaj Electricals focuses on consumer products.
