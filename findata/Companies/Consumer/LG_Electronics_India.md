## The Chatter — ONGC, Divi's, LG, Pinelabs & More

**EXCEL strategy for FY27:** Export expansion, new-factory capability, market-leadership/brand/new-business expansion, and localization form the four-pillar roadmap.

**Localization at 55.2%, rising 1–2 pp/yr:** Aimed at cost competitiveness and managing FX volatility; INR depreciated 5.6% YoY in Q4, a meaningful import-cost headwind.

**Premium TV & dishwasher traction:** Maintained absolute leadership in premium TV with 60.0% OLED market share (YTD March 2026); dishwashers emerged as an important Q4 growth driver on urban adoption.

**French door refrigerators scaling fast:** Market share jumped from 5% to 14% by March 2026 since November launch, on track to be #1 in French door; refrigerators trending toward larger, more energy-efficient models.

**Margin reset and recovery expected:** Q4 margins hit by 5.6% INR depreciation and ~1.1% channel-promotion investments (temporary); price hikes now in place, promotions rationalizing, strong summer demand — confident of early double-digit EBITDA margins for FY27.

**New chest-freezer category entry:** Launching five models in the upcoming quarter at competitive prices into a ~₹3,000 cr market to create a new revenue stream.

> "Looking ahead, with the price hikes now in place across categories, promotional intensity rationalizing, hot summer going on, we are confident of recovering our margins for financial year '27 and to deliver our early double digit EBITDA margins for '27 full year."
