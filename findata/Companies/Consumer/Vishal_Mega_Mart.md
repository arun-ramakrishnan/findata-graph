# Vishal Mega Mart

## Company Overview
Vishal Mega Mart Limited, incorporated in 2018 and headquartered in Gurugram, is a value-focused hypermarket chain specialising in fashion (apparel), general merchandise, and fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) across India. It caters to middle and lower-middle-income consumers in Tier I, II and III cities through a mix of private labels and third-party brands, sold via physical stores, a mobile application and a website. A subsidiary of Samayat Services LLP, it is rapidly building out an omnichannel / quick-commerce platform using its stores as fulfillment hubs.

- The company is using its physical stores as fulfillment hubs for quick commerce, a strategy that counters quick-commerce threats while maintaining superior unit economics versus standalone dark-store models.
- Scale and value positioning (private labels + vast store network in Tier I/II/III cities) target the value-conscious consumer segment, with everyday new offers and seasonal collections driving repeat traffic.
- Store pickup and doorstep express delivery, plus loyalty points on every order, are deepening customer engagement and the app's role as the primary digital storefront.
