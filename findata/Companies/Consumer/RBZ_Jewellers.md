# RBZ Jewellers

## Company Overview
RBZ Jewellers Limited is a leading organized manufacturer of gold jewellery in India, specializing in Antique Bridal Gold Jewellery. The company designs and distributes a wide range of jewellery, including jadau, Meena, and Kundan work, to retailers nationwide.
