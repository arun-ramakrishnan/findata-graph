# Felix Industries

## Overview

Felix Industries Limited is based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, and operates in environmental conservation and industrial services sector. The company operates through a B2B model, providing specialized services in Water and Waste Water Recycling, Industrial Piping, Nano Products, and E-waste Recycling. Felix Industries focuses on environmental sustainability, waste management, and innovative products for industrial and environmental applications.
