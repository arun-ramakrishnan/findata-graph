# P N Gadgil Jewellers

## Company Overview

P N Gadgil Jewellers Limited is a leading Indian jewellery retailer with a centuries-old legacy, primarily focused on Maharashtra. It designs and sells gold, silver and diamond jewellery through two formats — large-format PNG legacy stores (traditional/bridal) and lightweight-focused 'U by PNG' outlets — and is executing a three-year expansion from its Maharashtra core into a nationwide footprint across seven states.

## The Chatter — MazDock Mphasis Redington

**Demand recovered; mix shifted to jewellery:** August started slowly on gold-policy announcement noise, but demand picked up from Shravan (Mangalsutra Festival, Ganesh Chaturthi footfall healthy); consumer preference shifted from investment products (bars/coins) toward jewellery, with a noticeable silver surge during Ganesh Chaturthi — positive setup into Navratri/Diwali.

**Lower caratage heading north:** 22-carat dominates Maharashtra plain gold and diamonds are 18-carat, but as PNG expands into Central/North India, 14-carat is gaining acceptance, and 9-carat is being trialled for younger demographics and online shoppers — purchasing is shifting from strict caratage to design and budget preferences.

**Nationwide 3-year framework:** Operating across seven states with targeted expansion along the Maharashtra, Goa, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar and Delhi-NCR belts.

**103 stores by FY-end:** Dual-format strategy — PNG legacy flagships for heavy bridal plus U by PNG for lightweight, non-occasion impulse jewellery — closing the current financial year with 103 operational stores.

**Studded is the growth engine:** Diamond/studded jewellery growing >40% YoY and now 12–13% of the sales mix; bullion has fallen to ~20% of revenue (jewellery ~80%), tilting the business toward value-added design margins rather than commodity trading.

> "Studded jewellery performance has been exceptionally strong at PNG Jewellers, with our diamond segment growing at over 40% year-over-year. While diamond jewellery currently accounts for 12% to 13% of our overall sales mix, it remains a rapid-growth category."
> — Saurabh Gadgil, Chairman and Managing Director
