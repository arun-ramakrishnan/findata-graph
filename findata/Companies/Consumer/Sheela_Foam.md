# Sheela Foam

## Company Overview
Sheela Foam Limited, incorporated in 1971 and headquartered in Noida, is India's leading manufacturer of polyurethane (PU) foam and foam-based comfort products. Under its flagship Sleepwell brand (plus Kurl-on, Feather Foam, Furlenco, and Lamiflex), it sells mattresses, pillows, and home-comfort items, and supplies technical/institutional foam internationally (including Spain via Interplasp and Australia via Joyce Foam).
