## The Chatter — Hitachi Glenmark Titagarh

**Festive demand shifted into Q3:** the festive sale started the second week of October vs the third week of September last year, moving seasonal sales into Q3 — the company remains on track for its full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 18-20%.
