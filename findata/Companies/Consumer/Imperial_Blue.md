# Imperial Blue

## Company Overview
Imperial Blue is a mass-premium Indian whisky brand (originally launched by United Spirits/Diageo). Known for its smooth blend and accessible price point, it is one of India's largest-selling whisky brands by volume.

## Ownership

The brand was **acquired by Tilaknagar Industries** to expand its portfolio beyond its core Mansion House brandy franchise. The acquisition added a established whisky brand to Tilaknagar's stable, and the company subsequently changed its revenue-reporting presentation to reflect the Imperial Blue business (selling expenses such as discounts and schemes are now presented within revenue, rather than netted off).
