# Thyrocare Technologies Ltd.

## Company Overview
Thyrocare Technologies Ltd. is India's first fully automated diagnostic laboratory network, established as a pioneer in automated diagnostic services sector. The company focuses on providing quality diagnostic services at affordable costs to laboratories and hospitals across India and international markets.
