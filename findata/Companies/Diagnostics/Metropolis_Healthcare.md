# Metropolis Healthcare Limited

## Company Overview

Metropolis Healthcare Limited (founded 1980, headquartered in Mumbai) is a large-cap pathology and diagnostic services chain and one of India's leading diagnostic companies. The Shah family is the promoter with majority ownership.

The company operates an omnichannel model spanning patient service centers, a mobile app, a website, and home collection services. Metropolis also partners with standalone labs and hospital chains. Internationally, it serves markets across Africa, South Asia, and the Middle East.
