# Earkart Limited

Earkart Limited is a health-tech company in India focused on making hearing care accessible by manufacturing, distributing, and trading affordable hearing aids and accessories, plus offering mobility aids and educational kits for the disabled, using innovative models like shop-in-shop kiosks and telehealth to reach Tier 2/3 cities.

## Business Segments

### Hearing Aids
- **BTE/RIC Hearing Aids**: Own manufacturing line of Behind-The-Ear and Receiver-In-Canal devices
- **Brand Partnerships**: Distribution partnership with international hearing aid brands
- **Affordable Solutions**: Competitive pricing making hearing care accessible
- **Accessories**: Complete range of hearing aid accessories and batteries

### Healthcare Services
- **OMNI Technology**: Globally unique remote diagnostic device for hearing testing
- **Remote Audiometry**: Telehealth-enabled remote hearing diagnostics
- **Shop-in-Shop Model**: Partnering with ENT doctors for retail presence
- **Clinic Network**: Target of 500 OMNI clinic installations by 2028

### Disability Support Products

- **Rehabilitation Products**: Supporting rehabilitation and independent living
