# SRL Diagnostics

## Overview

SRL Diagnostics is a leading diagnostic laboratory network providing comprehensive pathology and radiology services across India with a focus on quality and accuracy. The company operates through a B2C model with extensive laboratory and diagnostic center network serving both direct patients and healthcare institutions.

## Business Segments

### Pathology Services
- **Clinical Pathology**: Comprehensive pathology testing including biochemistry, hematology, microbiology
- **Immunology**: Specialized immunological testing and antibody screening
- **Specialized Testing**: Disease-specific test panels and screening programs
- **Reference Laboratory**: Serving as reference lab for smaller diagnostic centers

### Radiology Services
- **Diagnostic Imaging**: X-ray, CT scan, MRI, and ultrasound services
- **Digital Radiology**: Advanced digital imaging technology and reporting
- **Teleradiology**: Remote radiology consultation services
- **Imaging Services**: Comprehensive imaging across multiple modalities

### Preventive Healthcare
- **Health Checkups**: Comprehensive preventive health screening packages
- **Wellness Programs**: Preventive care and wellness screening
- **Corporate Programs**: Corporate health and wellness programs
- **Home Collection**: Home sample collection services for convenience
