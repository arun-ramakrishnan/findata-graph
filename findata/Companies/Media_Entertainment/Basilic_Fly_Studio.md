# Basilic Fly Studio

## Company Overview
Basilic Fly Studio Limited, founded in 2012 and based in Chennai, is a visual effects (VFX) studio delivering high-quality VFX solutions for movies, television series, web/OTT content, and commercials. With subsidiaries in Canada and the UK, the company specialises in creating visual experiences for global audiences. It operates an asset-light, scalable services model where client interaction and creative supervision are handled overseas while large-scale execution is delivered from India.
