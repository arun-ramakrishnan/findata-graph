# Bodhi Tree Multimedia

Bodhi Tree Multimedia Limited's (formerly known as Bodhi Tree Multimedia Private Limited) business model is B2B, wherein the primary business activity includes content production for television, films and digital platforms. It has been in the forefront of creating differentiated and edgy content through its distinct line-up of shows.

## The Current Landscape

### Consumption is exploding. Ownership is concentrated.

Media Formats are under-utilized. Platforms, not producers, typically hold content ownership and long-term value.

India's M&E industry reached ₹2.3 trillion in 2023, expected to touch ₹2.9 trillion by 2026 (FICCI-EY 2024).

### Growth Metrics
- **13% Growth**: OTT video grew 13% in 2023; FAST TV and YouTube are redefining reach.

The opportunity lies in co-production, IP ownership, and scaling content and IPs to multiple media formats (gaming, music, TV shows, merch and more).

## YouTube as Platform

### YouTube isn't just social media. It's India's largest video platform and a key driver of the creator economy.

YouTube is India's largest video platform, capturing ~92% of online video consumption and ~38% of OTT revenue share. With ₹210 billion already invested in creator economy and users spending 72+ minutes daily, it has become a central growth engine for creators and IP-led businesses.

## Digital India at Tipping Point

The next decade belongs to IP owners & builders of 'Creative Universes'.

### Industry Pioneers
Industry pioneers are taking first step to build a scalable, franchise-first content studio in India.

### Market Projections
- **$13B OTT market by 2030** (CAGR 23%)

- **40%+ of video consumption**: FAST TV, YouTube, and creator-led content now account for over 40% of video consumption (FICCI-EY)

## The Opportunity

In 2024, India created ~50,000 hours of new content.
Less than 1% is owned by people who made it.

For over a decade, they've delivered stories for every major platform in India. The coming decades will be defined by those who own those stories.
