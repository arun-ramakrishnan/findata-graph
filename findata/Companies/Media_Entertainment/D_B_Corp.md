# D. B. Corp Limited

## Company Overview

D. B. Corp Limited is one of India's leading print media companies, headquartered in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. The company is primarily known for its popular Hindi newspapers "Dainik Bhaskar" and "Divya Bhaskar", along with several regional publications and magazines. D. B. Corp has diversified its business to include radio broadcasting through MY FM and has a growing digital presence. The company employs over 10,000 people and has a strong presence across multiple states in India.
