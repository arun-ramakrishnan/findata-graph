# Cash UR Drive Marketing

Cash UR Drive Marketing is an Out of Home advertising company providing Transit Outdoor, Print, and Digital Media Services to help clients reach their target audience. They have expanded into advertising on Electric Vehicles and charging stations to tap into the growing Electric Vehicle Industry.

## Business Overview
- **Service Portfolio**: Transit Outdoor, Print, Digital Media Services
- **EV Integration**: Advertising on Electric Vehicles and charging stations
- **Market Position**: Leading outdoor and transit advertising agency
- **Campaign Experience**: Over 5,000 campaigns executed
- **Client Base**: 2,000+ clients

India's EV charging market is projected to grow from USD 348.5M in 2024 to USD 1.65B by 2030, driven by strong EV adoption and government initiatives. Charging units expected to rise from 454,000 to 2.5M by 2030.


### Outdoor Advertising Market
India's outdoor advertising market is expected to grow from USD 1.34B in 2024 to USD 2.21B by 2033 at 5.80% CAGR:

**Market Characteristics**:
- Rapid urbanization and smartphone penetration driving demand
- Digital outdoor advertising (DOOH) is fastest-growing category
- High-resolution LED formats replacing static billboards
- Programmatic ad platforms gaining traction

India's smartphone users check their phones an average of 80 times daily, spending 4.9 hours per day, creating major opportunity for connected outdoor ads.
