# PVR INOX Limited

## Overview
PVR INOX Limited, formerly known as PVR Limited, is a leading player in the movie industry engaged in exhibition, distribution, and production. In addition to movies, the company generates revenue through in-house advertising, food & beverages, gaming, and restaurant business.

## The Chatter — Edition #46 (13 Feb 2026): Tailwinds building

> Q3 FY26 earnings-call coverage. Source: Zerodha "The Chatter", Edition #46, 13 Feb 2026. [Original](findata/The_Chatter/Tailwinds_building.md)

### Key Signals (Q3 FY26)
- **CY2025 strongest year ever for Indian theatricals**: All-India gross box office INR 13,400 cr (+13% YoY; ~32% above pre-pandemic).
  > "Calendar 2025 emerged as the strongest year ever for the Indian theatrical business with the highest ever all-India gross box office collections of Rs.13,400 crores up 13% year-on-year and nearly 32% above pre-pandemic levels." — Ajay Bijli, Managing Director
- **Pre-COVID margins at lower occupancy**: 18% EBITDA margins at ~28% occupancy (vs pre-COVID needing 350–400 bps higher occupancy) — 2 consecutive quarters.
  > "For two consecutive quarters now, the business has delivered 18% EBITDA margins at an occupancy of around 28%, compared to pre-COVID levels where similar margins were achieved at 350 to 400 bps higher occupancies." — Ajay Bijli, Managing Director
- **Cost optimization ongoing**: Solar panels (electricity -4% YoY); rental renegotiations + revenue-share deals.
  > "Electricity cost is one area where we have seen a 4% year-on-year drop because we are deploying solar panels on the rooftops of many of our cinemas... Also, rental renegotiations and seeking discounts and moving to more revenue share kind of deals will further make the overall rental cost more efficient." — Gaurav Sharma, Chief Financial Officer
- **Monitoring Warner Bros./Netflix combination**: Potential global exhibition-industry impact; US government actively engaged.
  > "It is an event which is going to have an impact on the global exhibition industry and, therefore, all exhibitors are looking at the events and the developments in a very close fashion... the U.S. government is also in a very proactive fashion, looking at this combination." — Kamal Gianchandani, Chief Business Planning & Strategy
