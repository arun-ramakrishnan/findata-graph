# Balaji Telefilms Limited

## Company Overview

Balaji Telefilms Limited is a leading content production house in Asia and the Middle East, known for creating Hindi and regional content. It has made a significant impact on Indian television and expanded to producing movies through its subsidiary, Balaji Motion Pictures.

- **Television:** 200+ shows produced; dominant Hindi and regional content house
- **Filmed Entertainment:** 50+ movies via Balaji Motion Pictures
- **Digital:** 102 shows live; 11+ million YouTube subscribers
