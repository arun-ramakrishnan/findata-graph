# Dachepalli Publishers

Dachepalli Publishers Ltd. (founded 1908) is an educational publishing house specialising in K-12 textbooks and learning materials, serving schools across India with curriculum-aligned print and digital resources. Brands include Apple Book Company, Orange Leaf Publishers, Pelican Publishing House, Sangam Publishing House, and School Book Company. Headquartered in Hyderabad.
