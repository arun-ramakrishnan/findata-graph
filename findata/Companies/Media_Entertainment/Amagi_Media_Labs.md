## The Chatter — TCS, Indian Bank, Amagi, CMR Green & More

**AI agents collapsing manual "human toil" in media ops** — customers spend $2–$4 of human labour for every $1 of tech spend, and Amagi's agentic layer targets exactly that cost pool: content preparation, globalisation, scheduling.

**TV scheduling: 8 hours → 10 minutes** — agentic systems build nightly schedules autonomously using viewership analytics, genre, business rules and even social signals (e.g. an actor's birthday). Materially lowers the marginal cost of running multiple channels.

**Incremental TAM from localisation** — speech-to-text subtitling, dubbing, artwork and promo creation are being automated, opening up markets that previously couldn't justify the human cost. Management frames this as net-new TAM rather than cannibalisation.

**"World Models" for immersive sport** — physics-based video synthesis could let fans watch a game from a player's boots or a goalkeeper's gloves; creates premium immersive ad inventory. Early-stage but a differentiated long-term option.

**Jevons-paradox thesis** — management expects volume of media jobs to multiply even as per-job cost deflates, expanding overall industry spend rather than compressing it.
