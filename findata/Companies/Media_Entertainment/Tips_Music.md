# Tips Music | Small Cap | Media & Entertainment

Tips Industries Limited is a Company limited by shares, incorporated and domicile in India. The company is engaged in the business of Production and Distribution of motion Pictures and acquisition and exploitation Music of Rights. The Equity Shares of the Company are listed on BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited.
