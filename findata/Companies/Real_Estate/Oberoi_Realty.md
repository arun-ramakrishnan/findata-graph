# Oberoi Realty

## Company Overview
Oberoi Realty Limited (incorporated 1998, HQ Mumbai) is a premium real estate developer operating through Real Estate and Hospitality segments. It develops and sells residential, commercial, retail, hospitality, and social infrastructure projects; leases commercial properties; and owns/operates a hotel (The Westin Mumbai Garden City). The portfolio targets the upper end of the market with mixed-use and single-segment developments.

- Replicating Goregaon's location-creation playbook in Thane; long-term bullish on India as an inward economy largely insulated from external shocks (barring oil).
- Management declines to give sales guidance, citing market sensitivity to promoter statements and execution risk.


## The Chatter — Reliance Axis Bank UltraTech

**Margins structurally decoupled from volume:** Management argues superior profitability stems from strategic project selection (land buying, product design, market approach) rather than limited scale — confident margins hold as sales volume scales.

**Q3 FY27 launch catalyst — Adarsh Nagar:** High-profile luxury residential project in Mumbai slated for Q3 FY27 launch (previously guided Q2–Q3); a key bookings-growth catalyst for the coming quarters.

**NCR land acquisition push:** Actively scouting large-scale land parcels in Noida and Gurugram to replicate the Mumbai township model; success would materially expand the total addressable market.

**Pricing power confirmed:** Implemented price hikes across Goregaon and Mulund with no demand drop; strong secondary-market performance and resale premiums confirm brand-driven pricing power.

**Operational readiness for scale:** Now constructing ~18 towers of 60–65 floors simultaneously; management believes construction-management maturity supports a more aggressive geographic and volume expansion phase.

> "Margins are not strictly tied to sales volume; it is about how you strategize your product — begins with how you buy the land, how you design the product, and how you approach the market. We are very confident that our margins will continue to hold going forward."
> — Mr. Oberoi, Chairman and Managing Director
