# Oberoi Realty

## Company Overview
Oberoi Realty Limited (incorporated 1998, HQ Mumbai) is a premium real estate developer operating through Real Estate and Hospitality segments. It develops and sells residential, commercial, retail, hospitality, and social infrastructure projects; leases commercial properties; and owns/operates a hotel (The Westin Mumbai Garden City). The portfolio targets the upper end of the market with mixed-use and single-segment developments.
