# NBCC (India)

NBCC (India) Limited is a Government of India enterprise (formerly National Buildings Construction Corporation) headquartered in New Delhi. It operates across three lines — Project Management Consultancy (PMC), Engineering Procurement & Construction (EPC), and real estate development — with a focus on institutional, housing and industrial projects, redevelopment of government colonies, hospitals, airports, civil works for the power sector (chimneys, cooling towers) and overseas assignments.

**Order book ₹1.2 lakh cr; ₹38,000 cr under execution; ₹20,000 cr more to be awarded in FY27.** FY27 turnover targeted at ₹16,000–17,000 cr. Large central-government PMC orders expected in Q3/Q4.

**Redevelopment model is the differentiator** — 50–60% of the PMC book is redevelopment (7 GPRA projects under way after Moti Bagh and Kidwai Nagar). Government contributes no cash; NBCC earns 8–10% PMC fees while nearly doubling units and quadrupling built-up area. Extending the model to state governments, which hold large prime land parcels.

**FY27 PAT guide ₹1,100–1,200 cr; ₹2,000–2,500 cr by FY29, improving further by FY30.** Real-estate business margins (>30%) are the swing factor toward the upper end.

**Real-estate monetisation at Bharat Business Park** — 2.34 lakh sq ft monetised; ~₹8,900 cr of inventory sold across two auctions; one tower remaining (~₹1,000 cr).

**Execution bottleneck = skilled labour, not contractors** — state welfare schemes are pulling workers out of the construction labour pool despite India's demographics.
