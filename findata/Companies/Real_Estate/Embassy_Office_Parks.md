# Embassy Office Parks

## Company Overview

Embassy Office Parks REIT is India's first publicly listed Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) and Asia's largest office REIT by area. It owns, operates, and invests in income-generating real estate and related assets in India, with a focus on properties that generate rental income.
