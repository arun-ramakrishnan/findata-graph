# Aditya Birla Real Estate

## Company Overview
Aditya Birla Real Estate (NSE: ABREL; formerly Century Textiles and Industries, renamed Sept 2024) is the Aditya Birla Group's real-estate platform (Birla Estate), developing residential and commercial projects. It also retains legacy viscose/salt/chemicals segments being repositioned.
