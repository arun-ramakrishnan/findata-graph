# Prestige Estates Projects

## Company Overview
Prestige Estates Projects Limited, incorporated in 1986 and headquartered in Bengaluru, is one of India's largest diversified real estate developers. A subsidiary of the Razack Family Trust, it develops residential projects (townships, apartments, villas, plotted developments), retail and commercial projects, and hospitality properties (hotels, resorts, convention centres, service apartments, golf resorts). It also provides fit-out, interior design, facilities/property management and project/construction management services — an integrated model spanning residential, commercial, retail, hospitality and services.
