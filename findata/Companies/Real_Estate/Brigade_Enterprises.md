# Brigade Enterprises

## Company Overview
Brigade Enterprises Limited, founded in 1986 and headquartered in Bengaluru, is a leading real estate development company with a strong presence in South India. The company operates across Real Estate, Hospitality, and Leasing segments — developing and selling residential, commercial, and retail properties while also managing star-rated hotels, clubs, and convention centres.
