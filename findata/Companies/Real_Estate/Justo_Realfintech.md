# Justo Realfintech

## Company Overview

Justo Realfintech is a technology-driven real estate service provider that partners with developers to strategize and execute marketing, sales, digital solutions, home loans, and CRM for real estate projects, focused on Western India.

## Key Insights (Points & Figures #22)

- Operates two revenue models: Direct (gross % of sales incl. CP fees) and Net (net % with developers paying CPs directly); developers bear marketing costs in both
- Mandate business (residential launches, leasing, strata sales, plots/villas) contributes ~95% of revenue; home loan advisory ~5%
- India real estate = ~6.8% of GDP (~₹20.1 trillion); housing shortage of ~93 million homes by 2036
- Organized real estate advisory/mandate firms growing at ~28-32% CAGR (FY20-FY25); mandate/exclusive share ~35-40% and rising
- Pune: ~3-3.5% market share (180-190 units/month of ~5,800-6,000); MMR: <1% share (~50-60 units/month of ~13,970) — large expansion headroom
