## Sector Overview

The Real Estate Development sector encompasses companies that create residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties, from individual buildings to large integrated townships. This sector plays a crucial role in urban development and infrastructure growth.

Key characteristics of the Real Estate Development sector:
- **Capital Intensive**: High investment requirements for land acquisition and construction
- **Long Development Cycles**: Extended project timelines from planning to completion
- **Economic Sensitivity**: Highly correlated with economic cycles and interest rates
- **Regulatory Environment**: Complex approval processes and compliance requirements

The sector is driven by urbanization, infrastructure development, and housing demand, while facing challenges in economic cyclicality, regulatory complexities, and financing constraints.
