# Mindspace Business Parks REIT

## Company Overview
Mindspace Business Parks REIT (BSE: MINDSPACE) is a leading Indian commercial real-estate investment trust with a Grade-A office portfolio across Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai. It reported **95.7% committed occupancy** as of March 2026 (the highest since listing, up from 93% in FY25) and is the only Indian REIT with data centres in its portfolio (~1.7 million sq. ft. on completion).

## The Chatter — Reliance, Infosys, VBL, Hind Zinc & More

**AI is a demand tailwind, not a risk:** Management rejects the "AI reduces office demand" thesis with empirical evidence — office demand has grown 18%/16%/15% YoY over three years with no mass-vacancy evidence; AI-driven work accelerates the flight to quality, favouring Mindspace's premium, amenity-rich portfolio.

**Record occupancy:** Committed occupancy reached 95.7%, the highest ever reported since listing (up from 93% in FY25); pan-India vacancy dropped to a multi-year low of 14.7% on record quarterly gross leasing of 21.5 million sq. ft.

**India structural advantage:** Rupee depreciation has made India more attractive to GCCs (dollar rents effectively flat while infrastructure/talent strengthened); GCCs expanded footprint 43% YoY.

**Mark-to-market upside:** With market rents well above in-place rents, lease expiries are currently a positive — the REIT achieved ~40% mark-to-market this quarter, with pre-leasing of 2 million sq. ft. (incl. 0.8 million to a healthcare GCC at ₹110 and 0.7 million at ₹121/sq. ft.).

**Data-centre optionality:** The only REIT with data centres in its portfolio — ~1.7 million sq. ft. on completion, a potential meaningful revenue driver.

**Cost headwinds:** Macroeconomic pressure is raising construction input costs (steel +2%, RMC/cement +8%, paints +15%, tiles +10%, PVC pipes +16%), causing slightly slower near-term lease decisions, though underlying demand remains intact.
