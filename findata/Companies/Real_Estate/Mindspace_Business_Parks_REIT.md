# Mindspace Business Parks REIT

## Company Overview
Mindspace Business Parks REIT (BSE: MINDSPACE) is a leading Indian commercial real-estate investment trust with a Grade-A office portfolio across Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai. It reported **95.7% committed occupancy** as of March 2026 (the highest since listing, up from 93% in FY25) and is the only Indian REIT with data centres in its portfolio (~1.7 million sq. ft. on completion).
