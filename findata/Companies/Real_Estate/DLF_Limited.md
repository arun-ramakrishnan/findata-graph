# DLF Limited

## Overview

DLF Limited is one of India's largest real estate developers, engaged in residential, commercial, and retail projects. It follows an end-to-end model, handling everythin from land acquisition to construction and leasing. The company also provides leasi property maintenance, and recreational services, complementing its core real estate development business and enhancing the overall value of its projects.

## The Chatter — Marico DLF BSE

- **FY28 = P&L inflection:** Large projects starting with The Arbor begin contributing from FY28, unlocking the ~₹39,000 cr gross margin potential embedded in the WIP book.
- **Dahlias ultra-luxury success:** ~65% sold; realisation now over ₹1 lakh/sq ft (touching ₹1.20–1.25 lakh on higher floors); 25–30% of buyers now from outside Delhi-NCR (rest of India + NRIs). Entry level raised to ₹100 cr+ as the pricing algorithm steepens.
- **Rental green shoots returning:** After AI-hiring and Iran-US uncertainty slowed GCC decisions for two quarters, the last 4–5 weeks show international enquiries resuming; Q2/Q3 expected to be strong. FY27 exit rentals guided at ₹7,300–7,500 cr at the group level.
- **Data centres = real-estate-only play:** DLF will build data-centre real estate but explicitly NOT buy the technology or run the facilities — a deliberate scope boundary.
- **Mumbai expansion intent:** First Mumbai project ("dipping toes in the water") done very well; actively evaluating more, though remaining selective. Goa delay (~10% of the ₹20,000 cr guidance) seen as comfortably recoverable.

> "We strongly believe that FY28 would be an inflection point from a reporting perspective wherein all our large products, starting with The Arbor, will start to contribute to the P&L… the gross margin potential as it stands today is approximately 39,000 crore."
> — Badal Bagri, Group CFO
