# Brookfield India REIT

## Company Overview
Brookfield India Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT), sponsored by Brookfield Asset Management, owns and operates premium Grade A commercial real estate assets across key Indian markets. The REIT focuses on high-quality assets, proactive portfolio management, a sound capital structure, and best market practices, leveraging Brookfield's global platform for resources, relationships, and expertise.
