# Greenpanel Industries

## Company Overview
Greenpanel Industries (NSE: GREENPANEL) is India's largest manufacturer of medium-density fibreboard (MDF) and plywood, with a presence in doors, flooring, and allied wood-panel products. Operates an integrated value chain serving both home and export markets.
