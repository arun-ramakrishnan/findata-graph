# Shree Digvijay Cement

## Company Overview

Shree Digvijay Cement Company is one of India's pioneer cement manufacturers, having started operations in 1944 at the coastal township of Digvijaygram (Sikka) in Jamnagar District, Gujarat. Since 2019 it has been part of True North (formerly India Value Fund Advisers / IVFA). The company has been a trendsetter in supplying superior-quality Ordinary & Special Portland Cement, and is India's leading supplier of oil well cement.

- **Founded:** 1942 (incorporated) | **Operations since:** 1944
- **Headquarters:** Digvijaygram (Sikka), Jamnagar, Gujarat, India
- **Parent (since 2019):** True North (IVFA)
- **Brands:** KAMAL CEMENT, CEMENT KA SARDAR
