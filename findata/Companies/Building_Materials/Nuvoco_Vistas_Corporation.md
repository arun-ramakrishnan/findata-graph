# Nuvoco Vistas Corporation | Small Cap | Building Materials

Nuvoco Vistas Corporation Limited is largest cement company in East India and fifth largest in India. It is a leading ready-mix concrete manufacturer with strategically located plants in East and North India. The company ensures steady supply of raw materials like limestone, slag, and fly ash to its plants. With captive railway sidings at six plants, Nuvoco has competitive edge in transporting materials efficiently.
