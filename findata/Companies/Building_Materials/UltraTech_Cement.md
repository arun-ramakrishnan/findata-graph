# UltraTech Cement

UltraTech Cement, part of the Aditya Birla Group, is a leading manufacturer of grey cement, ready mix concrete, and white cement in India. It is the third largest cement producer globally, operating in UAE, Bahrain, Sri Lanka, and India.

## The Chatter — Reliance Axis Bank UltraTech

**Record Q1 across the board:** Highest-ever first-quarter performance on volumes, revenue, EBITDA and profit; 13.1% domestic grey-cement volume growth tracking well ahead of industry — translating directly into market-share gains.

**Demand drivers structural, not cyclical:** Infrastructure, premium housing, redevelopment, offices, hotels all cement-intensive; ports, shipyards, rail corridors and data centres flagged as the most cement-intensive future asset classes. India's ~39% urbanisation by 2030 (vs Indonesia's 59%) underlines the long runway.

**Acquired-brand customer conversion:** Successfully converted buyers of B/C-category acquired brands to UltraTech's A-category premium — no volume loss, customers willing to pay a price premium for trust.

**Green power as cost moat:** 1,897 MW green-power capacity met ~47% of total power needs, letting UltraTech absorb the West Asia cost shock better than peers and harvest relief faster.

**₹17,000 cr capex, fully limestone-backed:** Expansion to take consolidated capacity beyond 242 MT; every tonne fully backed by secured limestone — no raw-material constraint. Funded via internal accruals with net-debt/EBITDA expected below 1.

**Monsoon-quarter cost headwind flagged:** Costs expected to rise ₹130–140/tonne sequentially (maintenance, operating deleverage, fuel); pricing expected to hold steady through the monsoon quarter nonetheless.

**Double-digit volume-growth target for FY27** despite seasonality and elevated costs; eastern India singled out for a structural demand upside over the next 2–4 years.
