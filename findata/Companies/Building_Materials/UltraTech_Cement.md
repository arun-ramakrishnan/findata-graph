# UltraTech Cement

UltraTech Cement, part of the Aditya Birla Group, is a leading manufacturer of grey cement, ready mix concrete, and white cement in India. It is the third largest cement producer globally, operating in UAE, Bahrain, Sri Lanka, and India.

## Company Overview

### Global Position
- **Third Largest Globally**: World's third largest cement producer
- **International Presence**: Operating in UAE, Bahrain, Sri Lanka, and India
- **Aditya Birla Group**: Part of one of India's largest conglomerates
