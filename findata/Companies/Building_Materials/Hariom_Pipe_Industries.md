# Hariom Pipe Industries

## Company Overview

Hariom Pipe Industries Limited manufactures iron and steel products including mild steel pipes, tubes, billets, hot/cold rolled square/rectangular/circular pipes, hot dipped galvanized pipes, pre-galvanized pipes, and scaffolding solutions (cuplocks, props, spans, H-frame systems). Founded 2007, headquartered in Hyderabad.
