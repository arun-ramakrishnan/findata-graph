## Company Overview
Astral Pipes, part of Astral Limited, is a leading manufacturer of plastic pipes and piping systems in India. The company is known for introducing innovative technologies such as CPVC pipes to the Indian market and offers a wide range of products for plumbing, drainage, agriculture, and industrial applications.
