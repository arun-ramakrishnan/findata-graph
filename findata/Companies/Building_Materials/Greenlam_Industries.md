# Greenlam Industries

Greenlam Industries is engaged in manufacturing laminates. Its products include greenlam laminates, digi junior, compact laminates, xtraordinaire super premium laminates, 0.6 millimeters (mm) green gloss laminates, 0.8 mm green touch laminate post forming laminates, switch board panels, chalk and marker grade laminates, green decoliner 0.7 mm and fire retardant laminates.

## Business Overview
- **Market Position**: Leading laminates manufacturer
- **Product Portfolio**: Wide range of laminate products
- **Revenue Growth**: 18.7% YoY in Q2 FY26, 54.6% gross margins
