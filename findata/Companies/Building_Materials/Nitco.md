# NITCO Limited

**Ticker:** NITCO.NS | **Sector:** Building Products & Equipment | **Market Cap:** ₹21.0B INR

## Overview
NITCO Limited engages in the tiles and marble business in India and internationally. It operates in two segments, Tiles and Related Products; and Real Estate. The company offers vitrified tiles, such as glazed, DCH, heavy and SST; wooden tiles, including wall, floor, ceramic and vitrified; ceramic wall and floor tiles; and digital, Italian, and porcelain tiles. It also provides anti-bacterial, anti-skid vitrified, anti-skid, as well as stain, scratch, and chemical resistance tiles. In addition, the company offers marble products; and is involved in the development of real estate properties. It sells its products under the Le Studio Express, NITCO Look, and NITCO Galore brands. NITCO Limited was founded in 1953 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India.
