# Ambuja Cement | Large Cap | Building Materials

Ambuja Cements Limited is one of India's leading cement manufacturers and a member of the Adani Group. The company is known for sustainable and environment-friendly cement solutions, operating multiple manufacturing plants across India.

## Business Model
- Manufacturing and distribution of cement (Ordinary Portland Cement and Portland Pozzolana Cement)
- Focus on infrastructure development and affordable housing
- Sustainable production practices (water positive, low carbon footprint)
- Pan-India distribution network

## The Chatter — Q4FY26 (Havells, Ambuja, Cipla & More)

**Industry-leading volume growth:** Delivered volumes growing at 2x the industry average — a decisive quarter for capturing national cement demand share.

**One Cement Platform (Adani integration):** The proposed amalgamation of ACC and Orient Cement with Ambuja Cements marks the beginning of a unified "One Cement Platform" — expected to boost profit margins and simplify the corporate structure.

**Pricing — firmer ground:** Cement prices rising across key regions — Southern non-trade +INR 15-20, Northern +INR 5-10. January entered on firmer ground, a departure from the rollback-prone patterns of previous years.

**Trade mix shift:** Current trade:non-trade is 65:35, moving toward 70:30 (and eventually 75:25), leveraging brand equity to regain trade market leadership and improve realizations.

**Cost efficiency targets:** INR 100-125/ton improvement possible quickly on power; ~INR 150/ton reduction targeted on fuel — flowing directly into earnings per ton.

**Capacity expansion:** Assam (greenfield, up by February) and Mundra (brownfield) to tap regional demand growth and reduce transport costs.

**Capex:** ~INR 10,000 crore annual capex (INR 8,000 crore growth + INR 2,000 crore efficiency).
