# Ambuja Cement | Large Cap | Building Materials

Ambuja Cements Limited is one of India's leading cement manufacturers and a member of the Adani Group. The company is known for sustainable and environment-friendly cement solutions, operating multiple manufacturing plants across India.

## Business Model
- Manufacturing and distribution of cement (Ordinary Portland Cement and Portland Pozzolana Cement)
- Focus on infrastructure development and affordable housing
- Sustainable production practices (water positive, low carbon footprint)
- Pan-India distribution network
