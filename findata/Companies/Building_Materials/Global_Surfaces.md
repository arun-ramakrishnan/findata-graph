# Global Surfaces Limited

Global Surfaces Limited specializes in processing natural stones like granite, marble and quartzite, which are known for their unique aesthetic appeal. They also manufacture engineered quartz countertops made of crushed stone bonded with resin.

## Product Portfolio
- **Natural Stones**: Granite, marble, quartzite with 4,71,164 SMT p.a. capacity
- **Engineered Stones**: Engineered quartz with 11,44,540 SMT p.a. capacity
- **Product Series**: Aurora, Kalmasa, Prismatic, Stratum, Quartzite series
