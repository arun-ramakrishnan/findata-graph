# JSW Paints

JSW Paints is the paints and coatings arm of the JSW Group. The company is privately held (no listed equity ticker). In December 2025 it acquired a controlling stake in Akzo Nobel India, gaining access to the premium Dulux brand portfolio and integrated decorative + industrial coatings capability. Pre-acquisition capacity was 210,000 klpa across Karnataka and Maharashtra, with a stated target of 800,000 klpa by FY30.
