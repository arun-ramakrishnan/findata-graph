# La Opala RG Limited

## Company Overview

La Opala RG Limited (incorporated 1987, headquartered in Kolkata) is a mid-cap manufacturer of glass and glassware products for the domestic and international tableware and lifestyle markets. The Jhunjhunwala family is the promoter with majority ownership.

The company sells its products through a network of distributors and retailers and also exports internationally.
