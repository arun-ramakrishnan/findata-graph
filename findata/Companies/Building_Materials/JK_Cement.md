# JK Cement

## Company Overview
J.K. Cement Limited, founded in 1975 and headquartered in Kanpur, manufactures and sells cement and related products in India and internationally. It is a leading player in white cement globally and a growing grey cement producer, with an expanding footprint in India's central and eastern regions. The portfolio spans grey cement, white cement, wall putty, tile adhesives and grouts, construction chemicals, and wood finishes, plus the JKMaxx paints range (interior/exterior emulsions, distemper, enamel, primers).

## Key Insights (from Edition #24, Mar 2026)
- JK Cement is seeing broad-based volume growth across both grey and white cement — grey cement drives the bulk of volumes and growth (strong double-digit YoY), while the white cement business, though smaller, is also scaling steadily; this is a balanced portfolio where grey provides scale/demand-led growth and white adds niche, higher-value expansion.
- Double-digit volume growth is being driven by robust demand and an extended footprint in the Central Region and East markets.
- Pet coke prices — one of the key cost drivers for cement — have largely stabilised after earlier volatility, trending in a narrow range; this suggests cost pressures are easing, which should support margin recovery if pricing holds.
