# Asian Paints | Large Cap | Building Materials

Asian Paints Limited is India's largest paint company and a leading decorative paints manufacturer. Founded in 1942, the company has grown into one of Asia's most trusted paint brands with leadership in both decorative and industrial coatings.

## Business Model
- **Decorative Paints**: Interior/exterior paints, enamels, wood finishes
- **Industrial Coatings**: Protective coatings for industrial applications
- **Home Decor**: Bathroom fittings, kitchen, wardrobes (SBU)
- **Services**: Waterproofing, wall coverings, painting services
- **Distribution**: Extensive dealer and retail network

**Volume growth guidance:** Management guides high single-digit volume growth of 8–10% for the year, citing early demand shoots in April–May despite a tougher pricing environment and elevated competition.

**Cost pass-through gap:** Raw material inflation plus currency depreciation drove cost pressures of ~20%, but only ~11% has been passed on via price hikes; further increases are planned while balancing inflation absorption.

**Aggressive discounting persists:** Competitive intensity shows no let-up — discounting to retailers, contractors, and other stakeholders continues aggressively and is expected to intensify as consolidated and new entrants compete.

**VAM-VAE strategic project:** First phase of the VAM-VAE project (a signature import-substitution initiative held by very few global players) is set for commissioning in H1, strengthening innovation capabilities and enabling green paints.

**Industrial coatings outpacing decorative:** Infrastructure spending, manufacturing investments, and government capex are driving industrial coatings growth materially higher than decorative, with strong traction from builder, factory, and government segments.
