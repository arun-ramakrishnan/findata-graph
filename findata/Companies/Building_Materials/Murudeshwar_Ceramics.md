# Murudeshwar Ceramics Limited

**Ticker:** MURUDCERA.BO | **Sector:** Consumer Durables/Ceramics | **Market Cap:** Small Cap

## Company Overview

Murudeshwar Ceramics Ltd. is a leading manufacturer and exporter of ceramic and vitrified floor and wall tiles, serving markets in India, the Americas, Europe, and internationally. Established in 1983 and headquartered in Bengaluru, the company has built a strong reputation for quality and innovation in the ceramics industry.

### Core Business Segments

- **Ceramic Tiles**: Comprehensive range of wall tiles including bathroom, kitchen, and outdoor applications
- **Vitrified Tiles**: Premium glazed and polished vitrified tiles for residential and commercial use
- **Digital Wall Tiles**: Advanced digital printing technology for customized designs
- **Specialty Tiles**: Double charge, soluble salt, full body, and exterior tiles for specific applications

### Brand Portfolio

- **Naveen Ceramic Tiles**: Flagship brand offering traditional and contemporary tile designs
- **Naveen Diamontile**: Premium brand focused on high-end vitrified and specialty tiles
