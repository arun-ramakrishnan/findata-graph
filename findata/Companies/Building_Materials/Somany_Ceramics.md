# Somany Ceramics Limited

## Company Overview

Somany Ceramics is a leading Indian tiles and sanitaryware company specializing in ceramic tiles, vitrified tiles, and building materials. The company has established itself as a significant player in India's ceramics and tiles industry, serving both domestic and international markets with a strong distribution network.
