# Cera Sanitaryware Limited

## Company Overview

Cera Sanitaryware Limited (founded 1980, headquartered in Ahmedabad) is a large-cap building materials company manufacturing and marketing a comprehensive range of sanitaryware and bath products across India. The company is a leading player in the Indian sanitaryware industry and also exports its products internationally.

Cera designs, manufactures, and sells sanitaryware products (including toilets, wash basins, cisterns, and seat covers), faucets, showers, faucet accessories, floor and wall tiles, shower partitions, kitchen sinks, vanity cabinets, and mirrors. Its flagship brands include Senator, CERA Luxe, and CERA, addressing premium and mass-market segments of the bath solutions industry.
