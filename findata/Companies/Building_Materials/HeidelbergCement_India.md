# HeidelbergCement India

## Company Overview
HeidelbergCement India Limited (incorporated 1958; HQ Gurugram) is a subsidiary of Germany's Heidelberg Materials South Asia B.V. It manufactures and sells cement (brands: mycem, mycem Primo, mycem Power, mycem Power Shield) and clinker from three plants — Ammasandra (Karnataka), Damoh (Madhya Pradesh), and Jhansi (Uttar Pradesh).
