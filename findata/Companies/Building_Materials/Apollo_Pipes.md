# Apollo Pipes

Apollo Pipes Limited, a key company of SUDESH GROUP, manufactures a wide range of PVC pipes for multiple sectors including Civil Infrastructure, Industrial, Agriculture, and Building & Construction. The company also offers solutions for the transportation of Oil and Gas through HDPE pipes and fittings, PLB Ducts, and P pipes.

## Business Overview
- **Product Range**: cPVC, uPVC, and PPR-C plumbing systems; uPVC pressure pipes and fittings; HDPE PLB duct pipes and MDPE and HDPE gas piping system
- **Infrastructure**: Advanced infrastructure, testing equipment and eco-friendly manufacturing processes
- **Geographic Focus**: Strong presence in Northern India with plants in Dadri and Sikanderabad, Uttar Pradesh
