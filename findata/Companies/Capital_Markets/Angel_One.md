# Angel One Limited (formerly Angel Broking)

## Company Overview

Angel One Limited (incorporated 1996; renamed from Angel Broking Limited in September 2021) is a Mumbai-based, large-cap financial services firm providing broking and advisory services, margin funding, and a broad suite of financial products to clients in India and internationally. The company had ~2,934 full-time employees.

The firm offers equity, commodities, derivatives, and currency derivatives; depository services; IPO application facilitation; and margin trading facilities. Beyond broking, it distributes insurance, mutual funds, sovereign gold bonds, and credit products, and provides market research and investor education. Asset/wealth management, portfolio management, DEMAT, and software consultancy services round out the offering.

Clients are served primarily through digital platforms — the **Angel One Super App**, **Angel One Trade**, and **Smart API** — reaching resident and non-resident individuals, salaried professionals, HNIs, corporates, trusts, partnership firms/LLPs, and co-operative societies.
