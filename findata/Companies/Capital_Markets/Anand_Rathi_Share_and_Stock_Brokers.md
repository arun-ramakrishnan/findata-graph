# Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers

Anand Rathi Share & Stock Brokers Ltd. is a full-service financial services firm specializing in stockbroking, margin trading, and investment product distribution. The company utilizes a relationship-driven approach supported by digital platforms to serve retail and HNI clients across India.

## The Chatter — Q4FY26 (Edition #55)

FY26 was a challenging year — geopolitical tensions, shifting global trade dynamics, tariff-related uncertainties, and fast-paced technological shifts weakened risk appetite and pressured trading volumes. Fresh bouts of adverse news (including the West Asia conflict) intensified these pressures.

**Structural tailwind — financialization of savings:**
- Demat accounts rose from 15.14 crore (Mar 2024) to 22.2 crore (Feb 2026).
- Mutual fund AUM grew from 65.7 lakh crore (Mar 2025) to ~82.02 lakh crore (Feb 2026) — a 24.8% YoY increase, reflecting sustained retail participation and the financialization of household savings.

**SEBI reforms:** Recent regulatory measures strengthen risk management, curb excessive leverage, and enhance investor protection. While requiring short-term adjustments, they lay the foundation for a more transparent, resilient capital market ecosystem.

**Strategy — balanced revenue mix:** The company is intentionally diversifying income toward a 50:50 split between non-broking and broking segments to reduce reliance on volatile stockbroking fees and improve earnings predictability.

**Distribution growth & new verticals:**
- Distribution income for FY26 was 1,129 million — a 44.1% YoY growth, driven by improved cross-selling across the client base.
- Received the corporate agency license to distribute insurance products (life and health), opening a new high-margin, recurring fee-based income stream.
