# Upstox (RKSV Securities)

## Company Overview
Upstox (formerly RKSV Securities) is a leading Indian discount brokerage and financial technology platform that provides commission-free trading services for stocks, derivatives, commodities, and currencies. The company operates as a technology-driven brokerage platform focusing on democratizing access to financial markets for retail investors.

## Industry Classification
- **Sector**: Financial Services - FinTech/Discount Brokerage
- **Type**: Discount Brokerage and Financial Technology Company
- **Business Focus**: Online trading and investment platform
- **Market Focus**: Retail investors and traders
