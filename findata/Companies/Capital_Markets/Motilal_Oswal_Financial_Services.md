# Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited

## Company Overview

Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, founded in **1987** and headquartered in **Mumbai**, is a diversified financial services firm serving high-net-worth individuals (HNIs), mutual funds, foreign institutional investors (FIIs), financial institutions, and corporates. The group operates across wealth management, capital markets, asset/private wealth management, home finance, and treasury investments.

## Business Segments

- **Wealth Management**: Advisory and portfolio services for HNIs.
- **Capital Markets**: Stock broking and securities/commodity brokerage; institutional equities (cash and derivatives).
- **Asset / Private Wealth Management**: Discretionary and non-discretionary investment management.
- **Home Finance**: Mortgages through the group's housing finance arm.
- **Treasury Investments**: Proprietary investment of group capital.
- **Investment Banking & Private Equity**: Corporate advisory and PE fund management.

## The Chatter — Infosys Adani Power

**Highest-ever quarterly profit:** PAT crossed ₹1,500 cr, including ~₹900 cr of mark-to-market gains; the repeatable core — operating profit of ~₹600 cr — was also an all-time high, up 14% sequentially.

**Asset management leads:** AMC drove the quarter (AUM +30%, profits ~+70% including the unlisted business), with strong contributions from the mutual fund and private equity businesses.

**Carry event near:** Fund II is nearing completion; a carry realisation event is expected within 12 months (timing depends on remaining exits) — a potential spike in high-margin income.

**Diversification cushioning soft broking:** Despite subdued retail broking and wealth management, AMC + housing finance grew 30–40%, lifting overall operating profit 14%; FY operating profit guided to ₹2,500–2,600 cr if the trajectory holds.

**Housing finance re-accelerating:** AUM >₹6,000 cr and growing ~25–30%; targeting 25–27% AUM growth and 30–32% bottom-line growth, with a CRISIL upgrade (AA → AA+) lowering the cost of capital.

> "One of the highlights is that this is our highest-ever quarterly profit. We have declared a PAT of over 1,500 crore for the quarter. Though it includes around 900 crore of mark-to-market gains, the operating profit of about 600 crore is also an all-time high, up 14% sequentially."
> — Raamdeo Agrawal, Chairman and Co-founder

## The Chatter — Adani Power Motilal Oswal

**Annuity mix at 66% underwrites 20%+ CAGR:** Annuity revenues are now 66% of operating revenue and rising, making profit growth qualitatively more sustainable; management calls a "minimum base case of 20% plus CAGR easily achievable" on the annuity-led asset-and-flow growth.

**AMC track-record maturing into an inflow engine:** Only 6 schemes had completed 3-year track records so far; 10 more funds cross the 3-year mark over the next 15 months — almost all ranked first over 3 years — driving the next leg of market-share gains through flows.

**New credit businesses scaling:** The first private credit fund has ₹2,500 crore of commitments and closes at ₹3,000 crore in Q3; a commercial credit fund (~₹2,000 crore) launches in Q3 — a fourth business line beyond equity alternatives.

**Custody license completes the institutional stack:** First non-bank custody license granted in ~15 years (since Edelweiss), removing the last missing piece of the institutional equities offering.

**Structurally low F&O exposure; no AMC demerger:** Only 5–6% of group revenue comes from F&O brokerage (retail broking ~15–16% of revenue × ~40% F&O share), muting regulatory-crackdown risk; management ruled out listing the AMC separately, arguing performing businesses don't deserve a holding-company discount.

> — Shalibhadra Shah, Group CFO
