# Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited

## Company Overview

Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, founded in **1987** and headquartered in **Mumbai**, is a diversified financial services firm serving high-net-worth individuals (HNIs), mutual funds, foreign institutional investors (FIIs), financial institutions, and corporates. The group operates across wealth management, capital markets, asset/private wealth management, home finance, and treasury investments.

## Business Segments

- **Wealth Management**: Advisory and portfolio services for HNIs.
- **Capital Markets**: Stock broking and securities/commodity brokerage; institutional equities (cash and derivatives).
- **Asset / Private Wealth Management**: Discretionary and non-discretionary investment management.
- **Home Finance**: Mortgages through the group's housing finance arm.
- **Treasury Investments**: Proprietary investment of group capital.
- **Investment Banking & Private Equity**: Corporate advisory and PE fund management.

**Asset management leads:** AMC drove the quarter (AUM +30%, profits ~+70% including the unlisted business), with strong contributions from the mutual fund and private equity businesses.

**Carry event near:** Fund II is nearing completion; a carry realisation event is expected within 12 months (timing depends on remaining exits) — a potential spike in high-margin income.

**Diversification cushioning soft broking:** Despite subdued retail broking and wealth management, AMC + housing finance grew 30–40%, lifting overall operating profit 14%; FY operating profit guided to ₹2,500–2,600 cr if the trajectory holds.

**Housing finance re-accelerating:** AUM >₹6,000 cr and growing ~25–30%; targeting 25–27% AUM growth and 30–32% bottom-line growth, with a CRISIL upgrade (AA → AA+) lowering the cost of capital.
