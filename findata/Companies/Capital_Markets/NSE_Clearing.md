# NSE Clearing

## Company Overview
NSE Clearing Limited (formerly National Securities Clearing Corporation Ltd, NSCCL) is the wholly-owned clearing corporation of the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) and a core piece of India's financial market infrastructure. Incorporated in 1995 and headquartered in Mumbai, it acts as the central counterparty (CCP) that novates and guarantees settlement of all trades executed on NSE across equity, equity derivatives, currency derivatives, and interest rate derivatives, thereby absorbing counterparty risk and ensuring systemic stability.

## Financial Profile (Yahoo Finance, Jun 2026)
- **No public ticker / data unavailable**: NSE Clearing is an unlisted, wholly-owned subsidiary of NSE and is not separately traded. Its financials are consolidated within NSE's books rather than reported via a public YF ticker.

## Product Portfolio
- **Equity Clearing**: Clearing and settlement of cash-market equity and equity-derivative trades.
- **Derivatives Clearing**: Futures and options (index and stock) clearing as central counterparty.
- **Currency & Interest Rate Derivatives**: Clearing of currency futures/options and interest-rate futures/options.
- **Risk Management**: Margin calculation and collection, intraday position monitoring, default/corpus fund management, and settlement guarantee.

## Management
- Operates under NSE Group governance and SEBI-regulated clearing-corporation board structure (managed by NSE leadership; Ashishkumar Chauhan leads NSE as MD & CEO).

## Key Insights (from note)
- Defensive, systemically critical market-infrastructure entity: stable, regulated cash flows driven by clearing fees tied to NSE trading volumes rather than market direction.
- Core function is central-counterparty (CCP) guarantee—novating trades and managing default risk through margins and a settlement guarantee fund—reducing systemic risk.
- Growth tracks NSE volume expansion, new derivative product launches, and regulatory mandates for central clearing; technology/risk-engine investments are ongoing priorities.
