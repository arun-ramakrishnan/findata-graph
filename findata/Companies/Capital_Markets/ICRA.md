# ICRA

## Business Overview
ICRA Limited, formerly known as Investment Information and Credit Rating Agency of India Limited, is a professional investment information and credit rating agency established in 1991. It is backed by leading financial institutions and operates across segments like rating, research, consulting, outsourcing, and information services.
