# M1Xchange

## Company Overview

M1Xchange is one of India's leading RBI-licensed **TReDS (Trade Receivables Discounting System)** platforms. TReDS platforms enable MSME suppliers to discount their invoices to large corporate buyers (and their financiers) for early payment, addressing the working-capital bottleneck in B2B supply chains. M1Xchange operates as a digital marketplace connecting MSMEs, corporate buyers, and financiers.

## Notable Investment

**Jindal Stainless** acquired a **9.62% stake** in M1Xchange, reflecting strategic interest by large industrial buyers in the invoice-discounting infrastructure that shortens their supply-chain payment cycles.
