# KFin Technologies

KFin Technologies Limited is a technology-driven financial services platform serving the capital-markets ecosystem — asset managers and corporate issuers across asset classes. Provides SaaS-based transaction management, compliance solutions, data analytics, and digital services to asset managers globally; also serves mutual funds and private retirement schemes in Malaysia, Philippines, and Hong Kong. Headquartered in Hyderabad.
