# Nippon Life AMC

## Company Overview

Nippon Life India Asset Management (NAM India) is a prominent Indian asset management company that manages Nippon India Mutual Fund. It offers a diverse range of investment products and services to individuals and institutions, combining global expertise from parent Nippon Life with local market knowledge.
