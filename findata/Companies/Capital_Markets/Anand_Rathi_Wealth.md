# Anand Rathi Wealth Limited

## Company Overview

Anand Rathi Wealth Limited (founded 1994; formerly Anand Rathi Wealth Services Limited; renamed January 2021; headquartered in Mumbai) is a wealth management firm and part of the Anand Rathi Group. It distributes and sells financial products and provides wealth solutions, investment advisory, and financial planning services to high-net-worth individuals (HNIs) and ultra-HNIs. The company had ~1,256 full-time employees and went public via IPO in September 2021.

It also operates **Omni Financial Advisor**, a technology platform for mutual fund distributors and independent financial advisors (client reporting, business dashboard, online mutual fund transactions, goal planning).
