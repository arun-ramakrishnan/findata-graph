# Computer Age Management Services Limited

## Company Overview

Computer Age Management Services Limited (CAMS) is a leading Indian technology company specializing in registrar and transfer agency services, digital platforms, and financial technology solutions. Established in 1988 and headquartered in Chennai, CAMS has evolved into a comprehensive financial services technology provider, offering innovative digital solutions for mutual funds, insurance, pension services, and wealth management.

### Core Business Segments

- **Registrar & Transfer Agency Services:** Comprehensive services for mutual funds and financial institutions
- **Digital Platforms:** Advanced digital solutions for financial services
- **Financial Technology:** Innovative fintech solutions for the BFSI sector
- **Wealth Management Technology:** Digital platforms for wealth management and alternative investments
- **Insurance Technology:** Digital solutions for insurance policy management
- **Pension Services:** Technology platforms for pension fund management

### Comprehensive Product Portfolio

**Investor Platforms:**
- **MF Central:** Investor platform for monitoring and managing mutual fund investments
- **myCAMS:** Mobile app and portal for retail investors to manage and transact across multiple mutual funds
- **Bima Central:** Insurance platform providing policy holder services, renewal reminders, and cover dashboard

**Distributor & Advisor Platforms:**
- **edge 360:** Mobile app and portal for distributors and advisors offering daily operations platform
- **GOCorp:** Platform for institutional investors to transact across CAMS-managed mutual funds

**Digital Innovation Platforms:**
- **digiLoan:** Digital platform enabling investors to pledge mutual fund units for loans
- **CAMS WealthServ:** Digital onboarding platform for alternate investment funds and PMS investors
- **Think360.ai:** Full stack data science and AI solutions for BFSI enterprises

**Financial Technology Solutions:**
- **CAMServ:** AI-powered chatbot assisting customers with mutual-fund transactions
- **eKYC Solution:** Digital KYC platform with Aadhaar authentication for mutual funds
- **CAMSfinserv:** Account aggregator platform for financial data consolidation
- **CAMS eNPS:** Advanced pension services platform for account opening and record keeping
- **CAMSPay:** BFSI payments platform with same-day NAV processing
- **Recon Dynamix:** AI-powered reconciliation platform for payments and transactions
- **Fintuple:** Capital markets digital transformation partner
- **Policy Genie:** Digital dashboard for insurance policy management
