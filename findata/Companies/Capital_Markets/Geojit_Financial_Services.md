# Geojit Financial Services

## Company Overview
Geojit Financial Services Limited, founded in 1987 and headquartered in Kochi, is an investment services company offering online broking, financial product distribution, portfolio management, and margin funding across India, Oman, Kuwait, the UAE, and Saudi Arabia. It also develops and maintains software through its Software Services segment.
