# BSE Limited (Bombay Stock Exchange)

## Company Overview

BSE Limited, founded in 1875 as the Bombay Stock Exchange and renamed in July 2011, is headquartered in Mumbai and is Asia's oldest stock exchange. It provides trading and related platforms across equities, currencies, debt, derivatives, and mutual funds. The company's service suite spans securities trading, data dissemination, index computation, repository and depository services, clearing and settlement, IT support, and training. BSE also operates an SME platform, insurance broking, an agricultural commodity electronic platform, and oversees investment adviser supervision.
