# NIIT Limited

**Ticker:** NIITLTD.NS | **Sector:** Education & Training Services | **Market Cap:** \~₹13.6B INR

## Overview
NIIT Limited engages in providing learning and knowledge solutions to individuals, enterprises, and various institutions internationally. The company offers a variety of training programs, certifications, and solutions for career seekers and working professionals. The company also offers a program portfolio such as AI/ML, generative AI, data science, full-stack product engineering, 5g, cloud technologies, cybersecurity, and game development, along with programs in digital marketing, business development, banking, insurance and finance, and virtual relationship management for digital enterprises. In addition, the company provides NIIT Digital, a platform that enables distinctive learning experiences for corporate and individual learners; the Institute of Finance Banking & Insurance (IFBI), a provider of learning services for early careers; NIIT StackRoute, a digital transformation partner for corporates to build multi-skilled full-stack professionals in advanced technologies; RPS Consulting, a provider of training programs on digital technologies; Talent Pipeline as a Service (TPaaS), helps organizations in hiring skilled talent; and Sales & Service Excellence (SSE) that helps businesses in creating an ecosystem for talent development. The company was incorporated in 1981 and is based in Gurugram, India.
