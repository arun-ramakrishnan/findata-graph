# Wall Education

## Overview

Wall Education Limited, also known as Hole-in-the-Wall Education, is a subsidiary of NIIT Limited. The company operates through a B2B model, providing educational services and learning solutions. Wall Education offers a fresh perspective for learning process with a unique collaborative learning approach that encourages children to express, learn, and explore themselves. The company focuses on innovative and child-centric educational approaches that differ from traditional learning methods.
