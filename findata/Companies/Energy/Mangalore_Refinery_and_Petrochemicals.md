# Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals

## Company Overview
Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL) is a subsidiary of Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC), engaged in petroleum refining and petrochemical manufacturing. The refinery has a capacity of 15 million tonnes per annum.
