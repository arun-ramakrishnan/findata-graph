# Samsung SDI Co., Ltd.

## Overview

Samsung SDI Co., Ltd. is a leading South Korean technology company specializing in battery manufacturing and electronic materials. The company operates through two main segments: Energy Solutions and Electronic Materials. Samsung SDI manufactures and sells small-sized lithium-ion batteries for laptops, tablets, mobile phones, wearable devices, power banks, power tools, garden tools, vacuum cleaners, e-bikes, e-scooters, and ignition products. The company also produces automotive batteries for electric vehicles and energy storage systems for residential, utility, commercial, UPS, and base transceiver station applications. Additionally, Samsung SDI provides electronic materials including semiconductor products (spin-on hardmasks, spin-on dielectrics, epoxy molding compounds), LCD products (polarizing films, color photo resist products), and OLED products (evaporation materials, thin film encapsulation products). The company also manufactures rechargeable battery raw materials and invests in new technology ventures.
