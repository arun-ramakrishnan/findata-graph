# Bharat Petroleum Corporation

## Company Overview
Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL; incorporated 1952; HQ Mumbai) is a leading state-owned oil marketing & refining company. It refines crude oil and markets fuels (MS, HSD, blended fuels), LPG (Bharatgas), lubricants (MAK), jet fuel/aviation, petrochemicals, bitumen, and runs city-gas distribution and a nationwide retail-outlet network with convenience stores and EV charging.
