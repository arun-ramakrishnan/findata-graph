# Bharat Petroleum Corporation

## Company Overview
Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL; incorporated 1952; HQ Mumbai) is a leading state-owned oil marketing & refining company. It refines crude oil and markets fuels (MS, HSD, blended fuels), LPG (Bharatgas), lubricants (MAK), jet fuel/aviation, petrochemicals, bitumen, and runs city-gas distribution and a nationwide retail-outlet network with convenience stores and EV charging.

## Key Insights
- Russian crude throughput fell from ~34% to ~24% of total in Q4 due to new sanctions limiting cargo availability — Russian crude had been a key driver of BPCL's refining-margin outperformance, so declining access pressures that advantage.
- Ambitious capex roadmap stepping up annually: ~₹20,000 cr (current year) → ~₹25,000 cr (FY26-27) → ~₹30,000 cr (subsequent year); major allocations to CGD, Mozambique expansion, Bina refinery expansion, and petrochemicals.
- Current-year capex split: ~₹5,900 cr refining, ~₹2,400 cr pipelines, ~₹5,600 cr marketing (incl. ~₹2,500 cr JV expansion). *(Source: The Chatter — Storm Clouds and Sunny Skies)*
