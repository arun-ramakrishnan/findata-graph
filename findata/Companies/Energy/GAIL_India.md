# GAIL (India) Limited

> **Note:** This entity is the GAIL (India) Limited *parent* (NSE: GAIL.NS) — India's largest natural-gas processing, transmission and marketing company. It is distinct from its *subsidiary* **GAIL Gas Limited** (unlisted), which runs city-gas-distribution networks. Financials and commentary here pertain to the listed parent.

## Company Overview

GAIL (India) Limited is India's largest state-owned natural-gas processing, transmission and marketing company, incorporated in 1984 and headquartered at GAIL Bhawan, New Delhi. It owns and operates the country's largest cross-country natural-gas pipeline network and is active across the full gas value chain — transmission, marketing, petrochemicals, LPG and liquid hydrocarbons, exploration & production, and increasingly renewable energy (wind, solar) and compressed biogas (CBG). The Government of India is the majority promoter.

## Business Segments

- **Transmission Services:** Operation of ~14,500+ km of cross-country natural-gas pipelines (HVJ, Dahej-Vijaipur, Jagdishpur-Haldia, MNJPL/Pradhan Mantri Urja Ganga, etc.) carrying gas to power, fertilizer, industrial, CGD, and city-gas customers.
- **Natural Gas Marketing:** Sourcing (LNG, domestic gas) and sale of natural gas to power, fertilizer, industrial, petrochemical, automotive (CNG), domestic (PNG) and commercial sectors.
- **Petrochemicals:** Manufacture of HDPE and LLDPE under the G-Lex and G-Lene brands, plus ethylene, propylene, polypropylene, at the Pata (Assam/Aurangya) and Usar complexes.
- **LPG & Other Liquid Hydrocarbons:** Production and marketing of LPG, propane, pentane, naphtha, mixed fuel oil from its gas-processing/fractionation units.
- **Exploration & Production (E&P):** Domestic and overseas equity stakes in gas/oil blocks.
- **Clean Energy:** Wind and solar power generation; compressed biogas (CBG) plants; city-gas distribution.
