# Mahanagar Gas

## Company Overview

Mahanagar Gas Limited (MGL) is a prominent Indian city gas distribution (CGD) company providing natural gas to Mumbai and its surrounding regions. The company manages an extensive network of pipelines supplying compressed natural gas (CNG) for the transport sector and piped natural gas (PNG) to domestic, industrial, and commercial users. A subsidiary of GAIL (India) Limited, MGL is one of India's largest CGD operators by volume.

## Core Business

- **CNG**: Transport-sector natural gas sold at retail stations — the volume engine, competitive vs petrol (~40–45% cheaper) and diesel (~12% cheaper).
- **Industrial & Commercial PNG**: Non-regulated segment priced linked to alternate fuels (bulk LPG, furnace oil / light diesel oil), so realisations float up when global energy prices rise.
- **Domestic PNG**: Regulated-priority segment, ~100% APM-sourced; volume growth tied to household conversions and the government's push to mandate PNG over bottled LPG in gas-equipped buildings.

## Gas Sourcing Mix

Blended cost of gas reflects a diversified procurement basket:

- ~30% domestically produced APM gas (regulated, cheapest, priority-protected for CNG/domestic PNG)
- ~21–22% New Well Gas (NWG) + pooled gas
- ~14–15% High Pressure High Temperature (HPHT) gas (bid-based, not allocation)
- ~21–22% Henry Hub (HH) indexed contract
- Balance via small Brent-linked contracts and IGX/spot purchases

Industrial/segment supply is partly curtailed during global tightness; CNG and domestic PNG are shielded by domestic-gas priority.

## Strategic Positioning

- **Cost-arbitrage moat:** CNG's ~40–45% discount to petrol sustains vehicle-conversion volumes.
- **Pricing flexibility:** Industrial/commercial PNG is indexed to alternate fuels, capturing margin when oil rises.
- **EV threat is narrow:** Confined largely to state-transport (STU) buses; private operators stay on CNG due to EV capex economics.
- **Inorganic optionality:** As APM allocation declines and weaker/newer CGD entrants struggle, MGL is open to acquisitions to expand its licensed footprint.
