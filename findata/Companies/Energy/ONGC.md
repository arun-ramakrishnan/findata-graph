## The Chatter — NSE ONGC SEBI

**First Samudra Manthan gas strike in Mahanadi:** Under the government's risk-sharing initiative ONGC struck natural gas where it had previously discovered gas — but this is the first strike under Samudra Manthan, with the government sharing exploration risk directly alongside operators for the first time.

**Scale is prospective, not proven:** Mahanadi basin estimated at ~600 million metric tonnes of oil equivalent (MMTOE) of total prospective resources; conversion to proven reserves is an ongoing process as the drilling campaign progresses.

**Baseline flow + depth economics:** Discovery well tested at ~0.25 MMSCMD (testing ongoing). Gas was struck at a relatively shallow, cost-effective depth; deeper prospective targets remain to be drilled, and the field development plan waits on consolidating volumes across both plays.

**Long appraisal runway; ignore speculative capex:** A single deepwater appraisal well takes ~3 months, so full appraisal needs more time — market capex figures floating around are unviable and unconfirmed.

**Three-tier state support + import-substitution math:** Government funds 2D/3D seismic, partly funds exploratory drilling (the major capex risk), and backs common offshore infra hubs — cutting upfront capex and break-even for deepwater. India imports ~50% of its natural gas; the find supports the 6–7% → 15%-by-2030 gas-mix target.

> "We now have a very enabling environment because, through Samudra Manthan, the government is for the first time sharing exploration risks directly alongside operating companies."
> — O. P. Sinha, Director (Exploration)
