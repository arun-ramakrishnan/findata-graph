# Jaiprakash Power

## Company Overview

Jaiprakash Power Ventures Limited (NSE/BSE: JPPOWER) is an Indian power-generation company and part of the Jaypee Group. It operates thermal power plants including the **Bina** and **Nigrie** facilities, and holds limestone/land banks with optionality for both thermal and future nuclear generation. The company has historically carried high debt, with several assets sold or restructured under NCLT proceedings.
