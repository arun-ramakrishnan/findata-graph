# Energy Infrastructure Trust

## Company Overview

Energy Infrastructure Trust (EIT) is an Infrastructure Investment Trust (InvIT) owning and operating a 1,485 km natural gas transmission pipeline and related infrastructure in India. It transports gas from the KG Basin (east coast) to demand centres in western and northern India, offers imbalance services, and provides stable investor distributions backed by energy-asset cash flows. The trust is sponsored by an affiliate of Brookfield Asset Management, which acquired the pipeline from the Reliance Group in March 2019.

- **Type:** InvIT (Brookfield-sponsored)
- **Core asset:** East-West bi-directional natural gas pipeline, 1,485 km, ~85 mmscmd capacity
- **Acquired:** March 2019 (from Reliance Group)
- **First pure gas-transmission company in India** (pipeline built 2008)
