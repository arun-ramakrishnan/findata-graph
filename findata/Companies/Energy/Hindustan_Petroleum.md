# Hindustan Petroleum

## Company Overview
Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL), founded in 1910 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a Maharatna central public sector enterprise and a subsidiary of Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC). HPCL is a leading Indian oil refining and marketing company with a large-scale refining footprint (35.8 MMTPA capacity, scaling to 45.3 MMTPA by 2027-28) and an extensive fuel marketing network. It operates through Downstream Petroleum and All Other segments, spanning light/middle/heavy distillates, CNG, lubes, biofuels, petrochemicals, and increasingly natural gas and renewable energy. The company holds a 20.3% share of domestic petroleum product sales against 13.87% refining capacity.

## Key Insights (from Edition #24, Mar 2026)
- HPCL's 20.3% market share significantly outpaces its 13.87% refining capacity, creating structural reliance on external product purchases; expanding refining capacity (to 45.3 MMTPA by 2027-28) is essential to internalise margins and reduce exposure to volatile procurement costs.
- Operating refineries at 105-115% utilisation means HPCL has reached its internal production ceiling — future earnings growth is now highly sensitive to global price spreads rather than further volume gains.
- The 9.5 MMTPA capacity addition (HRRL greenfield refinery 9.0 + HMEL 0.5) captures integrated margins by eliminating expensive third-party fuel purchases, protecting earnings from volatile global price spreads.
- Portfolio pivot: scaling CNG and petrochemical capacity (2.2 → 4.6 MMTPA) plus biofuels (300 TMT) hedges against plateauing domestic demand for traditional fuels and secures growth in cleaner energy markets.
- R&D pivot toward technological self-reliance: 302 granted patents and focus on indigenous catalysts (HP-ASAP, HP-RAMP, H2 PSA, HP-Trij H-CNG, HP-HiGAS, HP-DAK, HP-VRU) reduce dependence on foreign licences, lowering operating costs and protecting long-term refining margins.
- Green hydrogen is moving from research to industrial scale: India's first refinery-based green H2 plant (370 TPA at Visakh), plus 5,000 TPA (Visakh) and 4,300 TPA (HRRL) projects underway; HPGRDC working on PEM, alkaline, AEM, SOE/SOFC electrolyser technologies.
- Net Zero roadmap relies on biogas for nearly half of emission reductions — investors must weigh the massive capital commitment against long-term regulatory survival.
