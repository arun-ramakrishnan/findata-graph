# Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC)

## Company Overview
Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the exploration, development, production, and distribution of crude oil, natural gas, and value-added products in India and internationally. It operates through Exploration and Production, Refining & Marketing, and Petrochemicals segments. The company engages in the refining and marketing of petroleum products; liquefied natural gas supply; pipelines for transportation of petroleum products; SEZ development; helicopter services; and production of ethanol, sugar, petrochemicals, liquefied petroleum gas, naphtha, ethane, propane, butane, kerosene oil, low sulphur heavy stock, residual crude oil, mineral turpentine oil, aviation turbine fuel, and high speed diesel. It also generates wind power through a total installed capacity of 153.9 MW; and solar power through a total installed capacity of 39.96 MW. The company also exports its products. Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited was founded in 1955 and is based in New Delhi, India.

## The Chatter — ONGC, Divi's, LG, Pinelabs & More

**New-well gas pricing tailwind:** New-well gas is priced at 12% of international crude (~$10.8/mmbtu at $90 Brent); management expects 80–90% of ONGC's gas to qualify as 'new-well' within 4–6 years as old fields deplete, structurally lifting cash flow.

**OPaL turnaround materializing:** Petrochem subsidiary OPaL delivered ₹1,206 cr EBITDA last year (impacted by March gas diversion); FY guidance raised to ₹1,500–2,000 cr EBITDA, easing the drag on consolidated margins.

**OVL/Sakhalin back at full tilt:** Sakhalin production has recovered to pre-Ukraine levels and is OVL's top asset; FID taken and FPSO contracted for BM-C-34 (Brazil, Sergipe-Alagoas), with OVL's net share ~1.3 MTPA starting 2030 (oil) / 2031 (gas).

**No SAED, full Brent realization:** Government did not impose SAED despite OMC losses; ONGC received flat Brent prices (even at $140) vs. earlier discounts of up to ₹2 lakh cr — described as the best outcome for an ONGC investor.

**Dahej port JV + Q4 one-off:** New JV with Gujarat Maritime Board for a Dahej port (closest to northern demand, serving the 8MMT Kandla–Gorakhpur LPG pipeline); Q4 also included a ~₹1,800 cr one-time write-off on the returned DSF-4 block.

> "The macro picture is that gas is currently more lucrative for us. We should call ourselves a gas and oil company, rather than an oil and gas company."
> — Arun Kumar Singh, Chairman and CEO
