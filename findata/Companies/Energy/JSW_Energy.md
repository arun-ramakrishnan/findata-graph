# JSW Energy

## Company Overview
JSW Energy Limited, incorporated in 1994 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a leading Indian power producer transitioning from a thermal-heavy base toward a diversified green energy portfolio. Part of the JSW Group, it operates across the power value chain — generation, transmission, and trading — with capacity spanning thermal, hydro, wind, solar, and hybrid assets across Karnataka, Punjab, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Himachal Pradesh, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, and Rajasthan. The company also engages in coal mining in South Africa and is building a green hydrogen business.

## Key Insights (from Edition #24, Mar 2026)
- Liquidity risk has decreased as debtor days fell from 96 to 73, providing the cash-flow stability required for heavy capital investments; shifting toward higher-quality offtakers (SECI, group captives) secures more predictable collection cycles.
- A diversified fuel source and offtaker base de-risks the business model; ~3.3 GW (~25%) of current installed capacity is tied to group captive and C&I customers with higher tariffs, scaling to ~5.7 GW by 2030, driving quality of underlying EBITDA and stable cash flows.
- Transitioning to a 14 GW renewable pipeline secures long-term yields and protects margins from spot-market price volatility, moving the portfolio away from fuel-sensitive thermal assets toward stable green infrastructure.
- Energy storage (40 GWh target by 2030) — dominant in pumped hydro assets (26.4 GWh of PHESFA signed with MSEDCL 12.0 + UPPCL 12.0 + PCKL 2.4) — creates a structural moat by addressing renewable intermittency and capturing premium peak-demand pricing.
- Q3 FY26: overall generation down 1.2% YoY to 422 BUs due to subdued thermal generation; thermal share fell to 73% (from 76% in Q3 FY25) while RE (ex-hydro) grew 18% YoY (wind +26.5%, solar +18.7%) and hydro grew 13.5% to 35 BUs — renewables are actively displacing thermal generation.
