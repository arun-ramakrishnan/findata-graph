# JSW Energy

## Company Overview
JSW Energy Limited, incorporated in 1994 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a leading Indian power producer transitioning from a thermal-heavy base toward a diversified green energy portfolio. Part of the JSW Group, it operates across the power value chain — generation, transmission, and trading — with capacity spanning thermal, hydro, wind, solar, and hybrid assets across Karnataka, Punjab, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Himachal Pradesh, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, and Rajasthan. The company also engages in coal mining in South Africa and is building a green hydrogen business.
