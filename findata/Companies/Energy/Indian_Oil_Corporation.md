## overview
## Overview

Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) is India's largest oil refining and marketing company, operating as a Maharatna public sector undertaking. The company dominates India's downstream petroleum sector with extensive refining, pipeline, and marketing infrastructure.

## Core Business Areas

- **Refining**: Operation of 11 refineries with combined capacity of over 80 MMTPA
- **Pipeline Transportation**: Largest cross-country pipeline network for petroleum products
- **Marketing**: Extensive retail network of fuel stations across India
- **Petrochemicals**: Manufacturing of petrochemical products and specialty chemicals

## Key Products

- **Transportation Fuels**: Petrol, diesel, aviation turbine fuel, and marine fuels
- **LPG**: Indane cooking gas cylinders for domestic and commercial use
- **Lubricants**: Servo brand lubricants for automotive and industrial applications
- **Petrochemicals**: Polymers, synthetic rubber, and specialty chemicals

## Strategic Initiatives

- **Energy Transition**: Expanding into renewable energy and biofuels
- **Digital Transformation**: Digital fuel stations and customer engagement platforms
- **Clean Energy**: CNG stations, EV charging infrastructure, and hydrogen fuel
- **International Operations**: Overseas investments in exploration and marketing

## The Chatter — Indian Oil Muthoot Maruti

**Crude sourcing pivoted hard to spot:** Spot crude imports jumped to ~84% in the quarter (vs 51% a year ago), diversified across Russia, Venezuela, Brazil and African grades to keep refineries running through geopolitical disruption.

> "Spot import for the quarter stood at about 84%, against 51% last year."
> — Anuj Jain, Director - Finance

**Quarter loss driven by marketing-margin compression:** Revenue from operations rose to ₹2,75,972 cr (vs ₹2,32,855 cr sequentially) on higher product prices, but retail-fuel marketing margins were squeezed by volatile international crude/product prices, swinging the company into a quarterly loss.

**Absorbed crude spikes to hold domestic prices:** Management deliberately absorbed part of the international crude spike to shield domestic consumers from inflation, betting that solid volume throughput positions IOCL for a fast margin recovery as energy markets normalise.

**Major capacity additions commissioning FY26-27:** Refining and petrochemical expansion at Panipat (by Dec 2026), Barauni (by Dec 2026) and Gujarat (by Nov 2026) are at advanced execution; Paradip also nears completion — phased commissioning begins late 2026.

**Debt up ₹31,000 cr in a single quarter:** Borrowings rose sharply to fund working capital from expensive crude, but management says banking arrangements keep funding costs competitive given the balance-sheet strength.

**Green pivot via Tera Green:** 18 GW renewable target over 3-4 years; the 100%-owned subsidiary Tera Green already has 4-5 GW under execution. A JV (with MoPNG + Ministry of Shipping) to procure four MR vessels will secure freight and the crude supply chain.

**LPG under-recoveries — counting on sovereign support:** Management expects government compensation for subsidized-LPG under-recoveries, citing past practice of full support to PSU OMCs during high-price periods.
