# Deep Industries Limited

## Company Overview

Deep Industries Limited (DIL) is a leading Indian energy services company specializing in comprehensive solutions for the oil and gas industry. With a strong focus on innovation, technology, and operational excellence, Deep Industries has established itself as a key player in the post-exploration oil and gas value chain, offering a wide range of services and equipment for both onshore and offshore energy operations.

### Core Business Segments

- **Air & Gas Compression:** Advanced compression solutions for oil and gas operations
- **Drilling Services:** Comprehensive drilling and workover services
- **Gas Dehydration:** Specialized gas processing and dehydration solutions
- **Integrated Project Management:** End-to-end project management for energy operations
- **Offshore Support Services:** Marine operations and offshore support capabilities

### Comprehensive Service Portfolio

**Onshore Services:**
- **Natural Gas Processing:** Complete solutions for natural gas processing operations
- **Drilling & Workovers:** Advanced drilling and workover services for oil and gas wells
- **Compression Solutions:** Air and gas compression services for energy operations
- **Dehydration Services:** Gas dehydration and processing solutions
- **Production Enhancement:** Technologies and services for production optimization

**Offshore Services:**

- **PSV/AHTS Vessels:** Platform supply vessels and anchor handling tug supply vessels
- **Marine Operations:** Comprehensive marine support for offshore energy projects
- **Offshore Support:** Integrated offshore energy services and logistics
