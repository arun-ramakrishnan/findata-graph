# Adani Total Gas

## Company Overview
Adani Total Gas Limited (ATGL), founded in 2004 and headquartered in Ahmedabad, is one of India's largest city gas distribution (CGD) companies. A joint venture between the Adani Group and France's TotalEnergies, it supplies piped natural gas (PNG) to domestic, commercial and industrial customers and compressed natural gas (CNG) to the transport sector. Beyond core CGD, ATGL is building a diversified energy platform spanning EV charging, bio-energy (biogas, biofuel, biomass), LCNG, HCNG, and hydrogen. The company holds 34 CGD licenses across 53 geographical areas.
