# Bloom Energy

## Company Overview

Bloom Energy Corporation (NYSE: BE) is a US-based clean-energy company headquartered in San Jose, California, that designs and manufactures solid-oxide fuel cells (the "Bloom Energy Server" / "Bloom Box"). The fuel cells generate on-site electricity from natural gas, biogas, or hydrogen with high efficiency and low emissions. Customers include data centers, utilities, and large industrial users seeking reliable, low-carbon behind-the-meter power.
