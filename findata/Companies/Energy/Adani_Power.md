# Adani Power

**Ticker:** ADANIPOWER | **Sector:** Energy | **Market Cap:** Mid Cap

## Overview
Adani Power Limited is one of India's largest private thermal power generation companies. The company is part of the Adani Group and operates several coal-based power plants across India. It plays a significant role in the country's power generation infrastructure with a substantial installed capacity portfolio.

## The Chatter — Meesho, Marico, Bajaj & More

**Revenue visibility via PPAs:** 95% of operating capacity is tied up under long- and medium-term PPAs, derisking the business from short-term merchant-market volatility.

**International + nuclear push:** Adani Power is expanding beyond India — a new SPV in Bhutan for a 570 MW hydro plant, plus Indian SPVs to pursue nuclear projects (site identification and approvals underway), aligned with the country's 9 GW to 100 GW nuclear ambition.

**EBITDA aspiration:** Management targets ~INR 50,000 crore EBITDA conservatively by FY31 (potentially FY30 if current plans execute without unforeseen hurdles).

**Coal and merchant dynamics:** No domestic coal shortage, and imported-coal cost increases are passed through; with renewables rising, merchant prices are expected to decline, hence the push to lock in more long-term PPAs (~13 GW bids live plus ~4 GW newly issued from Gujarat).

**Bangladesh receivables improving:** Outstanding Bangladesh dues are declining with regular collections, and an expert has been appointed to adjudicate the disputed amount.

> "So, Bharat Bhai, we should be in a position to achieve INR50,000 crore conservatively by FY 2031. If what we have planned today, if we could achieve that... In that case, we touch this even in 2030."

## The Chatter — Infosys Adani Power

**Record power demand validates thermal baseload:** Peak demand hit ~250 GW in May 2026 and Q1 FY27 energy consumption rose 8.4% YoY to 485 bn units, reinforcing the need for large-scale thermal capacity additions.

**Capacity plan raised:** Beyond the existing 24 GW pipeline (now tagged to specific sites), Adani Power is planning ~3 GW of additional thermal capacity because state resource-adequacy studies show large baseload deficits over the next 5–6 years; ~13 GW of state bids are live (UP 4,000 MW, Gujarat 4,000 MW, Uttarakhand 1,320 MW, WB 3,800 MW).

**Nuclear ambition scaled to 10 GW:** Management raised its nuclear target to 10 GW, but investments and technology choices wait on the Government notifying operating rules under the amended Atomic Energy framework; the acquired Jaiprakash (Bina, Nigri) land banks offer optionality for both thermal and nuclear.

**Bangladesh collections improving:** Monthly inflows from Bangladesh Power Development Board now consistently ~$100 mn (above monthly billings), with Q1 receivables near ~$400 mn and expected to keep declining.

**Merchant exposure being eliminated:** The Butibori and Tuticorin plants moved from merchant to PPAs; merchant units fell from 6 bn (PY) to 4 bn this quarter — management intends to tie up almost everything via medium/long-term PPAs to remove market-price volatility.

> "During the quarter, India experienced a hotter than usual summer with sustained heat waves across most regions. Due to these high temperatures, peak demand shot up to a record high of around 250 gigawatts in May 2026, while overall energy consumption rose 8.4% year-on-year to 485 billion units for Q1 FY27."
> — S.B. Khyalia, Chief Executive Officer
