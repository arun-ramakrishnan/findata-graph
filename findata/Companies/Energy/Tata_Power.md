# Tata Power | Large Cap | Energy

Tata Power Company is India's largest vertically integrated power company, operating in renewable, thermal, and hydro energy generation, transmission, trading, distribution, and next-gen energy solutions.

## [Concall]

Discussing the massive untapped potential in India's rooftop solar market and Tata Power's leadership position
"So, rooftop, I think it's just the tip of the iceberg. Only 20 lakh rooftops have been installed till September and my expectation is that in this country at least 5 crore homes will have rooftop. And so there is a long way that we have to go to cater to the country's requirements. So this is a very good opportunity. We are one of the best in the country with maximum market share of more than 20% and this is going to be a very sustained effort from Tata Power side for many more years in future."

## Nuclear Energy Strategy

Private sector entry into nuclear requires law and liability framework amendments.

"We are waiting for the change in the law wherein government has to amend the law and allowing private sector participation in nuclear power plants also the civil liability law."

- Dr. Praveer Sinha, CEO & MD

## Fuel Strategy

Despite new government rules, the company won't switch Mundra to domestic coal due to specs mismatch and high transport costs.

"See the Mudra plant is designed for imported coal and imported coal has different chemical characteristics. It's not just the heat rate but what is the sulfur contained, nitrogen contained, what is the ash content. [...] Secondly, the cost of coal if we get from eastern part of India especially in terms of the transportation cost is very high."

## The Chatter — Reliance Axis Bank UltraTech

**First major government pumped-hydro bid won — Bhivpuri (1,000 MW):** SECI bid secured on the strength of advanced project readiness (3 × ~334 MW units; first unit tied up). Establishes Tata Power as a leader in a new high-growth storage segment.

**40-year PPA tenure + 8-hour daily supply:** Pumped hydro offers ~8 hours/day supply year-round and a much longer operating life than batteries — the structural advantage underpinning 40-year contract certainty of supply and tariff.

**Round-the-clock clean energy play:** Combined with solar/wind, pumped storage enables 24-hour clean energy for discretionary industries and data centres — a positioning edge in India's evolving power market.

**Complementary, not competitive, with BESS:** Batteries deploy faster but offer shorter duration (~10–12 yr life); Tata Power expects a 50/50 long-term split between pumped hydro and battery storage across the grid.

**Pipeline expanding — Shirawata (1,800 MW) next:** Government targets 35 GW energy storage by 2032; existing hydro assets give Tata Power a competitive advantage (one reservoir already in place).

**Returns higher than conventional renewables:** Pumped hydro carries higher execution risk than solar/wind, so management expects returns "in the higher teens range."

> "This is the first large bid from a government entity, and we were successful because the project is already at a very advanced stage."
> — Praveer Sinha, CEO & Managing Director

> "Pumped storage has the advantage of providing around eight hours of supply every day throughout the year. It also has a much longer operating life, which is why this PPA has tenure of 40 years."
> — Praveer Sinha, CEO & Managing Director
