# PTC India

## Company Overview
PTC India Limited is a prominent player in India's power trading sector, established in April 1999 to provide credit risk mitigation to private power project developers. Through a unique Public-Private Partnership model, PTC engages in long-term and short-term power trading to address supply and demand disparities across different regions.
