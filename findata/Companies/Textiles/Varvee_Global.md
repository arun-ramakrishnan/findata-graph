# Varvee Global

## Overview

Varvee Global Limited (formerly Aarvee Denims & Exports), is a global player in textile industry with a strong presence worldwide. The company follows a B2B business model, serving customers globally with textile products. Varvee Global, nurtured by experienced promoters from Arora & VB Group, showcases in-house manufacturing capabilities and cutting-edge technologies for stringent quality control. Its vertical integrated production process enables rapid response to market needs, offering diverse designs with swift turnaround times. The company is implementing a capital reallocation strategy to diversify away from core denim toward higher-margin non-denim fabrics to reduce concentration risk and improve return on capital.
