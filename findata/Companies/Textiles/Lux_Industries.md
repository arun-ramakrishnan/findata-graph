# Lux Industries Limited

## Company Overview
Lux Industries Limited, founded in 1957 (formerly Biswanath Hosiery Mills, renamed in 1995) and headquartered in Kolkata, is a leading innerwear and knitwear manufacturer serving men, women, and children. The company's brand portfolio includes Lux Cozi, ONN, GenX, Lux Ferno, Lux Venus, Lux Cott's Wool, Lux Nitro, Lyra, Lux Touch, and Lux Karishma, spanning vests, briefs, boxers, T-shirts, hoodies, polo, shorts, tracks, leggings, bras, and panties. Products are exported globally.
