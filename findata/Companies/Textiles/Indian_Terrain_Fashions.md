# Indian Terrain Fashions

Indian Terrain Fashions Ltd (ITFL) is a ready-made garment retailer offering men's wear, boys wear, and fashion accessories. Established in 2009 as a division of Celebrity Fashions Limited; independent since September 2010. Products span shirts, trousers, t-shirts, jeanswear, shorts, sweaters, belts, bags, wallets, socks — via D2C and Wholesale channels.
