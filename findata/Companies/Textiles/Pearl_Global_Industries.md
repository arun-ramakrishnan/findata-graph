**Global-vendor differentiation:** In a ~$550bn international garment trade (China $bn-scale, Bangladesh $48bn, Vietnam $44bn, India/Indonesia $15–17bn each), the Vietnam–Bangladesh–Indonesia–India footprint plus design hubs serving the US, EU, UK, Australia, Japan and Canada makes global retailers reliant on Pearl vs single-geography competitors.

**Margin levers + funded capex:** Operational leverage on front-loaded design teams plus vertical integration (in-house knit/jersey fabric) and in-sourced washing/dyeing for high-fashion denims. ₹725 crore capex over four years (₹300–325 crore capacity, ₹350–375 crore vertical integration), over ₹400 crore already Board-sanctioned; EPS/PAT to compound at or above EBITDA with no equity dilution; ROCE to stay above 22% on 40 tonnes of captive fabric consumption with 4–5-year capital turnover.
