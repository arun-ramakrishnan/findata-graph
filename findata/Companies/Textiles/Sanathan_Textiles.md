# Sanathan Textiles

## Company Overview
Sanathan Textiles Limited, incorporated in 2005 and headquartered in Mumbai, India, is a textile manufacturer specializing in high-quality yarns for domestic and international markets. It produces polyester filament yarns (PFY), cotton yarns, and technical-textile yarns, serving applications across apparel, athleisure, automotive fabrics, home furnishings, medical, and logistics. Key brands include Puro Cotton Yarns, Sanathan Reviro (recycled), Born Dyed, Sanathan Drycool, S-Flex, CDP, and Sanathan Stretch.
