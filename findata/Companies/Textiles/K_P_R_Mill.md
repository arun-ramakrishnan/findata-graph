# K.P.R. Mill

## Company Overview

K.P.R. Mill Limited is a vertically integrated apparel manufacturing company based in Coimbatore, operating through Textile, Sugar, and Others segments. The company produces a wide range of yarns (compact, combed, carded, melange, organic, BCI), knitted cotton fabrics, and readymade garments (casual, sports, active, sleep, and work wear) under the FASO brand. It also produces sugar, ethanol, and green energy through co-gen and wind power. Founded in 1984.
