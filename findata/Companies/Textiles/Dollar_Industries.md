## Brand Repositioning Strategy

Dollar is undertaking a comprehensive brand repositioning initiative to broaden appeal beyond men's innerwear segment.

### Change In The Brand Architecture

#### What Are We Doing?
- Created six new category identities: ASIAN, ANTIATIS, LIBRIS, INDIATIS, THERMALS, FRATISCT
- Established Dollar as brand contender for Dollar Man segment
- Signed Latin Eakston as brand contender for Dollar Women
- Refreshed logos to enhance consumer connection
- Implemented differentiated price recognition initiatives

#### Why This Transformation?
To change the market perception that Dollar is just a men's innerwear brand and expand into broader apparel segments.

#### Outcomes Achieved
- Significantly enhanced brand recall through dedicated branding initiatives
- Improved brand positioning for men's 45+ consumers through differentiated pricing
- Seamless connection with enthusiasts catering to their specific needs
- Better alignment with diverse consumer segments
