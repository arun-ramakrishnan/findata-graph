# The Cotton Corporation of India (CCI)

## Company Overview
The Cotton Corporation of India (CCI) is a government-owned public sector undertaking that plays a crucial role in India's cotton market. CCI operates as a Price Support Agency (PSA) under the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, primarily responsible for ensuring fair prices to cotton farmers through Minimum Support Price (MSP) operations.

## Industry Classification
- **Sector**: Agriculture - Cotton Trading & Price Support
- **Type**: Government Public Sector Undertaking (PSU)
- **Parent**: Ministry of Textiles, Government of India
- **Function**: Price Support Agency for Cotton

## Business Profile
CCI operates as a government agency focused on cotton market stabilization:

- **Price Support Operations**: Implementing MSP to ensure fair prices for cotton farmers
- **Cotton Procurement**: Large-scale procurement of cotton at support prices
- **Market Intervention**: Stabilizing cotton prices and preventing market volatility
- **Stock Management**: Maintaining significant cotton reserves for market stability
- **Farmer Support**: Providing price certainty to cotton growers across India
