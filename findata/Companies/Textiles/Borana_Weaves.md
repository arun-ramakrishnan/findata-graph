# Borana Weaves

Borana Weaves, a textile manufacturer in Surat, specializes in producing unbleached synthetic grey fabric. This fabric is a crucial material for industries like fashion, traditional textiles, technical textiles, home decor, and interior design, serving as a base for dyeing and printing processes.

## Company Overview

### Location & Operations
- **Based in Surat**: Textile manufacturing hub in India
- **Specialization**: Unbleached synthetic grey fabric production
- **Value Chain Integration**: From fabric to finished textile products
- **Base Material Provider**: Key supplier to dyeing, printing, and processing industries
