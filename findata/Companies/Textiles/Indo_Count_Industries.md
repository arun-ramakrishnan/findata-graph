# Indo Count Industries Limited

## Company Overview
Indo Count Industries Limited, incorporated in 1988 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a leading home textile manufacturer and exporter. The company produces bed sheets, fashion bedding, utility bedding, and institutional bedding, marketed under brands including Pure Earth, Boutique Living, Heirlooms of India, The Pure Collection, Atlas, Linen Closet, and Simply-put. Products are distributed through multi-brand outlets, large format stores, e-commerce, and showrooms, with significant international exports.
