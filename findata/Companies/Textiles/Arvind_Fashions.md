# Arvind Fashions Limited

## Company Overview
Arvind Fashions Limited, incorporated in 2016 and headquartered in Bengaluru, is a leading apparel retailer of owned and licensed international brands. Its portfolio includes US Polo Assn, Arrow, Tommy Hilfiger, Flying Machine, and Calvin Klein, spanning menswear, womenswear, kidswear, footwear, and accessories. Products are sold through retail and departmental stores, brand outlets, and e-commerce.
