# Garware Technical Fibres

## Overview

Garware Technical Fibres is a global manufacturer and supplier of technical textiles, specializing in high-performance products. The company follows a B2B business model, serving customers across multiple industries with specialized textile solutions. Garware Technical Fibres manufactures and supplies aquaculture cage nets, fishing nets, sports nets & safety nets, agricultural nets, coated fabrics, synthetic ropes, and geosynthetics. The company focuses on high-performance technical textiles with diverse applications across aquaculture, fishing, sports, agriculture, and infrastructure sectors.
