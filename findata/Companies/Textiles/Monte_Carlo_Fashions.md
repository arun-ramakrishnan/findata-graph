# Monte Carlo Fashions

## Company Overview
Manufactures and trades in woolen, cotton, and cotton-blended garments for men, women, and kids in India and internationally. Operates as subsidiary of Oswal Woollen Mills Limited. Focus on building comprehensive range of cotton and cotton-blended products catering to all seasons. India's apparel market expected to grow from USD 115.70 billion in 2024 to USD 171.60 billion by 2034 (4% CAGR), with per capita spending reaching ₹8,000 by FY26.
