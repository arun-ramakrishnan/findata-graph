# Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL)

## Overview

Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) is India's premier aerospace and defense company, engaged in the design, development, manufacture, and maintenance of aircraft, helicopters, avionics, and defense systems. As a government-owned enterprise, HAL plays a critical role in India's aerospace and defense capabilities.
