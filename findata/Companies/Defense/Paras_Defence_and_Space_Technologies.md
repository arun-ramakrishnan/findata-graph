# Paras Defence and Space Technologies

## Company Overview

Paras Defence and Space Technologies Limited (formerly Paras Flowform Engineering) designs, develops, manufactures, and tests defense and space engineering products in India and internationally. Founded in 2009, headquartered in Navi Mumbai. The company serves defense, aerospace, and space sectors with niche, high-precision capabilities.
