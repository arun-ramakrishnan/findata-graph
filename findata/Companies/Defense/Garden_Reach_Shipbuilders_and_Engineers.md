Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers Ltd is a premier shipbuilding company in India under administrative control of Ministry of Defence, primarily catering to shipbuilding requirements of Indian Navy and Indian Coast Guard. As one of India's oldest and most established shipyards, GRSE has played a crucial role in building naval vessels and supporting India's maritime defense capabilities.
