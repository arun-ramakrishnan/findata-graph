# MTAR Technologies

## Company Overview
MTAR Technologies Limited (founded 1969; HQ Hyderabad, Telangana) is a precision engineering company manufacturing critical, close-tolerance components and assemblies for sectors of national importance — primarily clean energy, nuclear, space, and aerospace & defense. Its capabilities span precision machining, assembly, testing, quality control, and specialized fabrication for strategic customers including ISRO, NPCIL, and global aerospace OEMs.
