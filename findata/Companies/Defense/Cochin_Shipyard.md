# Cochin Shipyard

## Company Overview
Cochin Shipyard Limited (CSL), founded in 1969 and headquartered in Kochi, Kerala, is a Government of India-owned (Ministry of Shipping) public sector shipbuilder. It specializes in shipbuilding (defence vessels, commercial ships, offshore structures) and ship repair, and is the only Indian yard capable of building up to 110,000 DWT and repairing up to 125,000 DWT vessels.

## The Chatter — KEI Ind Cochin Ship

**Order book at ₹22,000 cr, L1 on five Navy survey vessels:** Unexecuted order book ~₹22,000 cr provides strong revenue visibility; L1 for five Next Generation Survey Vessels (~₹5,000 cr) takes the book to ~₹27,000 cr once concluded.

**₹87,000 cr Navy pipeline in play:** Management is bidding for three upcoming programmes — LPD (4 vessels, ~₹32,000 cr), MCMV minesweepers (~₹6,000 cr) and P17 Bravo (7 frigates, ~₹49,000 cr).

**Conservative 12% growth guide, 14–15% blended margin:** Historical 12–15% turnover growth; shipbuilding (70% of turnover) earns 10–12% margins vs 20–22% in ship repair (30%), blending to ~14–15%.

**Nomination-order margin tailwind is over:** The last 3 years' high margins came from nomination orders (indigenous aircraft carrier, carrier refits); all future defence work is tender-based — margin normalization ahead. Negative operating cash flow is a payment-terms artifact of European export orders (only ~30% received during construction); ~6 vessels deliver this FY, releasing the remaining 60–70% and turning cash flow positive.

**Capex cycle with subsidy tailwind; HD KSOE JV scrapped:** 5-year expansion at 20:80 debt:equity, aided by the Shipbuilding Development Scheme (25% capex subsidy + ~3% interest subvention); ROCE threshold 14–15%. After failing to agree JV terms with HD KSOE, CSL will build the block fabrication facility alone at 60,000 tpa (72,000 tpa total) vs the originally planned 1 lakh tpa. Green tugs (electric at ~2x diesel cost) see real adoption in 3–4 years; the hydrogen fuel-cell vessel is a technology demonstration, not a near-term business.

> "Our current unexecuted order book stands at around ₹22,000 crores, which continues to provide us with good revenue visibility. We have also been declared L1 for five Next Generation Survey Vessels for the Indian Navy, valued at approximately ₹5,000 crores. Once this contract is concluded, the order book will be around ₹27,000 crores."
> — Jose V J, Chairman & Managing Director, Cochin Shipyard
