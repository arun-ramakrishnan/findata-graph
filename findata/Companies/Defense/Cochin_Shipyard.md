# Cochin Shipyard

## Company Overview
Cochin Shipyard Limited (CSL), founded in 1969 and headquartered in Kochi, Kerala, is a Government of India-owned (Ministry of Shipping) public sector shipbuilder. It specializes in shipbuilding (defence vessels, commercial ships, offshore structures) and ship repair, and is the only Indian yard capable of building up to 110,000 DWT and repairing up to 125,000 DWT vessels.
