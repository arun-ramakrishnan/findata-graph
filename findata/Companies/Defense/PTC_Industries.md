# PTC Industries

## Company Overview

PTC Industries Limited manufactures and sells high-precision metal castings in India and internationally. The company works with alloy steel, stainless steel, duplex/super duplex, nickel-based alloys, cobalt-based alloys, and nickel aluminum bronze, as well as creep and heat-resistant steels. Its proprietary processes include RepliCast, RapidCast, and ForgeCAST. Founded in 1963 (formerly Precision Tools & Castings), headquartered in Lucknow.
