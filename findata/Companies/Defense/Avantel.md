## Company Overview

Avantel Limited (incorporated 1990, Hyderabad) manufactures wireless communication products for defense and aerospace applications. The company provides software-defined radios, satellite communication equipment, HF communication equipment, radar systems, network management systems, and embedded systems/digital signal processing solutions. ~453 employees.
