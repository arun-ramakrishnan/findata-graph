# Bharat Dynamics Limited

**Ticker:** BDL.BO | **Sector:** Defense/Aerospace | **Market Cap:** Mid Cap

## Company Overview

Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL) is a premier Indian defense public sector undertaking operating under the Ministry of Defence. Established as a strategic defense manufacturer, BDL plays a critical role in India's defense indigenization program and the Atmanirbhar Bharat (Self-Reliant India) initiative. The company specializes in the design, development, and manufacturing of advanced missile systems, ammunition, and defense equipment for the Indian Armed Forces.

### Core Business Segments

- **Missile Systems:** Comprehensive manufacturing of various missile systems including:
  - Surface-to-air missiles for air defense
  - Air-to-air missiles for aerial combat
  - Anti-tank guided missiles for ground warfare
  - Strategic and tactical missile systems

- **Ammunition:** Production of advanced ammunition for diverse defense applications:
  - Precision-guided munitions
  - Artillery ammunition
  - Small arms ammunition
  - Specialized defense ordnance

- **Defense Electronics:** Manufacturing of sophisticated defense electronics and systems:
  - Guidance and control systems
  - Electronic warfare systems
  - Defense communication systems
  - Integrated weapon systems

- **Export Operations:** Strategic supply of defense products to friendly foreign countries:
  - International defense partnerships
  - Export of missile systems and components
  - Technology transfer agreements
  - Global defense collaborations

### Strategic Business Units

- **Missile Manufacturing Division:** State-of-the-art production facilities for missile systems
- **Ammunition Production:** Advanced manufacturing for defense ammunition
- **Defense Electronics:** High-technology electronics for defense applications
- **Research & Development:** Continuous innovation in defense technologies
- **Export & International Relations:** Global defense partnerships and exports
