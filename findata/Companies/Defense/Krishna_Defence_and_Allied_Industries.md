# Krishna Defence and Allied Industries

## Company Overview
Krishna Defence and Allied Industries Limited (founded 1996; HQ Mumbai) designs and manufactures equipment for defence & security, dairy, and kitchen verticals, serving the Indian Armed Forces and allied institutions. Its defence offerings include shipbuilding steel sections, armoured-vehicle profiles, ballast bricks, composite doors, and homeland-security equipment, complemented by a large dairy/kitchen equipment portfolio that supports India's milk value chain and midday-meal programs.

## Product Portfolio
- **Defence Products**: Shipbuilding steel sections, welding wire, ballast bricks, special profiles for armoured vehicles, composite doors
- **Homeland Security**: IED containment vessels, crowd-control barriers, modular vehicle barriers, space heating devices
- **Dairy Equipment**: Bulk milk coolers, milking machines/parlors, milk cans, BMCs, solar BMCs, iDATA monitoring
- **Kitchen Equipment**: Braising pans, dal/rice cookers, storage tanks, digital thaw monitors for large institutional kitchens
