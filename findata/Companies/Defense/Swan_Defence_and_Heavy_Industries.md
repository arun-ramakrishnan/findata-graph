# Swan Defence and Heavy Industries

## Company Overview

Swan Defence and Heavy Industries designs and manufactures ships primarily for the defence sector, along with commercial shipbuilding, refits, and repairs. It operates one of the largest integrated shipbuilding facilities (shipyards) in India and the world — the largest engineering infrastructure in India. Formerly Reliance Naval and Engineering Limited, it was renamed in January 2025. It is part of the Swan Group (diversified infrastructure conglomerate: textiles, real estate, energy, technology, shipbuilding).

- **Former name:** Reliance Naval and Engineering Limited (renamed Jan 2025)
- **HQ:** Mumbai, India
- **Group:** Swan Group (Merchant family)
- **Chairman & MD:** Nikhil V. Merchant | **CEO:** Rear Admiral Vipin Kumar Saxena (Retd.)

## Strategic Backdrop — India's Naval Build-out

- Indian Navy frontline combat vessels projected to expand from 47 to 85 between FY25–FY30 (~80% increase).
- **Near-term order pipeline (0–3 yrs):** ~47 vessels, ~₹1,447 bn (P-75 Repeat Scorpene subs, P-75(I) AIP subs, NGC, survey vessels, ASW craft, etc.).

## Market Opportunity

- **Ship repair:** India's domestic ship repair market poised to grow to ~$1.7 bn by 2033 (defence needs, commercial fleets, regional demand).
- **Global context:** Shipbuilding dominated by China/Korea/Japan; India holds <1% share despite handling ~95% of trade via sea.
- **Policy support:** GOI subsidies, incentives, and Maritime Vision 2047 aim to place India among top global shipbuilding nations.
