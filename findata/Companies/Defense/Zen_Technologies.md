# Zen Technologies

## Overview

Zen Technologies Limited is an Indian defense technology company specializing in training simulators and defense equipment. The company follows a B2B business model, serving military, paramilitary forces, and defense organizations with advanced training solutions. Zen Technologies is a pioneer in defense training simulation in India, specializing in virtual reality, augmented reality, and simulation technologies. The company contributes to the modernization of defense training and exports products to various countries.
