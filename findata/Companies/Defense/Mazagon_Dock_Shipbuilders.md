# Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders

## Company Overview
Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited, founded in 1774 and headquartered in Mumbai, is India's premier defence shipbuilder (government-owned). It builds and repairs warships, frigates, destroyers, conventional submarines, and commercial vessels, and is a critical supplier to the Indian Navy and Coast Guard under the Atmanirbhar Bharat indigenization push.

## The Chatter — MazDock Mphasis Redington

**₹15,000 crore greenfield bet:** A preliminary feasibility study estimates ~₹15,000 crore of total investment for the greenfield commercial shipbuilding expansion; formal approvals will be sought from the Board and government after the internal DPR and consultant recommendations.

**15–17% return/margin target for commercial venture:** Under the Maritime Amrit Kaal framework with central and state incentives, Mazagon Dock targets a 15–17% RoE/revenue-return profile for the new commercial shipbuilding business; top-line depends on order inflows and vessel throughput per the DPR.

**Project 75(I) decision imminent:** Commercial negotiations and bid submissions with ThyssenKrupp Marine Systems (TKMS) Germany are complete; the decision rests with the Government of India, with contract finalisation anticipated "very shortly — and certainly within the current fiscal year."

**₹70,000 crore frigate pipeline:** The Navy has issued the RFP for Project 17B next-generation frigates (~₹70,000 crore), for which Mazagon Dock plans to bid; the MCMV project remains in the pipeline and additional Project 15B/15C destroyer orders are expected this financial year.

**Costs and margins under control:** No inflationary headwinds on raw materials for ongoing order execution — all active projects are within projected costs — and management believes accumulated construction efficiency lets it protect margin profiles even under the Navy's competitive-bidding structures.
