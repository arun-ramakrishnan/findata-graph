# Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders

## Company Overview
Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited, founded in 1774 and headquartered in Mumbai, is India's premier defence shipbuilder (government-owned). It builds and repairs warships, frigates, destroyers, conventional submarines, and commercial vessels, and is a critical supplier to the Indian Navy and Coast Guard under the Atmanirbhar Bharat indigenization push.
