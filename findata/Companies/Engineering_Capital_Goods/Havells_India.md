# Havells India

## Company Overview
Havells India Limited (founded 1958; HQ Noida, Uttar Pradesh) is a leading fast-moving electrical and electronic durables company manufacturing and selling switchgears, cables, lighting, fans, motors, and consumer appliances under the Havells, Lloyd, Havells Crabtree, REO, and Havells Studio brands. It operates across six segments — Switchgears, Cables, Lighting & Fixtures, Electrical Consumer Durables, Lloyd Consumer, and Others — and is strategically expanding into renewables via its Goldi Solar investment.
