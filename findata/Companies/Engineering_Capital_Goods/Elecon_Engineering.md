# Elecon Engineering

## Company Overview
Elecon Engineering Company Ltd (founded 1951; HQ Vallabh Vidya Nagar, Gujarat) is India's largest manufacturer of industrial power transmission (gears/gearboxes) and material handling equipment. Its dual core offerings serve diversified end-user industries including steel, fertilizers, cement, coal, sugar, power, mining, and defense, with operations across India and overseas markets.
