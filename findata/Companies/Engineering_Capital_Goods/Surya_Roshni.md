# Surya Roshni

## Company Overview

Surya Roshni Limited manufactures steel pipes and strips, plus lighting and consumer durables. The company operates two divisions: Steel (GI pipes, API/3LPE coated spiral pipes, CR strips, hollow sections) and Lighting/Fans (LED lamps, luminaires, fans, home appliances, PVC pipes). Founded 1973, headquartered in New Delhi. ~3,328 employees. Exports to US, Australia, Canada, Middle East, Europe, Africa.
