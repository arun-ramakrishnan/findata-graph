### From Survival to Strategic Expansion
**Source**: Beyond Results Into the Plot - Edition #2

**Key Development**: Under Gainwell's ownership, TIL has rebuilt management, secured capital, and is re-focusing on defense, exports, and IP-led crane categories.

**Management Commentary**: 
"TIL is the only company in the country which has got an IP registered by Government of India for pick and carry crane... This product is going to be reintroduced in the country probably by the third quarter... Today's Board has decided that TIL has decided to set up separate SBU for defense, just to bring sharper focus on our defense business." - Sunil Kumar Chaturvedi, CMD

**Strategic Significance**: 
A company once written off is now using fresh capital and a clean slate to re-enter high-value niches-defense, IP-protected cranes, exports. By reintroducing proprietary products and leveraging synergies with Gainwell's mining IP, TIL is positioning for 10x growth from a tiny base. The defense SBU move is especially telling: management sees structural opportunity in supplying custom cranes to Army/Navy/Air Force, not just commercial buyers.

**Key Metrics to Monitor**:
- Launch of IP-protected pick-and-carry cranes by Q3 FY26
- Defense contributing 20%+ of order book within 2 years
