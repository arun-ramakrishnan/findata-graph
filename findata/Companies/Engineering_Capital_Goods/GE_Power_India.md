# GE Power India Ltd.

## Company Overview

GE Power India Ltd. provides solutions for power generation, focusing on services for thermal power plants in India. The company is strategically shifting from long-gestation EPC projects to asset-light, high-margin core services, targeting both GE and non-GE assets. GE Power India is part of the global GE (now GE Vernova) power equipment and services franchise.

### Core Business Segments

- **Core Services:** Maintenance, repair, and overhaul services for thermal power plants (GE and non-GE assets)
- **Boiler / FG Equipment:** Supply of boilers and Flue Gas Desulfurization (FGD) equipment
- **EPC Projects:** Legacy long-gestation engineering, procurement, and construction projects (being scaled back)
- **Spare Parts Supply:** 5-year supply agreements for critical boiler spare parts (e.g., with JSW Energy)
