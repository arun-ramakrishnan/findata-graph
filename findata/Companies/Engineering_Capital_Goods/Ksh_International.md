# KSH International Limited

**Ticker:** KSHINTL.NS | **Sector:** Engineering & Capital Goods | **Market Cap:** Small Cap

## Overview

India's largest exporter of magnet winding wires with ~75% revenue from high-value specialized products. Delivering highest EBITDA margin and revenue growth with exports to 24 countries. Products used across transformers, EV traction motors, generators, renewables, industrials, railways, home appliances, refrigeration, and air conditioning.

### Company Details
- **Founded**: 1979
- **Headquarters**: Pune, Maharashtra, India
- **Address**: Ambethan Road, Chakan, Pune
- **Website**: https://www.kshinternational.com
- **Employees**: 178
- **Industry**: Electrical Equipment & Parts
- **Exchange**: NSE (KSHINTL.NS), BSE (KSHINTL.BO)

### Magnet Wires
- **Round Enameled Wires**: For transformers, motors, and generators
- **Paper Insulated Rectangular Wires**: For transformers and power applications
- **Wrapped Rectangular/Copper Wires**: For electrical equipment and appliances
- **Transposed Conductors & Bunched Papers**: For transformers and electrical applications

### End-Use Industries
- **Power**: Transformers, motors, generators, alternators
- **Renewables**: Wind turbines, solar inverters, hydro generators
- **Industrials**: Industrial equipment, machinery, automation
- **Railways**: Traction motors, signaling equipment
- **Home Appliances**: Refrigerators, air conditioners, washing machines
- **Automotive**: Motors, alternators, starters

### Differentiating Product
- Continuously Transposed Conductors (CTC) is a key differentiating product for KSH International
- Contributes over half of its specialized winding wire business and distinguishes it from competitors
- "CTC is what differentiates us from all our listed peers, and accounts for more than half our specialized winding wire business."

### Market Demand
- Global transformer manufacturers are expanding capacity due to a significant supply bottleneck in power medium and distribution transformers
- Indicates robust demand for KSH's products
- "The primary bottleneck is the supply of power medium as well as distribution of transformers, which is driving capacity expansion by transformer manufacturers globally"

### Capacity Expansion
- Driven by strong T&D sector demand, company is prioritizing rapid expansion of CTC capacity
- Planning to introduce standard and EV wire machines by FY27
- "Given the current demand and environment in T&D sector, our endeavour is to rank up on CTC capacity as quickly as possible and bring some of the standard and EV wire machines over the course of FY '27."

### Strategic Technology Licensing
- KSH International is strategically licensing patented PEEK wire technology from HPW to indigenize production for emerging global shift to 800-volt EV traction motors
- "And globally, what's happening is, when you shift to 800 volts, because of faster charging, etcetera, then, you know, PEEK wires are generally used by these manufacturers of traction motors. Our, I mean, why we have gone with HPW is that HP is one of two manufacturers with patented technology for PEEK wires."

### PEEK Wire Timeline
- The PEEK wire venture for EV motors is a forward-looking investment
- Significant market impact and financial benefits projected to commence after FY27
- "But this will not have an immediate, impact. But, you know, we will be working on programs with some of the manufacturers for EV motors. And that's where real benefit will come maybe, after FY '27 is when we expect to."

### Profitability Advantage
- KSH International maintains a significantly higher EBITDA per ton than peers due to its 75% focus on specialized winding wires
- "Yes, so if you compare us to our peers, you know, we would, you know, out of the total capacity, almost 75% of our businesses actually specialized winding wires. Whereas if you compare with some of the other peers, they would be exactly reverse or maybe focused on on standard magnet winding wires."

### HVDC Products
- HVDC specialized magnet winding wires represent the highest value-added and most profitable product segment
- Reinforced by an initial order for 11 transformers
- "And HVDC, specialized magnet winding wires, which go into the HVDC segment are highest value addition, actually, or are most profitable orders within our family of products as well."

### US Tariffs & Demand
- Despite US tariffs, customers importing KSH International's specialized magnet winding wires are absorbing and passing on costs
- Demonstrates strong demand and product inelasticity
- "But today, customers who are buying this material, they are paying – they are paying this tariff and absorbing this tariff and passing it down to their customer, end customer."

### Risk Management
- The company's business model incorporates a direct pass-through for LME copper prices and exchange rates
- Effectively eliminates commodity price risk
- "Thus, LME copper prices and exchange rate is a direct pass-through for us, and we do not take any exposure to short or long-term variations in copper price."
