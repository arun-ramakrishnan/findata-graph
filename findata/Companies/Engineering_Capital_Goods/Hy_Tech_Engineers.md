# Hy-Tech Engineers

## Company Overview

Hy-Tech Engineers Limited designs, manufactures and supplies hydraulic fittings for diverse industrial applications, with over four decades of operational experience in the hydraulics industry. It is one of the few Indian players manufacturing the entire variety of hydraulic fittings, serving large OEMs (including John Deere) and equipment makers, with ~30% of business from exports at materially better margins than domestic sales.

## The Chatter — Maruti Hy-Tech Symbiotec

**Capacity doubling on IPO proceeds:** Production is being scaled from ~35 lakh fittings/month to 70 lakh/month using IPO proceeds, with a stated plan to double turnover over the next three years.

**₹500 crore in five years:** Management put an explicit number behind the long-term ambition — ₹500 crore revenue in five years, implying a 20–25% growth trajectory.

**Margin ladder to 25%:** EBITDA margin to expand ~0.5–1% year-on-year to ~25% over 2–3 years, driven by export mix (exports are ~30% of business and earn ~10% higher margin, plus a falling-rupee tailwind) and Kaizen-driven operational efficiency.

**John Deere wallet-share runway + US subsidiary:** Approved at all 47 John Deere US plants but supplying only 6–7 so far; John Deere wants Hy-Tech to supply all fittings from the US, so a US company was incorporated (board-approved). Management deliberately caps the US at ~25% of turnover and wants incremental exports from Europe, where it is ~25% cheaper on landed price.

**New verticals beyond fittings:** A stainless-steel fittings range developed over two years targets data centres and oil & gas, with market entry planned within a year; higher-value valves are under consideration. No customer exceeds 15% of business; OEM relationships are sticky (Windsor Machines, the first customer, has bought 100% from Hy-Tech for 48 years) and demand comes as annual schedules rather than a conventional order book. Parker's local price is exactly double, so no price competition.

> "Today, we are manufacturing around 35 lakh fittings every month. With the proceeds we have received from the IPO, we are going to increase this to 70 lakh per month. We are going to double the turnover over the next three years. This is our plan; this is our growth plan."
> — Hemant Mohonkar, Chairman & Managing Director
