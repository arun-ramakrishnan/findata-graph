# Lumino Industries

## Company Overview
Lumino Industries is an integrated Kolkata-based manufacturer and EPC services provider in the power transmission & distribution sector — overhead conductors, power cables and transformer products, plus turnkey infrastructure projects for state utilities and private power operators. BSE-listed (LUMINO.BO, BSE-only listing); recent IPO fresh-issue proceeds are retiring ₹337 crore of debt.

## The Chatter — Maruti Lumino Indian Bank

**Debt-free pivot:** Fresh-issue IPO proceeds repay ₹337 crore of debt carrying ~8.5% interest, leaving the company largely debt-free — management expects better performance and improved PAT margins.

**Order book:** ~₹3,200 crore total — ₹1,200 crore products (largely converting to revenue within the current financial year) and ₹2,000 crore EPC (executing over 3–4 years). Product orders execute in 3–4 months, so product order-book visibility stays at 1–2x capacity by design.

**Mix strategy:** Target revenue split ~70% products / 30% EPC, with management focused on growing higher-margin product revenue and reducing EPC reliance.

**Capacity expansion:** Kolkata base runs 40,000 MT (~85% utilisation last year) with another 11,000 MT being added in H2; a new Howrah factory will make 11 kV–66 kV cables, with a heavy focus on high-temperature low-sag (HTLS) conductors.

**Margins & growth:** No formal guidance until H1 results, but management points to a 10-year track record of 10–11% EBITDA margins and 25–30% revenue CAGR, which it expects to maintain.
