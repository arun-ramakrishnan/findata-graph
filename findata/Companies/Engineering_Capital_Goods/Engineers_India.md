# Engineers India Limited

## Company Overview

Engineers India Limited (EIL) is engaged in the construction and maintenance of industrial facilities such as refineries, chemical plants, and petrochemical complexes. The company also undertakes architectural and engineering activities, related technical consultancy, management consultancy, and research and experimental development in natural sciences and engineering. EIL is a government-owned engineering consultancy and EPC player serving India's energy and petrochemical sectors.

### Core Business Segments

- **Consultancy & Engineering:** Detailed engineering, project management, and consultancy services for industrial projects
- **Turnkey / LSTK Projects:** Lump-sum turnkey execution of refineries, petrochemical plants, and process facilities
- **Specialty / Niche Infrastructure:** Townships, R&D facilities, convention centers for institutional clients
- **Ramagundam Plant:** Operating plant contributing steady profits
