# Precision Wires India

## Company Overview

Precision Wires India Limited manufactures insulated enamelled winding wires and electric conductors. Products include enamelled round/rectangular winding wires, continuously transposed conductors (CTC), paper/mica/nomex insulated copper conductors, and submersible winding wires. Incorporated 1989, headquartered in Mumbai. Serves static/rotating electric equipment, auto-electrical, and power equipment industries.
