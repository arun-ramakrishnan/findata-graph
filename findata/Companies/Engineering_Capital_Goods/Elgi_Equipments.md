# Elgi Equipments Limited

## Company Overview

Elgi Equipments Limited is a global leader in manufacturing innovative and technologically advanced compressed air systems. The company offers a wide range of products, including rotary screw compressors, reciprocating compressors, centrifugal compressors, dryers, filters, and accessories. With a focus on productivity and low cost of ownership, Elgi's solutions are widely used across various industries worldwide.

### Core Business Segments

- **Rotary Screw Compressors:** Industrial-grade oil-lubricated and oil-free screw compressors
- **Reciprocating Compressors:** Smaller and specialty piston-type compressors
- **Centrifugal Compressors:** High-capacity compressors for large industrial applications
- **Air Treatment:** Dryers, filters, and accessories for compressed air systems
- **Aftermarket / Services:** Spares, service contracts, and customer support
