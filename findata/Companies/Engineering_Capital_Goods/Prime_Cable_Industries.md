# Prime Cable Industries

## Company Overview

Prime Cable Industries Limited manufactures and sells ISI-marked low-voltage cables and wires under "PRIMECAB" and "RENUFO" brands, serving power, oil & gas, mining, steel, and real estate sectors as an ISO and BIS certified company.

- Nationwide presence with ~88.3% revenue from top 12 states: Tamil Nadu (15%), Bihar (14%), Rajasthan (11%), Karnataka (11%)
