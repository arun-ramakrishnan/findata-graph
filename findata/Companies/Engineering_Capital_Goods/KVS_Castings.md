# KVS Castings

KVS Castings manufactures a wide variety of castings, including cast iron, SG iron, alloy steel, and stainless steel components, primarily for automobile, locomotive and engineering sectors. The company emphasizes innovation and strict quality standards in its production processes.

## Business Overview
- **Product Range**: Cast iron, SG iron, alloy steel, stainless steel castings
- **Target Industries**: Automobile, locomotive, engineering sectors
- **Quality Focus**: Innovation and strict quality standards

## Industry Context
India's foundry market is currently valued at USD 25.57 billion and expected to grow to USD 42.61 billion by 2030 - that's a 10.57% CAGR.

**Competitive Advantages**:
- **Natural Advantage**: World's second-largest steel producer means cheaper raw materials
- **Export Growth**: Auto component exports up 8% to ₹1.96 lakh crore in FY25
- **Infrastructure Investment**: Railway infrastructure investments creating fresh demand

**Growth Drivers**:
- "China + 1" shift improving global foundry positioning
- India's rich iron-ore & aluminum reserves strengthening manufacturing base
- Lightweight die-cast demand from urban transit and high-speed rail investments
