# Praj Industries

## Company Overview
Praj Industries Limited, incorporated in 1985 and headquartered in Pune, is a global leader in bio-based technology and process engineering, providing EPC solutions for biofuels, brewery & beverages, high-purity water systems (biopharma), critical process equipment, and zero-liquid-discharge wastewater treatment. Its Bio-Mobility and Bio-Prism platforms target the energy transition and bioeconomy.
