# De Neers Tools Limited

## Company Overview

De Neers Tools Limited is a specialized Indian manufacturer and supplier of high-quality hand tools, serving diverse industrial and automotive applications. With a strong focus on precision engineering and quality craftsmanship, the company has established itself as a trusted provider of essential tools for professional and industrial use.

### Core Product Portfolio

- **Automotive Tools**: Comprehensive range of spanners, wrenches, and sockets for automotive repair and maintenance
- **Industrial Hand Tools**: Heavy-duty pliers, cutters, and specialty tools for industrial applications
- **Precision Instruments**: High-accuracy measuring tools and calibration equipment
- **Specialty Tools**: Custom-designed tools for specific industrial requirements
- **Tool Kits**: Professionally curated tool sets for various applications
