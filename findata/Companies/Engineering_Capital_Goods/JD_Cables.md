# JD Cables | Nano Cap | Engineering & Capital Goods | **JDCABLES**

JD Cables Limited, established in 2015, manufactures and supplies ISO certified po and control cables, along with a range of aluminium-based conductors used in electricity transmission. Its modern manufacturing facility serves state electricity boards across India.

- **Aluminium Conductors**: Range of aluminium-based conductors

- **State Electricity Boards**: Primary customer base across India
