## Overview

ABB India Limited is a leading technology company specializing in electrification, robotics, automation, and motion solutions. The company serves as the Indian arm of ABB Group, a global leader in power and automation technologies.

## Business Areas

- **Electrification**: Products and solutions for electrical distribution, protection, and control
- **Motion**: Motors, generators, drives, and mechanical power transmission products
- **Process Automation**: Control systems, instrumentation, and analytical solutions for industries
- **Robotics & Discrete Automation**: Industrial robots, collaborative robots, and machine automation

## Key Offerings

- **Industrial Automation**: PLCs, SCADA systems, and industrial control solutions
- **Smart Grid**: Solutions for modernizing electrical grids and distribution systems
- **Electric Vehicle Charging**: Infrastructure solutions for electric mobility
- **Digital Solutions**: IoT-enabled products and digital transformation services

## Market Position
