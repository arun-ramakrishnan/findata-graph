# CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited

## Company Overview

CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited is a leading Indian multinational company specializing in electrical equipment manufacturing and engineering solutions. With a rich heritage spanning over eight decades, CG Power has established itself as a key player in India's engineering and capital goods sector, serving diverse industries including power generation, transmission, distribution, and industrial automation.

### Core Business Segments

- **Power Transformers**: Comprehensive range of power and distribution transformers for utilities and industries
- **Industrial Motors**: Energy-efficient motors for various industrial applications
- **Switchgear Solutions**: Advanced switchgear and protection systems for electrical networks
- **Semiconductor Manufacturing**: Emerging semiconductor business with government PLI scheme support
- **Engineering Services**: Turnkey solutions for power infrastructure projects

### Strategic Growth Initiatives

CG Power is executing an ambitious capacity expansion plan to capitalize on India's booming power infrastructure demand:

- **Transformer Capacity Expansion**: Doubling transformer manufacturing capacity to 85,000 MVA by FY27-28 through a ₹712 crore greenfield expansion
- **Semiconductor Venture**: Establishing semiconductor manufacturing under the PLI scheme with pari passu government funding
- **Export Market Focus**: Strengthening international presence to serve global transformer demand
- **Product Innovation**: Developing next-generation motors and smart electrical solutions
