# RR Kabel

## Company Overview
RR Kabel is a leading Indian manufacturer of electrical cables and wires—power and control cables, building wires, industrial cables, and specialty products (fire-resistant, LSZH, renewable energy cables)—serving residential, commercial, and industrial applications with ISI-marked, quality-certified products and pan-India distribution.
