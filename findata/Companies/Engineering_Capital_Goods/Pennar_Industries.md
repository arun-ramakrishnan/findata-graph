# Pennar Industries

## Company Overview
Pennar Industries Limited, founded in 1975 and headquartered in Hyderabad, is an engineering company providing engineered steel products, precision tubes, building systems, and custom-designed solutions to B2B customers across infrastructure, railways, automobiles, and solar end-markets. It operates through Diversified Engineering and Custom Designed Building Solutions & Auxiliaries segments.
