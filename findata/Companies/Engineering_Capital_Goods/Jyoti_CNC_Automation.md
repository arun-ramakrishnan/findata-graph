# Jyoti CNC Automation Limited

## Company Overview

Jyoti CNC Automation Limited is a pioneering Indian manufacturer of advanced computer numerical control (CNC) machines and precision engineering solutions, established in 1989 by visionary entrepreneurs Mr. P.G. Jadeja and Mr. S.L. Jadeja. With the founding vision to create a "Temple of Technology," Jyoti has evolved from manufacturing gear boxes to becoming India's first CNC machine manufacturer in Gujarat and a leader in precision engineering. The company's journey exemplifies India's manufacturing transformation, from traditional machining to advanced automation technologies that serve critical industries worldwide.

### Core Business Segments

- **CNC Machine Tools**: Comprehensive range of CNC turning centers, vertical machining centers, and multi-tasking machines
- **Precision Engineering**: High-accuracy machining solutions for complex industrial applications
- **Automation Solutions**: Advanced manufacturing automation systems and robotic integration
- **Custom Manufacturing**: Bespoke CNC solutions tailored to specific industry requirements
- **After-Sales Services**: Comprehensive maintenance, training, and technical support services

### Manufacturing and Technological Capabilities

- **R&D Excellence**: Dedicated research and development for machine tool innovation
