# Cummins India

## Company Overview
Cummins India Limited (incorporated 1962; HQ Pune, Maharashtra) is a subsidiary of Cummins Inc. (USA) and a leading manufacturer of diesel and natural gas engines, power generation equipment, and related components. It operates through Engine, Power Systems, Components, and Distribution business units, serving automotive, industrial, marine, data center, and power generation markets across India, Nepal, and Bhutan, with exports to the US, Europe, Middle East, and Africa.
