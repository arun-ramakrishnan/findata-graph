# KEI Industries Limited

## Company Overview
KEI Industries Limited manufactures, markets, and sells wires and cables in India and internationally. It operates in three segments: Cable & Wires; Engineering, Procurement and Construction; and Stainless Steel Wire. The company offers extra-high voltage, high tension, and low-tension power cables; control and instrumentation cables; specialty, single-core, and multi-core flexible cables; elastomeric/rubber and solar cables; fire survival/resistant cables; flat, EV charging, ESP, and medium voltage covered conductor cables; conflame green + wires; communication and thermocouple cables; and submersible marine and offshore cables, as well as stainless steel, winding, and house wires. It also provides engineering, procurement, and construction solutions in the areas of gas-insulated GIS, and air-insulated substations AIS; overhead and underground power transmission and distribution systems; and railway electrification/substation on a turnkey basis, as well as project management services. The company exports its products to approximately 60 countries. It serves the power, refinery, railway, automobile, cement, steel, fertilizer, textile, real estate, infrastructure, oil and gas, defense, chemical, metal, IT, pharma, manufacturing, renewables energy, non-metal, data center, consumer durable, government, public, private, and other sectors through dealers and distributors. The company was founded in 1968 and is headquartered in New Delhi, India.

- KEI's retail sales are growing significantly and now account for over 50% of total sales — a strategic shift toward direct consumer engagement that should support higher margins and more predictable demand. The retail push is backed by 27 depots across India, 38 marketing offices, and 4 overseas offices (UAE, South Africa, Nepal, Gambia), plus brand-building via TV advertising and IPL sponsorship (Super Brand status FY2011-16 and FY2019-25).
- India's wires & cables market is projected to grow at 11-13% CAGR between FY24-29E, with power transmission cables and building wires together contributing ~50% of the market — supported by favourable government initiatives (rural electrification, railway electrification) and rising construction/FMEG demand.
- Structural growth ambition for India's cables & wires industry: potential to almost 4X domestic production; reduce import dependence from ~10% to <6% by 2034 (via precision manufacturing, quality standards co-created with end-use industries like renewables/railways, and skill-base strengthening); lift India's global export share from ~1% to ~3% by 2034 (via quality standardisation, global certifications, trade marketing, and cost competitiveness vs China, currently 10-20% more expensive).
- Domestic consumption is expected to exceed $29 Bn by 2034, driven by demand across utilities, automobile, residential and other categories; growing demand is fuelled by infrastructure development including data centres, cloud computing and digital transformation, with government investments in renewable energy and power transmission ensuring a durable demand environment.
