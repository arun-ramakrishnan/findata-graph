# EMS Limited

## Company Overview

EMS Limited is an Indian engineering and infrastructure company specializing in water supply, sewerage solutions, waste treatment plants, and electrical transmission and distribution services. The company focuses on providing comprehensive engineering solutions for municipal infrastructure projects, primarily working on centrally funded government schemes and programs. It serves as a key player in India's urban infrastructure development with emphasis on sustainable water and waste management systems.

- **No exposure to Jal Jeevan Mission funding stress**: EMS clarified it does not execute Jal Jeevan Mission work — that program faces funding issues because actual project costs (₹1,000-2,000 crore per district) far exceeded central allocations (₹300-400 crore per district), pushing up receivables for participating companies. EMS avoids this entirely by working only on centrally funded schemes where funds are pre-approved. (H.K. Kansal, Consultant - Engineering Operations — Moves_and_Motives)
- **Funding security via central schemes**: EMS works exclusively on Central Government Funded Schemes — AMRUT, Namami Gange (NMCG), JICA, and ADB funded schemes — where tenders are done only after central government fund consent, ensuring no liquidity crises. (H.K. Kansal — Moves_and_Motives)
- **₹15 lakh crore urban opportunity**: Estimated ₹15 lakh crore potential in water supply and wastewater work in urban areas alone (municipal corporations, municipalities, town area committees), vs. only ₹3 lakh crore completed under AMRUT so far. Four AMRUT cycles projected to sustain funding flow. (H.K. Kansal — Moves_and_Motives)
- **Wastewater reuse as structural growth driver**: India becoming a water-scarce country is driving schemes based on reuse of treated wastewater (for agriculture, washing, gardening, industrial uses like NTPC/BHEL boilers). Quality and scope of wastewater treatment work is continuously increasing — no risk of losing scope. (H.K. Kansal — Moves_and_Motives)
- **Global EMS market growth**: Global EMS (Electronics Manufacturing Services) market projected to grow from USD 789B in CY23 to USD 1,304B by CY28, with India's share nearly doubling from 3.8% to 7.7% — driven by Make In India and rapid expansion of mobile phone and consumer electronics segments.
