# Finolex Cables Limited

## Company Overview

Finolex Cables Limited is one of India's leading and most diversified electrical and telecommunications cable manufacturers, with a rich heritage spanning over five decades. As a pioneer in the Indian cable industry, Finolex has established itself as a trusted provider of high-quality wiring solutions for residential, commercial, industrial, and infrastructure applications. The company has also strategically expanded into Fast-Moving Electrical Goods (FMEG) and home appliances, creating a comprehensive electrical solutions ecosystem.

### Core Business Segments

- **Electrical Wires & Cables**: Comprehensive range of wires and cables for power distribution, industrial applications, and residential wiring
- **Communication Cables**: Optical fiber cables and telecommunications infrastructure solutions
- **Industrial Cables**: Specialized cables for solar power, automation, and heavy industrial applications
- **FMEG Products**: Fast-moving electrical goods including switches, sockets, and home electrical accessories
- **Home Appliances**: Expanding portfolio of electrical appliances for consumer markets

### Manufacturing Excellence

Finolex operates with a strong focus on quality and innovation:

- **State-of-the-Art Facilities**: Modern manufacturing plants with advanced extrusion and insulation technologies
- **Quality Certifications**: Adherence to national and international quality standards
- **R&D Capabilities**: Continuous product innovation and technology development
- **Sustainability Focus**: Environmentally responsible manufacturing practices
- **Supply Chain Integration**: Efficient procurement and distribution network
