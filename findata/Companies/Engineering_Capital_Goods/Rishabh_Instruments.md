# Rishabh Instruments

Rishabh Instruments is a vertically integrated manufacturer of electrical automation, measurement, control and protection devices, portable test & measurement instruments, and solar string inverters. Through its subsidiary Lumel Alucast it also produces aluminium high-pressure die castings. The company serves data centres, pharma, FMCG, railways, utilities, petrochemicals and automotive customers across India, the US, Europe and the UK.
