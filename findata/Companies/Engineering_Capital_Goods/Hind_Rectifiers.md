# Hind Rectifiers

## Company Overview
Hind Rectifiers Limited (incorporated 1958; HQ Mumbai) designs, develops, manufactures, and markets power semiconductors, power-electronic equipment, and railway transportation equipment for India and international markets. Its products power electrostatic precipitators, electro-chemical and railway applications (traction transformers, IGBT propulsion, propulsion systems, on-board rectifiers), and it is now diversifying into AI/Web 3.0 and Middle East power-sector expansion through new subsidiaries.
