# Larsen and Toubro

Large Cap Engineering & Capital Goods company providing EPC services.

## Business Summary
Larsen & Toubro Limited engages in engineering, procurement, and construction projects (EPC) in India and internationally. The Infrastructure Projects segment is involved in the engineering and construction of building and factories, transportation infrastructure, heavy civil infrastructure, power transmission and distribution, water and effluent treatment, and minerals and metals. The Energy Projects segment provides EPC solutions in oil & gas, refineries, petrochemicals & offshore wind energy sectors, from front-end design through detailed engineering, modular fabrication, procurement, project management, construction, installation and commissioning; CarbonLite solutions for power generation plants including power generation equipment with associated systems; and carbon capture utilization and utility packages. The Hi-Tech Manufacturing segment designs, manufactures/constructs, supplies, and revamps/retrofits custom designed engineered critical equipment and systems for the process plants, nuclear energy, and green hydrogen sectors; marine and land platforms, related equipment and systems, and aerospace products and systems; precision and electronics products and systems for defense, security, space, and industrial sectors; and electrolysers. The IT & Technology Services segment offers information technology and integrated engineering services, e-commerce/digital platforms and data centers, and semiconductor chip design solutions. The Financial Services segment provides retail finance. The Development Projects segment develops, operates, and maintains metro project comprising transit-oriented development, toll roads, power generation and development, thermal power, and green energy. The Others segment engages in realty, manufacture and sale of industrial valves; marketing and servicing of construction equipment, mining machinery and parts, and processing machinery. Larsen & Toubro Limited has a strategic partnership with General Atomics Aeronautical Systems, Inc. to manufacture Medium Altitude Long Endurance (MALE) Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems (RPAS) in India, for the Indian armed forces. Larsen & Toubro Limited was founded in 1938 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India.

## The Chatter — Meesho, Marico, Bajaj & More

**Middle East still core:** L&T holds an ~₹3 trillion order book in the Middle East despite regional tensions; all sites are functioning and no projects have been cancelled, though some near-term execution impact from supply-chain constraints is expected.

**Energy margins bottoming:** The energy segment margin fell to 6.5% in Q4 FY26 (from 8.2% prior year) due to cost overruns and close-out costs on legacy projects, but management expects recovery after a couple of quarters as those low-margin end-stage contracts conclude.

**Realty consolidation:** Realty pre-sales more than doubled to ₹94 billion in FY26 (helped by Noida/Panvel launches), and the group is consolidating all real-estate undertakings under a single simplified platform — a likely precursor to value-unlocking.

**Future-tech capex:** Planned capital outlays include ~₹50B industrial electronics, ~₹30B semiconductors, ~₹150B green hydrogen, and ~₹100B data-centre business over the plan period.

**AI-enabled data centres over plain real estate:** L&T is targeting ~200 MW of AI-enabled data-centre capacity (with servers/GPUs for hyperscalers and quantum-computing clients) rather than lower-yield tenancy real estate.

> "We want our data centers to be AI-enabled, with servers and GPUs to enable high computing. Global hyperscalers and quantum computing organizations would be our clients. The current thinking is to create 200 MW of data center capacity over time."
> — P. Ramakrishnan, CFO

## The Chatter — BEL, HUL, Tata Capital

**FY27 guidance reiterated (10–12% order inflow & revenue):** 14% YoY order-inflow growth in Q1 despite project deferments; a ₹15 tn prospects pipeline underpins confidence, with no project cancellations seen across active pursuits.

**Middle East conflict — managed, not missed:** execution momentum continues for most of the order book; only select segments (e.g. renewables/Energy-Green) face intermittent supply-chain disruption. Alternate logistics routes are in place and clients are being consulted; uncompensated incremental costs will not be incurred (revenue/timeline impact deferred instead).

**Force-majeure cost recovery via client agreements:** additional costs are recognised in accounting only once a reimbursement agreement is formalised; L&T will not absorb exorbitant logistics costs without client agreement — protecting margins even with ~50% of the order book fixed-price.

**Offshore wind — strategic long-term platform:** ~8 GW of offshore wind ≈ ₹57,000 cr of a ₹60,000 cr order book, executed over 4–5 years; margins better than core Middle-East EPC; L&T intends to build global leadership (incl. HVDC platforms) — the only EPC line it will geographic-diversify beyond India/Middle-East.

**Private capex now ~half of domestic pipeline:** domestic prospects at ₹7.45 tn (~50%), with a structural shift — private-sector orders (real estate, industrial, power, data centres, energy transition) now rival public spend, making the pipeline "more balanced and sustainable."

**H2 FY27 execution step-up expected:** second half historically stronger; once delayed awards close and logistics normalise, execution intensity is set to rise meaningfully.
