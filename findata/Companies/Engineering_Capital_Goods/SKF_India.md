# SKF India

## Company Overview
SKF India Limited, headquartered in Pune and part of the global SKF Group (founded 1923 in India), is a leading manufacturer of bearings, seals, lubrication systems, and rotating-equipment solutions serving automotive and industrial customers. It is undergoing a strategic demerger to split its automotive and industrial businesses into separate listed entities.
