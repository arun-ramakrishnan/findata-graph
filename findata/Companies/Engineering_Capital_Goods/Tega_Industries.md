# Tega Industries

## Company Overview

Tega Industries is a global engineering and capital goods company operating through its Molycop subsidiary, specializing in forged and high-chrome grinding media and industrial consumables for the mining and mineral processing industries. The company maintains a global manufacturing footprint with a local, customer-proximate supply strategy.

## Key Insights (Pressure Points)

- Molycop's core business is forged grinding media, with high-chrome media added recently as a higher-value growth lever.
- Forged media is expected to grow ~5% annually; the high-chrome strategy lifts blended growth to ~7.5% over the next three years, per MD & Group CEO Mehul Mohanka.
- High-chrome penetration scaled from 62,000 tons to a targeted 200,000 tons, supported by expanded manufacturing capacity.
- Local manufacturing footprint near customers mitigates tariff and trade risks; Molycop's US facilities insulate it from import disruptions.
- Management reports normalized growth in both 2023 and 2024 despite two mine closures, with a medium-to-long-term commitment to the high-chrome strategy.
