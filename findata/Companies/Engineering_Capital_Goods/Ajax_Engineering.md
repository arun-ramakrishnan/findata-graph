**Regulatory Context:**
- **CEV-5 Standards:** Implementation of CEV-5 emission norms effective July 1, 2025
- **Cost Impact:** 400 basis points increase in material costs due to emission compliance
- **Margin Pressure:** Temporary squeeze on gross margins due to higher compliance costs

**Strategic Response:**
- **Pricing Strategy:** Carefully calibrated pricing approach considering market response and elasticity
- **Market Discipline:** Holding pricing until market digests the regulatory change
- **Long-Term Focus:** Preserving market share while maintaining financial resilience

**Management Perspective:**
> "The shift from CEV-4 to CEV-5 emission standard effective 1st of July 2025 has had a notable impact... The transition to CEV-5 standards has led to an increase in the material cost, leading to an impact on the gross margin... Our pricing strategy will be very carefully calibrated after taking into account the market response and elasticity."
> - Shubhabrata Saha, Managing Director
