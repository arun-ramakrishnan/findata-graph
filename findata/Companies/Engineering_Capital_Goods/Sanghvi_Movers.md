# Sanghvi Movers

## Company Overview
Sanghvi Movers Limited (SML), incorporated in 1989 and headquartered in Pune, is India's largest crane rental company. It operates a fleet of medium-to-large hydraulic truck-mounted telescopic and lattice boom cranes, plus hydraulic and crawler cranes, serving construction, power, oil & gas, heavy engineering, infrastructure, renewable wind energy, steel, cement, fertilisers, petrochemical/refinery, and metro railway industries. The company operates through three segments: Crane Hiring and Ancillary Services; Wind EPC; and Project EPC (heavy lift, rigging, foundation work). SML is BS OHSAS 18001:2007 certified.

## Key Insights (from Edition #24, Mar 2026)
- SML is transitioning from a domestic, asset-heavy crane business to a global, hybrid platform; the shift toward value-added services, diversified offerings and professional management (under the "Elevate 2030" roadmap) signals a move from cyclical execution to scalable, structured growth.
- Strong market leadership across India's infrastructure verticals: ~50–60% share in wind EPC, ~40–50% in nuclear, ~40–45% in refinery/petrochem, ~25–35% in steel, ~50–60% in cement, and ~20–25% in core infra (metros, bridges, ports).
- Key risks — margin compression (3–5%), leverage and execution volatility — are being mitigated via asset-light models, supplier credit (2–3 years), FD-backed margin structures, IPP-backed financially-closed projects, and institutional counterparties.
- Upstream energy (oil & gas) is a high-value opportunity: India's O&G capex averages ₹50,000–80,000 cr/year, with heavy-lift representing 3–8% of project value on critical-path lifts (rig assembly, flare towers, compressor modules) for ONGC, OIL, RIL.
- Marine infrastructure is a major emerging driver: India's shipbuilding order book is USD 3.6 bn (2025) with a target of USD 30 bn by 2030 (Amrit Kaal), requiring extensive shipyard erection, pre-outfitting lifts and slipway equipment work.
