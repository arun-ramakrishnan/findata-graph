# Sanghvi Movers

## Company Overview
Sanghvi Movers Limited (SML), incorporated in 1989 and headquartered in Pune, is India's largest crane rental company. It operates a fleet of medium-to-large hydraulic truck-mounted telescopic and lattice boom cranes, plus hydraulic and crawler cranes, serving construction, power, oil & gas, heavy engineering, infrastructure, renewable wind energy, steel, cement, fertilisers, petrochemical/refinery, and metro railway industries. The company operates through three segments: Crane Hiring and Ancillary Services; Wind EPC; and Project EPC (heavy lift, rigging, foundation work). SML is BS OHSAS 18001:2007 certified.
