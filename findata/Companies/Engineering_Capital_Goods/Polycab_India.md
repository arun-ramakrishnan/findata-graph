# Polycab India

## Company Overview
Polycab India Limited, founded in 1964 and headquartered in Mumbai, is India's largest manufacturer of wires and cables, with a growing presence in Fast Moving Electrical Goods (FMEG) and EPC projects. Its three segments — Wires & Cables, FMEG (fans, lighting, switches, water heaters, solar), and EPC (power transmission/distribution) — make it a play on power infrastructure, electrification, and consumer electrical demand.
