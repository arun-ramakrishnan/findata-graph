# Pitti Engineering

Pitti Engineering Limited (formerly Pitti Laminations Limited) is India's leading manufacturer of Electrical Steel Laminations, Motor Cores, and related engineered products. It also produces castings, fabricated parts, and machined components used in hydro/thermal generation, wind energy, mining, cement, steel, sugar, railways, e-mobility, appliances, and oil & gas applications. Headquartered in Hyderabad.
