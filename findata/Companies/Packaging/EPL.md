# EPL Limited (formerly Essel Propack Limited)

## Company Overview
EPL Limited, formerly Essel Propack Limited, is a global company specializing in manufacturing laminated plastic tubes for FMCG and Pharma sectors. They produce packaging materials like collapsible tubes and laminates for Beauty & Cosmetics, Health & Healthcare, Food, Home, and Oral care products, along with offering packaging solutions and ancillary services.
