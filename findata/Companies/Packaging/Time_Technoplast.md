# Time Technoplast

## Company Overview

Time Technoplast Limited (incorporated 1989; headquartered in Mumbai) manufactures polymer and composite products across two segments: Polymer and Composite. Products include industrial packaging (drums, containers, jerry cans, PET sheets), infrastructure (HDPE pipes under Max'M, VRLA batteries under MaxLife), technical/lifestyle products (Duro Turf, Meadowz matting, Regal moulded furniture), auto components (TechTank fuel tanks, 3S Rainflaps), composite cylinders (LiteSafe LPG, NEX-G CNG cascades), cross-laminated films (Techpaulin), and intermediate bulk containers (GNX). Stock split 2:1 in Sep 2025.
