# Huhtamaki India

## Company Overview

Huhtamaki India Limited (formerly Huhtamaki PPL) is a flexible packaging manufacturer headquartered in Thane (founded 1935). Subsidiary of Huhtavefa BV (Finland). Provides packaging for beverages, dry/wet culinary, home and personal care, pet food, pharmaceuticals, plus specialty labels (anti-counterfeit, extended text). Also manufactures PE/PO/PP Retort products under blueloop and blueLite brands, and laser engraved cylinders. ~2,500 employees.
