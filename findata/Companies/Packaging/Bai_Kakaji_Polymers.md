# Bai-Kakaji Polymers

Bai-Kakaji Polymers Ltd. is an Indian manufacturer of PET preforms, plastic caps & closures used in water, carbonated beverages, juices and dairy packaging. The company focuses on advanced molding technology to deliver consistent quality and low-rejection packaging solutions across India.

## Business Overview

Bai-Kakaji specializes in manufacturing:
- PET preforms
- Plastic caps & closures
- Advanced molding solutions

The company delivers consistent quality and low-rejection packaging solutions for water, carbonated beverages, juices, and dairy products across India.
