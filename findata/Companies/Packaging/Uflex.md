# Uflex Limited

Uflex Limited is an Indian packaging company engaged in the manufacturing of flexible films and other packaging materials. The company is a leading player in the flexible packaging industry in India with a strong export presence.

## Business Segments

### Flexible Films
- BOPET films
- BOPP films
- Specialty films
- Film coatings

### Packaging Products
- Flexible packaging laminates
- Pouches and bags
- Industrial packaging
- Print packaging
