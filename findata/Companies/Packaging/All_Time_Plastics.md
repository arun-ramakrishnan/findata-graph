# All Time Plastics

## Company Overview

All Time Plastics specializes in manufacturing plastic consumerware products for everyday household needs and offers white-label manufacturing services to its customers. The company operates in the chemicals/plastics sector as a small-cap entity.

## Key Insights (Moves and Motives)

- High customer stickiness due to mold dependency—shifting US sourcing away from All Time Plastics is difficult for customers
- Tariff costs fall on customers, who decide how much to absorb or pass on to end consumers
- Per CFO Manish Gattani, long-standing sourcing relationships and mold manufacturing requirements create barriers to overnight supplier switching
