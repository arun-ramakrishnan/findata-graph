# Jindal Poly Films

## Company Overview

Jindal Poly Films Limited manufactures BOPET and BOPP films in India and internationally (incorporated 1974; headquartered in Gurugram). Operates through Packaging Films, Nonwoven Fabrics, and Coated Products segments. Products include BOPP, thick/thin BOPET, CPP, lamination films, metallized films, coated films, thermal lamination films, capacitor films, and PP-based spunmelt/spunbond nonwoven fabrics. ~346 employees.
