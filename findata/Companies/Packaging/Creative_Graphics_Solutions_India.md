# Creative Graphics Solutions (India)

## Overview

Creative Graphics Solutions India Ltd. (CGS) is a leading pre-press company specializing in manufacturing flexographic printing plates for packaging. The company operates through a B2B model, serving major brands across India and internationally in FMCG, pharmaceutical, and consumer goods industries. CGS is India's largest processor of digital flexo plates, providing essential printing blocks like Digital Flex Metal Back and Coating Plates.
