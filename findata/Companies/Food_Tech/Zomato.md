# Zomato

Zomato is an Indian online food delivery and restaurant discovery platform.
Founded in 2008, it connects customers with restaurants and operates a
logistics network for food delivery across major Indian cities.

## Business Model

- **Food Delivery**: Core platform connecting restaurants with customers
- **Restaurant Discovery**: Reviews, menus, and ratings platform
- **B2B Supplies**: Hyperpure ingredient supply chain for restaurants
- **Subscription**: Zomato Pro membership program
