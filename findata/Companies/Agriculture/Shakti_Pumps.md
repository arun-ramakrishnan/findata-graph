# Shakti Pumps

Shakti Pumps (India) Limited (established 1982) manufactures energy-efficient pumps and motors for agriculture, industrial, and water-supply applications, plus solar pump/hybrid controllers, inverters, EV motors, and solar PV modules. Headquartered in Pithampur, MP.
