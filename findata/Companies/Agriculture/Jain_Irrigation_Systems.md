# Jain Irrigation Systems

## Company Overview

Jain Irrigation Systems Limited (JISL) is a diversified agri-business conglomerate founded in 1963 and headquartered in Jalgaon. Operates across micro-irrigation systems (Hi-Tech Agri Input Products), plastics, agro-processing, and other businesses. Products include drip/sprinkler irrigation, PVC/HDPE pipes, solar PV modules and pumps, dehydrated onions/vegetables, aseptic fruit purees, frozen foods (Jain Farm Fresh brand), and tissue-cultured plants. Global footprint across India, Europe, North America.
