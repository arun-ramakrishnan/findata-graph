# Piccadilly Agro Industries

## Company Overview

Piccadilly Agro Industries Limited manufactures sugar and its by-products, operating through Sugar, Distillery, and Premium Beverages divisions. The company is undergoing a strategic shift from commodity sugar sales toward higher-margin branded and premium alcoholic beverages.

## Key Insights (From Visual Noise to Narrative)

- Premium Beverages now contributes 42.9% of FY25 revenue, overtaking sugar (28.1%) and distillery (29.0%), reflecting a successful portfolio transformation.
- EBITDA margin expanded to 21.4% in FY25, with distillery margins at 30.2% partially offsetting sugar's -1.1% margin.
- Product range spans white crystal sugar, molasses, bagasse, ethanol, malt, and branded Indian Made Foreign Liquor (IMFL).
- Strategic focus on brand building in IMFL, distribution expansion, and premiumization is driving a four-year shift toward value-added products.
