# Kuantum Papers Limited

Kuantum Papers Limited, formerly ABC Paper Limited, is an India-based company engaged in manufacturing and selling of writing and printing paper.

## Industry Overview

India accounts for about 5% of global paper production and is fastest-growing paper market in world. The industry is expected to grow at 4-5% annually until 2030, with consumption projected to rise from around 22 million tons today to over 35 million tons by 2035.

## Market Segments

Packaging boards dominate domestic market at 55% while writing and printing paper-Kuantum's focus area-holds 25%.

## Growth Potential

India's per capita paper consumption is just 15-16 kg, far below global average of 57 kg, which signals significant room for growth. Demand is being driven by:

- Expanding e-commerce (boosting packaging needs)
- Rising corporate use of copiers and computers
- Government spending on education
- Shift away from single-use plastics toward paper alternatives

## Import-Export Dynamics

- **Exports**: India's paper is in demand in neighboring countries such as Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Iran, and UAE
- **Imports**: Significant imports come from ASEAN countries, Japan, and China due to Free Trade Agreements
- **Challenge**: Declining global pulp prices have led to stagnation in paper export growth due to surplus of international products in Indian markets

## Industry Advocacy

Indian industry players are lobbying for higher import duties on paper products to protect domestic production.
