# Narmada Agrobase

## Overview

ISO 9001:2015 certified company specializing in manufacturing and processing of cotton seed meal cake, cattle feeds, and soya bean meal. Exports under brand names 'Churma' and 'Gaay Chhaap Narmada Pashu Aahar'. India's animal feed market expected to double from ₹1,110 billion in 2024 to ₹2,025.1 billion by 2033 (6.9% CAGR), backed by India's position as global dairy leader contributing 24.76% to world's milk production.
