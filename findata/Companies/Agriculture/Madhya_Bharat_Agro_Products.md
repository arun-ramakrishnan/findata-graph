# Madhya Bharat Agro Products

## Company Overview
Madhya Bharat Agro Products Limited (MBAPL), incorporated in 1997 and based in Bhilwara, is a phosphatic-fertilizer manufacturer producing Single Super Phosphate (SSP), Beneficiated Rock Phosphate, and complex fertilizers (NPK) under the Bharat and Annadata brands. An ISO 9001:2008 certified company brought under Ostwal Group management in 2004, it began beneficiated rock phosphate production in 2012, reducing India's import dependency for this critical raw material.
