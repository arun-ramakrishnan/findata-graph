# HDB Financial Services

HDB Financial Services Ltd., a subsidiary of HDFC Bank, is India's 7th largest retail-focused NBFC with a 1,068.78 billion rupee loan book as of Mar 31, 2025. Classified NBFC-UL by RBI, it offers diverse loans via Enterprise, Asset, and Consumer Finance verticals.

> — Ramesh Ganesan, Managing Director and CEO

Management has set a target for loan book growth that significantly outperforms India's nominal GDP — a "nominal plus 6-7%" framework — reflecting confidence in taking market share from competitors over the next few years.

Despite market fluctuations, HDB has successfully maintained its lending rates to protect profitability — holding rates across every product for three quarters to ensure good risk-adjusted returns.

**Operational pivot to digital-first lending:** HDB is shifting toward paperless, point-of-sale credit delivery, reducing reliance on physical branch infrastructure. Its DIY (Do It Yourself) self-service platform multiplied disbursements ~2.2x in FY26, lowering acquisition costs and improving scalability.

**Asset finance — used-vehicle focus:** Targeting a 50/50 mix between new and used vehicles over a 4-year period to grow its high-yield used-vehicle business and compete with established players.

**Q4FY26 highlights:**
- Disbursements of 19,922 crores — an all-time high quarterly figure.
- Net interest income of 2,399 crores — up 5% QoQ and 21.6% YoY.
- Conscious reduction of reliance on volatile short-term funding (low CP levels as dry powder; bank loans below 50% of the mix).

**Outlook:** Management expects positive momentum in unsecured lending as portfolio quality stabilizes; consumer finance growth driven by discretionary demand (consumer durables, auto loans). Key monitorables: West Asia conflict and probable weather disruption from El Niño.
