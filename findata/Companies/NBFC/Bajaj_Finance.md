# Bajaj Finance

## Overview

Bajaj Finance is a leading NBFC (Non-Banking Financial Company) in India, offering a comprehensive range of financial products including consumer finance, rural business, and various lending solutions. The company operates as part of the Bajaj Finserv group and serves both urban and rural markets with a focus on digital transformation and AI-driven solutions.
