# Manappuram Finance Limited

## Company Overview

Manappuram Finance Limited, founded in **1949** (formerly Manappuram General Finance and Leasing, renamed **Manappuram Finance in 2011**) and headquartered in **Thrissur**, is one of India's leading gold-loan NBFCs. Its core product is gold loans, offered via branches, doorstep service, and online channels, complemented by a range of secured and unsecured lending, digital financial services, and ancillary products.
