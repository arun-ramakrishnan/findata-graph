# Sundaram Finance

## Company Overview
Sundaram Finance is a leading financial services company providing vehicle finance, home loans, and various other financial products and services. Specializing in asset financing since 1975, the company offers various credit solutions like vehicle financing, personal loans, small business loans, and deposit schemes. The company promotes financial inclusion by providing affordable finance options for preowned commercial vehicles to First-Time Buyers and Small Road Transport Operators.

### Business Model & Operations
- **Geographic Focus**: 85% of branch network is in semi-urban and rural areas, directly benefiting from agricultural recovery
- **Agricultural Recovery**: Agriculture growth accelerated from 1.4% to 3.8%, with above-normal monsoon predictions supporting rural demand
- **Liquidity Position**: Excess liquidity of Rs. 31,000 crores (double of normal Rs. 19,000 crores) is masking true operational performance, with 20-25 basis points impact on Net Interest Margin (NIM)

### Market Dynamics
- **Used Commercial Vehicle Market**: Faces structural supply shortage due to weak sales from 2019-2021; management expects shortage to reverse into supply surge in FY26-28 as 2022+ vehicles enter the used market
- **Stress Concentration**: Geographic stress concentrated in borders of UP, Bihar, MP, and Chhattisgarh, affecting both passenger vehicle and MSME portfolios
