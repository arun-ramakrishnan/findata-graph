# Bajaj Finserv

## Company Overview
Bajaj Finserv Ltd. (incorporated 2007, headquartered in Pune) is a diversified financial services holding company with controlling stakes in Bajaj Allianz Life Insurance, Bajaj Allianz General Insurance, Bajaj Finance, and Bajaj Housing Finance. Leveraging a century-old brand and a patient-capital approach, the group offers lending, insurance, savings, investments, and broking products with pan-India reach; it also operates 138 windmills (65.2 MW).

- Exceptionally strong balance sheet cushions: BAGIC (general insurance) at 325% solvency and BALIC (life insurance) at 359% solvency, well above regulatory minimums — supporting a patient-capital competitive positioning.
- Devang Mody (MD & CEO, Bajaj Finserv Health) highlighted early success converting domestic healthcare claims processing (2.8 million claims) into international capabilities with "significantly higher" profitability margins.
- Management emphasized long-term orientation, leveraging surplus capital and brand strength; positioned alongside SBI Cards and Arman Financial Services in diversified financial services coverage.

## The Chatter — Bajaj Finserv Zydus BEL

**Insurance distribution reform — near-term volatility, structural bounce-back:** Management expects the proposed changes to insurance distribution economics to disrupt current fee structures briefly, then improve the industry — citing the post-reform recoveries after GST exemptions, ULIP norm changes and surrender-value revisions.

**Bajaj General GWP optics vs core retail:** GWP grew 10% to ₹7,556 cr, below the industry's ~12% — but excluding bulky tender-driven crop and government health business, GWP grew 21% vs industry ~13%, led by health (+31%) and motor (+18%). The slowdown was a deliberate avoidance of low-margin government tenders.

**Life insurance margin expansion:** VNB grew 40% to ₹512 cr with NBM at 21.6% (vs 17.1% last year, +4.5 pts); rolling 12-month NBM crossed 20% (20.8%) despite the GST impact — volume growth is being balanced with high-quality earnings.

**Product-mix pivot to annuity:** Non-par products cut from ~18% to ~13% of mix in two quarters (high pricing competition), with annuity lifted from ~5% to ~10% — a deliberate low-margin-avoidance shift that has held for a year.

**Don't extrapolate the margin expansion:** Tarun Chugh explicitly warned the 4.6% NBM expansion is not sustainable — investors should lower expectations on further expansion of this magnitude.

**₹4,700 cr surplus funds new ventures:** Capital surplus plus subsidiary dividends are sufficient to fund the asset-management business and the reinsurance venture without external debt; a one-time Ind AS migration surplus (subject to IRDAI) could add more.

> "GWP for Bajaj General grew 10% for the quarter to about 7,556 crore, which is marginally lower than the industry growth of about 12%. However, if we were to exclude the bulky tender-driven crop and government health business, GWP for the company increased by a very healthy 21%, which is significantly higher than the industry growth of close to 13%. This was primarily driven by the health and motor segments, which grew 31% and 18%, respectively."
> — Ramandeep Singh Sahni, CFO, Bajaj Finserv Limited
