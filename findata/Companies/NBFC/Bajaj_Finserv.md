# Bajaj Finserv

## Company Overview
Bajaj Finserv Ltd. (incorporated 2007, headquartered in Pune) is a diversified financial services holding company with controlling stakes in Bajaj Allianz Life Insurance, Bajaj Allianz General Insurance, Bajaj Finance, and Bajaj Housing Finance. Leveraging a century-old brand and a patient-capital approach, the group offers lending, insurance, savings, investments, and broking products with pan-India reach; it also operates 138 windmills (65.2 MW).
