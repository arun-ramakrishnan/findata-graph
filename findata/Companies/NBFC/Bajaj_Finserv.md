# Bajaj Finserv

## Company Overview
Bajaj Finserv Ltd. (incorporated 2007, headquartered in Pune) is a diversified financial services holding company with controlling stakes in Bajaj Allianz Life Insurance, Bajaj Allianz General Insurance, Bajaj Finance, and Bajaj Housing Finance. Leveraging a century-old brand and a patient-capital approach, the group offers lending, insurance, savings, investments, and broking products with pan-India reach; it also operates 138 windmills (65.2 MW).

**Digital marketplace back on growth, shifting to trail revenue:** Bajaj Markets is past the tech-transition slowdown that hit FY26; some revenues now trail-based, adding stability and predictability. 50+ lenders on the platform are growing volumes again as credit-quality concerns ease.

**Insurance cycle is soft:** India's general-insurance market is in a soft cycle (no major catastrophe losses) — pricing below average across crop/fire/motor/health, pressuring underwriting profit until the cycle hardens.

**Motor-court liability already reserved:** Conservative reserving already bakes in potential motor-insurance liability increases from court rulings — unlikely to face sudden hits that thinner-reserved peers might.

**Life-insurance distribution de-risked:** Bajaj Allianz Life added 20+ partners in 15 months, cutting dependence on a 2-3 partner cluster that once drove >50% of business — materially lowers partnership-concentration risk.
