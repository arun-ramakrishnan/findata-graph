## Muthoot Microfin | Small Cap | NBFC

Muthoot Microfin, a part of Muthoot Pappachan Group, is a leading microfinance institution providing micro loans to women in rural India. They follow a JLG mode organizing women in groups to promote entrepreneurship and inclusive growth by offering income-generating loans for small businesses.
