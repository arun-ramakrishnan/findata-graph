# SBI Cards and Payment Services

## Company Overview
SBI Cards and Payment Services Limited (SBICARD), incorporated in 1998 and headquartered in Gurugram, is India's largest pure-play credit card issuer and a non-banking financial company. A subsidiary of State Bank of India, it offers corporate, travel, utility, purchase and virtual cards plus insurance agency services to individual and corporate customers, leveraging SBI's massive branch network for customer acquisition and scale.
