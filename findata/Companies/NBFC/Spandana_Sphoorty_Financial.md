# Spandana Sphoorty Financial

## Company Overview

Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited is a leading Indian microfinance institution serving low-income women in rural and semi-urban areas through the Joint Liability Group (JLG) model. The company provides unsecured credit for income-generating activities and is diversifying into individual loans and newer geographies.

## The Chatter — Infosys Adani Power

**Geographic diversification priority:** Spandana is targeting under-penetrated Tamil Nadu (~₹15 cr of an ₹38,000 cr industry) and Maharashtra (~₹288 cr of ₹24,000 cr), aiming to at least double its ~1.1% share to ~2% — reducing concentration in its six core states (65% of book: Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, etc.).

**Individual-loan pilot launching:** An individual-loan product (separate from JLG) is ready to pilot in 8 Madhya Pradesh branches, staffed with 8–10 people over 3 months; better underwritten with an eNACH facility to test acceptability and improve retention.

**Funding cost improving:** Recent credit-rating upgrades are enabling cheaper institutional funding and access to government-backed credit schemes; PSU banks entering the lending mix is expected to lower the cost of funds further.

**Legacy cleanup nearly done:** Management will stop collecting on much older 90+ day-past-due accounts from next year (standard 90+ collections continue) — the final phase of legacy-portfolio cleanup post-restructuring.

**Execution priorities:** Expand into Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra, and reduce staff attrition (experienced loan officers maintain better borrower relationships and collection discipline).

> "Two states we really want to grow in the coming quarters are Tamil Nadu, where our share currently is just about 15 crores out of an industry of 38,000 crores, and Maharashtra, where we are at about 288 crores as against an industry of 24,000 crores. With a 1.1% share, we want to at least double it to a 2% share."
> — Venkatesh Natarajan, Managing Director and CEO
