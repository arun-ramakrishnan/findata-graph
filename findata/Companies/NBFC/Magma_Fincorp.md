# Magma Fincorp

## Company Overview
Magma Fincorp Limited, a Mumbai-based NBFC incorporated in 1978, was renamed **Poonawalla Fincorp Limited in July 2021** following the acquisition of a controlling stake by Rising Sun Holdings (Adar Poonawalla group). The company now provides consumer and MSME financing — personal, business, vehicle, gold, education, and machinery loans, and loans against property — through a widespread branch network across India.
