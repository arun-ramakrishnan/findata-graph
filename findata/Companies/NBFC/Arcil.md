# Arcil

## Company Overview
Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Ltd (Arcil) — India's first ARC, incorporated 2002 under the SARFAESI framework and regulated by the RBI; marquee shareholders include State Bank of India and Avenue Capital Group. Buys stressed loans from banks and NBFCs and works resolutions to return capital and liquidity to the original lenders: corporate stressed assets are ~69% of AUM, with a retail stressed-loan franchise (built since 2008) handling ~35 lakh NPA accounts of individuals and small businesses on a technology-and-analytics backbone. AUM over ₹20,000 cr (vs ~₹17,000 cr the prior year); annual resolution rate consistently 22–25% of beginning AUM; has returned over ₹30,000 cr to banks and NBFCs through recoveries. Preparing to become the first ARC to list on Indian exchanges.

## The Chatter — HUL Fortis Shiprocket

**₹20,000+ cr AUM, 22–25% resolution engine:** AUM grew from ~₹17,000 cr to over ₹20,000 cr in a year; the annual resolution rate — share of beginning-of-year AUM resolved — has held at 22–25% for two straight years.

**Corporate core, retail rocket:** Corporate stressed assets remain ~69% of AUM (25 years of large complex cases), but the retail business — started in 2008 in anticipation of household credit creation — has grown ~56% over the last three years and handles ~35 lakh NPA accounts on heavy tech/analytics investment.

**Cycle-independent addressable market:** India's systemic credit market exceeds ₹200 lakh cr with ~₹30 lakh cr of fresh credit added annually — even a 1–2% stress ratio across that base is a very large market regardless of where the NPA cycle sits; ARC industry penetration remains small.

**Not private credit:** CFO Pramod Gupta draws the line — private credit funds provide financing; Arcil enters once an asset is already stressed, working recoverability, sustainable debt and future potential. Resolution is case-specific, and borrowers can get "a very long rope" to revive rather than immediate liquidation.

**IPO on the runway:** Management positions a listing as industry visibility — the ARC industry has given back ~₹2.5 lakh cr to the banking/NBFC system over ~25 years (Arcil itself: ₹30,000+ cr); "this being the first ARC to list will give visibility to the whole industry." Regular RBI regulatory changes pose no disruption, per management.
