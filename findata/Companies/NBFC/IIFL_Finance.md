# IIFL Finance Limited

## Company Overview

IIFL Finance Limited, incorporated in **1995** (formerly IIFL Holdings, renamed **IIFL Finance in 2019**) and headquartered in **Mumbai**, is a non-banking financial company (NBFC) offering a diversified loan book. Lending products span home, gold, business (including for manufacturers, women, and e-commerce sellers), MSME, loans against securities, digital finance, and supply chain finance. The company also undertakes construction/real estate financing, capital market financing, and wealth management.
