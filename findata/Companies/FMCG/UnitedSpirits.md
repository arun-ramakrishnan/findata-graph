# United Spirits

United Spirits, a subsidiary of Diageo, manufactures, purchases, and sells beverage alcohol and allied spirits through tie-up manufacturing units.

## Sector Overview

The FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods) sector is a sub-sector of Consumer Defensive within the broader Consumer sector. FMCG companies manufacture and distribute essential consumer goods including food and beverages, personal care products, and household items with high consumer turnover and brand loyalty.

Key characteristics of the FMCG sector:
- **Essential Consumption**: Products consumed regularly with high frequency
- **Brand Loyalty**: Strong consumer loyalty and brand preference
- **Distribution Network**: Extensive distribution through multiple channels
- **Marketing Intensive**: Heavy investment in brand building and advertising

The sector is driven by population growth, urbanization, and premium demand, while facing challenges in raw material costs, consumer demand fluctuations, and competition.
