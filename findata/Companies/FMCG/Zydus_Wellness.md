# Zydus Wellness

## Company Overview
Zydus Wellness Limited, founded in 1988 (formerly Carnation Nutra Analogue Foods Limited, renamed January 2009) and headquartered in Ahmedabad, develops, produces, markets and distributes health and wellness products across India and internationally. A subsidiary of Zydus Lifesciences Limited, its portfolio spans sugar substitutes (Sugar Free Gold/Natura/Green), health food drinks (Complan), glucose beverages (Glucon-D), skincare (Everyuth, Nycil), and foods (Nutralite spreads, RiteBite). The company is aligning with structural trends in low-sugar, high-protein, functional nutrition and natural personal care.
