# Marvel Decor

## Company Overview
Marvel Decor Limited (MDL) manufactures and supplies a wide range of window-covering fashion blinds and their components, serving both residential and commercial clients across India and internationally. The company is pivoting from a retail-blinds focus toward a B2B, project-driven model targeting architects, interior designers, and system integrators.
