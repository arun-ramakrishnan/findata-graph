# Hatsun Agro Products

## Company Overview
Hatsun Agro Product Limited, founded in 1970 and headquartered in Chennai, is India's largest private sector dairy company. It operates advanced milk collection and processing infrastructure (including Bactofuge technology for clarified liquid milk) and offers a wide range of products — ice cream, milk products, beverages, and dairy ingredients — under brands such as Arun Icecreams, Arokya, Hatsun, HAP Daily, Ibaco, and Milky Moo.
