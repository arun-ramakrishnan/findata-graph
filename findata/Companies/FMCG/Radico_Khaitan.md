- **India consumer market**: nearly doubled from $1.1 tn (2013) to $2.1 tn (2023); on track to be world's 3rd-largest by 2026; consumption growth 7.2% over the past decade (outpacing China, US, Germany).

- **Spirits industry**: sales crossed 400 mn cases in CY2024 (+2.8% YoY); volume CAGR 5.1% vs value CAGR 13.4% (CY24–29) — strong premiumization.
- **Category mix**: whisky dominates; white spirits (vodka, gin) fastest-growing; vodka +14% value (2024); Indian single malt ~12.1% CAGR.
- **Radico Prestige & Above**: grown 13% CAGR since FY2019; premium share of volume rose from 28.3% (FY19) to 46.1% (FY25) — now ~half of sales; >212% volume growth since FY19.
- **Profitability**: ROCE improved ~15% → ~22% (late 2026); EBITDA margins recovered to ~17% as commodity costs normalized.
