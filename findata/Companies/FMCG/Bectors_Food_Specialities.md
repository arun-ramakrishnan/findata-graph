# Bectors Food Specialities

## Company Overview
Mrs. Bectors Food Specialities Limited, founded in 1978 and based in Gurugram, is a leading player in the premium biscuits and bakery segment in North India. The company serves both retail and institutional customers and exports its products internationally, under flagship brands 'Mrs. Bector's Cremica' and 'English Oven'.
