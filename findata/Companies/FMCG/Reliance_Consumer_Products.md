# Reliance Consumer Products

## Overview
Reliance Consumer Products Ltd. (RCPL) is the fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) arm of Reliance Industries Limited, focused on building a large portfolio of consumer brands in India.

## Key Insights

### Strategic Expansion
RCPL is pursuing aggressive nationwide expansion across all major FMCG categories within 12 months, leveraging its extensive distribution network to compete with established players.

### Category Expansion Plans
- **Food**: Expansion into biscuits, snacks, and ready-to-cook and ready-to-eat products
- **Beverages**: Continued growth of Campa brand
- **Home and Personal Care**: Launch of Velvet brand as the beginning of expansion
- **General Merchandise**: Nationwide availability across all categories

### Key Brands and Targets
- Set ambitious target to build at least five brands with annual revenues exceeding ₹1,000 crore each by the end of 2027
- Focus on establishing strong brand equity beyond distribution strength
- Leveraging Reliance's extensive retail and distribution network

## Management Commentary
"As I mentioned earlier, by this time next year, you will find us available nationally across almost every major FMCG category - food, beverages, home and personal care, and general merchandise. Within food, we'll expand into biscuits, snacks, and ready-to-cook and ready-to-eat products. In beverages, Campa will continue to grow. And in home and personal care, Velvet is just the beginning."

"I won't name specific brands, but our aim is that by the end of 2027, we should have at least five ₹1,000 crore brands in our portfolio - if not more."

- T Krishnakumar, Director, Reliance Consumer Products
