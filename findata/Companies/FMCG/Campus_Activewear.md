# Campus Activewear

## Company Overview
Campus Activewear Limited ("Campus"), established in 2005 and headquartered in Gurugram, is one of India's largest sports and athleisure footwear brands by value and volume. It designs, manufactures, and distributes a wide range of footwear — running, walking, casual, floaters, slippers, flip-flops, and sandals — across multiple colors and styles at affordable prices, using digitization to enhance demand forecasting and reduce time to market.
