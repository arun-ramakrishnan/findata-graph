# Hermès International

## Company Overview
Hermès International is a French luxury goods manufacturer that creates and sells a wide range of high-end products, including leather goods, ready-to-wear apparel, silk scarves, watches, jewelry, and fragrances. It is renowned for its craftsmanship, heritage, and exclusivity in the luxury market.
