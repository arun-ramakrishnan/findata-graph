# Honasa Consumer

Honasa Consumer Limited is a digital-first house of beauty and personal care brands focused on millennial customers. Portfolio includes Mamaearth, The Derma Co, Aqualogica, Ayuga, BBlunt, and Dr Sheth's. Headquartered in Gurugram.
