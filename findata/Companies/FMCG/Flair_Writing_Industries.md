# Flair Writing Industries

## Company Overview
Flair Writing Industries Limited, founded in 1976 and based in Mumbai, is a leading Indian manufacturer of writing instruments and stationery. Known for brands such as Flair, Hauser, Pierre Cardin, and Zoox, the company has diversified beyond pens into creative tools and houseware (steel bottles, lunch boxes, storage containers), with a growing domestic and export presence.
