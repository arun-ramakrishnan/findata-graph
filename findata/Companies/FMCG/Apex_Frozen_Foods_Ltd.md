# Apex Frozen Foods Limited

Apex Frozen Foods Limited is a leading exporter of processed L. Vannamei and Black Tiger shrimp, with a strong presence in value chain. The company has strategic focus on backward integration and value addition, positioning itself as a premier supplier of high-quality shrimp.

## Industry Performance

Frozen shrimp has become backbone of India's seafood export story. Over the past 17 years, shrimp exports have grown from just 126,000 MT in FY09 to 742,001 MT in FY25-nearly a 6x jump. While other seafood categories have remained largely flat, shrimp now accounts for bulk of India's total seafood shipments.
