# Varun Beverages Limited

## Company Overview
Varun Beverages is a significant player in the beverage industry as the second largest franchisee of PepsiCo products worldwide (outside the US). The company produces and distributes a wide range of carbonated soft drinks and non-carbonated beverages, including popular brands like Pepsi, Seven-Up, Mirinda, Mountain Dew, and Tropicana, along with packaged drinking water under the Aquafina brand.
