# Devyani International

## Overview

Largest franchisee of Yum Brands in India, operating KFC, Pizza Hut, and Costa Coffee stores in India, Nepal and Nigeria. Also owns brands like Vaango and Food Street. Merged with Sapphire Foods, creating one of India's largest QSR operators with ~3,002 stores and revenues of ₹78,265 crores.
