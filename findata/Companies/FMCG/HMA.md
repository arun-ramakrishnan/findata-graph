# HMA (Hindustan Meat Export Company)


## Company Overview
HMA is a meat products and poultry company engaged in meat export operations, specializing in buffalo meat and poultry products. The company has established itself as a significant player in India's meat export industry, with a strong presence in international markets. HMA operates in the food processing sector, focusing on processed meat products and live animal exports.

## Strategic Developments & Market Expansion

### Historic Latin America Entry (2025)
**Major Milestone:** HMA has successfully entered Cuba, marking the first-ever Indian meat export approval into Latin America, representing a significant strategic breakthrough for the company and Indian meat industry.

**Management Commentary:**
"This was the first initiative which was not happened before for the Indian product and we hope, slowly, we will build a good clientele from Latin America side also. This was the first step entering into the American continentals." - Gulzeb Ahmed, CFO

**Strategic Significance:**
This development represents India's formal entry into the Americas meat trade. Latin America, traditionally a competitor to Indian meat exports, is now also becoming a customer, creating new strategic opportunities for market diversification.
