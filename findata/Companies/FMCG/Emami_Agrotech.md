# Emami Agrotech

## Company Overview
Emami Agrotech is an Emami Group edible-oil and food-products manufacturer — branded oils, spices and snacks on a national distribution network — diversifying into value-added foods (Mantra spices, snacks, chocolate spreads, wheat products, soya nuggets) on top of its edible-oil supply-chain backbone.
