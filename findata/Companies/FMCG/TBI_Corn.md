# TBI Corn

TBI Corn, an ISO certified company based in India, specializes in producing high-quality Corn / Maize Grits and related products. Established in 2000, it offers a range of products including Corn Flakes, Corn Flour, and Turmeric Finger, all manufactured without chemical additives or preservatives and GMO-free.

## Business Overview
- **Founded**: 2000
- **Certification**: ISO certified
- **Product Range**: Corn Flakes, Corn Flour, Turmeric Finger
- **Quality Standards**: No chemical additives, preservatives, GMO-free
- **Manufacturing Focus**: High-quality corn/maize products

## Customer Base
TBI Corn has a diverse customer base across leading Indian snack and food manufacturers like ITC, Prataap Snacks, Balaji Wafers and Vasaya, along with international players such as Oriental Brewery and Mudieb Haddad. This demonstrates strong B2B relationships in both domestic and global food industries.

## Geographic Focus
- **Domestic Business**: Contributes over 90% of revenues (FY25)
- **Key Markets**: Maharashtra leads with 62% of sales, followed by Gujarat and Karnataka
- **Regional Concentration**: Strong presence in Western India
