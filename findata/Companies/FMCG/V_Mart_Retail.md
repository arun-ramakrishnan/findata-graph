# V-Mart Retail Limited

## Company Overview
V-Mart Retail Limited, incorporated in 2002 and headquartered in Gurugram, operates a retail department store chain. Its apparel range covers men (casual, formal, ethnic, sports, inner), women (western, ethnic, sports, inner, night), and boys, girls, and infants. Non-apparel offerings include fashion accessories, footwear, home needs, bags and luggage, and toys, while general merchandise spans personal and home essentials, packaged food, staples, and home furnishings.
