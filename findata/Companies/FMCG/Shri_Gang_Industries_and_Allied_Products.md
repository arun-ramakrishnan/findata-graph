# Shri Gang Industries and Allied Products

## Company Overview
Shri Gang Industries & Allied Products Limited, incorporated in 1989 (formerly Suraj Vanaspati Limited, renamed April 2008) and based in New Delhi, operates across two segments: Liquor Operations and Edible Oil Operations. It manufactures vanaspati, refined oils and bakery shortening under the Apna and Mr. Baker brands, plus extra neutral alcohol (ENA), country liquor and Indian-made foreign liquor (IMFL) under Golden Cascade and Bulldozer brands. The company runs an in-house 66 KLPD distillery and is a long-term exclusive contract manufacturer for Diageo (United Spirits) in Uttar Pradesh.
