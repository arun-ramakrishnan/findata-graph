## The Chatter — Maruti Lumino Indian Bank

**Policy tailwinds:** Structural reforms in Karnataka, Jharkhand and Maharashtra are driving explosive category growth — the beer category grew more than 50% in Karnataka over the last three months and over 50% in Jharkhand following retail distribution changes.

**Premiumization:** Premium portfolio growing at over 25% (volumes +34% in FY24, +24% in the subsequent period, ahead of plan this year); the premium segment is now margin-accretive, with 300–400 bps of premium-segment share already gained and ~18–20% premium sales mix targeted by 2030.

**Margin drivers:** Product mix first (premiumization), then capacity utilisation and state mix across the brewery network, then internal productivity — maximising output from existing facilities at zero incremental capex.

**Growth algorithm:** Double-digit net revenue growth with year-on-year operating margin expansion; management will keep investing to fuel the category, betting India becomes the world's largest beer market with 25 million new legal-age consumers every year.

**Innovation:** Sessionability is the emerging trend — Kingfisher Strong Smooth (mild after-smell), Heineken Silver becoming a top premium mild offering in launch states, and Heineken 0.0 accelerated for non-alcoholic occasions.

> "Double-digit revenue growth is not automatically guaranteed in a highly regulated state-by-state market. We must consistently deliver superior products in a competitive landscape. Looking at macroeconomic drivers, 25 million young consumers reach legal drinking age every year in India. Kingfisher serves as the primary entry brand for the category, making us very buoyant about macro tailwinds."
> — Vivek Gupta, Managing Director and CEO
