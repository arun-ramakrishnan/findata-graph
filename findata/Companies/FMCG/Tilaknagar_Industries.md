# Tilaknagar Industries

## Company Overview
Tilaknagar Industries (NSE: TI; HQ Mumbai; incorporated 1933) is an Indian-made foreign liquor (IMFL) manufacturer. Its portfolio spans brandy (Mansion House, Courier Napoleon), rum (Madiraa), whisky (Mansion House, Senate Royale), and gin (Blue Lagoon), plus extra-neutral alcohol and cocktails. Exports to Africa, Middle East, East/South-East Asia, and Europe.

**Reporting change:** From Q4 FY26, selling expenses (discounts, schemes) will be netted from gross revenue instead of sitting under other expenses — reduces reported revenue and gross margin but lifts reported EBITDA and PAT margins.
