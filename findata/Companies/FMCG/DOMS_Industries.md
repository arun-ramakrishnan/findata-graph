# DOMS Industries

## Company Overview
DOMS Industries, formerly Writefine Products, is a leading Indian manufacturer of school stationery and art materials. Founded in 2006 and headquartered in Gujarat, the company has established strong brand recognition through its 'DOMS' and 'C3' brands, catering to students and artists with a comprehensive range of stationery products.
