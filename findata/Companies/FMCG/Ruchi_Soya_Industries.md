# Ruchi Soya Industries

## Company Overview
Patanjali Foods Limited (formerly Ruchi Soya Industries Limited) is an Indian FMCG company that processes oil-seeds, refines crude oil, and produces food products and value-added items. It operates in FMCG and FMHG (Fast-Moving Health Goods) segments with products spanning food, biscuits, and nutraceuticals, and has diversified interests including wind energy power generation and trading.
