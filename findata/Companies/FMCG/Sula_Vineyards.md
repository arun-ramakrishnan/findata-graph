# Sula Vineyards

Sula Vineyards Limited is the largest wine producer and seller in India, dominating all price segments and types including red, white, and sparkling wines. They distribute popular brands like 'Sula', 'RASA', 'Dindori', 'The source', 'Satori', 'Madera', and 'Dia' with 'Sula' being the category creator in the Indian wine market.

## Business Overview
- **Market Position**: Largest wine producer in India
- **Brand Portfolio**: Sula, RASA, Dindori, The Source, Satori, Madera, Dia
- **Price Segments**: All price segments (red, white, sparkling)

## Recent Performance
- **Wine Tourism**: Record growth in Q2 (+8%) and H1 FY26 (+15%)
- **Market Share**: Gaining back share in Premium & Elite segments despite competitor discounting
- **"The Source" Brand**: Strong double-digit growth, now 10% of own brand revenue
- **Revenue Mix**: Government 45%, Private 55% in H1 FY26 (shift from previous 69%/31%)
- **Regional Focus**: Maharashtra recovery (key market), Telangana disruption impact

## Strategic Focus
- **Wine Tourism**: Third resort launched (The Haven by Sula)
- **Premium Brands**: Focus on Premium & Elite segments
- **Market Share Recovery**: Strong performance despite unsustainable competitor discounting
- **Import Distribution**: Planning re-expansion with expected duty reductions

## Recent Innovations
- **Sula Muscat Blanc**: India's first premium low-alcohol wine with positive market response

## Outlook
- **Margin Improvement**: 250 basis points year-on-year operating margin improvement expected in H2 FY26
- **Telangana Recovery**: Strong recovery anticipated in H2 FY26 as license disruptions resolve
- **Low-Alcohol Segment**: Strong future potential for premium low-alcohol wines
