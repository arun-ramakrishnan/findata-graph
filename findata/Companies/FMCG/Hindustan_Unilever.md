*Source: The Chatter Edition #47 (Bets and blueprints), Zerodha, Feb 20, 2026*

> "Starting January, we have made a few important changes to the HUL organisation. The intent is to build a simpler, more agile organization that can execute our priorities with greater speed, accountability and precision. All Business Unit Heads will now directly report to me instead of the earlier dual-reporting structure."
> - Priya Nair, Chief Executive Officer & Managing Director

**Quick Commerce Scaling (3% of business, doubling every quarter):**
> "At the forefront of channels of future is quick commerce, the fastest-scaling route to market and a structurally critical channel for the future. It is doubling every quarter and reshaping how consumers discover, shop and replenish. While it contributes around 3% of our business today, we expect it to scale meaningfully in the near term."
> - Priya Nair, Chief Executive Officer & Managing Director

> "To capture fully this opportunity and lead the channel shift, we have established a dedicated quick commerce organisation. ## The Chatter — BEL, HUL, Tata Capital

**Best growth in 13 quarters:** Q1 turnover ₹17,184 cr with 10% underlying sales growth — split equally between volume and price; the growth trajectory stepped up from 3% (1H FY26) to 10%, with continued turnover-weighted market-share gains.

**Broad-based, diversified recovery:** growth spans laundry, dishwash, coffee, Horlicks/Boost, Beauty & Wellbeing, hair and skin care, and is broad-based across rural/urban and mass/premium — a "solid platform" rather than a narrow pocket.

**Rural step-up a key driver:** a marked step-up in rural growth over recent quarters, with overall demand stable across both rural and urban.

**Pricing discipline over gross-margin chase:** commodities remain elevated; management is being measured in passing price increases to consumers and still expects EBITDA "around the guidance range" — a playbook of passing on ~half of inflation to hold margins.

**Premiumization is structural:** "barriers to scale have gone up" (Minimalist scaling fast inside HUL); body-wash/liquid conversion of the bathing category is the biggest structural opportunity; quick commerce growing 40–50% and being treated as a segmentation channel, not just another shelf.

> "Turnover for the quarter stood at ₹17,184 crores with an underlying sales growth of 10% given equally by volume and price. This represents our highest growth in 13 quarters... this growth is competitive as we continue to gain turnover-weighted market share."
> — Priya Nair, CEO & Managing Director
