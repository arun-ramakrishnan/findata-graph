# Gulf Oil Lubricants India

## Company Overview
Gulf Oil Lubricants India Limited (founded 1901; HQ Mumbai) is a leading manufacturer, marketer, and trader of automotive and industrial lubricants, greases, and specialty fluids. A subsidiary of Gulf Oil International (Mauritius), it serves the auto aftermarket (cars, CVs, two-wheelers, tractors), industrial sectors, and marine customers, and exports to ~25 countries. India is the world's third-largest lubricants market and a key long-term growth geography.

## Key Insights
- India's lubricants demand expected to grow from 2.6 MT (2023) to 3.6 MT by 2033; volume CAGR of 3–4% but value growth of 6%+ driven by premiumization, despite decarbonisation.
- Auto under-penetration (only 8% of Indian households own cars) plus BS3/BS4 → BS6 shift, SUV preference, and stricter emission norms are pushing demand toward premium lubricants — a volume-to-value transition.
- Massive infrastructure tailwind: ₹143 trillion expected investment between FY24 and FY30 (Bharatmala, Sagarmala, UDAN, industrial corridors) driving demand for industrial lubricants — hydraulic oils, metalworking fluids, premium oils, greases across manufacturing, power, mining, metals, textiles, and cement.
