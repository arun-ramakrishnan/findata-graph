# Gopal Snacks

## Company Overview
Gopal Snacks Limited, founded in 1999 and headquartered in Rajkot, is a manufacturer and marketer of traditional Indian snacks (namkeen, gathiya), papad, western snacks, and allied packaged foods. It sells under the Gopal, Bonova, Cristos, Shot Go, and Cornigo brands across India and internationally through retail and online channels.

## Key Insights
- Rajkot facility fire in Q4 FY25 significantly impacted operations — the factory contributing ~65% of topline was lost, with a per-day revenue loss of ₹1 crore and total exceptional loss of ₹47 cr (insurance-covered).
- Q4 FY25 gross margin compressed to 20.2% (vs 28.1% LY) on raw material inflation: palm oil +54%, potato +56%, maida +21%.
- Strategic response: grammage cuts on ₹5/₹10 SKUs, price hikes on larger packs, and a TV ad campaign.
- Shifting strategy away from dependence on ₹5 SKUs (currently 65% of sales) toward higher-MRP products and reduced palm oil dependency (Decoding the Dialogue).
