# Gopal Snacks

## Company Overview
Gopal Snacks Limited, founded in 1999 and headquartered in Rajkot, is a manufacturer and marketer of traditional Indian snacks (namkeen, gathiya), papad, western snacks, and allied packaged foods. It sells under the Gopal, Bonova, Cristos, Shot Go, and Cornigo brands across India and internationally through retail and online channels.
