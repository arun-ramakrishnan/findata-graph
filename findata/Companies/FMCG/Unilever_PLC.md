# Unilever PLC

## Company Overview
Unilever PLC (NYSE: UL; HQ London) is a global consumer-packaged-goods company spanning beauty, personal care, home care, foods, and ice cream. Its Indian listed subsidiary is Hindustan Unilever (HINDUNILVR.NS).
