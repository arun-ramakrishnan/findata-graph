# Milky Mist Dairy Food

## The Chatter — Milky Mist, Bluestone, Leap India, Avanti & More
**Premium dairy mix shift:** Q1 FY27 growth was led by high-margin categories — yogurt revenue +153% QoQ to ₹84.5 cr, ice cream revenue +60% YoY (volumes +45%), paneer volumes and revenue both +34% to ₹248.29 cr (~26–27% of top line), cheese +38%, curd +27%.
**Capacity + retail expansion:** Perundurai additions (whey protein concentrate unit, yogurt and cream cheese capacity, natural cheese lines), 50,000+ visi coolers/freezers over the next 3 fiscal years, and inorganic growth via the Asal and Briyas acquisitions.
**Regulatory tailwind:** Zero GST on paneer (Sept 2025) plus FSSAI/state bans on analogue paneer collapsed the organized–unorganized price gap — a significant volume uptake for India's largest paneer manufacturer.
**Cold-chain edge:** 375+ own AI/IoT-tracked vehicles run round the clock (reaching destinations in ~60–75% of third-party time); reverse logistics carrying third-party return cargo saves 18–20% on logistics costs.
**Whey monetization + Maharashtra:** ~1M litres/day of cheese whey feeds a protein-extraction plant (orders placed, live in 15–18 months) for B2B/B2C protein lines; Maharashtra procurement is being built ahead of a new plant (~3 years out) targeted at ≥40% utilization from day one.
