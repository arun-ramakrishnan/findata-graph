# Crompton Greaves Consumer Electricals

## Company Overview

Crompton Greaves Consumer Electricals manufactures and markets consumer electrical products in India through two segments: Electrical Consumer Durables and Lighting Products. The company offers fans, pumps, home appliances (coolers, heaters, irons, kitchen appliances), and lighting products (LED bulbs, luminaires, professional lighting). Incorporated in 2015, headquartered in Mumbai.
