**Record VAP without bulk SMP/butter support:** highest-ever VAP number achieved with no support from bulk SMP/butter sales; like-for-like VAP (ex-bulk) grew 40.6% YoY — decoupling from industrial milk-powder volatility.

**Strategic stake in Sid/Sit Farm:** taking a stake to enter the premium D2C dairy segment — exposure to fast-growing niche without the risk/cost of building a new brand; also a low-cost R&D lab to learn modern high-value D2C consumption patterns.

**Pivot to net commodity buyer:** expecting to be a net buyer of commodities this year (procurement growth moderate, ~25% not on the cards), reflecting strong internal consumption of raw milk for branded products.

**East Africa the growth engine:** Kenya at ~80% utilisation (1.5 lakh-litre capacity); Uganda at full capacity targeting yogurt growth; a greenfield Uganda project is being planned.

**Defensive stance vs subsidised cooperatives:** in Karnataka/Tamil Nadu, focusing on market-share maintenance rather than aggressive pushing where subsidised cooperatives hold prices down; Maharashtra positioned as a procurement + balancing operation (~2 lakh litres/day local sales).
