## The Chatter — IDFC, Tata Consumer, Bank of India, Lal PathLabs & More

**Procurement-price-led margin squeeze:** held procurement prices high to secure inventory despite rising supply — the milk realisation (₹59.4) vs procurement (₹41.3) spread narrowed to ₹18.1/litre (from ₹19.8), dragging margin from 8.2% to 5.4% as costs weren't fully passed through.

**Record VAP without bulk SMP/butter support:** highest-ever VAP number achieved with no support from bulk SMP/butter sales; like-for-like VAP (ex-bulk) grew 40.6% YoY — decoupling from industrial milk-powder volatility.

**Strategic stake in Sid/Sit Farm:** taking a stake to enter the premium D2C dairy segment — exposure to fast-growing niche without the risk/cost of building a new brand; also a low-cost R&D lab to learn modern high-value D2C consumption patterns.

**Pivot to net commodity buyer:** expecting to be a net buyer of commodities this year (procurement growth moderate, ~25% not on the cards), reflecting strong internal consumption of raw milk for branded products.

**East Africa the growth engine:** Kenya at ~80% utilisation (1.5 lakh-litre capacity); Uganda at full capacity targeting yogurt growth; a greenfield Uganda project is being planned.

**Defensive stance vs subsidised cooperatives:** in Karnataka/Tamil Nadu, focusing on market-share maintenance rather than aggressive pushing where subsidised cooperatives hold prices down; Maharashtra positioned as a procurement + balancing operation (~2 lakh litres/day local sales).

> "To put the squeeze in context, the spread between our milk realization of 59.4 and the procurement cost of 41.3 narrowed to 18.1 per liter from 19.8 a year ago, which largely explains the margin decline from 8.2% to 5.4%."
> — BVK Reddy, CEO

## The Chatter — PNB, Central Bank of India, Rentomojo & More

**FY27 guidance: 18–20% revenue growth despite a 15–16% H1** — the margin story is H2-loaded: gross margin 22–23.5% today vs 26–27% needed for the 9% EBITDA-margin target; management expects the 3–4% gross-margin recovery to land in coming quarters as procurement-price corrections stick ("prices will not go beyond this level — everyone now has sufficient milk").

**Maharashtra plant timeline set:** liquid milk operations start March; powder plant at full capacity by May–June (flush season). The ~3 lakh litres currently shipped from Maharashtra to Hyderabad gets processed locally from next year, while a replacement procurement network is built for Hyderabad — ending the cross-region dependence.

**Pricing power demonstrated:** Karnataka saw ~₹4/litre of sales-price increases against ~₹2 of procurement inflation — double pass-through, setting up per-litre margin expansion from Q3. In Tamil Nadu, the cooperative's procurement failure created a supply vacuum Dodla filled even while raising prices.

**Value-added ramp + paneer push:** VAP at 30–32% of mix, targeted to rise 1–1.5%/yr; combined paneer capacity now 17–18 tons (Dodla 12 + HR Foods enhanced to 7–8) against 6–7 tons sold. Osum Dairy backend corrections (SAP, procurement, SOPs on par with Dodla) position it to turn margin-positive.

**Capex plan:** ~₹200 cr Maharashtra (post net-loan drawdown), ~₹100 cr Uganda, ₹10–20 cr Osum, plus ₹50–60 cr backend infrastructure beyond the regular ₹60 cr.

**Africa recovered fast:** El Niño hit July–August milk supply, but September rebounded "beyond expectations" on both revenue and profitability; 10–12% margin targets intact.

**Structural note — the inventory-trade era is over:** the 3–4-year flush surpluses that once let Dodla stock SMP/butter cheaply and sell into price recoveries have disappeared (input-output matched for 1.5–2 years) — earnings will be operationally driven, not inventory-driven. FY27 is framed as a two-shock outlier (West Asia war raising packaging/logistics costs + El Niño).

> "Earlier, once in 3 years or once in 4 years, we used to get a significant flush... over the last 1.5–2 years, there has not been much surplus. Our input and output are mostly matching, so there is not much inventory."
> — B. V. K. Reddy, CEO
