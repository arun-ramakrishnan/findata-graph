# Nestlé India

## Company Overview
Nestlé India Ltd is a leading large-cap FMCG company known for its wide range of food and beverage products including Maggi, Nescafé, and KitKat. The company focuses on innovation, quality, and premium products, maintaining strong market leadership in India with steady revenue growth and consistent dividends.
