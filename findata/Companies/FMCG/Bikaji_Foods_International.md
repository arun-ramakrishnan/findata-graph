## The Chatter — NSE ONGC SEBI

**Festive loading + core momentum:** Channel filling starts 30 to 15 days before Diwali; sweets (short shelf life) move closer to October. Core snacking portfolio growing in the high teens, in line with consumption trends.

**Top line intact, margins guide down:** Fully confident on mid-teens revenue guidance, but operating margins slip ~150 bps for the full year off the 15–16% peak on crude and commodity inflation.

**6–7% input-cost basket, two-thirds passed on:** Edible oils (double-digit rises), crude-linked packaging, plus dals and sugar drive the YoY basket impact; 4.5–5% passed through in select categories only — a volume/market-share defence into peak season.

**Quick-commerce scaling in multiples:** E-commerce and quick-commerce growth no longer measured in percentages; even packaged sweets see strong festive q-comm demand — improving urban access and cutting inventory lag.

**Exports throttled by freight; western snacks pivot:** Ocean freight up ~4x with container scarcity bottlenecking the export division. Western snacks (TAM as large as traditional) to grow 1.5x the traditional rate, reaching 11–12% of revenue in two years.

> "The festive season momentum and excitement are very high. Channel loading begins around 30 to 15 days before Diwali as we fill the distribution pipeline across our distributor network to reach retail outlets."
> — Manoj Verma, Chief Operating Officer
