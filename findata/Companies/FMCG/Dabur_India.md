# Dabur India (Dabur India Limited)

## Company Overview
Dabur India Limited, founded in 1884 and headquartered in Ghaziabad, India, operates as a fast moving consumer goods company worldwide. The company operates through Consumer Care Business, Foods Business, Retail Business, and Other segments. Dabur is known for its Ayurvedic and natural health products, with flagship brands including Dabur Chyawanprash, Dabur Honey, Hajmola, Pudin Hara, and Vatika hair care products.
