# Sapphire Foods India Limited

**SAPPHIRE.NS | NSE: ₹1,200-1,400 range | Market Cap: ₹18,000 crores | P/E: 120-140x**

Sapphire Foods India Limited is India's largest YUM Brands franchisee and a leading Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) operator in the Indian subcontinent. The company operates KFC, Pizza Hut, and Taco Bell brands across India, Sri Lanka, and the Maldives. With a strong presence in key metropolitan areas, Sapphire Foods serves millions of customers annually through its 1,000+ restaurants.

## Company Overview

- **Founded:** 2015 (IPO in 2021)
- **Headquarters:** Mumbai, India
- **Revenue:** ₹4,500 crores (FY2024)
- **Restaurant Count:** 1,000+ (KFC: 600+, Pizza Hut: 350+, Taco Bell: 50+)
- **Geographic Presence:** India (95%), Sri Lanka (4%), Maldives (1%)
- **Employee Count:** 25,000+

### Franchise Structure
- **Master Franchisee:** Exclusive rights for YUM Brands in India, Sri Lanka, and Maldives
- **Revenue Model:** Franchise fees + royalty payments + direct operations
- **Brand Portfolio:** KFC (60% revenue), Pizza Hut (35% revenue), Taco Bell (5% revenue)

### Supply Chain & Technology
- **In-house Supply Chain:** End-to-end control from procurement to distribution
- **Technology Stack:** SAP ERP, custom POS systems, AI-powered demand forecasting
- **Digital Initiatives:** Mobile apps, online ordering, delivery partnerships (Swiggy, Zomato)
- **Loyalty Programs:** KFC Krushers, Pizza Hut Rewards (10M+ members)

### Geographic Distribution
- **India:** 950+ restaurants across 150+ cities
  - Top Markets: Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai
  - Tier 2/3 Expansion: 30% of new stores in emerging cities
- **International:** 50+ restaurants in Colombo, Sri Lanka and Male, Maldives

## Key Insights

**Merger with Devyani International**
- Sapphire Foods and Devyani International are merging
- "The merged entity will have more than 3,000 stores globally and a turnover of approximately 8,000 crores on an annualized basis."
- "By the time merger gets consummated, merged entity is likely to cross $1 billion annual revenues."

**Operational Structure Post-Merger**
- The merged entity will assume marketing, innovation, technology, and supply chain for Pizza Hut
- Only technology and supply chain for KFC, leaving KFC marketing with Yum!
- "For Pizza Hut, we will be taking over marketing, innovation, technology, and supply chain functions. Whereas for KFC, as of now, it is only going to be technology and supply chain management."

**Turnaround Strategy**
- Immediate priority for Pizza Hut post-merger is to achieve a positive, low double digit brand contribution margin
- Focus on business turnaround rather than aggressive store expansion
- "In the first year post-merger, we expect to achieve a positive brand contribution margin Pizza Hut, aiming for low double digits. We have negotiated with Yum! that our priority to turn the business around rather than being under stress to open net new units."

**Technology & Innovation**
- Unified technology roadmap with common basic tech stack will drive speed and innovation across all brands
- Focus on faster in-store service and delivery to enhance QSR experience
- "Our technology roadmap will be common for all brands. While the UI/UX will be brand specific, basic tech stack will be unified. In the QSR world, speed and innovation are paramount—meaning faster turnaround in stores and faster delivery."

**Cost Synergies**
- The 320 crores payment to Yum! is a one-time merger cost
- Recurrent annual synergies of 210-225 crores will benefit to business long-term
- "The 320 crores is a one-time payment to Yum!, whereas synergies of 210-225 crores will accrue year after year for the entire lifespan of the business. It is not a direct comparison."
