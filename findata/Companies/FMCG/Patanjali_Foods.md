# Patanjali Foods

## Company Overview
Patanjali Foods Limited (formerly Ruchi Soya Industries Limited) processes oil seeds, refines crude edible oil, and manufactures food and FMCG/FMHG products. Incorporated in 1986 and based in Indore, it operates across Edible Oils, Food & FMCG, Home & Personal Care, and Wind Power segments, with brands including Patanjali, Ruchi Gold, Nutrela, Mahakosh, Sunrich, Dant Kanti, and Kesh Kanti, plus an oil-palm plantation business.
