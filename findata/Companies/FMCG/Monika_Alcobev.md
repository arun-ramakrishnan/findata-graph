# Monika Alcobev

## Company Overview

Monika Alcobev is a prominent player in the imported liquor sector, focusing on premium and luxury alcoholic beverages. It specializes in importing, sales, distribution, and marketing of luxury spirits, wines, and liqueurs in India and the Indian Subcontinent, including Travel Retail Duty Free Shops.

### Core Business Segments

- **Imported Spirits:** Premium and luxury spirit brands distributed in India
- **Wines & Liqueurs:** Imported wine and liqueur portfolio
- **Travel Retail:** Duty-free distribution across Indian Subcontinent

## Industry Context: FMCG / Premium AlcoBev

Monika Alcobev operates within the [[FMCG]] sector, in the premium imported alco-bev niche:

- **Tax Burden:** Customs and state duties account for ~50-60% of MRP across key states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Haryana), compressing trade margins
- **Distribution Models:** State-driven structures (private distributor vs corporation model) cause non-uniform pricing across India
- **Premiumization:** Imported spirits dominate India's premium segment with ~68% share in MRP > ₹1,000 category
- **Demographic Tailwinds:** India median age ~29.5; ~68% working-age; longest demographic dividend projected globally
