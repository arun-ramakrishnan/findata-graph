## Bajaj Consumer Care | Small Cap | FMCG

Bajaj Consumer Care Limited, a part of the Bajaj Group, is a leading player in the oil category in India. It offers popular brands like Bajaj Almond Drops Hair oil, Baj Brahmi Amla Hair Oil, Bajaj Amla Hair Oil, and Bajaj Jasmine Hair oil. The company's flagship product, Bajaj Almond Drops Hair oil, holds a premium positio in the market with the highest unit price. In addition to hair oils, the company also markets oral care products under the brand Bajaj Red/Kala Dant Manjan.
