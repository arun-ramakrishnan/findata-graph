# Pajson Agro India

Pajson Agro India Ltd. processes raw cashew nuts into kernels and dry-fruit products under the "Royal Mewa" brand. It sources nuts from India and Africa, operates a modern plant in Visakhapatnam, and supplies to domestic and export markets.

## The Chatter — Q4FY26 (Havells, Ambuja, Cipla & More)

**Supply — African cashew crop:** Quantity is sufficient across all countries, but early indications suggest slightly poorer quality compared to last year (still early to conclude).

**Demand profile — capacity-constrained:** The company is consistently short of product — more than 80% of revenue comes from repeat customers, and it is unable to serve many new customers. No pre-planned off-take agreements needed given this demand backdrop. Top institutional clients include major packers VRO, Farmley, and Happilo; wholesale presence concentrated in Delhi, UP, and Rajasthan.

**Scale economics:** A minimum 15,000 metric tonne capacity is essential for efficient cashew processing. Moving from 18,000 to 50,000-55,000 tonnes provides an additional 2-3% margin advantage; smaller 1,000-tonne plants may only make ~1% margin or lose money.

**Technology & SOPs:** High-capex technology improves yields of cashew wholes — reduces broken percentages, contamination, and improves kernel color — increasing both revenue and margins.

**Supply chain resilience:** Middle East disturbances have not disrupted the West Africa → India supply chain (no direct route impact), though higher fuel prices have marginally increased freight costs. Rupee depreciation is largely passed through to customers via higher finished kernel prices.

**Growth targets:**
- Anticipates 30-40% revenue growth in FY27 (new plant commencing production in late FY27); 10-15% baseline growth from the existing plant.
- Aiming to raise the B2C segment's contribution to 15% of total revenue within the next three financial years.
