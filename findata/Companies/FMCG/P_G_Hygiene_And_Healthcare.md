# Procter & Gamble Hygiene and Health Care Limited

**Ticker:** PGHH.NS | **Sector:** Consumer Goods/Personal Hygiene | **Market Cap:** Large Cap (₹420.5B INR)

## Company Overview

Procter & Gamble Hygiene and Health Care Limited (P&G Hygiene) is a leading manufacturer and marketer of branded fast-moving consumer goods in India, operating as a subsidiary of The Procter & Gamble Company. Established in 1964 and headquartered in Mumbai, the company has built a strong presence in the Indian FMCG market with a diverse portfolio of trusted brands.

### Core Business Segments

- **Healthcare Products:** Comprehensive range of healthcare solutions including ointments, creams, cough drops, and tablets
- **Hygiene Products:** Feminine hygiene products, skin care hygiene solutions, and feminine care pads
- **Baby Care:** Nappies, pants, and baby wipes under the Pampers brand
- **Grooming Products:** Shaving and personal grooming solutions
- **Laundry Products:** Detergents and fabric care products
- **Hair Care:** Shampoos, conditioners, and hair treatment products
- **Home Care:** Home cleaning and air care products
- **Personal & Skin Care:** Skin care products and personal hygiene solutions
- **Oral Care:** Oral hygiene products including toothbrushes and toothpaste

### Brand Portfolio

P&G Hygiene markets its products under a comprehensive portfolio of well-established brands:

- **Vicks:** Healthcare products including cough drops and cold remedies
- **Whisper:** Feminine hygiene and care products
- **Pampers:** Baby care products including nappies and wipes
- **Ariel & Tide:** Laundry detergents and fabric care
- **Gillette:** Grooming and shaving products
- **Head & Shoulders, Herbal Essences, Pantene:** Hair care products
- **Ambi Pur:** Air care and home fragrance products
- **Oral-B:** Oral care products
- **Olay:** Skin care products
- **Old Spice:** Personal grooming products
