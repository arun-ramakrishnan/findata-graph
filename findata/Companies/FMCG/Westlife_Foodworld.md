## Business Overview
Westlife Development Limited, through its subsidiary Hardcastle Restaurants Pvt. Ltd., is a major player in India's QSR (Quick Service Restaurant) sector. The company focuses on setting up and managing QSR outlets, primarily operating McDonald's restaurants in India.
