- HBM3E/HBM4 demand "far in excess of our ability to supply" — committed volumes still below what customers actually want.
- **HBM TAM** raised: now expected to cross **$100B in 2027** (previously 2028). Market tightness to continue beyond 2027.
- **SCAs**: 5-year take-or-pay (ex-auto), uncancellable, ~$22B total commitments incl. ~$18B cash; target covering ~half of revenue.
- **LPDRAM** gaining share in data centers (pioneer, leader) — power efficiency driver.
- Mix maintained at ~75–80% DRAM / 20–25% NAND; HBM share pegged to overall DRAM share.
- Enterprise SSD momentum strong — $5B quarterly run-rate within $25B data-center business; QLC leader, 245TB SSD shipping.
- CXMT/YMTC competition largely confined to China; not seen meaningfully outside.
- Greenfield fabs + higher-performance mix (HBM) to lift DRAM bit costs near term.

> "The demand that we have for our HBM products — HBM3E, HBM4 and beyond — is far in excess of our ability to supply. ... Even when we sign multi-year agreements, the committed volumes are still less than what customers actually want."
