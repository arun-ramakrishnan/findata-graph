# Advanced Micro Devices

## Company Overview
Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a global semiconductor company headquartered in Santa Clara, California, United States. Founded in 1969, AMD is a leading developer of high-performance computing, graphics, and visualization technologies. The company has grown to become a major player in the global semiconductor industry, competing directly with industry giants like Intel and NVIDIA. AMD employs approximately 28,000 people worldwide and operates three main business segments: Data Center, Client and Gaming, and Embedded.

### Data Center
- **AI Accelerators:** AMD Instinct accelerators for machine learning and AI workloads
- **Server Microprocessors:** AMD EPYC processors for data center applications
- **Data Center Graphics:** Professional GPUs for server and cloud computing
- **Infrastructure:** Complete data center solutions and optimization

### Client and Gaming
- **Consumer Processors:** AMD Ryzen series for desktop and mobile computers
- **Gaming Graphics:** AMD Radeon graphics cards for gaming enthusiasts
- **Professional Graphics:** AMD Radeon Pro for professional workstations
- **APUs:** Accelerated Processing Units combining CPU and GPU

### Embedded
- **Embedded Processors:** Low-power solutions for embedded applications
- **FPGA Products:** Virtex, Kintex, Artix, and Spartan series
- **Adaptive SoCs:** Zynq and Versal series for intelligent edge computing
- **System-on-Modules:** Ready-to-use embedded computing platforms
