# Frontier Springs Limited

## Company Overview

Frontier Springs Ltd is a global leader in manufacturing hot wound springs for various industries including transportation, industrial, and government applications. Its diverse product range includes Hot Coil Springs, Leaf Springs, and Industrial Springs for applications like mass transit, railroad pipe hangers, and power generators.

### Core Business Segments

- **Hot Coil Springs:** Hot wound springs for heavy-duty transport and industrial use
- **Leaf Springs:** Suspension springs for commercial vehicles and rail
- **Industrial Springs:** Specialty springs for power generators, railroad pipe hangers, and mass transit
- **Primary Customer:** Indian Railways (direct beneficiary of railway capex)

## Industry Context: Metals / Railway Spring Manufacturing

Frontier Springs operates within the [[Metals]] sector, specifically in spring manufacturing tied to Indian Railways:

- **Railways Budget (FY26-27):** ₹3.02 lakh crore revenue from operations
- **Railway Capex:** ₹2.65 lakh crore (up 5% YoY)
- **Freight Target:** 1,765 MT for FY26-27; goal of 3,000 MT by 2027
- **Wagon Orders:** Record ~72,000 wagons ordered, 40,000 more anticipated; 90,000 wagon procurement plan over 3 years
- **Vande Bharat:** 260 rakes planned for manufacture
- **Replacement Cycle:** ~35% of existing wagons older than 15 years due for replacement
