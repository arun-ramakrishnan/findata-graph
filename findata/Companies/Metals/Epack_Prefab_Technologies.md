# Epack Prefab Technologies

Epack Prefab Technologies, established in 1999, operates in two key segments: Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB) and EPS Packaging. The company provides turnkey solutions for pre-engineered steel buildings and structures, and manufactures EPS products for insulation and packaging. With multiple manufacturing units and design centers, Epack serves industries such as construction, packaging, and consumer goods.
