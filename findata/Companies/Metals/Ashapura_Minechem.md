# Ashapura Minechem | Small Cap | Metals | **ASHAPURMINE**

Ashapura Minechem Limited is a leading multi-mineral solutions provider with a global footprint, having a wide network of operations Pan-India and in many other countries. The company is engaged in the mining, manufacturing and trading of various minerals and its derivative products and related services. From soaps to ste, energy to edible oils, metal to medicine and cement to ceramics, company offer multi-mineral solutions across several industries.

## Business Operations

### Core Products & Services

- **Multi-Mineral Solutions**: Serving diverse industries including:
  - Soaps & Detergents

  - Energy & Edible Oils
  - Medicine & Pharmaceuticals
  - Cement & Ceramics

### Geographic Presence
- **Pan-India Operations**: Extensive network across India
