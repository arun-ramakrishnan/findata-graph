# JTL Industries

JTL Infra Limited (now JTL Industries Limited), an India-based company, specializes in manufacturing and selling ERW Black and Galvanized steel pipes, tubes, hollow sections, and structural steel for engineering and construction projects. The company offers a diversified product portfolio catering to a wide range of industries including infrastructure, construction, energy, agriculture, water, solar, and heavy engineering.

## Company Overview

### Manufacturing Capabilities
- **Large-Scale Manufacturing**: Six modern facilities across India
- **Backward Integration**: Raw material to finished products
- **Pan-India Presence**: National footprint
- **Global Footprint**: Distributor network
- **Deep SKU Portfolio**: Wide product range

### Product Portfolio

#### Galvanized Steel Tubes
- **Applications**: Infrastructure, water supply, irrigation
- **Specifications**: Various sizes and thickness
- **Quality**: High galvanization quality

#### Structural Steel Products
- **Hollow Sections**: Square and rectangular hollow sections
- **LTZ Sections**: Light-gauge tees
- **Mild Steel Angles/Channels**: Structural steel sections
- **Applications**: Construction, engineering projects
