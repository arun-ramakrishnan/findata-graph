# KIOCL

## Company Overview

KIOCL Limited (formerly Kudremukh Iron Ore Company) engages in iron ore mining, beneficiation, and production of pellets in India and internationally. The company operates through Pellet and Pig Iron segments, producing iron ore pellets, pig iron, and iron ore fines. Incorporated in 1976, headquartered in Bengaluru. Government-owned PSU (99% promoter holding).
