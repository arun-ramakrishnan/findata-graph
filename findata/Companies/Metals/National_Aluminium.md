# National Aluminium

## Company Overview
National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO), incorporated in 1981 and headquartered in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, is a Government of India enterprise and one of the largest integrated aluminium producers in Asia. It operates bauxite mines, an alumina refinery, an aluminium smelter, and captive power plants, and manufactures aluminium metal products (ingots, billets, wire rods, T-ingots), alumina/hydrate products, zeolite-A, and specialty alumina. It also owns wind and solar power assets and mechanized port-handling facilities.
