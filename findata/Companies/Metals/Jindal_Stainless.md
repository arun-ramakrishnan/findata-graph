# Jindal Stainless

Jindal Stainless Limited (JSL) is a major stainless steel conglomerate in India and globally. Established in 1970, it leads in digital transformation in manufacturing through advanced technology solutions. As India's largest producer of various stainless steel series, it offers a wide range of stainless steel products.
