# Lloyds Metals & Energy

## Company Overview
Lloyds Metals & Energy (NSE: LLOYDSME) is an integrated iron-ore mining, pellet, and sponge iron company in the Gadchiroli region of Maharashtra. Vertically integrating into iron and steel, it is a fast-growing mid-cap metals play with captive ore advantage.

## The Chatter — Meesho, Marico, Bajaj & More

**Strong demand traction:** Management sees steady upward demand in both steel and iron ore, guiding to ~27 million tons of sales plus ~7.5–8 million tons of pellet sales in FY27.

**Steel plant commissioning this year:** The steel plant is expected to be commissioned by end of this year, adding ~800,000 tons of DR sales and ~150,000 tons of steel sales — a major step toward becoming a fully integrated steel producer.

**Copper & cobalt ramp:** The copper/cobalt (Suraj) assets are expected to produce ~10,000 tons this year, ultimately ramping to ~30,000 tons, making them a meaningful long-term contributor.

**Aggressive capex, controlled debt:** Capex was ~₹8,000 crore in FY26 (~₹30,500 crore over 3 years) and is set to rise to ~₹12,000 crore this year, yet net debt remains only ~₹4,000 crore.

**Potential mining/infra IPO:** Management hinted at a possible future IPO for the mining & infrastructure business to unlock a better valuation multiple, though detailing is still on the drawing board.

> "Being a more steady business we think that the valuation should be given a better multiple and at some point of time we would go in for an IPO. The detailing is yet being done and on the drawing board but that's not off the cards."
> — Rajesh Gupta, MD, Lloyds Metals & Energy Ltd.
