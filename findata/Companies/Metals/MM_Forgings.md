# MM Forgings

## Company Overview

M M Forgings Limited (formerly The Madras Motors Ltd) manufactures and sells steel forgings. Products include sprockets, flange housings, connecting rods, rail forgings, hubs, knuckles, front axle beams, universal joint crosses, steering/pivot arms, shifter forks, rear axle spindles, crankshafts, and high-pressure valve bodies/bonnets. Founded 1945, headquartered in Chennai. Serves passenger cars, commercial vehicles, off-highway vehicles, valve/oilfield, agriculture, and engineering.
