  > "Our key upstream expansion projects of Aditya Alumina refinery and aluminum smelters are progressing well and remain on schedule as we move ahead with our objective of doubling down on our upstream capacity." — Satish Pai, Managing Director
- **CBAM not yet a barrier for Indian aluminum**: Power emissions not yet included; EU exports potentially more attractive under trade agreement.
  > "In aluminum CBAM, power is not included right now... Until that gets included, CBAM is not a restriction for any Indian aluminum exports. In fact, following the current trade agreement, exporting to Europe will become more attractive for us." — Satish Pai, Managing Director
