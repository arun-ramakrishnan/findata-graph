## The Chatter — TCS, Indian Bank, Amagi, CMR Green & More

**45% share of India's automotive recycled-aluminium market** — ICRA ranks CMR #12 globally (incl. China). Roughly every second Indian vehicle (2W or 4W) uses CMR-supplied aluminium, a deep moat built on scale + logistics.

**Diversification beyond auto** — (1) Beverage-can recycling plant in Odisha near Hindalco supplying liquid metal to a primary producer (a first-of-its-kind reverse flow); (2) a new billets & sheet-ingots facility letting CMR compete with primary miners for solar and construction demand.

**EV structural tailwind** — EVs use ~3x more aluminium than ICE vehicles (battery weight offset, lower centre of gravity). A multi-year demand lever layered on top of the existing ICE replacement cycle.

**Molten-metal delivery = customer lock-in** — 24x7 line-side delivery makes customers operationally dependent on CMR; very low risk of displacement even during cyclical downturns. CMR gained share even in FY23–24 when auto de-grew.

**EU scrap-export regulation (Sep '26 duty, May '27 ban)** — management expects the 15% export duty to be absorbed largely by European suppliers or passed through to buyers, protecting CMR's processing spreads.

> "Molten metal kind of locks in our customer very, very strongly with us… A liquid metal customer is tied with us at the hip. So they don't move to anybody else, we don't move to anybody else."
> — Mohan Agarwal, Chairman and Managing Director
