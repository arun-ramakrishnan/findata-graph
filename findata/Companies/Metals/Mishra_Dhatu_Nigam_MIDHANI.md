# Mishra Dhatu Nigam (MIDHANI)

## Company Overview
Mishra Dhatu Nigam (MIDHANI) is a Government of India enterprise under the Ministry of Defence, focused on achieving self-reliance in the production and supply of super alloys, special steels, and advanced materials for the Defense, Nuclear, Aeronautical, and Space sectors.
