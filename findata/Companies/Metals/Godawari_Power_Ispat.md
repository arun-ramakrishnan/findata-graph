# Godawari Power & Ispat

## Company Overview

Godawari Power & Ispat (GPIL) operates across the full steel value chain — from iron ore mining to pellets, sponge iron, steel billets, wire rods, H.B. wire, and ferro alloys, with captive electricity generation. This integrated model ensures strong raw-material security and margin stability.

## Key Insights (Points & Figures — The Truth Behind the Trendlines)

- FY25 utilization levels high at 77–100% across the steel value chain, reflecting strong demand, operational efficiency, and integration cost advantages
- Global iron ore prices stable in the $95–110/t band; China's muted demand partly offset by stimulus measures
- India steel demand projected to grow 9% in both 2025 and 2026, supported by infrastructure, construction, and government-led capex
- Captive ore cost steady at ~₹2,200–3,000/t over several years — significantly below volatile market ore prices, providing strong competitive edge and protecting margins during commodity cycles
- Fully integrated across steel value chain (iron ore → pellets → sponge iron → billets → ferro alloys → HB wires → captive power) ensures consistent profitability vs non-integrated peers

## The Chatter — Tata Steel, LIC, Vodafone, BPCL & More

**Margin resilience despite soft realizations:** GPIL held EBITDA margin at ~23% and PAT margin at ~15% in FY26, with sponge iron, structural rolled products, and ferro alloys exceeding production targets (>100% of plan).

**Ari Dongri mining capacity more than doubles:** Environmental approval secured to lift Ari Dongri mine capacity from 2.35 to 6 million tons; a tenfold iron ore beneficiation plant expansion (to 6 MT) is targeted for commissioning by Q3 FY27, with full-scale mining from FY28.

**1 MT integrated steel plant approved:** Board approved a new 1 million ton integrated steel plant for structural steel and wire rods; land and environmental approvals in place, construction expected to begin October 2026.

**₹30,000 cr revenue ambition in 4-5 years:** Combining the 20 GWh battery energy storage business (~₹15,000 cr), the new steel plant (~₹6,000 cr), CRM (~₹3,000-4,000 cr), and expanded pellet capacity, management targets ~₹30,000 cr top line in 4-5 years.

**EV truck fleet conversion:** Diesel-led transport cost inflation (~₹200-250 higher) is being countered by a plan to convert the entire mining fleet to EV trucks, with substantial mining cost reductions expected from Q3/Q4 onwards.
