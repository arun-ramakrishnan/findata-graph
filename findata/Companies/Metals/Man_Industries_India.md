# Man Industries (India)

## Company Overview

Man Industries (India) Limited manufactures submerged arc-welded pipes and steel products. Products include longitudinal submerged arc-welded (LSAW) and helically submerged arc-welded (HSAW) line pipes, external coatings and internal linings, electro resistance-welded (ERW) pipes, and hot induction bends (HIB). Incorporated 1988, headquartered in Mumbai. Serves oil, gas, petrochemicals, water dredging, and fertilizer industries. Exports internationally.
