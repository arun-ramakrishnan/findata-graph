## Company Overview
Usha Martin Limited, established in 1960, is a global leader in wire rope manufacturing with facilities across multiple continents. The company is engaged in the manufacture and sale of steel wires, strand wire ropes, cords, related accessories, and other products, serving diverse industrial sectors worldwide.
