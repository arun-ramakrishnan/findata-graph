# NMDC

## Company Overview

NMDC Limited is India's largest iron ore producer, exploring and producing iron ore domestically and internationally. The company also explores for lithium, copper, rock phosphate, limestone, coal, diamond, tungsten, gold, and rare earth metals. Incorporated in 1958, headquartered in Hyderabad. Government-owned (Navratna PSU).
