# Shivalik Bimetal Controls

## Company Overview

Shivalik Bimetal Controls Limited is a process and product engineering company manufacturing thermostatic bimetals, shunt resistors (SMDS), precision stainless steel strips, and snap action discs. Products are used in switchgears, circuit breakers, protective relays, metering, automotive devices, and energy/battery management systems (EVs). Founded 1984, headquartered in New Delhi. Serves electronics, automotive, medical, defense, and agriculture sectors.
