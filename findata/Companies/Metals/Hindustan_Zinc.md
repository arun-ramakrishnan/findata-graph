# Hindustan Zinc | Large Cap | Metals

Hindustan Zinc is world's largest integrated zinc producer and a top global silver producer. It excels in mining critical minerals and metals with a diversified portfolio, known for operational excellence, innovation, and sustainability. The company integrates mining to marketing, ensuring efficiency and high-quality zinc, lead, silver and value-added products. It invests in technology, safety, and capacity expansion to enhance operations and support India's mineral self-reliance.

## The Chatter — Reliance, Infosys, VBL, Hind Zinc & More

**Critical minerals diversification:** HZL has secured three critical mineral blocks (potash, tungsten, rare earths) with clear timelines, reducing dependence on zinc/lead and tapping energy-transition demand.

**Lowest quarterly cost:** Achieved the lowest quarterly zinc cost of production (ex-royalty) since underground transition at $903/t — down 9% YoY and 4% QoQ — on lower power cost, better by-product realisation and operating leverage.

**FY27 hedging:** Portions of FY27 zinc and silver output are hedged (e.g., 71 KT zinc at ~$3,225/t), providing earnings visibility despite price swings; silver largely kept unhedged (~10%) to capture upside.

**Multi-metal flexibility:** Flexible production tilts between zinc, lead and silver based on relative prices, with surplus lead MIC sold to maximise silver recovery via the zinc route — protecting margins when individual commodity prices are volatile.

**Renewable cost lever:** Moving renewable share from 20% to 70% could permanently reduce cost by ~$25/ton (every 2% renewable increase = ~$1/t reduction), reinforcing low-cost positioning.
