# Graphite India Limited

## Company Overview

Graphite India Limited (GIL) is a pioneering Indian company specializing in carbon and graphite products, established in 1964 through a collaboration with The Great Lakes Carbon Corporation of USA. With nearly six decades of expertise, GIL has evolved into one of India's leading manufacturers of graphite electrodes and specialized carbon products, serving critical applications in steel production and industrial processes. The company's comprehensive product portfolio includes graphite electrodes, graphite equipment, steel products, GRP pipes and tanks, and hydel power generation, making it a diversified player in the engineering and capital goods sector.

### Core Business Segments

- **Graphite Electrodes**: High-quality graphite electrodes for Electric Arc Furnace (EAF) steel production
- **Carbon Products**: Specialized carbon and graphite solutions for industrial applications
- **Steel Manufacturing**: Integrated steel production capabilities
- **GRP Products**: Glass Reinforced Plastic pipes and tanks for industrial and infrastructure use
- **Hydel Power**: Renewable energy generation through hydropower facilities
- **Specialty Applications**: Advanced carbon and graphite solutions for emerging industrial uses

### Manufacturing and Technological Capabilities

Graphite India operates with advanced manufacturing facilities featuring:

- **State-of-the-Art Production**: Modern graphite processing and electrode manufacturing technologies
- **Quality Certifications**: Adherence to international quality standards for carbon products
- **R&D Excellence**: Continuous innovation in graphite applications and product development
- **Environmental Compliance**: Advanced pollution control and sustainable manufacturing practices
- **Capacity Expansion**: Ongoing investments in production capacity enhancement
