# Gravita India

Gravita India Ltd. is a major global recycling company specializing in environmentally friendly processing of lead, aluminum, plastics, and rubber, with a growing focus on lithium-ion battery recycling. The company operates 13+ eco-conscious facilities worldwide, producing refined metals and plastic granules for industries like battery manufacturing, cables, autos, paints, and industrials.
