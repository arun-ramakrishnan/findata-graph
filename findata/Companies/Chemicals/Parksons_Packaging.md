# Parksons Packaging

## Company Overview
Parksons Packaging is an Indian packaging company specializing in flexible packaging solutions, including multi-layer laminates and specialized packaging films, serving FMCG, pharmaceutical, and consumer goods industries.

- Core products: flexible plastic packaging, multi-layer laminates, and specialized films
