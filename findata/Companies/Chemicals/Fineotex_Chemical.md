# Fineotex Chemical

## Company Overview
Fineotex Chemical Limited (founded 1979; HQ Mumbai) manufactures specialty chemicals and enzymes for the textile & garment, construction, leather, water-treatment, paint, agrochemical, and adhesives industries. It also serves the home-care, hygiene, mining, and oilfield-chemicals segments.
