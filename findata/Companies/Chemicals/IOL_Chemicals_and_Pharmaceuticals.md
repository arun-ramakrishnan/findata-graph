# IOL Chemicals and Pharmaceuticals

## Company Overview
IOL Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Ltd (est. 1986) — generics API/bulk-drug and specialty chemicals manufacturer with significant organic-chemicals operations; therapeutic spread across pain management, anti-diabetics, anti-hypertensives and anti-convulsants, serving domestic and international markets. Among the world's significant Ibuprofen producers, fully backward integrated.
