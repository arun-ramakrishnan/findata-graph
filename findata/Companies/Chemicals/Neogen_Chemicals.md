# Neogen Chemicals

## Overview
Neogen Chemicals is a specialty chemical manufacturer focusing on bromine and lithium-based compounds. It is a leading supplier to pharmaceutical, agrochemical, and specialty chemical companies in India, Europe, and Japan. The company has expertise in a variety of chemical processes and also offers custom synthesis and contract manufacturing services.

## Business Model
Neogen Chemicals specializes in manufacturing and supplying specialty chemicals. Their primary customers are in the pharmaceutical, agrochemical, and engineering sectors. They leverage their expertise in various chemical processes to provide advanced intermediates and other compounds.

## Products and Services
- **Bromine based compounds**
- **Lithium-based compounds** (including inorganic lithium salts)
- **Grignard Reagents**
- **Advanced intermediates** for various industries
- **Lithium-ion battery electrolyte solutions**
- **Custom synthesis and contract manufacturing services**
