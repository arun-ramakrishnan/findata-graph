# SPEB Adhesives

## Company Overview

SPEB Adhesives Ltd. manufactures synthetic rubber, resin, and water-based adhesives under the SPEB-7 brand, serving woodworking, footwear, insulation, and industrial segments with a strong distribution network across India and export markets.

## Key Insights (Points & Figures #22)

- Strong regional concentration: Maharashtra ~69% of revenue, Gujarat ~11%, Karnataka ~5.7%; domestic markets ~93%
- Sales predominantly via dealer-distributor networks (~81.7%); digital presence on IndiaMART, Amazon, Flipkart, Justdial, Ajio
- Global synthetic rubber adhesive market: ~$18-22B (CY2024), projected to reach ~$26-27B by 2030 at ~4-5% CAGR
- Indian market valued at ₹15,000-18,000 crore (FY2024), expected to grow at ~7-9% CAGR
- Solvent-based adhesives offer stronger bonding/faster drying but higher VOC; water-based are cleaner, low-VOC, eco-friendly
