# TCPL Packaging

## Company Overview

TCPL Packaging Limited manufactures and sells paperboard-based and flexible packaging products in India. Offers folding cartons, litho-laminated cartons, blister packs, plastic cartons, shelf-ready packaging, flexible laminates, shrink sleeves, pouches, and rigid/specialty gift boxes. Serves tobacco, FMCG, food & beverage, liquor, and pharmaceutical industries. Headquartered in Mumbai; incorporated 1987.
