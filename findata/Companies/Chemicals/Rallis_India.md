# Rallis India

## Company Overview
Rallis India Limited, a subsidiary of Tata Chemicals Limited and part of the Tata Group, is an agri-sciences company founded in 1948 and headquartered in Mumbai, India. It provides farmers with innovative crop care and seed products, focusing on enhancing farm yield, soil health, and farmers' income. Its portfolio spans crop protection (insecticides, herbicides, fungicides, bio-pesticides), seeds (maize, cotton, paddy, vegetables), soil & plant health solutions, and household products (Termex, Sentry, Ralligel Plus).

**Export competitiveness under pressure:** Catalog/generic exports face intense Chinese pricing pressure (Acephate singled out — raw materials sourced from China, competing head-to-head in Brazil/US); CSM (contract manufacturing) remains structurally more resilient as terms are negotiable.

**Cotton flattish this year:** Lower North-India acreage (Punjab/Haryana decline), deficient South/Central rainfall and illegal HTBT cotton compressing the cotton crop; focus shifting to rice, maize and millet where several new products were launched.

**Monsoon catch-up underway:** Despite El Niño-driven deficient rainfall, ~50% of acreage is irrigated; crop sowing catching up — worst case still ~90–95% planting. Crop shift visible: pulses area up, groundnut down, soybean planted despite seed challenges.

**No further mid-season price hikes:** Whatever increases were needed have already been taken; future growth must come from volume, market-share gains, dealer relationships and stock positioning — not pricing.

**Biologicals a faster-growing, higher-margin segment:** +10% in Q1; positive regulatory developments and rising farmer adoption of micronutrients/biostimulants expected to accelerate growth.

**Rupee depreciation net-positive:** Rallis is a net exporter, so the weaker rupee added to profitability despite higher dollar-denominated raw-material costs.

**Working capital stretched ~15–20 days:** Farmers' liquidity absorbed by early fertilizer purchases, rationing spend on other agri-inputs — an industry-wide phenomenon currently.
