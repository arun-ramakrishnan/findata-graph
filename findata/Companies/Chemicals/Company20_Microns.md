# 20 Microns Limited

## Company Overview
20 Microns Ltd is an Indian specialty minerals company that manufactures and supplies industrial minerals, functional fillers, and extenders used in paints, plastic ceramics, paper, and coatings. It is a leading player in the micronized minerals segment with a focus on innovation and value-added products.

## Business Segments
- Specialty minerals manufacturing
- Industrial minerals, functional fillers, and extenders
- Micronized minerals segment
- Products used in paints, plastic ceramics, paper, and coatings

## Financial Performance & Guidance
- Management reaffirms steady medium-term growth guidance of 15-18% despite global risks in the paint sector
- Inventory went up over 40% from last year due to import timing and shipping plans, not low demand
- Planning to invest about ₹15 to ₹18 crores in a calcination facility for the rubber industry and specialized paint grades

## Strategic Developments
