# Deepak Nitrite

Deepak Nitrite Limited is a rapidly growing Indian company known for manufacturing Phenol, Acetone, and Iso Propyl Alcohol (IPA) at its advanced facility in Dahej, Gujarat. **Preparedness, not windfall, drove Q1 strength:** "Who built a roof beforehand?" — supply chain discipline (secured raw materials, locked in customers) enabled outperformance during geopolitical volatility; operational resilience to persist post-normalization.

**Volume-led growth ahead, not price-dependent:** Expects consumption normalization; volumes to drive top and bottom line as raw material prices moderate; secured supply avoids Middle East volatility impact.

**₹11,000 crore capex (₹3,500 cr spent) shifting to high-value downstream:** 95% allocated to specialty chemicals/upstream integration (nitration, hydrogenation, fluorination, polycarbonate, bisphenol-A, R&D); first-phase upstream projects (nitric acid, MIBK, MIBC) operational; downstream commissioning through FY29.

**Return ratios to exceed 20% post-capex (H2 FY29):** Current depressed returns reflect investments under construction; integrated assets coming online by H2 FY29 should deliver sustainable 20%+ returns.

**Strategic repositioning: commodity → materials & application chemistry:** Evolving from intermediate chemical manufacturer to materials/application chemistry company across ammonia and propane chains; aims to improve margins and reduce cyclicality long-term.

> "One thing I can say is that the entire sector experienced the same weather. The question is who was able to build a roof beforehand. In any such situation, companies that are able to secure both their raw material supplies and their customer base generally perform better than those that cannot."
