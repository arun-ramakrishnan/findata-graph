# Phillips Carbon Black

## Company Overview
PCBL Chemical Limited (formerly Phillips Carbon Black Limited) is a specialty chemicals manufacturer founded in 1960 and headquartered in Kolkata, India. The company was renamed to "PCBL Chemical Limited" in October 2024. It is a leading global producer of carbon black—the essential reinforcing agent for tires and rubber—alongside specialty blacks used as pigments in inks, plastics, and coatings, and it has expanded into water-treatment chemicals and battery materials (nano-silicon anode additives). Key brands include CarboNext, Royale Black, ECOZEN 6000, Bluemina, NuTone, Energia, and AQUACID.
