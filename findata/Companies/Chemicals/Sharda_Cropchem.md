# Sharda Cropchem

## Company Overview
Sharda Cropchem Limited, founded in 1987 and headquartered in Mumbai, India, is an asset-light, export-focused crop protection company. It exports a wide range of agrochemical and non-agro products worldwide, including formulations and generic active ingredients for fungicides, herbicides, insecticides, and disinfectants, catering to diverse crops and geographic markets. The company also supplies industrial chemicals, conveyor belts, and pharma intermediates.
