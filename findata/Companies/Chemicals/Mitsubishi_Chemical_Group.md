# Mitsubishi Chemical Group

## Company Overview
Mitsubishi Chemical Group Corporation (TSE: 4188; HQ Chiyoda, Japan) is a global chemical and materials conglomerate operating across specialty materials, MMA & derivatives, basic materials & polymers, and industrial gases. Founded 1933.
