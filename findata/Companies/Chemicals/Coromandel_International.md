# Coromandel International

## Company Overview
Coromandel International Limited (incorporated 1961; HQ Chennai) is a leading agri-inputs company and India's second-largest phosphatic-fertilizer player, operating as a subsidiary of EID Parry (Murugappa Group). It spans fertilizers (DAP, NPK, urea), crop protection, specialty nutrients, bio-products, and a rural retail network (Gromor Touch).

## The Chatter — Infosys Adani Power

**Resilient top line:** Revenue grew 15% despite delayed monsoon, the Middle East crisis and shortages of key raw materials (ammonia, sulphur); operating EBITDA dipped only ~3%. Diversification into crop protection and retail cushioned fertilizer-margin pressure.

**Subsidy revision is the margin catalyst:** Subsidy rates set before the Middle East crisis haven't kept pace with rising ammonia/sulphur costs; Coromandel has petitioned the Government of India for a revision, received favourably — a positive Q2 margin lever if granted.

**Backward integration paying off:** The captive acid plant ran at ~80% utilisation through the quarter, keeping production running while the broader industry faced raw-material shortages.

**Capex + retail expansion:** 7.5 lakh-tonne capacity expansion on track to commission by December (volumes available Q4); a new water-soluble MAP plant will secure specialty-nutrient feedstock. Rural retail (Gromor Touch) scaling from ~1,200 to 1,400–1,500 stores by year-end.

**Specialty nutrients hedge weak monsoon:** Weak-monsoon periods lift irrigation usage and specialty-nutrient demand (20–25% growth expected); farmers also trade down to lower-priced SSP / lower-phosphate grades when incomes are squeezed, which Coromandel captures.
