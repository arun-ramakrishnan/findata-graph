# Gujarat Fluorochemicals

## Company Overview
Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited (incorporated 1987; HQ Noida) is a leading manufacturer of fluorochemicals, fluoropolymers, and refrigerants (brands: INOFLON, FLUONOX, INOFLAR, INOLUB, Refron). It is aggressively expanding into battery materials for EVs and energy storage, positioning itself as a non-Chinese global supplier in the lithium-battery value chain.
