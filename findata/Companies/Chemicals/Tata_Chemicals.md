# Tata Chemicals

## Company Overview
Tata Chemicals Limited, founded in 1927 and headquartered in Mumbai, India, is a leading basic and specialty chemicals company and part of the Tata Group. It manufactures soda ash, sodium bicarbonate, cement (Tata Shudh), salts, specialty silica/zinc oxide, and agri-inputs (fertilizers, bio-stimulants, seeds), with exposure to high-growth end-markets such as solar glass, lithium-ion batteries, and energy storage. It serves customers across agriculture, construction, glass, pharma, soaps & detergents, and batteries.
