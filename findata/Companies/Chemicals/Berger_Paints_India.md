# Berger Paints India

## Company Overview
Berger Paints India Limited is a leading Indian paint and coatings company, established in 1923 and headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal. The company manufactures and sells paints for home, professional, and industrial users in India and internationally. Founded as British Paints (India) Limited, it was renamed to Berger Paints India Limited in December 1983. The company is part of the U.K. Paints India Private Limited group and employs approximately 4,760 people.

### Home & Decorative Paints
- **Interior Wall Coatings:** Designer finishes, interior emulsions, ceiling paints
- **Exterior Wall Coatings:** Exterior emulsions, textures, roof and floor coatings
- **Wood Finishes:** Enamels, wood stains, and protective coatings
- **Specialty Products:** Aerosol spray paints, DIY kits, and stickers

### Industrial & Protective Coatings
- **Automotive Coatings:** Vehicle paint systems and refinishing products
- **Fireproof & Protective Coatings:** Fire-resistant and corrosion protection
- **Floor Coatings:** Industrial and decorative floor systems
- **Powder Coatings:** Thermoset powder coating systems
- **Road Markings:** Traffic and safety marking solutions

### Construction & Specialty Products
- **Waterproofing Solutions:** Complete waterproofing systems
- **Construction Chemicals:** Adhesives, sealants, and building materials
- **Putty & Primers:** Surface preparation products
- **Bitumen Products:** Coal tar-based industrial products
