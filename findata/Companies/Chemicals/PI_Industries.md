# PI Industries

## Company Overview
PI Industries Limited is a leading agricultural sciences company founded in 1946 and headquartered in Gurugram, India. It specializes in custom synthesis and manufacturing (CSM) of agrochemicals, domestic branded formulations, and biologicals, and is transforming into a diversified life sciences platform with expansion into pharmaceuticals, electronic chemicals, and proprietary R&D. Key brands/crops served include soybean, rice, cotton, corn, and horticulture solutions.
