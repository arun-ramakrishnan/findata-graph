# Cosmo First

## Company Overview

Cosmo First Limited (formerly Cosmo Films) is a manufacturer of specialty films and pet care products, headquartered in New Delhi (incorporated 1976). Core business: BOPP, BOPET, CPP, PET-G films, synthetic paper, and capacitor films for packaging. Diversified into specialty chemicals and a pet care retail platform (Zigly brand) offering food, supplements, vet care, grooming, and pharmacy services.
