**Record Q4 profitability:** Q4 FY26 was the highest-ever profitability quarter in the company's history — EBITDA of ₹157 crores (up 29% YoY) at 26.2% margins, and PAT of ₹108 crores (up 39.1% YoY) — driven by operating leverage, better realization, and a stronger product mix.

**Sun control is the flagship:** Sun control films contribute ~50% of revenue and are the fastest-growing segment, supported by unique, hard-to-replicate manufacturing capabilities that competitors cannot match.

**MENA growth engine:** The Middle East & North Africa region (~$15M sales) is targeted to grow at a 25–30% CAGR to $20–22M this year, backed by a new subsidiary and a dedicated team.

**Export-heavy mix to persist:** Despite domestic growth, the 75–80% export / 20–25% domestic revenue split is expected to hold as overall expansion targets scale both channels proportionally.

**Inflation passed through:** Garware has largely passed raw-material inflation to customers, preserving margins, while its presence across 90+ countries diversifies geopolitical concentration risk.
