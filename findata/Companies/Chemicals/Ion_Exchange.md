# Ion Exchange

## Company Overview
Ion Exchange (India) Limited, incorporated in 1964 and headquartered in Mumbai, India, is a leading water and environmental management solutions provider. It operates across Engineering, Chemicals, and Consumer Products segments, offering raw/process/drinking water treatment, wastewater treatment and zero-liquid-discharge systems, ion-exchange resins, membranes, specialty water-treatment chemicals, and related equipment and O&M services. It serves industries such as power, pharma, chemicals, food & beverage, textiles, refineries, and municipalities.
