# Ion Exchange

## Company Overview
Ion Exchange (India) Limited, incorporated in 1964 and headquartered in Mumbai, India, is a leading water and environmental management solutions provider. It operates across Engineering, Chemicals, and Consumer Products segments, offering raw/process/drinking water treatment, wastewater treatment and zero-liquid-discharge systems, ion-exchange resins, membranes, specialty water-treatment chemicals, and related equipment and O&M services. It serves industries such as power, pharma, chemicals, food & beverage, textiles, refineries, and municipalities.

## Key Insights
- Order intake was muted in the last quarter due to aggressive market pricing and the company's selective bidding strategy — a few large orders were lost and some key jobs spilled into the next fiscal year.
- Management continues to be selective on engineering projects, choosing only jobs attractive to overall engineering margins.
- The UP Jal Nigam project has been progressing slowly due to funding/allocation challenges; a conservative reassessment reduced the balance order backlog, and receivables/collections on that contract have slowed, lifting overall debtors.
- Exposure to water scarcity and tightening environmental regulations underpins long-term demand, but near-term execution (especially on the UP project) remains a watch-item.
