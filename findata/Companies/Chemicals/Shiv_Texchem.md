# Shiv Texchem

## Company Overview
Shiv Texchem Limited, incorporated in 2005 and headquartered in Mumbai, imports and distributes specialty hydrocarbon-based chemicals in India. Its portfolio spans acetyls, alcohols, aromatics, nitriles, monomers, glycols, phenolics and ketones — sourced from 60+ global suppliers and supplied to 650+ Indian customers across paints & coatings, printing inks, specialty polymers, pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals and specialty industrial chemicals. The company's model is built on global sourcing, logistics execution and supply reliability, with value-added compliance-led handling capabilities.
