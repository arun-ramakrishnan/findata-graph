# Supreme Industries

## Company Overview

The Supreme Industries Limited is India's largest plastics processor, manufacturing and selling plastic products across four verticals: plastic piping systems, industrial products, packaging products, and furniture. Incorporated 1942; headquartered in Mumbai. ~6,081 employees. Products span uPVC/CPVC/HDPE pipes, material handling, flexible/protective packaging, cross-laminated films, and fabricated uPVC windows.
