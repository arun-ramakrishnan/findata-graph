# Kingfa Science & Technology (India)

## Company Overview

Kingfa Science & Technology (India) Limited (formerly Hydro S&S Industries; incorporated 1983; headquartered in Pune) is a subsidiary of Kingfa Science & Technology Co. Ltd, China. Manufactures reinforced polypropylene compounds, thermoplastic elastomers (HYPRENE brand), engineering plastics (ABS, HIPS, PA, PBT, PC and alloys), biodegradable nitrile gloves, PPE masks/coveralls, POM/LFT products, fiberglass honeycomb composite panels, and high-temperature nylon/LCP.
