# Finolex Industries

## Company Overview
Finolex Industries Limited (founded 1956; HQ Pune) is a backward-integrated manufacturer of PVC pipes & fittings and PVC resin in India. It produces pipes for agriculture, plumbing, and sanitation under the Finolex brand, and also manufactures its own key raw material (PVC resin), giving it cost control and margin stability against resin-price volatility.
