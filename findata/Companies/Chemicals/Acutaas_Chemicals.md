# Acutaas Chemicals Limited

## Company Overview
Acutaas Chemicals Limited (formerly Ami Organics Limited) is a specialty chemicals manufacturer based in Surat, India, focused on the research, development, and production of pharmaceutical intermediates and specialty chemicals. The company serves regulated and generic API markets across therapeutic areas such as anti-retroviral, anti-cancer, and anti-Parkinson's, and has expanded into battery chemicals, agrochemicals, coatings, electronics, and photoresist/electrolyte additives for semiconductor and lithium battery applications.
