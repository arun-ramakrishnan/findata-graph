# Pidilite Industries

Pidilite is India's leading manufacturer of adhesives and sealants, dominated by its flagship brand Fevicol. The company also maintains a significant presence in construction chemicals, art materials, and industrial resins.

Underlying profitability is better than reported numbers suggest.

**Pigments — US exposure:** The pigments division faced a sharp downturn this quarter due to high reliance on the American market (large component of pigment exports) and tariff discussions.

**Outlook — B2B stabilization:** Management believes the worst of the export decline is behind them as they adapt to new tariff rates and alternative supply strategies (Plan Bs built during prolonged negotiations). B2B impact in this and future quarters is expected to be minimal.
