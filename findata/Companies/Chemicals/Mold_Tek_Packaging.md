The company is directly impacted by regulatory changes in paint packaging requirements:

- **Upcoming Change**: Statutory obligation increasing to 30% by June/July
- **Impact**: This regulatory shift affects material sourcing and potentially costs for the industry
- **Industry Response**: Major players have already started adapting to the 20% requirement
