# Tatva Chintan Pharma Chem

## Company Overview
Tatva Chintan Pharma Chem Limited is a leading specialty chemicals manufacturer in India, producing structure directing agents (SDAs), phase transfer catalysts (PTCs), electrolyte salts for super-capacitor batteries, and pharmaceutical and agrochemical intermediates. The company holds India's largest manufacturer position for SDAs for zeolites and ranks among the top global producers of PTCs, providing key ingredients for customers' industrial processes.

### Market Position
- **SDA Leadership**: India's largest manufacturer of SDAs for zeolites
- **PTC Leadership**: Second position globally in PTC manufacturing
- **Top Global Producer**: Among top global PTC producers
- **Specialization**: Focus on high-value specialty chemicals
