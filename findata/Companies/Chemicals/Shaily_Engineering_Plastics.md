# Shaily Engineering Plastics

## Company Overview

Shaily Engineering Plastics Limited (incorporated 1980; headquartered in Vadodara) is a precision injection-molding specialist serving pharmaceutical/healthcare and consumer/industrial markets. Healthcare: specialty devices, inhalers, sprays/pumps, insulin injector pens, auto-injectors, CDMO services, and specialty pharma packaging. Consumer: kitchen/cooking devices, sheet steel furniture. Industrial: lighting, appliances, automotive components, and personal care (razors, pumps). Exports to ~40 countries. Stock split 5:1 in Nov 2023.
