# Captain Polyplast Limited

## Company Overview

Captain Polyplast Limited (CPL) is a leading manufacturer, exporter, and trading company specializing in top-quality HDPE Pipes and Irrigation Equipment such as Drip and Sprinkler Irrigation Systems, various Filters, Fittings, Valves, and more.

### Core Business Segments

- **Drip Irrigation:** Emitting pipes, inline/online emitters, lateral pipes
- **Sprinkler Irrigation:** Sprinkler pipes, mini sprinklers, sprinkler nozzles
- **HDPE Pipes:** Pipes and fittings for agricultural and industrial use
- **Solar Pump Enabler:** Irrigation infrastructure tied to PM-KUSUM solar pump adoption
