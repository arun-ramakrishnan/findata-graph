## The Chatter — Solar Ind PhonePe

**Transformational Omnia acquisition ($1.35 bn):** Solar South Africa's proposed acquisition of Omnia Holdings brings the BME brand (open-cast mining, bulk explosives, electronic detonation systems, digital blasting, mining chemicals) plus Omnia's agriculture segment (Nutriology science-based crop nutrition / agribiosolutions) — "a transformational milestone in Solar Group's strategic ambition to become a leading global explosives and mining solutions provider" (MD & CEO Manish Nuwal).

**Backward integration into SADC raw materials:** Omnia's agriculture segment operates the region's largest, most reliable nitric acid and ammonium nitrate plants; a new 5,000-ton ammonium-nitrate storage tank doubles storage — securing raw-material supply and strengthening long-term cost competitiveness across the explosives value chain.

**No-equity funding:** The $1.35 bn deal is funded entirely by internal accruals and debt — no dilution in the parent or any subsidiary; total debt (acquisition + regular) peaks at ~₹10–11,000 cr by FY28 with net debt/EBITDA staying below 2x.

**Footprint 11 → 25 manufacturing countries, 100+ distribution countries:** Combining BME's network with Solar's gives distribution presence across 100+ countries; management plans to push Solar's packaged explosives (world's largest producer) and initiating systems through BME's channels — with higher Indian exports as a stated goal.

> "Our distribution presence will increase to more than 100 countries. Our manufacturing presence, which is currently in 11 countries, will expand to 25 countries."
> — Manish Nuwal, MD & CEO, Solar Industries
