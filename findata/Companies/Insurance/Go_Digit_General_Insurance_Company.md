# Go Digit General Insurance Company

## Company Overview

Go Digit General Insurance Limited is a digital-first general insurance provider in India, focusing on motor, health, and commercial lines. The company uses a technology-driven approach to streamline underwriting and claims processing while maintaining a lean operating structure.

## The Chatter — Infosys Adani Power

**Intentionally shrinking motor share:** Digit is cutting market share in private-car own-damage and non-new cars where the combination of commission and premium rates no longer justifies the volume — a deliberate move to avoid underpriced, lossmaking business.

**Walking away from commercial vehicles:** Gave up commercial vehicle (third-party) business because claim costs are rising while government-regulated tariffs have been stagnant for ~5 years; management questions how peers can grow aggressively in a segment with deteriorating economics.

**Equity allocation tripled:** Investment equity allocation raised from 3.5% (IPO) to ~9%+ of AUM under strict capital-allocation discipline — a more active investment strategy to grow the asset base.

**Lower reliance on capital gains:** Capital-gains dependence is ~20% (vs ~40% for top players) over 3 years, so core insurance profits are more durable through market downturns.

**Claims efficiency via early settlement:** 83% of 36,000 TP claims settled via compromise rather than dragged through court — saving on legal costs and interest.

> "The reduction in motor market share is primarily due to the corrective actions we have taken, essentially in private cars, both in the standalone own damage section, about which [we] have spoken in the past, and also non-new cars, where we feel the combination of commission and premium rates do not justify writing business in the same volume as we were doing earlier."
> — Kamesh Goyal, Chairman
