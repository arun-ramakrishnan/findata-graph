# Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited

## Company Overview

Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited was incorporated in 2005 and is headquartered in Chennai. It is the largest retail health insurer in India, offering a comprehensive range of health, personal accident, and travel insurance products to individuals, families, groups, and senior citizens.
