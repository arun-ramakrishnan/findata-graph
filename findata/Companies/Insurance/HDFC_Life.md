# HDFC Life Insurance

## Overview
HDFC Life Insurance Company Ltd. is a leading Indian life insurance company that provides a wide range of long-term individual and group insurance solutions. Its products include protection, pension, savings, investment, annuity, and health plans. The company offers these products through a variety of channels, such as agents, brokers, banks, and online platforms.

## Key Business Updates

### GST Impact Mitigation Strategy
Five-pronged strategy to mitigate GST impact:
1. **Distributor Commission Adjustments**: Pragmatic renegotiation with distributors based on changed economics
2. **Vendor Renegotiations**: Conversations with vendors on outsourcing costs, technology costs, and more (non-commission costs are 50% of input tax)
3. **Product Mix Optimization**: Joint focus with distributors on product mix optimization
4. **New Product Launches**: Launching new market-friendly products to drive growth
5. **Volume Growth**: Focus on overall volume growth to offset margin pressure
