# The New India Assurance

The New India Assurance Company is India's largest non-life insurer with a significant global footprint across 24 countries and a century-long legacy. The company maintains leadership in the domestic market through a diversified portfolio including health, motor, fire, and marine insurance.

The Chatter (Reliance, PayTM, Nykaa & More)

**Portfolio recalibration:** The company is intentionally walking away from low-margin corporate contracts to prioritize more profitable retail and small business segments — improving book quality and reducing capital consumption.

> "Over the past few Quarters, we have taken deliberate steps to recalibrate our portfolio by exiting or restructuring select large corporate accounts where pricing did not adequately compensate for risk or capital consumption. This has been offset by an increased focus on Retail, SME and better quality risk."
> — Girija Subramanian, Chairman cum Managing Director

**Wage-related provisions:** Underwriting results were impacted by provisions for wage arrears and retirement benefits — Rs.759 crore in Q3 FY26 and Rs.1,877 crore for 9M FY26. Despite these, PAT rose to Rs.372 crore (vs Rs.353 crore YoY). An additional Rs.700-800 crore provision for family pension revision (FPS) is expected in Q4 once the government notifies it.

**Health insurance margin actions:** Raising prices on group medical covers (GMCs) or exiting unprofitable accounts; significantly increasing mandatory inspections (30% → 50%) and anti-fraud monitoring to lower the claims ratio.

**Motor insurance:** Growth is denting as the company aggressively exits loss-making accounts and risky segments — a necessary step to restore motor portfolio profitability.

**New lines — parametric insurance:** Diversifying beyond the highly competitive motor and health markets into niche areas like parametric insurance (payouts based on weather-event thresholds), requiring collaboration with FinTech/InsureTech companies.
