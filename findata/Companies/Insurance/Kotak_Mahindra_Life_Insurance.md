# Kotak Mahindra Life Insurance

## Overview
Kotak Mahindra Life Insurance is a prominent private sector life insurance company in India, offering a comprehensive range of life insurance products including term insurance, whole life insurance, endowment plans, and unit-linked insurance plans (ULIPs). Part of the Kotak Mahindra Group.

### Insurance Products
- **Term Insurance**: Pure protection products with death benefit coverage

- **Annuity Products**: Pension and retirement products
