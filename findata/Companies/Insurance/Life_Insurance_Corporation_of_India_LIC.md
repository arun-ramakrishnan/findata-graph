# Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC)

## Company Overview
Life Insurance Corporation of India is the largest insurance company in India, offering life insurance, health insurance, and investment products. It is a government-owned corporation serving millions of customers across India.

**Non-par APE surging:** Non-participating APE grew 43.78% YoY (from ₹10,581 cr to ₹15,210 cr), validating the strategy to rebalance the mix toward higher-margin non-par products.

**Bancassurance milestone crossed:** LIC crossed ₹5,000 cr through bancassurance and alternate channels for the first time since listing, reducing over-reliance on the agency network.

**Bima Sakhi rural push:** 1,43,924 Bima Sakhis appointed across 59% of India's 2,44,876 Gram Panchayats as of March 31, 2026, advancing the goal of one Bima Sakhi per Gram Panchayat.

**Non-par drives VNB concentration:** Individual non-par business contributes 22.7% of APE but ~53% of value of new business — a small slice of sales now generating the majority of new-business value.

**Quality over volume:** LIC is consciously moving away from low-ticket microfinance sales toward larger ticket sizes to improve persistence and build a more stable, profitable book.
