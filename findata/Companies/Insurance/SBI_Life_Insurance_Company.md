# SBI Life Insurance Company Limited

## Company Overview

SBI Life Insurance Company Limited, incorporated in 2000 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a subsidiary of State Bank of India. The company offers individual and group life insurance products spanning participating, non-participating, pension, group gratuity, leave encashment, superannuation, immediate annuity, unit-linked, variable insurance, health, and micro insurance lines. It also provides accident/disability, level term, and critical illness riders, alongside online savings, protection, retirement, and child plans. Distribution is multi-channel, using agents, brokers, corporate agents, bancassurance, and insurance facilitators.
