## The Chatter — Jio Financial, Wipro, Polycab, Piramal & More

**Strong demand backdrop:** Insurance industry benefiting from robust macroeconomic indicators; ICICI Lombard continues to pursue profitable growth over scale.

**Supreme Court motor third-party impact:** Court judgment raised industry motor third-party loss ratios by 12–15%; IL took a ₹165 crore charge in Q1 (combined ratio impact ~2.8%), fully recognised upfront per conservative reserving philosophy. Industry case for motor third-party price revision now imminent.

**Large fire losses:** Couple of large fire claims hit combined ratio by ~1% in Q1; underlying combined ratio otherwise flat.

**Fire market pricing discipline returning:** Industry degrowth improved from 27% in Q1 to 22% in June (IL's own from 32% to 18%) — pricing irrationalisation cannot persist long-term.

**Retail health within target range:** Retail health indemnity loss ratio at 66% in Q1 (vs 74% in Q1 last year), within 65–70% target band.

**Wide outperformance vs industry motor:** Industry motor combined ratios worsened from 123% to 128%; IL held at 105–106% — gap has further widened.

> "Industry combined ratios in motor increased from 123% last year to 128% this year, whereas for us it broadly remained within the range of 105% to 106%. The gap between ICICI Lombard and the industry has further widened."
> — Gopal Balachandran, Chief Financial Officer
