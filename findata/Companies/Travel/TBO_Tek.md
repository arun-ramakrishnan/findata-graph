# TBO Tek

## Company Overview
TBO Tek Limited (incorporated 2006, HQ Gurugram) operates a global B2B travel-distribution platform that connects travel suppliers (airlines, hotels, car rentals, transfers, cruises, insurance, rail) with travel buyers (agencies, OTAs, tour operators). Its technology platform simplifies discovery, booking, and transaction of global travel inventory, with the company positioning itself in the premium outbound travel space.
