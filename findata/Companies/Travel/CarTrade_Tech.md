# CarTrade Tech

## Company Overview
CarTrade Tech Limited, incorporated in 2000 and headquartered in Navi Mumbai, operates a multi-channel online automotive platform connecting buyers and sellers of new and used vehicles. Its brands — CarWale, BikeWale, CarTrade, Shriram Automall, and OLX India — span new/used car research, dealer & OEM solutions, vehicle auctions, inspection/valuation, and recommerce across cars, bikes, jobs, and household goods.
