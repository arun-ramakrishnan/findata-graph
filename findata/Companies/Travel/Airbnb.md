# Airbnb Inc. | International

Airbnb Inc. is an online marketplace for arranging or offering lodging, primarily homestays, or tourism experiences. The company has been focusing on expanding its service offerings and leveraging AI to improve customer experience.

## Company Overview
Airbnb operates across more than 100,000 cities worldwide, with a strategic focus on specific markets like Paris and Los Angeles for product-market fit before scaling. The company is expanding its offerings beyond accommodation to include services and experiences.

## Key Product Launches and Features

### Reserve Now Pay Later
A new payment feature launched in Q3 that offers flexible payment options:
- Available to US customers booking domestic trips with flexible or moderate cancellation policies
- Achieved 70% adoption among eligible users
- Despite higher cancellations, resulted in net booking lift
- Addresses customer demand for flexible payment options

### AI Customer Support
Airbnb has rolled out an AI customer service assistant with advanced capabilities:
- 15% reduction in human agent contacts in the US
- Smarter responses that reference reservation details
- Custom UI for direct actions like cancelling or changing dates
- Rich user interface modules built directly into messaging platform
- Planned expansion to over 50 languages in 2026

## Strategic Focus Areas

### Services & Experiences
- Expected to take 3-5 years to become material revenue contributor
- Following a city-by-city scaling playbook from initial markets (Paris, LA)
- Early traction signs in pilot markets
- CEO Brian Chesky sees strong potential in this expansion

### Historical Scaling Pattern
The company draws on its founding experience, noting that in its first two years, most revenue came from New York City, suggesting a successful model for market-by-market expansion.

## AI Strategy and Market Positioning

### Contrarian AI Thesis
CEO Brian Chesky positions Airbnb as a beneficiary of AI proliferation based on a contrarian thesis:
- As online content becomes increasingly artificial with AI, consumers will yearn for authentic, real-world experiences
- Younger generations (Gen Z) surrounded by AI-generated content will especially seek real connections
- Airbnb represents "the best way to experience the magic of the real world"
- "A bet on Airbnb is a bet on AI" - as AI proliferates, demand for real connections increases

## Management Insights

### On Services Expansion Timeline
"As far as when this provides meaningful income or revenue for the company, it's going to be a few years for this to happen. We're really focused on getting a few markets right... So it's going to take three to five years, I think, for service experiences to become a material part of our business. But I'm very, very bullish on them." - Brian Chesky, CEO

### On AI Customer Service Impact
"we initially launched this in the United States where it's already reduced people's need to contact a human agent by 15%. So now we're going to expand it to more countries in more languages and we expect this to be in over 50 languages next year." - Brian Chesky, CEO

### On AI and Real-World Connection
"I think what makes our approach different is that we're not just using AI to pull people deeper into the screens. We're using it to get them off their phones and help them connect the real world. Because I believe in the age of AI, more and more, what's on a screen will be artificial. You won't know if it's real or not. In the age of AI, people are going to increasingly want what's real. And what's real is in real life. They're going to create real experiences with real people in the real world." - Brian Chesky, CEO

### On Reserve Now Pay Later
"about 70% of people that we offer reserve now pay later take us up on that offering. Now to your question of cancellations, we obviously tested this product pretty extensively before launching it in the US to ensure that the benefit of the incremental lift in those bookings from the payment offering was not more than offset by increased cancellations. So yes, there are increased cancellations but we're highly confident that the net impact of the product is a lift to net bookings." - Ellie Murr, CFO
