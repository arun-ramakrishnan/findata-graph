## Company Overview

### Key Data Points & Signals (Investor Presentation)
- **Brand diversification**: Taj 69% of revenue; upper-upscale (Vivanta, Gateway) ~10%; new businesses 21%.
- **Geographic diversification**: domestic business cities 53%; international 22%; domestic leisure 15%; other domestic 10%.
- **Asset-light model**: of 32,300 operational keys — 55% managed, 27% owned/leased, 13% capital-light leases, 5% JVs → 68% capital-light overall.
- **Pipeline even more asset-light**: 30,200 keys under development, 94% asset-light (80% managed, 14% capital-light leases) — supports RoCE expansion without heavy capital tie-up.
- **Room-revenue mix (9M FY26)**: transient 57%, MICE 23%, corporate 9%, leisure 6%, long-stay 3%, crew 2%.
- **Channel mix**: direct via hotel reservation system 47%; own website 18% (+170 bps YoY); OTAs/ADS 22% — rising direct share lowers commissions and aids margins.

## The Chatter — Titan, Dixon, JSW Steel, Cipla & More

**Asset-light model at scale:** 68% of the operating portfolio and 93% of the pipeline are under management or asset-light formats, enabling disciplined expansion with superior returns; EBITDA margin held at 35%.

**Capex commitment:** ₹1,000–1,200 crore of annual investment planned to strengthen existing competitive advantages and build new ones, with over ₹500 crore deployed across four strategic acquisitions in high-growth adjacencies.

**Ginger & ama outperformance:** The flagship Ginger hotel at Mumbai Airport crossed ₹100 crore in revenue with an industry-leading 56% EBITDA margin; Qmin expanded to 100+ outlets and ama crossed 375 bungalows (85 villas signed during the year).

**Mid-scale scale-up:** Targeting a Ginger portfolio of ~250 hotels (under development or in operation) by end of FY27 to dominate the mid-scale segment.

> "Our capital-light strategy continues to be a defining competitive advantage with 68% of our operating portfolio and 93% of our pipeline under management or asset-light format. This enables disciplined expansion with superior returns."
> — Puneet Chhatwal, Managing Director and CEO
