# SAMHI Hotels

## Company Overview

SAMHI Hotels is a leading hotel ownership and asset management platform in India with a portfolio focused on major urban consumption centers, partnering with global hospitality brands like Marriott, Hyatt, and IHG to operate upscale and mid-scale hotels.
