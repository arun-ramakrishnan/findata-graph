## The Chatter — Bajaj Finserv Zydus BEL

**Two-speed quarter:** Strong recovery in the India portfolio, offset by West Asia conflict exposure concentrated in Desert Adventures, DEI and parts of long-haul outbound — those businesses are not where management wants them but are clearly better than three months ago.

**Education forex +33% against a -34% industry:** RBI data (April-August 2026) shows education outward remittances down 34% and travel-related forex down 1%, yet Thomas Cook's education forex grew 33% — distribution strength, NBFC relationships, overseas counsellor ties and the Study Buddy forex card winning share in a shrinking market.

**Sterling's biggest-ever expansion quarter:** 7 resorts opened, taking the portfolio to 83 properties and past 4,000 keys; room nights sold +~23%, occupancy +~400 bps, and average room rates in the owned/leased portfolio +~12% — scaling without sacrificing revenue productivity.

**GST/ITC comparison bar on Sterling:** Input tax credit is no longer available on rooms below ₹7,500 (until Sept 23, 2025 it was); in a seasonally weak quarter rates average closer to ₹6,500, so the premiumisation still reads as a profit headwind on comparison.

> "RBI data for April to August 2026 shows that outward remittances under the education category declined by 34%, while travel-related foreign exchange remittances declined by 1%. Against this backdrop, our education forex business grew by 33%."
> — Mahesh Iyer, Managing Director & CEO
