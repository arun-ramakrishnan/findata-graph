# EaseMyTrip (Easy Trip Planners) Limited

## Company Overview

EaseMyTrip (Easy Trip Planners) is an Indian online travel company providing comprehensive travel booking and tourism services through digital platforms. The company offers flights, hotels, holiday packages, and other travel-related services to both domestic and international travelers. Founded in 2008, EaseMyTrip has grown to become one of India's leading online travel agencies (OTAs).
