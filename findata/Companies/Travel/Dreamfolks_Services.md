# Dreamfolks Services

## Company Overview
Dreamfolks Services (NSE: DREAMFOLKS.NS; HQ Gurugram; incorporated 2008) is India's leading airport-services platform, providing lounge access, meet-and-assist, airport transfers, food & beverages, door-step baggage, and spa/wellness services. It operates a B2B2C model aggregating services for credit-card issuers and airlines.
