# Accelya Solutions India Limited

## Company Overview

Accelya Solutions India Limited, founded in 1976 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a subsidiary of Accelya Holding World S.L.U. The company provides software solutions for the airline, cargo, and travel industries. It employs approximately 2,500 people and serves the global travel ecosystem with billing, settlement, and operational platforms.
