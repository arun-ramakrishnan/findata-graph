# Hotel Leelaventure Limited

## Overview
Hotel Leelaventure Limited (HLV) is primarily engaged in the business of owning, operating & managing hotels, palaces and resorts under The Leela brand. The company offers luxury hospitality experiences across India with a focus on premium properties and personalized service.

### First Major International Expansion
- **Co-Investment Model**: Announcing Leela's first major international expansion using asset-light co-investment model with Brookfield
- **Investment Details**: For 25% stake, Leela will acquire upfront capital investment of ~$49 million (₹437 crores)
- **Brookfield Partnership**: Brookfield acquiring remaining 75% stake
- **Valuation**: Transaction at attractive entry multiple of 12.8x calendar year 2025 EBITDA
- **Payback Period**: Equity payback for Leela in 2-3 years through sale of branded residences
