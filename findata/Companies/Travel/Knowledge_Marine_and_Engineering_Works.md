# Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Ltd.

## Company Overview

Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Ltd. is a dredging and marine engineering company based in India. The company also engages in commercial shipbuilding and chartering of various vessels, aiming for a prominent industry position with expanding operations and fleet strength. The company is a key beneficiary of India's push to develop inland waterways and modernize port infrastructure.

*Dredging is the process of removing sand, silt, or debris from the bottom of rivers, ports, or seabeds. It is done to deepen waterways, prevent flooding, or create land for construction.*

### Core Business Segments

- **Dredging:** Port, river, and dam dredging services with owned fleet of cutter suction dredgers
- **Commercial Shipbuilding:** Building workboats, accommodation boats, survey boats, and cutter suction dredgers (notably for Inland Waterways Authority of India)
- **Chartering:** Long-term chartering of green tugs and port ancillary craft
- **Port Ancillary Craft:** Fleet of vessels deployed across 12 major Indian ports

## Industry Context: Engineering & Capital Goods Sector

Knowledge Marine operates within the [[Engineering_Capital_Goods]] sector, specifically in dredging and marine engineering:
