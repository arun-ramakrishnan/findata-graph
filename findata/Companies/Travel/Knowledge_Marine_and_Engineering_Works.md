# Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Ltd.

## Company Overview

Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Ltd. is a dredging and marine engineering company based in India. The company also engages in commercial shipbuilding and chartering of various vessels, aiming for a prominent industry position with expanding operations and fleet strength. The company is a key beneficiary of India's push to develop inland waterways and modernize port infrastructure.

*Dredging is the process of removing sand, silt, or debris from the bottom of rivers, ports, or seabeds. It is done to deepen waterways, prevent flooding, or create land for construction.*

### Core Business Segments

- **Dredging:** Port, river, and dam dredging services with owned fleet of cutter suction dredgers
- **Commercial Shipbuilding:** Building workboats, accommodation boats, survey boats, and cutter suction dredgers (notably for Inland Waterways Authority of India)
- **Chartering:** Long-term chartering of green tugs and port ancillary craft
- **Port Ancillary Craft:** Fleet of vessels deployed across 12 major Indian ports

## Management Insights

### Concall Highlights (Q3 FY26)

**Entry into Commercial Shipbuilding:**
> "We entered the commercial shipbuilding segment, securing orders worth over 230 crore from the Inland Waterways Authority of India for the supply of workboats, accommodation boats, survey boats, and cutter suction dredgers to be executed over a period of 2 years."
> - Mrs. Kanak Kewalramani, Director and Chief Financial Officer

**Long-Term Green Tug Orders:**
> "We received significant long-term orders from Visakhapatnam Port and VOC Port for construction and chartering of 60-ton bollard pull green tugs with a total contract value of approximately 700 crores to be executed over a period of 15 years."
> - Mrs. Kanak Kewalramani, Director and Chief Financial Officer

**Capital Raise:**
> "To support our growth journey, we successfully raised 285 crores through a preferential issue of equity shares and warrants. This reinforces investor confidence in our long-term vision. The capital will primarily be deployed towards fleet expansion and for projects at hand and in the pipeline."
> - Mrs. Kanak Kewalramani, Director and Chief Financial Officer

**Tonnage Tax Benefit:**
> "Our guidance is that it will be less than 1% of the total turnover. That is what we believe the total tax implication will be. Regarding the performance, we have moved toward higher revenue and higher profit margins, so this can be considered the benchmark going forward."
> - Sujay, CEO

**Fleet Utilization:**
> "It is 100% utilized; none of our equipment is idle as of today. Dredgers are deployed for between 270 to 300 days out of 365. Regarding port ancillary craft, they are utilized for full 365 days."
> - Sujay, CEO

**Inland Waterway Demand:**
> "There is sufficient demand for Knowledge Marine, DCI, and several other competitors because, as seen in the budget, approximately 20 more national waterways are required to be made navigable. This demand is huge. Over the next 5 years, inland waterway dredging demand will increase multifold."
> - Sujay, CEO

**Differentiation from DCI:**
> "Their equipment is for ports and is not directly overlapping our asset base. The cutter suction dredger DCI plans to invest in is a 3,000-kilowatt cutter with a draft in excess of [several] meters. Rivers require a draft of 2.5 to 3 meters. Their vessel cannot enter rivers; it is meant for rock dredging or hard strata dredging in ports."
> - Sujay, CEO

**Market Share Headroom:**
> "In dredging, across ports, rivers, and dams, we are currently at less than 2% market share. In the small craft business across 12 major ports, there are between 550 to 600 vessels required. We presently have fewer than 25 vessels deployed."
> - Sujay, CEO

**Shipbuilding Strategy Mix:**
> "We have a shipbuilding contract from the Inland Waterways Authority of India for 240 crores to build and supply vessels. The green tugs are being built for our own fleet. It is a mix; we are building for supply to the government and building for our own operating company."
> - Sujay, CEO

**Shipyard Investment Outlook:**
> "We are going to invest close to 100 crores in the shipyard via a mix of debt and equity. It will create a facility for building tugs and smaller vessels. We believe we can reach a top-line of between 500 to 700 crores with this facility three years down the line."
> - Sujay, CEO
