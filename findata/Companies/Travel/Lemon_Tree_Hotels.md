## Key Business Updates

### Corporate Reorganization
- Creating two separate, well-funded entities:
  - **Lemon Tree**: asset-light management company (debt-free, high-margin, high-ROE)
  - **Fleur Hotels**: asset-heavy hotel ownership and growth platform
- Timeline: 12-15 months for completion

### Strategic Positioning
- Demerger aims to cater to distinct investor types:
  - Asset-heavy (Fleur): development risk, inflation-indexed annuity returns
  - Asset-light (Lemon Tree): brand management and growth with little capital
- Fleur may operate as multi-brand platform, not exclusively tied to Lemon Tree

### Growth Targets
- Aim to reach 30,000-40,000 rooms in 3-4 years
- Become largest operator with significant fee income
- Board to define shareholder reward policy as company becomes asset-light

### Brand Strategy
- Premium Aurika brand: strategic placement in high-value resorts/locations
- Avoiding tier-2 or tier-3 towns for Aurika
- Capital deployment based on market depth and consumer willingness to pay

### Cost Management
- Target: 50% power consumption from renewable energy sources
- Implementing technological solutions for long-term cost efficiency
