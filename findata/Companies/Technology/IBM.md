## The Chatter — IBM Tata Tech

**Three no-regret AI zones today:** software/coding (30-40% productivity gains within a year), customer experience and support, and core enterprise operations (procurement, accounting, AP/AR, HR) — the last needs business-process re-engineering most organisations are only beginning.
**Workforce math:** ~60% of the world is frontline roles that change only at the margins (5-10%); of the 40% white-collar segment, roughly half — about 20% of the total workforce — could be tackled by AI agents within five years; net employment ultimately rises (writing, maintaining, verifying a billion agents) but the transition needs policy paths for the ~10% caught in the shift.
**Value migrates to the apps layer:** the 1980-90 PC cycle rhymes — chips, then systems, then OS/productivity, then 100,000 applications whose value dwarfed every layer beneath; foundation models will commoditise like operating systems before enterprise/national applications capture the real value.
**Open-source shift:** IBM expects ~80% of AI activity to move to open-source models, capping proprietary-lab economics.

> "That leaves the remaining 40% in white-collar roles. I would not be surprised if roughly half of that white-collar segment—or about 20% of the total workforce—is tackled by AI agents in the next five years."
> — Arvind Krishna, Chairman & CEO, IBM
