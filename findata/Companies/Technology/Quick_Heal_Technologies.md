# Quick Heal Technologies Limited

## Company Overview

Quick Heal Technologies Limited was incorporated in 1995 and is headquartered in Pune. It is a cybersecurity company providing security software for consumers, small businesses, government, and corporate customers. Consumer offerings are marketed under the **Quick Heal** brand, and enterprise products under the **Seqrite** brand, complemented by AntiFraud.AI for fraud detection.
