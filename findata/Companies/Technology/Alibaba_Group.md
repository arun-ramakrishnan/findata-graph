# Alibaba Group

## Overview

Alibaba Group is a massive Chinese technology conglomerate operating as a global leader in e-commerce, cloud computing, digital media, and fintech services. The company follows a B2B2C business model, serving businesses and consumers through multiple platforms. Alibaba's flagship platform Alibaba.com is the world's leading global B2B marketplace, connecting businesses worldwide to wholesale manufacturers for bulk sourcing. The company has expanded into cloud computing (Aliyun), digital media (Youku), artificial intelligence, and fintech, creating a comprehensive ecosystem of technology services.
