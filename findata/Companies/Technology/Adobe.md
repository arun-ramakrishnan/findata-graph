# Adobe

## Company Overview

Adobe Inc. (NASDAQ: ADBE) is a global software company headquartered in San Jose, California, known for its Creative Cloud suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects), the Acrobat/PDF document platform, and the Experience Cloud digital-marketing stack. Founded in 1982, Adobe is one of the largest SaaS/software companies in the world.
