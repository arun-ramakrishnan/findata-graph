# Ceinsys Tech

## Company Overview

Ceinsys Tech (brand "CS TECH AI") is a specialist in GIS and an engineering services solutions provider, serving clients across corporate and government segments to improve engineering efficiency, support global footprint, and enhance competitiveness. It combines **geospatial intelligence** (water, energy, transportation, oil & gas, telecom) with **mobility engineering** (interiors, structures, electrical systems across PV/CV/off-highway/2-3W). Listed on NSE/BSE.

- **Brands:** CS TECH AI; mobility JV AllyGram (70% CS TECH AI / 30% Grammer AG)
- **Verticals:** Geospatial; Mobility; Emerging Tech (digital twins, AI/ML, LiDAR, UAV photogrammetry, connected-vehicle IoT)
