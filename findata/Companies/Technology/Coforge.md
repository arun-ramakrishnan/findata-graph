## Sector Overview

The Software Services sector in India encompasses IT/ITES services including application development, maintenance, cloud computing, business process outsourcing, and digital engineering. India is a global leader in software exports with a market size exceeding $200 billion, serving clients across 100+ countries.

- **Global Leadership**: India supplies over 50% of global IT services demand
- **Digital Transformation**: Rapid adoption of cloud, AI, and digital technologies
