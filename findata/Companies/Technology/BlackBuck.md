# BlackBuck

BlackBuck Limited (formerly Zinka Logistics Solutions Limited) is India's largest digital platform for truck operators. It offers digital payments for tolling and fueling, driver/fleet monitoring via telematics, a load-finding marketplace, and financing for used-vehicle purchases. Headquartered in Bengaluru.
