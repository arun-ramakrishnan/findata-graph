# InfoBeans Technologies

## Company Overview
InfoBeans Technologies Limited, founded in 2000 and headquartered in Indore, designs, builds, and manages digital applications serving the US, UAE, Germany, India, and other markets. It offers AI/gen-AI, enterprise application, cloud, application modernization, ServiceNow, Salesforce, and SLA-based managed services for clients across BFSI, manufacturing, standards organizations, and technology.
