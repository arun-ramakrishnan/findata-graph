# Accenture plc
## Company Overview
Accenture plc is a global professional services firm specializing in IT consulting, management consulting, technology transformation, and outsourcing. Headquartered in Dublin, Ireland and listed on NYSE (ACN), it leverages AI, cloud computing, and cybersecurity — organized under "Reinvention Services" — to help businesses digitize operations across 40+ industries.
