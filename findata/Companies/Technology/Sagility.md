# Sagility

## Company Overview

Sagility is a pure-play, healthcare-focused business process management (BPM) / technology-enabled services provider serving the U.S. healthcare industry. Clients include **Payers** (U.S. health insurers) and **Providers** (hospitals, physicians, medical-device companies). It offers claims cost containment, member/provider engagement, enrolment, payment integrity, clinical/case management, revenue cycle management, and analytics, with delivery across India, Jamaica, the Philippines, the U.S., and Colombia. Formerly Sagility India Limited; renamed Sagility Limited in Aug 2025; subsidiary of Sagility B.V.

- **Incorporated:** 2021 | **HQ:** Bengaluru, India
- **Parent:** Sagility B.V.
- **MD & Group CEO:** Ramesh Gopalan | **Employees:** ~46,860
- **Delivery geographies:** India, Jamaica, Philippines, U.S., Colombia
