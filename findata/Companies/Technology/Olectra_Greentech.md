# Olectra Greentech

## Company Overview

Olectra Greentech Limited (formerly Goldstone Infratech) is a leading Indian manufacturer of electric buses and composite polymer insulators. The company is at the forefront of India's EV bus transition with a technology partnership from BYD.

## Key Insights (The Chatter — A Strange Quarter & Moves and Motives)

- Olectra's revenue CAGR of 63% significantly outpaces the Indian EV bus market's 35% CAGR (2019–2025); market expected to grow at ~40% CAGR going forward
- Highest-ever quarterly deliveries of 375 EVs in Q2 (including 25 electric tippers); Q2 revenue up 25% YoY driven by higher deliveries
- First in India to homologate blade battery technology (cell-to-pack concept); BYD partnership extended through December 2030; blade battery ~30% more efficient in weight and mileage
- India's bus population is ~1.8 million with less than 1% currently electrified — massive untapped market including the private bus segment
- Targeting 2,000 EV deliveries in FY26; depot infrastructure readiness is the key bottleneck (payments start only when buses run), not manufacturing capacity
- Insulator division growing strongly: H1 revenue ~₹130 crore vs ₹180 crore full-year prior; good order book
- Battery cells, packs, BMS, and motor components continue to be imported from BYD until a domestic ecosystem develops; no geopolitical sourcing concerns expected
