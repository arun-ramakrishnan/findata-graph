# Shiprocket

## Company Overview
E-commerce enablement platform (inc. 2011) providing the infrastructure layer for off-marketplace/D2C commerce: domestic and cross-border shipping aggregation, fulfilment, checkout, payments and merchant marketing solutions. FY26 scale: over ₹32,000 cr GMV powered, 20 cr transactions, ~15 cr consumers served across lakhs of MSME sellers. Majority of GMV comes from Tier-2/3 cities.

## The Chatter — HUL Fortis Shiprocket

**Operating leverage in the core:** Core shipping business EBITDA margin doubled from ~6% (three years ago) to ~12% in FY26 — incremental transactions are margin-accretive once overheads are covered.

**Data is the moat, not cheaper shipping:** Routing across 42 courier partners guided by 70+ cr historical shipment data points and a predictive routing model; a connected stack (checkout, shipping, RTO, advertising, consumer data) beats point software — 93% of checkouts get address auto-fill, and RTO fraud-scoring runs on consumer/address data.

**Martech thesis — delivered ROAS:** AI Ads generates ad creatives in minutes (static banners, short-form video, 360° product views) personalised from connected ad-account and category performance; because Shiprocket sits on actual purchase, catalog, consumer and inventory data, it sells a net return-on-ad-spend model rather than click optimisation (CFO Tanmay Kumar).

**Quick-commerce infrastructure for small brands:** Aggregates D2C purchase orders into dark-store slots with automatic pickup/delivery-slot booking — letting smaller brands participate in quick-commerce growth without enterprise supply chains.

**Q3 is counter-seasonal:** During the festive e-commerce peak, larger merchants pull marketing spend and inventory toward marketplaces — making Q3 the weakest quarter for Shiprocket's direct-commerce business, unlike broader e-commerce trends.

**Transactions first, monetisation later:** Focus is adding orders — each transaction creates margin pools across shipping, payments, checkout and advertising (~25–30% of the ₹30,000 cr GMV already flows to marketing); entry point can be any product, including marketing-first adoption.

> "Shiprocket is becoming the one-stop shop for MSMEs to run and digitize their business end-to-end. As of FY26, we powered over 32,000 crores GMV and 20 crores in transactions, and we served about 15 crore consumers."
> — Sahil Goyal, MD & CEO, Shiprocket
