# Figma

## Company Overview
Figma, Inc. is a San Francisco-based design and prototyping software company founded in 2012 by Dylan Field and Evan Wallace. The company provides a cloud-based design and prototyping platform that enables real-time collaboration for digital product design, including websites, mobile applications, and user interfaces. Figma went public via direct listing in June 2021 (ticker: FIG) and was subsequently acquired by Adobe Inc. in September 2022 for approximately $20 billion in cash.
