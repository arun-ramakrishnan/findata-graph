# Hexaware Technologies

Hexaware Technologies Limited is a global digital and technology services company specialising in AI-led transformation, cloud computing, application management, automation, and business process outsourcing. Headquartered in Navi Mumbai, India, the company serves enterprise clients across key industry verticals, including banking & financial services, healthcare, insurance, manufacturing, and travel. Hexaware leverages automated delivery frameworks and AI capabilities to modernise legacy IT systems and optimise operational efficiency.

**Guidance cut to 6–7% (mid 6.5%):** Management officially lowered the annual growth outlook on macro headwinds and project delays; the revised target needs only a 2.7% constant-quarterly growth rate, which mgmt is "very confident" of delivering.

**Deal ramp delays, not deal losses:** At least four Q1/Q2 wins are slipping ~2–3 months on ramp-up; the business is being won but converting to billable work more slowly than forecast — mgmt chose to guide conservatively given the runway.

**AI driving net pricing deflation:** Hexaware was early to call net deflation this year (against mixed industry commentary) and now sees it materialising slightly more than planned; the structural implication is that high volume is needed just to hold revenue flat as traditional tasks get cheaper.

**Token-vs-labour budget tension:** The bigger client decision factor is now the split between spend on AI compute (tokens) and human labour — flagged as more impactful on IT-services budget allocation than any other single factor.

**Growth highly uneven by vertical:** Hi-Tech and manufacturing growing in high teens, banking double-digit, but FS/PS negative — diversification is absorbing the divergence rather than amplifying it.

**AI now in ~100% of deals:** Virtually every new contract — incremental or net-new — is built with AI value fully baked in; AI proficiency is the minimum bar, no longer a differentiator.
