# Dassault Systèmes

## Company Overview

Dassault Systèmes is a French multinational software corporation that develops 3D product design, simulation, manufacturing, and other 3D-related products. Its flagship 3DEXPERIENCE (3DX) platform is a cloud-based business and innovation platform providing organizations with a holistic, real-time vision of their business activity and ecosystem.
