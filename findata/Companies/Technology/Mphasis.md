# Mphasis Limited

## Company Overview

Mphasis Limited was incorporated in 1992 and is headquartered in Bengaluru. The company provides IT solutions focused on cloud and cognitive services across Banking & Financial Services, Logistics & Transportation, Technology Media & Telecom, Insurance, and other verticals. It is majority-controlled by Blackstone, the largest shareholder.
