# Fusion CX

## Company Overview
Fusion CX is a global technology-led customer experience (CX) provider operating an AI-first platform for omnichannel business process management — customer management, transaction processing and data processing across voice, chat and email. 22 years old; delivery footprint spans Tier-2/3/4 locations in India (primarily Eastern India), the Philippines, and the United States. Revenue is ~80% North America, 7–8% Europe, ~7% Southeast Asia/ANZ, remainder India; largest verticals are utilities & telecom, with strong BFSI and healthcare presence.

**B2B enterprise workflows as the moat:** a significant share of delivery manages client-to-business interactions (enterprises, medium and small businesses), which "requires significantly higher technical skill and process rigour than handling basic B2C consumer queries" — higher contract stickiness and automation resistance.

Fewer competing employers → talent stability and lower wage pressure; formal-tech employment in underserved markets doubles as the social-impact pitch. Attrition ~30%, in line with ITeS benchmarks.
