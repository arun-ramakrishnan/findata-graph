# IndiaMART InterMESH Limited

## Company Overview

IndiaMART InterMESH Limited, incorporated in 1999 and headquartered in Noida, operates India's largest online B2B marketplace for business products and services. The platform functions as an interactive hub connecting domestic and international buyers with suppliers, serving small and medium enterprises (SMEs), large enterprises, and individual users. The company also offers the Live Keeping Tally mobile app for real-time access to Tally accounting data.
