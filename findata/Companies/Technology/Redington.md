## The Chatter — MazDock Mphasis Redington

**Premiumisation lifting India mix:** Pro and Pro Max models are taking a larger share of sales, with Indian premium buyers shifting toward them; focus has moved to camera, AI capabilities and health monitoring — initial demand for the iPhone 17 line is strong.

**Orchestrating all four AI layers:** Redington operates across hyperscaler AI compute, enterprise CPU→GPU hardware transitions, non-hyperscaler cloud/data-centre operators (sovereign and non-sovereign workloads), and edge/private cloud — bridging hardware brands, hyperscalers, local operators and partners, then layering SaaS, platform, application software and managed services over hybrid deployments.

**SSG to $5 billion:** The Software Solutions and Services Group (higher gross margin) is targeted to grow from $2.3–2.4 billion to $5.0 billion in 2–3 years; it contributes ~17% of revenue in India and 30–35% globally, driving the shift from distribution to technology orchestration.

**AI ROI gap = solutioning play:** 90% of enterprises are experimenting with AI but only 30–40% have measurable bottom-line returns; Redington's response is an AI Exchange platform with ~500 pre-built agents across 20 ISVs, ISV partnerships, Partner Academy AI-fluency training, and COEs in Singapore, Bengaluru, Dubai and Saudi Arabia for standardised "cookie-cutter" use cases — moving from bespoke consulting to solution distribution.

**Margin roadmap:** PAT margin target 2.0% (from a 1.4–1.5% baseline); EBITDA currently 2.1–2.2% with active expansion toward 2.5% (3.0% called overly aspirational); the strategy "creates customer stickiness and establishes a new high-margin revenue line that sits atop our distribution scale."

> "Historically, our baseline Profit After Tax (PAT) margin has ranged between 1.4% and 1.5%... We previously communicated a public target to expand our SSG revenue from $2.3–$2.4 billion currently to $5.0 billion within two to three years."
> — V. S. Hariharan, Managing Director and Group CEO
