## The Chatter — Jio Financial, Wipro, Polycab, Piramal & More

**Clients moving beyond modernisation to AI-enabled operating models:** Shift toward AI-enabled operating models that improve service quality, reduce operational complexity, and unlock productivity gains — long-term shift to higher-value consulting/transformation.

**AI productivity varies by codebase type:** Greenfield projects see significant productivity gains; complex legacy environments limit AI deployment — suggests traditional maintenance revenue may be more resilient to AI-driven automation.

**17–17.5% margin goal retained, timeline not given:** Committed to returning to the narrow 17–17.5% operating margin band, but not providing firm timeline due to volatility.

**Workforce pyramid restructuring via AI agents:** Investigating "agentify-ing" projects to restructure the workforce pyramid as pricing models evolve.

**US healthcare vertical under pressure:** Budget cuts and regulatory pressure causing flat-to-negative growth in the US healthcare ecosystem — significant near-term headwind.

**Productivity gains being priced in:** Cost-saving deals now bake in AI productivity upfront (margin pressure), but premium pricing expected for advanced AI consulting and data transformation (Reimagain AI offerings).
