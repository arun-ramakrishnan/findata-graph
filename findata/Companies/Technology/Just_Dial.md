# Just Dial Limited

## Company Overview

Just Dial Limited (incorporated 1993; headquartered in Mumbai) is India's leading local search engine and business directory, connecting users with local businesses across categories via its website, the JD mobile app, telephone (8888888888), and SMS. The platform generates leads for SMEs and offers transaction-enabled services.

Its product suite includes **JD App** (business discovery with ratings, location-based search, 360° images, news, stocks, AR-based listing finder), **JD Mart** (B2B marketplace for MSMEs), **JD Omni** (cloud-based CRM platform for businesses), **JD Analytics** dashboard, **JD Pay** (digital payments), **JD Social**, and **JD Xperts** (on-demand services such as salon, repairs, plumbing, cleaning, pest control, fitness).

> **Note:** Just Dial is a subsidiary of Reliance Industries Limited (acquired by Reliance Retail Ventures Limited; effective October 2021).
