# Aptus Value Housing Finance India

## Company Overview
Aptus Value Housing Finance India Limited is a housing finance company focused on providing housing finance solutions to self-employed individuals from low and middle-income families in semi-urban and rural markets. The company offers a range of financial products including home loans, loans for property purchase and self-construction, home improvement loans, extension loans, loans against property, and business loans.

- **Headquarters**: Chennai, Tamil Nadu
- **Target Market**: Self-employed individuals in semi-urban and rural areas

## The Chatter — Indian Oil Muthoot Maruti

**Margins held despite rate cuts:** NIM stayed resilient even after rationalising pricing on certain loan segments — funded by a lower cost of funds via prudent liability management; asset quality broadly in line despite growth.

> "Our margins remained resilient during the quarter, despite rationalization of pricing in certain loan segments and prudent liability management, including a reduction in the cost of funds."
> — M. Anandan, Executive Chairman

**Aggressive branch build-out:** 33 new branches opened in the quarter, taking the network to 322 — distribution expansion plus a widening connector network is the primary AUM-growth engine.

**Ticket-size calibration is working:** Calibrated lending rates on select housing-loan ticket sizes lifted July disbursements ~25% YoY — trading some yield for volume/market-share.

**Product diversification beyond housing/SME:** Evaluating a new lending product to sustain the journey from ₹15,000 cr → ₹25,000 cr → ₹50,000 cr+ AUM — a deliberate shift toward a fuller financial-services franchise.

**Skipping NHB for cheaper market funding:** Can raise from banks/securitisation/NCDs at 7.8-7.9%, so NHB is avoided unless it prices competitively — reflects strong credit standing and funding flexibility.

**Low leverage = pricing power:** Modest debt-to-equity lets Aptus negotiate lender terms and rates, supporting NIMs even in a tough rate environment. Borrowing mix is 66% variable (25% repo-linked, 21% MCLR), so a repo hike has some, but manageable, impact.

**Write-off-led provisioning + Stage 2 top-up:** Tax rate is lower thanks to an aggressive write-off policy (write-offs, not provisions, dominate cost); Stage 1 provision trimmed to 0.24% as repayment behaviour improved, while Stage 2 coverage was raised on early stress.

**Field-level attrition still high:** Senior/mid-management attrition is low, but field-level attrition — though down from 50-60% to ~45% — remains the key operational challenge to sustained growth and credit oversight.
