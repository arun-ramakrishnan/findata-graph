# Aadhar Housing Finance

## Company Overview
Aadhar Housing Finance is a leading player in low-income housing finance in India, dedicated to making home ownership accessible to underserved populations. The company focuses on providing affordable housing finance solutions to millions of low and middle-income individuals, enabling them to achieve their dream of owning a home.

### Key Information
- **Founded**: 2010
- **Headquarters**: Mumbai, Maharashtra
- **Business Model**: B2C affordable housing finance
- **Target Market**: Low and middle-income individuals
- **Geographic Focus**: Pan-India with emphasis on emerging markets
- **Product Range**: Home loans, construction finance, property loans
- **Ticker**: AADHAR.NS
- **Market Cap**: Small Cap
