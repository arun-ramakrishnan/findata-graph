# LIC Housing Finance Limited

## Company Overview

LIC Housing Finance Limited, incorporated in **1989** and headquartered in **Mumbai**, is one of India's largest housing finance companies and a subsidiary of Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC). It provides loans for the purchase, construction, repair, and renovation of houses and also operates assisted living centers for the elderly, PE fund management, and asset management. The company additionally markets insurance, mutual funds, deposits, and credit cards.
