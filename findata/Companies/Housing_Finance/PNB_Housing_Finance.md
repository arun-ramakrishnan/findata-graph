# PNB Housing Finance Limited

## Company Overview

PNB Housing Finance Limited is an Indian housing finance company incorporated in **1988** and headquartered in **Gurugram**. It provides loans for the purchase, construction, and repair of houses, flats, and commercial properties, serving individual homebuyers, NRIs, and real estate developers. The company is jointly promoted by Punjab National Bank (PNB) and Carlyle.

## Product Portfolio / Business Segments

- **NRI Loans**: Home financing for non-resident Indians.
- **Loan Against Property (LAP)**: Secured lending against residential/commercial property.
