# Can Fin Homes

## Company Overview
Can Fin Homes Limited is a Bengaluru-based housing finance company founded in 1987 and a subsidiary of Canara Bank. It provides home loans, loans against property, composite housing, affordable housing, and construction finance primarily to first-time homebuyers and professionals, with a strong presence in South and West India.
