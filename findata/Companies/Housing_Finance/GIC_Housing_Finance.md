# GIC Housing Finance

## Company Overview

GIC Housing Finance Limited (incorporated 1989, formerly GIC Grih Vitta Limited) is a specialized small-cap housing finance company providing housing loans for purchase, construction, repair, and renovation of residential property to individuals, cooperative societies, associations, and corporate entities across India. Headquartered in Mumbai, the company has ~422 employees and a market cap of ~₹9.37 billion.
