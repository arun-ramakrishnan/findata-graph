# Xiaomi

## Company Overview
Xiaomi Corporation is a global consumer electronics and smart manufacturing company focused on smartphones and smart hardware connected by an IoT platform. The company also produces laptops, home appliances, and other consumer electronics, and is actively developing electric vehicles.
