# Samsung Electronics

## Company Overview
Samsung Electronics (KRX: 005930; HQ Suwon, South Korea) is a global technology conglomerate spanning consumer electronics (DX), semiconductors/memory/device solutions (DS), display panels (SDC), and Harman (automotive electronics). It is the world's largest memory chipmaker and a leading smartphone/TV maker. Distinct from its listed subsidiary Samsung SDI (battery arm).
