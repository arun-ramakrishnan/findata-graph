# Escorts Kubota

## Company Overview
Escorts Kubota Limited, incorporated in 1944 and headquartered in Faridabad, is a leading engineering company specialising in agricultural and construction equipment. A subsidiary of Japan's Kubota Corporation (post the 2022 rebranding from Escorts Limited), the company manufactures and sells tractors (Farmtrac, Powertrac, Digitrac, Steeltrac, Farmpower, Kubota brands), construction equipment (cranes, soil compactors, backhoe loaders, tandem rollers, mini excavators), railway components, and automobile parts. It also produces diesel engines/generators and offers crop and financing services.
