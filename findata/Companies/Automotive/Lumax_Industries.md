# Lumax Industries

## Company Overview

Lumax Industries Limited manufactures and sells automotive lighting systems and electronic components. Products include LED lights, headlamps, tail lamps, fog lamps, and auxiliary lamps for passenger cars, commercial vehicles, two-wheelers, and tractors. Founded 1945, headquartered in Gurugram. Serves OEM manufacturers. Has a strategic technology partnership with Stanley Electric (Japan).
