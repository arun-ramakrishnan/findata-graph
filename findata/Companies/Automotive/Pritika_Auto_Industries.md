# Pritika Auto Industries

## Company Overview
Pritika Auto Industries Limited (incorporated 1980; formerly Shivkrupa Machineries and Engineering Services; renamed 2017; HQ Mohali, Punjab) is a precision-machined castings and components maker for the tractor, commercial-vehicle, and industrial sectors — the listed entity behind ticker PRITIKAUTO.NS, commonly referenced as "Pritika Engineering" in industry coverage. It supplies axle housings, wheel housings, hydraulic-lift housings, brake housings, engine covers, and cylinder blocks to tractor and automotive OEMs.
