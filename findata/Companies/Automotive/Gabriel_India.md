# Gabriel India | Small Cap | Auto Ancillary | **GABRIEL**

Gabriel India Ltd is a producer and supplier of high quality Ride Control Products Indian Automotive Industry participating in all segments i.e. Passenger Cars, Utility Vehicles, Commercial Vehicles and Two Wheelers.The company has overseas markets at Europe, Africa, Middle East, Asia Pacific, China, Russia and Far East.

## Business Operations

### Core Product Lines
- **Ride Control Products**: High-quality suspension systems
- **Market Coverage**: All automotive segments including passenger cars, utility vehicles, commercial vehicles, and two-wheelers
- **International Presence**: Exports to multiple global markets

### Product Portfolio
- **Premium Products**: Focus on high-performance inverted front forks and mono shocks
- **Standard Products**: Conventional ride control components
- **Market Position**: Established player in automotive components
