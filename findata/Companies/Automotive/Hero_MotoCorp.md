# Hero MotoCorp
## Company Overview
Hero MotoCorp is the world's largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters, having held its leadership position for 25 consecutive years. The company is aggressively expanding into the electric vehicle market under the VIDA brand, strengthening its premium-segment presence via 'Premia' stores, and growing internationally (notably Latin America).

## Key Insights (from Edition #51, Mar 2026)
- Urban demand leading the recovery on income-tax benefits, while rural demand showed unexpected festive-season strength; channel inventory has been significantly and judiciously reduced to align supply with real demand.
- Margin pressure from rising aluminium/precious-metal prices and forex (~40-50 bps commodity impact last quarter), but management believes market conditions are strong enough to absorb judicious price increases without volume impact.
- EV affordability push via Battery-as-a-Service (decoupling battery cost from vehicle price) to widen the addressable budget-conscious customer base for the VIDA brand.
- Premiumisation pivot: 106 Premia stores live (covering >50% of premium industry footprint) to compete in the high-margin premium segment where Hero has historically been weak; structural shift toward scooters expected as urbanization and female workforce participation rise.
