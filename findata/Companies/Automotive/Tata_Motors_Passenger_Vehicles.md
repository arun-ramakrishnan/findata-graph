# Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles

Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles (TMPV) is the new name for the entity that manages Tata's domestic passenger vehicle and electric vehicle (EV) businesses, which was created after a demerger from its commercial vehicles business. This entity also includes the operations of Jaguar Land Rover (JLR). The split created two separate, publicly listed companies to allow each to focus on its own strategy and market.

## Q2 FY26 Performance

Industry demand recovered sharply toward the end of Q2, aided by GST 2.0 price cuts, improving sentiment, and strong CNG and EV growth. Tata Motors delivered a standout quarter with 10% YoY wholesales growth, record EV/CNG volumes, and strong traction across Nexon, Harrier.ev, and Safari, supported by a robust booking pipeline.

## Industry Highlights

- Operating environment improves due to GST 2.0 driving price reduction for PVs by up to ~10%
- First 5 months of FY26 (Apr-Aug) witnessed 1.6% decline in sales. Industry grew 5% in Sep'25 and 17% in Oct'25
- CNG segment continued to see faster growth than industry with 17% YoY growth in Q2
- EV industry grew 126% YoY in H1 driven by launch of new models especially Harrier.ev

## Company Highlights

- Actions undertaken in the last 18 months on business processes and service yield strong returns
- Recorded 10% YoY growth in offtakes in Q2 FY26
- In Sep'25, achieved record wholesales with 60.9k units of sales with 47% YoY growth with record-breaking EV & CNG sales at 9k+ and 17k+ units respectively
- #2 player with Vahan shares of 14% in Sep'25
- Strong demand across portfolio with Nexon emerging as #1 model in India & record volumes for Harrier, Safari

## EV Performance

Tata Motors posted its highest-ever quarterly EV offtake at 24.9K units (+59% YoY) driven by new launches and accelerating demand. EV Vahan market share rose to 41.4% in Q2, while public charging infrastructure expanded rapidly to 26,310 points supporting sustained EV momentum.

## The Chatter — Edition #46 (13 Feb 2026): Tailwinds building

> Q3 FY26 earnings-call coverage. Source: Zerodha "The Chatter", Edition #46, 13 Feb 2026. [Original](findata/The_Chatter/Tailwinds_building.md)

### Key Signals (Q3 FY26)
- **India PV outperforming**: Q4 growth ~40% vs industry ~13–14%; FY26 expected mid-teens vs industry 8–9%.
  > "The first month of quarter four was about 14% growth for the industry, we were at almost 46%... we expect that for FY26 therefore, the industry would grow by about 8% to 9%... Whereas for us, we should be somewhere in mid-teens." — Shailesh Chandra, MD & CEO, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited
- **Sierra demand strong, supply-constrained**: Bookings in six digits (vs 70,000 announced Dec 16); ramp limited by supplier capacity.
  > "70,000 is what we had announced on 16th December. It's, of course, in six digits... even before I talk about in-house ramp up, the first level problem is, on the supply ramp up from the suppliers itself." — Shailesh Chandra, MD & CEO
- **JLR busy launch period**: Range Rover Electric, new production Jaguar, and first EMA-platform model (built at Halewood) all this year.
  > "We're going to launch the Range Rover Electric this year and start delivering to customers. And we'll also unveil the new production Jaguar car this year. And finally, also unveil the first car off our EMA platform." — Richard Molyneux, CFO, Jaguar Land Rover
- **JLR on Chinese OEMs**: Confident in brand strength; prefers free trade over protectionism.
  > "The one thing Chinese OEMs do not have is brands. And brands is where we are focusing... JLR prefers a free trade model... we welcome competition. It will force us to be better." — Richard Molyneux, CFO, Jaguar Land Rover
- **JLR China is structurally weak**: Viewed as permanent, not cyclical; recent months worse.
  > "This is not a short-term boom-bust cycle. This is structural and permanent in China." — Richard Molyneux, CFO, Jaguar Land Rover
- **Cyber incident hit production**: ~50,000 units lost; Q wholesale at 59,100 units.
  > "The cyber event cost us around 50,000 units of production, and that led to a wholesale result of 59,100 units in the quarter." — Richard Molyneux, CFO, Jaguar Land Rover
- **JLR debt recovery delayed**: Won't return to net cash in next 2–3 quarters.
  > "Our debt has increased. It will certainly not get back to net cash over the next two or three quarters." — Richard Molyneux, CFO, Jaguar Land Rover
