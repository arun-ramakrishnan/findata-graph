# Samvardhana Motherson International Limited

**Ticker:** MOTHERSON.NS | **Sector:** Auto Ancillary | **Market Cap:** Large Cap

## Company Overview

Samvardhana Motherson International Limited, formerly known as Motherson Sumi Systems Limited, is a global manufacturing specialist and the leading supplier to automotive OEMs. With a presence on five continents, the company serves customers in various industries including Aerospace, Logistics, Health & Medical, and Information Technology, offering support for evolving industrial trends worldwide.

### Key Business Divisions
- **Wiring Harness**: Manufacturing and supplying wiring harnesses for automotive applications
- **Vision Systems**: Providing advanced vision systems and automotive electronics
- **Polymer Products**: Producing polymer-based components for automotive and industrial use

### Technology Expansion
- Planning to add 5,000+ engineers over next five years, doubling the current 5,000 count to build AI and technology capabilities
- New building coming in H2 to support this expansion of design, software, and automation competencies
- "We are wanting to add more than 5,000 engineers in the next five years, building and bolstering our competency with global business services that we have built in-house and our AI platform. That's one of key strengths for when we do acquisitions, that we can get access to relevant data and create strategies of how we can improve operations of plants that we acquired."

- Current EV revenues are 11%, with the order book's EV share decreasing from 24% to 22%
- Due to new launches including non-EV powertrains and recalibrated EV growth pace
- "Our EV revenues right now is 11%. And in the order book, you see a dip from 24% to 22% which is largely reflective of what's happening in terms of new launches that are happening along with non-EV powertrains as well."

- Existing 5,000 design and software engineers enable data-driven improvement strategies for newly acquired plants
- This creates competitive advantage in acquisition integration

## The Chatter — Dixon Motherson Biocon

**Margin recovery from commodity normalization:** Temporary pressure from elevated copper and polymer prices; expects margins to improve as commodities normalize through the year, aided by new high-margin aerospace/consumer electronics businesses.

**Acquisitions adding immediate value:** Utaka Giken and Nexen Auto Electric acquisitions completed; will consolidate from July (Q2) and are accretive with significant growth potential in coming years.

**Chinese OEM global expansion as opportunity:** As Chinese automakers expand internationally, Motherson's global footprint and China presence position it to capture new supply contracts; European restructuring over 1.5 years aligned with customer plans.

**Pivot to broader industrial supplier:** Actively evaluating robotics, data centers, humanoid robotics to support customer diversification; early stage but already associated with several customers in these emerging areas.

**Strategic diversification target:** Aims for 40–45% of revenue from new non-automotive businesses (aerospace, consumer electronics, etc.) to de-risk from automotive cyclicality; 50% of ₹6,000 crore annual capex is growth capex, of which 60% allocated to new businesses.

**Aerospace as major growth engine:** Order book at ~$1.6B + 17–18% growth; adding new customers; expects segment to significantly outpace traditional automotive growth.

> "We are very pleased that both acquisitions have been completed, and from July onward they will be consolidated into our financial results. These acquisitions are accretive, and we believe there is significant scope to create additional value. In the coming years, you will see substantial growth from both Utaka Giken and Nexen Auto Electric."
> — Pankaj Mittal, Whole-Time Director and President
