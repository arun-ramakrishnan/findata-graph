# Fiem Industries Limited

## Company Overview

Fiem Industries is a leading automotive components company specializing in lighting systems, electrical equipment, and other automotive products for the automotive industry. The company has established itself as a key supplier to major automotive OEMs in India, with a growing presence in international markets. Fiem Industries focuses on providing high-quality, innovative lighting and electrical solutions for various vehicle types.
