# Shriram Pistons & Rings

## Overview

Shriram Pistons & Rings Limited is a leading automotive components manufacturer in India, specializing in piston rings, pistons, and related engine components. The company serves both domestic and international markets, supplying critical engine components to automotive OEMs and aftermarket customers. As a key player in the automotive value chain, the company benefits from India's growing automobile industry and expanding aftermarket services.
