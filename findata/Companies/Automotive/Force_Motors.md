# Force Motors

## Company Overview

Force Motors Limited is an integrated automobile company designing, developing, and manufacturing automotive components, aggregates, and vehicles. Products include the Urbania (modular monocoque van), Traveller (passenger/cargo platform), Trax (rural utility vehicle), Gurkha (off-road), Monobus, agricultural tractors, ambulances, school buses, special utility and military vehicles, and electric vehicles. Founded 1958, headquartered in Pune. Subsidiary of Jaya Hind Industries.
