# CIE Automotive India Limited

## Company Overview

CIE Automotive India is a diversified global auto-component manufacturer specializing in forging, casting, and stamping for major vehicle segments. The company maintains a strong geographic footprint in India and Europe, serving leading OEMs across passenger and commercial vehicle platforms. It is part of the global CIE Automotive group (multi-technology, multi-location auto-components player).

### Core Business Segments

- **Forging:** Closed-die and open-die forged components for CVs, PVs, tractors, and two-wheelers
- **Casting:** Iron and aluminum castings across automotive and industrial applications
- **Stamping & Sub-assemblies:** Metal stamped parts and sub-assemblies for OEMs
- **Geographies:** India (diversified mix, margin cushion) and Europe (high-risk forging specialization, cyclical)
