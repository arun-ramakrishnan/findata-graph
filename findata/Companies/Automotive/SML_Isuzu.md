# SML Isuzu

## Company Overview

SML Isuzu Limited (formerly Swaraj Mazda Limited) is an Indian commercial vehicle manufacturer producing buses, trucks, and multi-utility vehicles. The company operates a manufacturing facility at Nawanshahr, Punjab, and sells through a network of dealers across India, with its buses widely used in state transport undertakings (STUs) and school/private fleets.

## Ownership

On 1 August 2025, **Mahindra & Mahindra** completed the acquisition of a **58.96% controlling stake** in SML Isuzu. The board and key leadership were reconstituted the same day, and the company has since been rebranded/repositioned under the Mahindra group as **SML Mahindra**. Synergy and integration projects were kicked off immediately post-acquisition.
