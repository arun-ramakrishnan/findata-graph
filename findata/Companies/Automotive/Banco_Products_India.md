# Banco Products (India)

## Company Overview

Banco Products (India) Limited manufactures heat exchangers/cooling systems and precision sealing solutions, headquartered in Vadodara (incorporated 1961). Cooling products: radiators, charge air coolers, oil coolers, condensers, fuel/inverter/battery coolers. Sealing products: cylinder head gaskets, peripheral gaskets, heat shields, rubber/cork gaskets. Serves passenger cars, LCV/HCV, agricultural tractors, motorcycles, construction/mining equipment, locomotives, and data center backup power. ~737 employees.
