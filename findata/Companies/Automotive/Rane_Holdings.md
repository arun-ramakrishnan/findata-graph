# Rane Holdings

## Company Overview

Rane Holdings Limited is the holding company of the Rane Group, a 1929-founded auto components conglomerate headquartered in Chennai. Through subsidiaries, it manufactures steering & suspension systems, engine valves, brake linings, occupant safety systems (seat belts, airbags), and light metal castings. Serves passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, farm tractors, two-wheelers, railways, and stationary engines globally.
