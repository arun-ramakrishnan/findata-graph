# Hero Motors

## Company Overview
Hero Motors — global precision engineering firm specialising in integrated powertrain systems and automotive components; high-margin brakes and aerospace divisions plus JVs with ZF, Mitsui, Sumitomo and Yamaha. Distinct from listed Hero MotoCorp. (Unlisted; ticker null.)

## The Chatter — NSE ONGC SEBI
