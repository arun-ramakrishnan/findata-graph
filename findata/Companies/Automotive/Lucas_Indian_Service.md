# Lucas Indian Service

Indian auto-electrical component distribution company established 1930, part of the TVS Group. Sells and services Lucas-TVS manufactured auto electrical components (starters, alternators, ignition systems) across India via four regional offices and a widespread aftermarket network. Joint-venture partner (with Kokusan Denki) in India Nippon Electricals.
