# Precision Camshafts

## Company Overview
Precision Camshafts Limited (PCL), incorporated in 1992 and based in Pune with manufacturing in Solapur, Maharashtra, is one of the world's leading camshaft manufacturers by global market-share volume in the passenger-vehicle segment. It supplies chilled cast iron, ductile iron, hybrid, and assembled camshafts for passenger vehicles, tractors, LCVs, and locomotives, plus fuel-injection parts, balancer shafts, and electric powertrains for heavy vehicles.
