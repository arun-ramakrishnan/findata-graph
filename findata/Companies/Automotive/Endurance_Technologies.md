# Endurance Technologies Limited

## Company Overview

Endurance Technologies is a leading automotive components company specializing in precision engineering products, polymer solutions, and advanced materials for automotive and industrial applications. The company has established itself as a key supplier to major automotive OEMs both in India and international markets, with a growing presence in the aftermarket segments.

## The Chatter — Interview (Aluminium Pass-Through, Two-Wheeler Demand)

- Note: Endurance is an Auto Ancillary business (aluminium die casting — alloy wheels, suspension, transmission, braking systems) currently filed under Technology
- Geopolitical disruptions managed via enterprise risk planning and alternate fuels for smelters; production unaffected so far thanks to operational agility and a supportive vendor base
- Aluminium is ~54% of material content; spot prices spiked ~20-25% — but the company has arrangements to recover 100% of commodity increases (some on a quarterly lag, some real-time)
- Margins may compress in percentage terms due to the pass-through structure, but are protected in absolute (rupee) terms
- Two-wheeler demand strong and sustained; premiumisation trend emerging in two-wheelers, supporting higher-value realisations

> "Almost 54% of our material has some amount of aluminium in it... however, we have arrangements to pass it on to the customers."
> — RS Rajagopal Sastry, Group CFO

*Source: Zerodha The Chatter — Havells, Ambuja, Cipla & More (interview; RS Rajagopal Sastry, Group CFO)*
