# Tata Technologies

## Company Overview

Tata Technologies Limited is a global engineering services company providing product development and digital solutions to OEMs and tier 1 suppliers, primarily in the automotive industry and extending into aerospace, transportation, and construction heavy machinery. The company leverages diverse global teams to solve complex engineering challenges and is part of the Tata Group.

- ESTEC acquisition adds depth in ADS, comfort electronics, and embedded software (architecture, requirements, test/validation), complementing Tata Technologies' India software capabilities.
- ESTEC operates at 28-29% margins (higher than Tata Technologies), making the deal accretive on both margin and EPS from day one, expected to close late November or December.
- The BMW joint venture scaled to 1,000+ employees ahead of schedule across Pune, Bangalore, and Chennai, handling increasingly complex, high-value work.
- Q2 saw deferred customer investment decisions from tariff uncertainty begin to convert, as customers align to the new normal.
- European automotive OEMs are shifting sourcing toward India and low-cost regions, opening new addressable market tailwinds for the company.
