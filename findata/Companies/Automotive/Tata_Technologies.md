# Tata Technologies

## Company Overview

Tata Technologies Limited is a global engineering services company providing product development and digital solutions to OEMs and tier 1 suppliers, primarily in the automotive industry and extending into aerospace, transportation, and construction heavy machinery. The company leverages diverse global teams to solve complex engineering challenges and is part of the Tata Group.
