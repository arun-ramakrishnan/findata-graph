# Ramkrishna Forgings

## Company Overview

Ramkrishna Forgings Limited manufactures forged components for automobiles, railway wagons/coaches, and engineering parts. The company's portfolio spans steering components (beam, knuckle, steering arm, tie-rod-arm), driveline products (front hub, crankshaft, camshaft, connecting rod), axle/differential components, railway bogie parts, earthmoving equipment, and tractor-trailer products. Founded 1981, headquartered in Kolkata.
