# Exide Industries

## Company Overview
Exide Industries is India's leading battery manufacturing company and a pioneer in energy storage solutions. With over 75 years of experience, Exide has established itself as a trusted brand in the battery industry, serving diverse sectors with innovative and reliable energy storage products.

### Key Information
- **Founded**: 1947
- **Headquarters**: Kolkata, India
- **Business Model**: B2B and B2C battery manufacturing
- **Product Range**: Lead-acid storage batteries for automotive, industrial, and specialty applications
- **Manufacturing**: 9 state-of-the-art factories across India
- **Distribution**: Pan-India network with 60,000+ dealers
- **Ticker**: EXIDEIND.NS
- **Market Cap**: Small Cap
- **Revenue**: INR 15,000+ crores (FY2025 estimate)
