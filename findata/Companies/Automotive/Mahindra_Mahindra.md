# Mahindra Mahindra

Mahindra Mahindra Limited (M&M) is a prominent Indian automobile manufacturing company known for its wide range of mobility products and farm solutions. With a history dating back to 1947, M&M has established itself as a leading player in the industry by offering SUVs, pickups, commercial vehicles, tractors, electric vehicles, two-wheelers, gensets, and construction equipment.

**GST Rate Cut Impact**
- CEO calls GST rate cuts a "very good move" with strong multi-year benefits for simplification and demand
- Short-term disruption worth long-term gains across 70% of India's GDP where M&M operates
- "This is a very, very good move by the government because for the longer term, it simplifies things as well as reduces GST. And there will be, in our view, a fairly strong multi-year benefits from this move."

- LCV and tractor customers endured unprecedented 25-30% price increases over five years due to commodity inflation and BS6 regulations

- The short-term impact of GST cuts is seeing strong fundamentals of the economy waiting for stimulus translate into optimism
- "In short term, what we are seeing is, the fact that the strong fundamentals of economy were waiting for some stimulus to be able to translate that into optimism from an overall feel standpoint, which is important as well."

## The Chatter — RBI Tata Steel

**+34% YoY profit in a tough quarter:** Management frames the Q1 result as resilient rather than benign — strong operational control protected the bottom line against a difficult global backdrop.

**Commodity inflation priced through:** Price hikes have been taken to offset rising steel/rubber costs; with commodities easing late in June then nudging up again, mgmt feels "fairly comfortable" margins are solved for at current levels.

**Lower monsoon sensitivity:** Earnings are less weather-dependent than 5–7 years ago; mgmt calls the seasonal concern "overblown" given how much the franchise has de-risked from agriculture cycles.

**SUVs at capacity, supply-constrained:** Production is running flat-out; intermittent labor shortages and supply issues held back incremental volume that capacity could otherwise have delivered.

**EV path to subsidy-free profitability:** Scale, not incentives, is the margin lever — the electric 3-wheeler transition (now at 40% penetration post-FAME) is the proof case that passenger EVs can reach self-sustaining economics as government support dials down.

> "Government incentives cannot last forever; it should not last forever. The incentive is there for the transition from ICE to EV, and as scale comes in, profitability for companies will improve… [the electric three-wheeler story] is a great case study across the world for a government giving a subsidy, helping transition the industry to electric, and then that subsidy coming down and not being required anymore."
> — Dr. Anish Shah, MD & Group CEO
