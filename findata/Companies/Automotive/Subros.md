# Subros

## Company Overview

Subros Limited is India's largest manufacturer of thermal products for automotive applications, founded in 1985 and headquartered in New Delhi. Manufactures compressors, condensers, HVAC systems (heaters, evaporators, blower units), tubes/hoses, bus AC, railway cabin AC, transport refrigeration, and off-roader AC systems. Supplies to all major Indian OEMs (Maruti Suzuki, Tata, Mahindra, etc.) and exports.
