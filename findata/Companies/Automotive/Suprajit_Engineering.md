# Suprajit Engineering

## Company Overview

Suprajit Engineering Limited manufactures automotive cables, halogen lamps, speedometers, and automotive components in India, the US, and internationally. Products include control cables, halogen/LED bulbs, electro-mechanical actuators, TFT clusters, braking systems, throttle position sensors, gear shifter systems, lighting systems, and USB charging modules. Founded 1985, headquartered in Bengaluru.
