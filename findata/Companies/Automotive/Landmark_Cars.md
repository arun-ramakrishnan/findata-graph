# Landmark Cars Limited

## Company Overview
Landmark Cars Limited, founded in 1998 and headquartered in Mumbai, is an automotive retail dealership group representing a broad portfolio of passenger and commercial vehicle brands across India. Beyond vehicle sales, it offers accessories, after-sales service, insurance, and financing. The company listed via IPO in December 2022.
