# Bosch Limited

## Company Overview

Bosch Limited is the Indian subsidiary of Robert Bosch GmbH, a leading global technology and engineering company. The company operates across multiple business segments including automotive, industrial technology, consumer goods, and energy and building technology. With a strong presence in India for over 90 years, Bosch has established itself as a diversified technology leader serving both domestic and international markets.

## Key Insights (The Chatter #49)

- Expects record FY26 production in Passenger Cars, Tractors, and 2-Wheelers — a strong revenue growth indicator
- 2-Wheeler business grew 58.3% driven by OBD-II norm rollout (effective April 1, 2025) boosting exhaust gas sensor sales
- EV strategy focused on high-value e-axles; also developing hydrogen engine technology with all major Indian CV/bus OEMs
- Localizing components (e.g., NOx sensors for common rails) to improve margins; India emerging as export hub for spark plugs and pumps (relocated from Japan)
- Flexible CapEx approach with buffer capacity; FTA does not alter localization strategy

## The Chatter — Q4 FY26 Concall (RBIC Acquisition, Regulatory Tailwinds)

- Acquiring Robert Bosch India Chassis (RBIC) — powertrain-agnostic braking/safety portfolio (iBooster, Integrated Power Brakes) positions Bosch well for EV regenerative braking demand
- Pro-forma impact: consolidated revenue +22% (Rs.18,000cr to Rs.22,000cr); FY25 EBITDA margin 12.8% to 13.9% as RBIC is higher-margin than the existing portfolio
- Regulatory tailwinds — mandated airbags, ABS, ESP and Bharat NCAP provide predictable, structural demand; shift to ESP adds sensors per vehicle (more revenue per car)
- Deliberately passed on the automotive electronics unit — capital-intensive and not margin-accretive — reflecting disciplined capital allocation
- Post the ~Rs.9,000cr acquisition, Bosch Ltd remains debt-free with significant cash; Tata AutoComp JV to scale EV e-axles/motors; modular capex using idle international production lines keeps investment low

## The Chatter — Bosch Amara Zydus

**Sustained margin expansion:** Bosch has achieved durable margin improvement through localization, productivity gains, and a richer product mix. Management expects the upward trend to persist as internal operations and sourcing continue to optimize.

**Global procurement buffer:** The worldwide Bosch Group purchasing organization helps the Indian entity navigate volatile sourcing markets and shield domestic margins from cost disruptions.

**Technology-agnostic strategy:** Bosch continues investing in ICE technology alongside EV/ADAS/CNG — volume growth in combustion technologies persists, and the company supports whatever the market demands.

**Chassis systems acquisition is strategic, not cost-saving:** The acquired chassis systems business adds a powertrain-agnostic, highly profitable segment to Bosch Ltd.'s portfolio rather than delivering cost synergies.

**TACO & TSFS JVs nearing operational status:** E-axle production from the Nashik (TACO) and Chennai (TSFS) JVs expected to contribute revenue by late next fiscal year, pending international merger-control clearances.

> "We have done quite a few things consistently over the last several years... continuous improvement in our operational excellence... increase in our localization content... volume growth has been favorable... improvement in productivity... product mix has been quite favorable... we are on an upward trend and we would say that we will sustain this."
> — Guruprasad Mudlapur, MD & CEO
