# Plastic Omnium

## Company Overview

Plastic Omnium is a global leader in automotive exterior systems and clean energy storage solutions, with significant operations in India manufacturing automotive components and systems. The company specializes in automotive exterior modules (bumpers, fenders, body panels), hydrogen storage and fuel systems, and advanced polymer/composite engineering for major automotive OEMs.

- Strategic focus on the hydrogen economy: hydrogen storage systems, fuel cell components, and battery enclosures.
- Capitalizing on EV adoption and industry lightweighting trends through advanced polymers and composites.
- R&D-driven with continuous investment in next-generation materials, fuel system technologies, and Industry 4.0 manufacturing.
- Geographic diversification balanced across multiple markets with established OEM relationships.
