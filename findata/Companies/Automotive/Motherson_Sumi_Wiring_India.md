# Motherson Sumi Wiring India

## Company Overview
Motherson Sumi Wiring India Limited (MSWIL), headquartered in Noida, is a leading manufacturer of wiring harnesses and integrated electrical/electronic distribution systems for automotive OEMs. Demerged from Samvardhana Motherson (the global auto-ancillary parent) in 2020 and listed in 2022, it supplies passenger cars, two-/three-wheelers, commercial vehicles, and off-highway/farm equipment, plus electrification solutions (high-voltage harnesses) for ICE, hybrid, and EV powertrains.

## The Chatter — Reliance, Infosys, VBL, Hind Zinc & More

**Q4 margin pressure:** Margins were hit by temporary copper price increases and forex mix; the impact is expected to be largely offset over the next 3-6 months via customer pass-through arrangements.

**Pass-through structure:** Contractual raw-material pass-through (with a time lag) protects the underlying profitability of the core business despite short-term commodity volatility.

**Greenfield ramp-up:** New manufacturing facilities are ramping up; management expects margin improvement as capacity utilisation at these plants increases.

**EV / hybrid content accretion:** EV contribution is ~8.5% of Q4 revenue, with management highlighting EV and hybrid platforms as content-accretive per vehicle — expected to grow as more platforms launch.

**Core profitability stable:** Leadership believes the fundamental drivers and underlying profitability of the core business remain intact, framing recent headwinds as temporary rather than structural.
