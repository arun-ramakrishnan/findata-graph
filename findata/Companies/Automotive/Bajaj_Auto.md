## The Chatter — Meesho, Marico, Bajaj & More

**Record export revenue:** Exports crossed 600,000 units for a second consecutive quarter (+25% YoY); FY26 was the second-highest in volume but the highest-ever in revenue at USD 2.2 billion, with a target of lifting monthly volumes to ~220,000 units.

**Pulsar refresh working:** 10 new Pulsar variants/update introduced Oct–March now account for ~50% of sales, driving sequential month-on-month market-share gains and validating pricing power in the 150cc+ segment.

**EV at scale and profitable:** The combined two-/three-wheeler EV business — the largest in the Indian auto industry — now tops 20% of domestic revenue and contributes double-digit EBITDA, with Chetak reaching EBITDA-neutral and e-three-wheelers the key margin driver.

**Domestic slowdown, premium offset:** Domestic motorcycle growth is set to decelerate from Q4's ~20% to an estimated 7–9% on inflation/LPG/migration headwinds, but growth is expected to come almost entirely from the 125cc+ (and especially 150cc+) segments growing at twice the industry rate.

**Capital return + commodity inflation:** A ₹5,600 crore buyback (payouts ~mid-July) signals balance-sheet strength, but Q1 sees sharply inflationary commodities — steel +15%, copper +20%, aluminum/noble metals +35–45%.

> "In totality, our two-wheeler and three-wheeler electric business is actually now the largest in the auto industry, accounting for almost 20%+ of our domestic revenues and contributing double-digit EBITDA percentage."
> — Rakesh Sharma, Joint Managing Director
