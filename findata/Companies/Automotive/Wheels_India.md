# Wheels India

## Company Overview
Wheels India — Wheels India Limited (NSE: WHEELS) is a Chennai-based TVS Group (TSF) manufacturer of automotive suspension and wheel components for passenger and commercial vehicles, with significant export operations.
Its board approved the joint-venture agreement with Bosch Limited in May 2026 (CV air-systems JV).
