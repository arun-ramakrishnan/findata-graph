# Indag Rubber

## Company Overview
Indag Rubber pioneered cold retreading technology in India. The company offers a complete range of application-specific tread patterns for the transportation industry with cost-effective tyre solutions, including Precured Tread Rubber, Un-vulcanized Rubber Strip Gum, Universal Spray Cement, and Envelopes.
