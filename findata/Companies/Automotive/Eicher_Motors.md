# Eicher Motors

## Company Overview

Eicher Motors Limited is a leading Indian automotive company operating through two main business segments: Royal Enfield (premium motorcycles) and VE Commercial Vehicles (VECV), a joint venture with the Volvo Group. The company has established itself as a leader in the premium motorcycle segment and the commercial vehicles market.

## The Chatter — RBI Tata Steel

**₹1,225 cr Kadapa greenfield approved:** Board-sanctioned Phase 1 of a new Royal Enfield plant in Andhra Pradesh, adding 4.5 lakh motorcycles/year at full utilization, with completion targeted for FY29-30 — a long-cycle bet on sustained global middleweight demand.

**Dominance held as segment doubled:** India's middleweight segment grew from 70k to ~1.2 lakh units/month over three years with 20+ rival launches; Royal Enfield grew with it, from 61k to ~1.01 lakh/month, retaining share against the influx.

**Commodity hit partially value-engineered:** Input-cost inflation drove a 4–4.5% margin impact in the quarter, offset ~0.4% by value engineering; cost-reduction programs continue as steel/aluminium pressure persists.

**Allied business scaling:** Spare parts, service, accessories and apparel now ~15% of revenue and growing — service job cards +20% YoY (~9 lakh/month), a high-margin recurring stream that de-risks reliance on new-bike sales.

**VECV enters small commercial vehicles:** 1,041 Pro-200 trucks delivered in the SCV launch (including 172 EVs), opening a high-volume segment VECV hadn't addressed; Indonesia CKD assembly also under evaluation to bypass import restrictions.

> "In the last three years… the middleweight segment in India grew from 70,000 units per month to 1.2 lakh per month, with about 20-plus launches… When the base for the middleweight was 70,000 per month, Royal Enfield was about 61,000 per month; now that the base is almost about 1.2 lakh per month, Royal Enfield is almost about 1.01 lakh per month."
> — B. Govindarajan, Managing Director, EML and CEO of Royal Enfield
