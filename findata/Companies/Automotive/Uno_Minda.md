**Sector:** Auto Ancillary | **Market Cap:** Mid Cap | **Geography:** India

## Company Overview
Uno Minda Limited (formerly Minda Industries Limited) is a leading global manufacturer of proprietary automotive solutions and systems supplying to OEMs Tier-1. The company has made important contributions to the automotive industry supply chain for more than six decades with its innovative products and comprehensive automotive solutions.
