# Amara Raja Batteries Limited

**Ticker:** ARE&M.NS | **Sector:** Auto Ancillary | **Market Cap:** Small Cap

## Company Overview

Amara Raja Batteries Limited is a technology leader and one of the largest manufacturers of lead-acid batteries in India for industrial and automotive use. The company provides batteries for various applications including Passenger Vehicles, Two Wheelers, Commercial Vehicles, and Industrial needs like UPS, Telecom, Railways, Defence, and Motive power.

## The Chatter — Edition #46 (13 Feb 2026): Tailwinds building

> Q3 FY26 earnings-call coverage. Source: Zerodha "The Chatter", Edition #46, 13 Feb 2026. [Original](findata/The_Chatter/Tailwinds_building.md)

### Key Signals (Q3 FY26)
- **Entering BESS**: Board approved ~INR 280 cr capex for a 5 GWh integrated plant; expected operational by FY27; BESS market seen at 25–30 GWh by FY31.
  > "Our board has approved setting up a 5 gigawatt hour integrated solution plant with an estimated capex outlay of approximately 280 crores... We expect this plant to be operational by the end of FY27." — Y. Delli Babu, Chief Financial Officer
- **Lithium subsidiary funded**: INR 200 cr infused in Q3 into Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies; total investment now INR 1,400 cr.
  > "During Q3, we infused approximately 200 crores into Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies, which is our lithium subsidiary. With this, the total investment is now 1,400 crores." — Y. Delli Babu, Chief Financial Officer
- **Telecom lead-acid declining fast**: Volumes -45% in Q3; revenue share now <5% due to lithium shift.
  > "In telecom, volumes declined by more than 45% during the quarter... the share is now less than 5% of the overall revenue because of the transition to lithium." — Y. Delli Babu, Chief Financial Officer
- **BESS economics**: High asset turnover (9–10x) initially via imported components, future localization + potential cell manufacturing; better ROCE despite lower op margins.
  > "Asset turnover ratios are expected to be very high anywhere around 9-10 times... Over time, this paves the way for localizing components, particularly cells." — Y. Delli Babu, Chief Financial Officer
- **BESS target ~15% share**: Leveraging 55–60% telecom share and EPC solar expertise.
  > "To meet our 5 gigawatt hour target in a 30-40 gigawatt hour demand market would give us approximately a 15% market share, which is a rightful goal." — Y. Delli Babu, Chief Financial Officer
- **Exports pressured**: Could not supply US this quarter; Middle East/APAC competition rising; forming US subsidiary.
  > "This year, we were not able to supply any volume to the US in the quarter. In the Middle East and Asia-Pacific... competitive intensity is on the rise." — Y. Delli Babu, Chief Financial Officer

## The Chatter — ONGC, Divi's, LG, Pinelabs & More

**Lead-acid still 92% of revenue:** Q4 FY26 consolidated revenue ~₹3,530 cr (~16% YoY); the new energy business remains small but is where the capex is being redirected.

**Capex tilting sharply to new energy:** Of ₹1,500–1,700 cr FY27 capex, only ~₹400 cr goes to lead-acid; the remaining ₹1,100–1,200 cr funds the lithium/new-energy build-out.

**Pivot from EV to ESS:** Strategic re-prioritization toward energy storage systems (ESS), where domestic demand has accelerated on the renewables push; EVs remain the larger long-term opportunity.

**Gigafactory on track, but tech is now in-house:** Giga-1 (first 2 GWh line) targeted for June 2027 production; Chinese technology licensing is disrupted, so NMC/LFP development is now largely driven by Indian teams.

**Cost disadvantage vs. China ~$15–20/kwh:** Local-cell business case depends on anticipated government localization mandates (similar to solar); until then Chinese imports remain cheaper.

> "India cannot leapfrog directly into EVs because we cannot replace oil imports with total dependency on imported raw materials for batteries. This means hybrids will likely accelerate, and we have solutions for all levels of hybridization."
> — Harsha Vardhana Gourineni, Executive Director - Automotive and Industrial

## The Chatter — Bosch Amara Zydus

**New energy surging:** Q1 consolidated revenue ~₹4,215 crores (+24% YoY); new energy business grew >70% to ~₹290 crores, with EV and telecom pack volumes up >50% each.

**Heavy capex cycle:** FY27 capex estimated at ~₹1,700 crores, with ~₹1,300 crores directed to new energy (Giga 1 plant, BESS, E-positive). ₹450 crores spent in Q1 FY27.

**BESS economics:** 10 GWh capacity for ₹250-300 crore initial outlay; targeting 5 GWh utilization within ~6 months of commissioning. Operating margin expected to mirror current pack business at 5-7% EBITDA.

**15-20% lithium cell market share target:** Strategic direction intact despite timing shifts. Management prioritizing ESS cells over standard EV cells given faster demand visibility from grid-scale and C&I storage.

**Chinese imports are the real competitor:** India-based cell makers face a 15-20% price disadvantage vs Chinese imports today; cathode material supply chain remains China-dependent in the near term.

> "During FY27, we estimate that we will spend around 1,700 crores towards our capex projects, with a major outlay towards the new energy business of around 1,300 crores... This capex outlay is mainly towards our upcoming Giga 1 plant, which is expected to commercialize during H1 FY28."
> — Sujatha Rathetti, Head - Corporate Finance
