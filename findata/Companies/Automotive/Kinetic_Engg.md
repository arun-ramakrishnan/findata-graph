# Kinetic Engineering

Kinetic Engineering Limited specializes in manufacturing high technology components and assemblies for various vehicles including passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, tractors, two-three wheelers, and construction vehicles. They also cater to non-auto segments such as tractors, recreational products, and construction equipment.

## Business Overview
- **Product Range**: High-technology components for diverse vehicle segments
- **Market Segments**: Passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, tractors, two-three wheelers, construction equipment
- **Non-Auto Business**: Tractors, recreational products, construction equipment
- **Technology Focus**: High-technology components and assemblies

## EV Initiative
Kinetic is making a comeback in two-wheeler space with an electric avatar of its iconic DX scooter through its EV subsidiary, Kinetic Watts and Volts Ltd.

### DX Electric Scooter Features
- **Range**: Up to 116 km with LFP battery
- **Battery Technology**: LFP batteries offering 4x longer lifecycle than typical NMC batteries
- **Thermal Stability**: Better thermal stability for Indian summers
- **Charging**: 50% in 2 hours, full charge in 4 hours
- **Regenerative Braking**: Built-in regenerative technology for efficiency

## Investment Plans
- **Current Investment**: ₹72 crore already invested
- **Planned Investment**: Additional ₹177 crore for platform scaling
- **Total Commitment**: ₹249 crore for EV platform development

## Industry Context
India's auto component industry clocked a turnover of $74.1 billion in FY24, contributing about 2.3% to India's GDP and employing over 1.5 million people directly.

### Growth Drivers
- **Strong Domestic Demand**: Especially for UVs and higher-capacity motorcycles
- **Rising Exports**: Auto components have doubled to 6% of total production in FY24
- **EV Transition**: Ongoing shift to electric vehicles creating new opportunities
- **Government Support**: PLI schemes and Automotive Mission Plan driving localization
