# Apollo Tyres
## Company Overview
Apollo Tyres is a leading global tyre manufacturer with a dominant presence in India and a significant production footprint in Europe (Hungary, Netherlands). The company specializes in manufacturing high-performance tyres for passenger vehicles, commercial trucks and buses (TBR), two-wheelers, off-highway vehicles, and agricultural equipment, marketed under the Apollo Tyres and Vredestein brands.
