# Setco Automotive

## Company Overview

Setco Automotive Limited (formerly Gujarat Setco Clutch) manufactures clutches and automotive components for commercial vehicles and farm tractors. Founded 1982; headquartered in Mumbai. Sells under LIPE and Lava Cast brands. Products include clutch cover/disc assemblies, repair kits, release bearings, flywheels, universal joint crosses, water pumps, load cushions, torque rod bushes, and automotive lubricants. Exports to the US.
