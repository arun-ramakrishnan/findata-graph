# KPIT Technologies Limited

## Company Overview

KPIT Technologies Limited was founded in 1990 in Pune (formerly KPIT Engineering, renamed in 2019). The company provides embedded software, AI, and digital solutions for the automobile and mobility industries. It has a global footprint across the Americas, UK, and Europe, delivering AUTOSAR-compliant platforms, fuel-cell electric vehicle (FCEV) solutions, and battery management systems to leading automotive OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers.
