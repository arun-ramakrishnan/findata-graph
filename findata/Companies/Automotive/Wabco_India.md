# Wabco India

## Company Overview

Wabco India is a leading automotive technology company specializing in commercial vehicle braking systems, stability control, and advanced safety technologies for the automotive industry. The company has established itself as a key supplier to major commercial vehicle manufacturers in India and international markets, with a strong focus on vehicle safety and advanced braking solutions.

## Business Segments

### Braking Systems
- **Air Braking Systems**: Complete air braking systems for commercial vehicles
- **Brake Components**: Air compressors, brake valves, and related components
- **Anti-lock Braking Systems (ABS)**: Advanced ABS technology for improved safety
- **Electronic Braking Systems**: Electronic braking and stability control systems
- **Parking Brakes**: Parking brake systems for commercial vehicles

### Advanced Safety Technologies
- **Stability Control**: Electronic stability control systems for vehicles
- **Collision Avoidance**: Advanced driver assistance systems
- **Tire Pressure Monitoring**: TPMS for improved safety
- **Advanced Driver Assistance**: ADAS technologies for commercial vehicles
- **Electronic Control Systems**: Electronic control units for vehicle safety

### Vehicle Control Systems
- **Pneumatic Systems**: Complete pneumatic system solutions
- **Suspension Systems**: Air suspension and related components
- **Transmission Control**: Automated transmission control systems
- **Coupling Systems**: Vehicle coupling and trailer connection systems
