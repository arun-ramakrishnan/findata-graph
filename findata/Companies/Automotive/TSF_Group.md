# TSF Group

## Company Overview
TSF Group is the Chennai-based automotive group (founded by the TVS family) whose operating companies include Brakes India and Wheels India. In 2026 its companies formed a 50:50 joint venture with Bosch Limited for next-generation electronically-controlled air systems for commercial vehicles (Bosch concall: the JV "will operate out of Chennai").
