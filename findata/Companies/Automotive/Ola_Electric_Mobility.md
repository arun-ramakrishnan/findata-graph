# Ola Electric Mobility

## Company Overview
Ola Electric Mobility Limited (incorporated 2017, HQ Bengaluru) develops, manufactures, and sells electric vehicles in India through Automotive and Cell segments. It offers electric scooters plus EV components (battery packs, motors, vehicle frames, cells), charging solutions, and service centres, and is building its Gigafactory to vertically integrate battery-cell production.

- Next growth phase targets "follower" customers via education/marketing emphasizing ~90% lower OpEx versus ICE vehicles.
