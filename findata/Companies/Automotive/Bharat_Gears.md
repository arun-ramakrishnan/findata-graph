# Bharat Gears

## Company Overview

Bharat Gears Limited (incorporated 1971; headquartered in Mumbai) manufactures automotive gears and precision-machined components. Products include ring gears, pinions, transmission gears, shafts, differential gears, bevel gears, clutch assemblies, turbochargers, driveline products, axle shafts, flywheel assemblies, propeller shaft components, and heat-treating furnaces. Serves OEMs across automotive, agriculture, construction, utilities, and EV segments. Exports to the US, Mexico, Spain.
