# VinFast Auto

## Business Overview
VinFast Auto Limited is a Vietnamese automobile company that designs and manufactures a range of electric vehicles (EVs), including cars, e-scooters, and buses. The company focuses on creating a global presence with its smart EV lineup, advanced technology, and a focus on sustainable mobility.

## Key Highlights from Recent Quarter
- E-scooter sales exploded 535% YoY driven by government policy banning gasoline motorbikes from city centers starting mid-2026
- They delivered 12,052 e-scooters and e-bikes representing a 535% increase year-over-year and 73% quarter over quarter growth
- The strong momentum in e-scooters volume showcases the accelerated shift toward electric two-wheelers following the announcement of a new policy to restrict gasoline motorbikes from entering central districts in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City starting in mid 2026
- US expansion paused due to tariffs and EV market instability; no new dealership openings planned until conditions stabilize
- They're not going to push hard in the US given the tariff situation and the instability in the market until they see how that settles
- Battery costs declining 10-12% annually with transition to next-gen batteries expected to drive further unit cost reductions
- Battery costs have continued to decline quarter over quarter, extending the downward trajectory established in 2024
- On average, they have seen battery prices come down by approximately 10% or 12% year-over-year across various models
- They are transitioning to new, more cost-efficient battery generation underpinned by their suppliers' technology advancements that bring meaningfully lower unit costs
- Vietnam to remain 70-80% of 2026 deliveries; new VF6/VF7 platform designed for improved cost competitiveness to drive international growth
- In 2026, they expect more contribution as the green series scales more and the new VF6 VF7 platform will come online
- These products are designed to be more competitive both from the cost as well as market perspective
- Vietnam will remain their anchor market in the near term, with next year they expect Vietnam to account for roughly 70% to 80% of total deliveries with balance coming from international markets as they continue to develop
