# Emerald Tyre Manufacturers

Emerald Tyre Manufacturers is an Indian company specializing in Off-Highway Tyre (OHT), manufacturing and supplying a wide range of industrial tyre-wheel solutions including solid and pneumatic tyres, for material handling (forklifts, loaders), airport ground support, port trailers, mining, and agricultural equipment under the brand name "GRECKSTER," serving both domestic and global markets.

## Business Overview
- **Market Focus**: Off-Highway Tyre (OHT) specialist
- **Product Range**: Industrial tyre-wheel solutions for material handling
- **Brand**: GRECKSTER
- **Geographic Markets**: Domestic and global (Europe, Australia, Latin America, Saudi Arabia, Southern Africa)
