## The Chatter — Dixon Motherson Biocon

**Supply-constrained 80% growth with structural demand shift:** Q1 FY27 deliveries up 80%; management believes growth could have been significantly higher with more capacity — 50–60K units/month demand vs 35K capacity. Views surge as structural (11% penetration, rising in Tier 2/3 towns), not cyclical.

**Dealership model to double footprint without capex:** Targeting 1,400–1,500 stores in 2 years (from ~700); dealers invest in stores, carry inventory, bear opex; new store additions deliberately paused until Maharashtra plant comes online.

**Capacity doubling to 77K units/month by early FY28:** Phase 1 of 100-acre Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar plant (42K units/month) goes live by Diwali FY27; Phase 2 adds another 42K; land secured for further expansion.

**₹2,500 crore fundraise for capacity + product velocity:** ₹1,300 cr QIP done; ₹1,200 cr preferential allotment pending (Hero MotoCorp ₹960 cr, India-Japan Fund ₹200 cr, founders ₹40 cr); funds for Phase 2 capex and accelerated product launches.

**1,600 bps EBITDA margin improvement; profitability in 3–4 quarters:** EBITDA margin from –16% to +1% (ex-other income: –20% to –2–3%); driven by calibrated price hikes, premium mix shift, non-vehicle revenue (13%→14%), fixed cost discipline. Targets sustainable recurring EBITDA profitability in 3–4 quarters.

> "We've delivered a very strong improvement in our EBITDA trajectory over the last four to five quarters. Compared to Q1 last year, our EBITDA margin improved by 1,600 basis points, moving from negative 16% to positive 1% including other income. Excluding other income, margins improved from roughly negative 20% to around negative 2–3%. This improvement came from effectively managing commodity inflation."
> — Tarun Mehta, Co-founder and CEO

## The Chatter — Adani Power Motilal Oswal

**Demand outrunning supply:** Inquiries +95% YoY and paid pre-orders +160% (from ~19–20K/month a year ago to ~50K/month), but Hosur can supply only ~35K units/month — ~15K units of unserved demand; even Hero's stores are no help ("we do not have sufficient supply to keep our own stores stocked").

**Aurangabad doubles capacity:** The new plant takes output from 35K to 70–77K units/month (~9.2 lakh annualised), with a further raise to ~1.4 million units within a year to a year and a half.

**First positive EBITDA, but not yet sustainable:** Q1 EBITDA came in at ~+0.8% for the first time; management explicitly does not consider it sustainable yet given aluminium/copper/steel/polymer inflation — no price hike was taken in Q2 while they watch demand.

**Structural, not cyclical:** E2W penetration is ~11% (+44% YoY) and scooter EV penetration ~25% ("one out of every four scooters sold is electric"); scooters have gone from 30% to 40% of the two-wheeler market with premium (125cc+) scooters rising from ~20–25% to ~54–55%. Karnataka/Kerala already at 1-in-3, heading to 50–60% over time.

**Moat in battery + charging, not in Hero:** 8.5–9-year-old Gen 1 fleet still retains ~83% charge; 6,000+ DC fast chargers (India's largest 2W network); LEX charging standard adopted by BIS and open-licensed, with IEC application pending. Hero MotoCorp is a purely financial investor — no shared R&D, IP, or distribution; Ather built its own network from ~350 to 700+ stores ("effectively one store a day").

> "Our capacity through the new plant in Aurangabad... will improve our capacity from 35,000 per month to approximately 70,000–77,000 per month."
> — Abhinav, VP Finance
