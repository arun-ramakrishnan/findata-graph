# Automotive Axles Limited

## Company Overview

Automotive Axles Limited is a leading manufacturer of automotive components specializing in drive axles, non-drive axles, and related products for commercial and defense vehicles in India. The company serves truck and bus manufacturers, original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), military, and off-highway vehicle sectors. Established in 1981 and headquartered in Mysore, India, the company has built a strong reputation as a reliable supplier in the automotive components industry.
