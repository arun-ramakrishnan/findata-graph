# Federal-Mogul Goetze (India)

## Company Overview

Federal-Mogul Goetze (India) is a leading automotive component manufacturer specializing in engine bearings, pistons, and other precision components for the automotive industry. The company operates as part of the Federal-Mogul global network, providing high-quality components to automotive OEMs and aftermarket customers in India and international markets.
