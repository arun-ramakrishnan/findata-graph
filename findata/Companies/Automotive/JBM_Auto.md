# JBM Auto

## Company Overview
JBM Auto Limited (incorporated 1996; HQ Gurugram, Haryana) is an automotive components and electric bus manufacturer serving passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, two-wheelers, and farm equipment OEMs. It operates through three divisions — Component (sheet metal, BIW, chassis, assemblies), Tool Room (tools, dies, moulds), and OEM (low-emission/electric buses, lithium-ion battery solutions, EV charging infrastructure) — and is expanding internationally, including into the Middle East.
