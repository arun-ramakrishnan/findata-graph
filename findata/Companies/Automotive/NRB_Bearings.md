# NRB Bearings

## Company Overview

NRB Bearings Limited (incorporated 1965; headquartered in Mumbai) manufactures ball and roller bearings for OEMs in automotive and mobility applications in India and internationally. Product range includes drawn cup needle bearings, cylindrical roller bearings, polyamide/steel needle bearing cages, special ball bearings, thrust bearings, tapered/spherical roller bearings, and rocker-arm bearings. Subsidiary NRB Thailand serves international markets.
