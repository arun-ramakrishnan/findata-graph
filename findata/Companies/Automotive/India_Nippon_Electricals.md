# India Nippon Electricals

## Company Overview
India Nippon Electricals Limited (INEL), incorporated in 1984 and headquartered in Chennai, is a leading manufacturer of electronic ignition systems and powertrain components for two-wheelers and three-wheelers. A joint venture with Japan's Kokusan Denki (and a subsidiary of Lucas Indian Service Limited / the Uno Minda group), the company produces flywheel magnetos, CDI/TCI units, regulator-rectifiers, ignition coils, and a fast-expanding portfolio of sensors (speed, TMAP, TPMS, RPAS ultrasonic) and EV components (DC-DC converters, traction motors, motor controllers, ISG machines, EFI ECUs). Products serve 2W, 3W, small gasoline engines, portable gensets and general-purpose engines.
