# Jamna Auto Industries

## Company Overview

Jamna Auto Industries Limited (JAI) is India's largest manufacturer of leaf springs and suspension products for commercial vehicles, founded in 1954 and headquartered in Faridabad. Products include parabolic and tapered leaf springs, lift axles, air suspension, stabilizer bars, bushes, and allied suspension components. Serves OEMs (Tata, Mahindra, Ashok Leyland) and aftermarket; exports to 25+ countries.
