# Waaree Renewable Technologies | Small Cap | Energy

Waaree Renewable Technologies is a Public Limited Company based in Mumbai, specializing in power generation from renewable sources and offering EPC services. The company's energy generation site is situated in Maharashtra.

Strong growth in EPC services demand across India's solar energy sector.

### Key Government Initiatives

#### Solar Parks
- GOI has approved 55 solar parks with aggregate capacity of ~41GW
- By Sep'25: 13 GW commissioned, 12.9 GW under tendering, 15 GW under construction
- Solar Park policy extended till FY2029

#### PLI Scheme
- Government-backed initiative to promote domestic manufacturing of Solar PV modules
- Over ₹53.9k crore invested & 44.4k jobs created under PLI scheme till Sep'25

#### Rooftop Solar
- PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana: Proposed outlay of 75k Cr for 1 Crore households in India
- Rooftop solar installed on ~24 lakhs households
- ~13k Cr subsidy released by Dec 2025
- Government targets to add 30 GW of residential rooftop solar capacity by 2027

#### Energy Storage Initiatives
##### VGF Scheme (PSDF)
- 30 GWh of BESS
- Budgetary allocation of ₹5,400 Cr through PSDF

##### ISTS Waiver
- ISTS charges waiver for co-located BESS projects commenced till June'28 for 12 years
- Supports storage project economics

##### Minimum Content Requirements
- ≤20% local content requirement for VGF scheme
- ≤2 hours duration ESS requirement for solar PV tender

#### Other Supportive Measures
- Minimum domestic content requirement
- Approved list of models & manufacturers to ensure quality & performance of modules
- Reduction in GST on solar modules from 12% to 5%, reducing project costs for developers

### End-to-End Renewable Energy Ecosystem

The group offers comprehensive solutions across solar value chain:
- **PV Modules**: Solar photovoltaic module manufacturing
- **EPC**: Engineering, procurement, and construction services
- **O&M**: Operations and maintenance of solar plants
- **Rooftop Solar**: Residential and commercial rooftop installations
- **Floating Solar**: Water-based solar installations
- **BESS**: Battery energy storage systems
- **Allied Equipment**: Supporting equipment and components

This integral portfolio positions the company to capture value across the full solar and energy transition value chain.
