# Emmvee Photovoltaic | Small Cap | Engineering & Capital Goods

Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Ltd is India's second largest integrated solar PV module and cell maker with module and cell capacity. It produces TOPCon and Mono PER modules, ALMM-listed, serving major IPPs and C&I clients.
