# Suzlon Energy

## Company Overview
Suzlon Energy Limited (incorporated 1995, HQ Pune) is India's leading pure-play wind-energy company, designing, developing, manufacturing, and supplying Wind Turbine Generators (WTGs) and providing turnkey wind-farm solutions across the WTG, Foundry & Forging, O&M services, and Others segments. It offers project execution, procurement, marketing, and operation & maintenance services, with advanced R&D centres focused on lowering the cost of energy and maximizing turbine availability.

## Key Insights (from newsletter)
- Order book scaled sharply to ~6.4 GW by Jan'26, diversified across captive/C&I, auctions, and PSUs with a healthy state/scope mix; increased EPC offering (~27%) enhances competitiveness.
- Wind and solar are complementary across intraday and seasonal cycles—blended RE-RTC solutions with storage lower delivered power cost and manage intermittency; wind has a strong domestic supply chain (India is a net exporter), unlike import-dependent solar/BESS.
- India's wind sector is in a structural upcycle: onshore potential ~1,164 GW (150m hub height), India targeting ~160 GW wind by 2035; demand drivers include grid balancing for rising peak load, data-centre RTC-RE mandates, GST cut on wind equipment (12%→5%), repowering (~25.4 GW), and exports.
- Energy demand projected to grow at ~7% CAGR (FY24–FY30, vs. 5% historically), underpinned by urbanization, data centres, EVs, and green hydrogen.

## The Chatter — BEL, HUL, Tata Capital

**Structural multi-year wind demand:** peak power demand has crossed 270 GW; annual installations expected to cross 10 GW near-term and reach 15 GW by FY31 — India is on track for ~100 GW of wind by 2030 (57 GW installed, strong STU/PGCIL/C&I pipeline).

**Record Q1 deliveries despite Red Sea disruption:** 506 MW delivered in Q1 — highest-ever first-quarter deliveries; supply-chain/logistics disruptions deferred ~10–20% of deliveries, expected to be recovered in coming quarters (delayed, not lost).

**Devco model scaling fast:** ~60% of new orders now via the development (Devco) model, in which Suzlon funds up to 25% (land) then recycles capital by transferring the site to the customer once a term sheet is in place — keeping momentum while conserving capital.

**ASP improving on product mix:** Average Selling Price rose from ₹5.6 cr/MW (Q1 FY26) to ₹6.3 cr/MW (Q1 FY27); market pricing remains "healthy and rational."

**BESS target 3.1 GW by FY31:** in discussions with multiple partners; initial partnership arrangements expected to close in the next couple of months.

**Repowering — ~25 GW latent opportunity:** much of India's installed wind fleet is 10–20 years old, low-hub-height, small turbines; modern turbines can generate substantially more energy from the same footprint.

**ALMM framework favours Indian OEMs:** the Approved List of Models & Manufacturers SOP levels the playing field against import-dependent competitors; Suzlon is fully compliant.

**Suzlon 2.0 ambition intact:** despite Q1 margin pressure, long-term target of 25% CAGR over five years; a new independent engineering business (Foundry, Forging, Bearings) targets higher non-Suzlon / non-wind / export exposure.
