# KPI Green Energy

## Company Overview

KPI Green Energy is a renewable energy company focused on developing and operating solar and wind power projects in India. The company operates as both an Independent Power Producer (IPP) and Captive Power Producer (CPP), providing clean energy solutions to diverse customers. KPI Green Energy is strategically transitioning towards a higher IPP mix and exploring opportunities in Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) to capitalize on emerging market opportunities in grid stability and energy storage.
