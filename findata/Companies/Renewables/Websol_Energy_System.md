# Websol Energy System

Websol Energy System Limited, an Indian company established in 1990 and located in Falta SEZ, specializes in manufacturing photovoltaic crystalline solar cells and related modules. Their top-quality products cater to commercial and industrial setups in India and abroad.

## Business Overview
- **Core Business**: Solar cell and module manufacturing
- **Technology Evolution**: From mono-crystalline to multi-crystalline to Mono-PERC
- **Capacity Expansion**: From 1MW in 1990s to 1200 MW projected by FY25
