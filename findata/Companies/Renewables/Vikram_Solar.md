# Vikram Solar

## Company Overview

Vikram Solar and its subsidiaries specialize in manufacturing and selling solar photovoltaic modules and systems. Their product portfolio includes a variety of monocrystalline silicon-based modules such as PERC, N-Type, and HJT modules, available in bifacial or monofacial configurations.
