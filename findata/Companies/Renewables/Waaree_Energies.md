- ALMM extensions: cells since June 2026, ingots/wafer from June 2028

**FY26 Guidance**

- Projects progressing as planned
- Maintained strong financial outlook

### Margin Management

**Gross Margin Strategy**
- Margins stable and increasing versus trailing quarter
- Managing at gross margin level due to input cost trends
- Manufacturing cost optimization with back-to-back orders
- DCR modules earning 300-350 bps margin premium over non-DCR
- Export mix: normally 20-25%, currently heavy on exports

**Inventory Management**
- Inventory spike of INR 1,300 crore due to goods in transit for export orders
- Export orders shipped but not yet reached customers
- Revenue recognition pending delivery
- Normalization expected next quarter
- Typical goods in transit: INR 300-400 crores

### Advanced Technologies

**Battery Energy Storage Systems**
- Global battery storage capacity expected to grow fivefold by 2030
- Over 1,800 gigawatt hours capacity target
- India's government plan: 236 gigawatt hours by 2032
- Investment of INR ~8,000 crores in battery energy storage
- Right time to enter this important segment

**PLI Projects**
- Won PLI for 6 GW of ingots-wafers, cells and modules
- Uped capacity from 6 to 10 GW for ingots/wafer and cells
- Projects on schedule: 2027 completion
- Cells: 2027, Ingots/wafer: 2027
