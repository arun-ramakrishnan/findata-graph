# Oswal Pumps

## Company Overview

Oswal Pumps manufactures solar-powered and grid-connected submersible and monoblock pumps, electric motors (induction and submersible), and solar modules under the 'Oswal' brand.

## Key Insights (The Chatter #50)

- Jal Jeevan Mission extension and increased funding is a strong positive for the pumping industry's large pump requirement
- Not directly in JJM EPC contracts but benefits via pump supply to EPC contractors; entire current order book is solar pumps
- Scheme had slowed earlier due to funding delays; expects receivables to be comfortable by Q1 next year
- Nationwide distribution network across all states positions it to scale JJM participation quickly
- Indirect (EPC-supply) participation shielded it from major receivable challenges
