# Inox Wind

## Company Overview

Inox Wind manufactures and sells Wind Turbine Generators (WTGs). The company also provides Erection, Procurement & Commissioning (EPC), Operations & Maintenance (O&M), and common infrastructure facilities for WTGs and wind farm development services.
