# Smarten Power Systems Limited

**SMARTEN-SM.NS | NSE: ₹120-150 range | Market Cap: ₹350 crores | P/E: 25-30x**

Smarten Power Systems Limited is an emerging player in India's power backup and renewable energy solutions market. The company designs, manufactures, and markets a comprehensive range of power backup products, solar energy solutions, and energy storage systems for residential, commercial, and industrial applications.

## Company Overview

- **Founded:** 2010
- **Headquarters:** New Delhi, India
- **Revenue:** ₹250-300 crores (FY2024)
- **Product Portfolio:** 50+ SKUs across power backup and solar categories
- **Manufacturing:** 2 facilities in North India (150,000 sq. ft. total)
- **Distribution:** 1,500+ dealers, 10,000+ retail touchpoints
- **Export Markets:** Africa, Middle East, Southeast Asia (20+ countries)

### 1. Power Backup Solutions (65% of revenue)
- **Home UPS Systems:** 600VA to 10KVA range
- **Inverters:** Pure sine wave and square wave inverters
- **PCUs (Power Conditioning Units):** For telecom and industrial applications
- **Charge Controllers:** PWM and MPPT controllers
- **Batteries:** Lead-acid and lithium-ion battery solutions

### 2. Solar Energy Solutions (30% of revenue)
- **Solar PCUs:** Hybrid solar inverters (1KVA to 10KVA)
- **Solar Charge Controllers:** MPPT controllers for solar applications
- **Solar Panels:** Polycrystalline and monocristalline panels (trading)
- **Solar Home Lighting Systems:** Off-grid solar solutions
- **Solar Water Pumps:** Agricultural and residential applications

### 3. Energy Storage Systems (5% of revenue - fastest growing)
- **Lithium-ion Battery Packs:** For home and commercial use
- **Energy Storage Solutions:** For solar power systems
- **Portable Power Stations:** For outdoor and emergency use

## Industry Position & Market Insights

**Power Backup Market Leadership:**
- Emerging player in India's ₹15,000 crore power backup market

**Solar Energy Market Opportunity:**

**Market Trends:**

- Growth in rural electrification and off-grid solutions

- Government initiatives for renewable energy adoption

**Competitive Landscape:**
- **Direct Competitors:** Luminous Power Technologies, Microtek International, Su-Kam Power Systems

## Key Insights

**Vision & Growth Strategy**
- Aims to become a prominent consumer electronics brand for energy solutions in India and other emerging global markets
- "Our long term vision is to transform Smarten into a trusted mass market consumer electronics brand, especially in batteries, inverters and distributed solar energy solutions, not just in India but across emerging global markets such as Africa, Asia and Middle East."

**Revenue Targets**
- Aims for approximately 30% annual revenue growth
- Targeting a doubling of its current revenue within next three years
- "Every year we are targeting about 30% growth, and within the next three years, we are targeting for the double of this revenue."

**Battery Technology**
- While lead-acid batteries still dominate Smarten's sales, lithium-ion batteries are gaining traction
- Local "manufacturing" of lithium-ion batteries is currently limited to assembly of imported cells
- "Right now, our majority of batteries portion come from lead - acid batteries only, but for the last two years, lithium - ion batteries are taking place instead of lead - acid batteries. But in lithium - ion batteries, all companies are importing cell from outside India and lithium - ion batteries are more of an assembly of electronics because there is a BMS unit and there is a cell."
