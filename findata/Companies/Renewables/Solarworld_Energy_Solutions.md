# Solarworld Energy

Solarworld Energy Solutions Limited provides solar energy solutions in India. The company offers engineering, procurement, and construction services for solar power projects. It also provides solar photovoltaic (PV) modules for ground mount solar EPC services, rooftop installations, as well as battery energy storage systems for energy grids and renewable energy applications.

## Business Overview
- **Incorporated**: 2013, based in Noida, India
- **Services**: Solar EPC, PV modules, battery energy storage systems
- **Clients**: Public sector undertakings, commercial & industrial clients
- **Project Experience**: 253.67 MW AC/336.17 MW DC completed, 765 MW AC/994 MW DC ongoing

## Industry Context

### India's Solar Growth Story
India's solar capacity has grown exponentially:
- **FY16**: 7.12 GW
- **FY26**: 119 GW (over 50% share as of July 2025)
- **Target**: 280 GW by 2030 (part of 500 GW non-fossil fuel goal)

### Growth Drivers
- Falling PV module prices
- Low-cost financing availability
- Government policy support (ALMM, PLI incentives)
- Emerging green hydrogen push
- Large global players entering Indian market
- New export opportunities for Indian companies

### Market Projections

- **EPC Market**: Projected to grow from ₹365 billion to ₹875-900 billion by FY30 at ~11% CAGR
