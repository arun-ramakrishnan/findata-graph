# Shubhshree Biofuels Energy

## Company Overview

Shubhshree Biofuels Energy Ltd is an Indian bioenergy company manufacturing biomass fuels (pellets and briquettes) from agricultural waste as sustainable alternatives to coal and fossil fuels, serving industrial and commercial sectors across northern India.

## Key Insights (Points & Figures #22)

- Aggregates crop residue from 2,50,000+ farmers; converts waste into bioenergy while reducing stubble-burning emissions
- $21.68 billion addressable TAM (2023); India generates 683M+ tonnes of agricultural residue annually, much unutilized or burned
- Active in 20+ thermal power plants, targeting 50+ plants by fiscal year-end
- India surplus biomass ~228 MMT annually (less than 20% tapped); biomass power capacity forecast to grow from 11.58 GW (2025) to 14.97 GW (2030)
- Ecosystem involves 1,000+ villages, 20,000+ livelihoods, 2,500+ green jobs; partners include NTPC, Sun Pharma, Reliance, ITC
