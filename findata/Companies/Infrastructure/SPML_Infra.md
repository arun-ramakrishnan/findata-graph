# SPML Infra

SPML Infra Limited (formerly Subhash Projects and Marketing; renamed 2010; incorporated 1981) is a leading Indian infrastructure developer focused on sustainable smart-city services — water supply, bulk-water transmission, wastewater/sanitation, power distribution, irrigation, and BESS. Headquartered in Kolkata.
