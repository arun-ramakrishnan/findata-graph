# VA Tech Wabag

VA Tech Wabag Limited is a multinational water technology company based in India, providing drinking water, waste/industrial water treatment, and desalination solutions to industries (oil & gas, power, steel, fertilizer, F&B) and municipalities globally. Operations span multiple continents. Headquartered in Chennai.
