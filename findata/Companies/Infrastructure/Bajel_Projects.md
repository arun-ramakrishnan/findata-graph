# Bajel Projects

## Company Overview
Bajel Projects (NSE: BAJEL), a Bajaj Group company demerged from Bajaj Electricals, manufactures power transmission and distribution (T&D) towers, monopoles, and related infrastructure for the electricity and renewables sectors.
