# Federal Bank

## Company Overview
The Federal Bank Limited, established in 1931 and headquartered in Aluva, Kerala, is a leading private sector bank with over 90 years of heritage. It operates 1,300+ branches and 1,600+ ATMs with a pan-India footprint and a particularly strong South/West presence, focusing on retail banking, NRI banking, SME financing, and digital banking delivered through strategic fintech partnerships.
