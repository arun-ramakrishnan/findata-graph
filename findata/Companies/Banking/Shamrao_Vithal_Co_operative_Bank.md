## Comprehensive Overview

Shamrao Vithal Co-operative Bank Limited is an urban co-operative bank established in 1906, with headquarters in Mumbai, Maharashtra. With over 115 years of banking heritage, SVC has grown from a small co-operative society to one of India's prominent urban co-operative banks.

The bank operates through a network of 180+ branches and 150+ ATMs primarily in Maharashtra, with some presence in other states. SVC focuses on retail banking, SME financing, and community-based banking while maintaining strong co-operative principles and customer relationships.
