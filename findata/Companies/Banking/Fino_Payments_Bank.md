# Fino Payments Bank

## Company Overview
Fino Payments Bank operates a high-volume, digital-first financial services platform focused on transaction banking and remittances for the unbanked population. It utilizes a vast merchant-led distribution network to maintain an asset-light model and is transitioning into a Small Finance Bank.
