## Comprehensive Overview

Saraswat Co-operative Bank Limited is a leading urban co-operative bank in India, established in 1918 with headquarters in Mumbai, Maharashtra. With over 105 years of banking heritage, Saraswat Bank has grown from a small co-operative society to one of India's largest co-operative banks.

The bank operates through a network of 280+ branches and 260+ ATMs primarily in Maharashtra, with some presence in other states. Saraswat Bank focuses on retail banking, SME financing, housing finance, and digital banking while maintaining strong community relationships.
