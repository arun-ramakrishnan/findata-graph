# South Indian Bank

## Company Overview

South Indian Bank is one of India's oldest private sector banks, established in 1929 during the Swadeshi movement. Headquartered in Thrissur, Kerala, it has grown from a regional player to a nationwide bank with over 900 branches and 1,500+ ATMs across India, focusing on service orientation while leveraging technology for customer experience.

## Key Insights (Scaling_Through_Slowdowns)

- One of the earliest banks in South India, established during the Swadeshi movement to provide a safe repository for savings and offer need-based credit, freeing the business community from exploitative money lenders
- **NIM compression trough**: Q2 NIM at 2.8% represents the trough with full rate cut transmission; more severely impacted than peers due to concentrated exposure to short-duration corporate lending that repriced faster. Management expects NIMs to recover going forward. (P R Seshadri, MD & CEO)
- **Balance sheet mix strategy**: 40% of advance book from corporate segment; 18-month plan to reduce corporate to ~33% while improving yields via mid-corporate focus and expanding higher-yielding retail/MSME segments. (Dolphy Jose, Executive Director)
- **Gold loan growth**: Book at ₹18,845 crore (20.4% of total loan book) with ~₹1,800 crore recent growth — but approaching prudential caps, so indefinite expansion is not possible
- **Investment book expansion**: Directional bet on falling interest rates; HTM book growth plus additional ₹7,000 crore added to AFS book in quarter to capture capital gains and trading opportunities when rates soften
- **ECL norms impact minimal**: New Stage-II provisioning requirement (from 40 bps to 500 bps) applies to a portfolio under 100 bps; with 90%+ provision coverage including write-offs, P&L impact is not material
