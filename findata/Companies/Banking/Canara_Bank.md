# Canara Bank

## Company Overview

Canara Bank is a major Indian public sector bank (large-cap) with an extensive branch network across India and a particularly strong presence in South India. The bank provides comprehensive banking and financial services—including personal, business, agricultural, and government banking—to retail, corporate, and government customers, alongside its housing finance subsidiary Can Fin Homes.

## Key Insights (Embracing the Unknown)
- Management notes higher slippages this quarter driven by newly automated asset-classification logic applied to agriculture and MSME segments; framed as a one-off quarter impact rather than an ongoing trend.
- Priority Sector Lending Certificate (PSLC) income is supply-demand driven; last year the bank sold nearly ₹886,000 crore of PSLCs, aided by gold loans classified under agriculture.
- RBI regulations reclassifying Agri Gold Loans as Retail Gold Loans may reduce sellable PSL volume, but tighter supply is pushing commission rates up (recently ~3% vs <2% earlier, and historically 0.4%-0.5%).
- Management expects PSLC income to be broadly stabilized as higher commissions offset lower volumes.
