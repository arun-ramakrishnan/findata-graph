# HDFC Bank
## Company Overview
HDFC Bank is India's largest private sector bank, formed by the landmark HDFC Limited merger. It offers a comprehensive range of banking and financial services — retail banking, wholesale banking, treasury, insurance, and asset management — through 9,600+ branches serving 100+ million customers, with overseas operations in Bahrain, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Dubai.
