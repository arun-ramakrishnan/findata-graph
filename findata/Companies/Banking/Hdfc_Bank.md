# HDFC Bank
## Company Overview
HDFC Bank is India's largest private sector bank, formed by the landmark HDFC Limited merger. It offers a comprehensive range of banking and financial services — retail banking, wholesale banking, treasury, insurance, and asset management — through 9,600+ branches serving 100+ million customers, with overseas operations in Bahrain, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Dubai.

## The Chatter — HDFC, Groww, Yes Bank, Havells & More

**Credit growth re-accelerating:** Delivered 12% system credit growth (vs. 5.5% last year), ahead of the 10.5-11.5% system estimate, signalling regained lending momentum.

**Distribution & tech as long-term investments:** Branch network nearly doubled to ~9,700, customers to ~100 million, and tech investments more than quadrupled to ~$1B — positioning the post-merger franchise for future profit growth.

**LDR no longer a constraint:** Loan-to-deposit ratio is no longer a binding constraint; the bank has gained deposit market share (~30-50 bps annually for five years), removing a key investor concern on loan-book growth.

**Unified in-house AI platform:** Built a unified AI platform spanning enterprise search, document extraction, voice agents and the full AI development lifecycle — enabling rapid AI-agent deployment without custom system interfaces.

**Granular deposit mix improving:** Less-than-₹3-crores deposits now make up 47% of net accretion (vs. 31% in FY25), reducing reliance on volatile wholesale funds; CASA from the merger group has grown from ~₹50,000 cr to ₹86,000 cr in 2.5 years.

**ROA over ROE discipline:** Management prioritizes Return on Assets over ROE to avoid masking credit risk in leverage — underscoring disciplined capital allocation.

> "ROA is what we should focus on. ROE is an intermediate metric. If you take higher risk and bring that into the top line, you may lose it in the credit loss below the ROA line. So focus on the returns on assets."
> — Management, Executive Team
