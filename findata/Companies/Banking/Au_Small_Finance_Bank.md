# AU Small Finance Bank Limited

## Company Overview

AU Small Finance Bank Limited (incorporated 1996 as AU Financiers (India) Limited; renamed in May 2017) is a Jaipur-headquartered universal bank. It operates through four segments: **Treasury**, **Retail Banking**, **Wholesale Banking**, and **Other Banking Operations**.

The bank serves individuals, senior citizens, SMEs, NRIs, professionals, government institutions, corporates, partnerships, associations, and trusts through bank branches, correspondent banking outlets, and business correspondents.
