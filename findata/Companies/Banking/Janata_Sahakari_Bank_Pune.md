## Comprehensive Overview

Janata Sahakari Bank Pune Limited is an urban co-operative bank established in 1949, with headquarters in Pune, Maharashtra. With over 70 years of banking heritage, Janata Sahakari Bank has grown from a small co-operative society to one of India's leading urban co-operative banks.

The bank operates through a network of 70+ branches and 60+ ATMs primarily in Maharashtra, with a strong presence in Pune region. The bank focuses on retail banking, SME financing, and community-based banking while maintaining strong co-operative principles and customer relationships.
