## Comprehensive Overview

Tamilnad Mercantile Bank (TMB) is a scheduled private sector bank established in 1921, with headquarters in Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu. With over 100 years of banking heritage, TMB has grown from a regional bank to a pan-India presence with 500+ branches and 1,200+ ATMs.

The bank focuses on retail banking, SME financing, NRI banking, and agricultural banking. TMB has a strong presence in Tamil Nadu and has been expanding to other states including Karnataka, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Maharashtra.

TMB is known for its prudent banking approach, strong asset quality, and customer-centric service philosophy. The bank has been consistently recognized for its strong governance and operational standards.
