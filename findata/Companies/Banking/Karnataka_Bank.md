## Comprehensive Overview

Karnataka Bank Limited is a private sector bank established in 1924, with headquarters in Mangalore, Karnataka. With over 100 years of banking heritage, Karnataka Bank has grown from a regional bank to a pan-India presence with 900+ branches and 1,200+ ATMs.

The bank focuses on retail banking, SME financing, agricultural banking, and NRI banking. Karnataka Bank has a strong presence in Karnataka and has been expanding to other states including Maharashtra, Goa, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana.
