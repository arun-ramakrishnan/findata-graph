## Comprehensive Overview

Dhanlaxmi Bank Limited is a scheduled private sector bank established in 1927, with headquarters in Thrissur, Kerala. With over 95 years of banking heritage, Dhanlaxmi Bank has grown from a regional bank to a pan-India presence with 280+ branches and 250+ ATMs.

The bank focuses on retail banking, SME financing, NRI banking, and agricultural banking. Dhanlaxmi Bank has a strong presence in South India, particularly in Kerala, and has been expanding to other states.
