## Comprehensive Overview

Central Bank of India is a public sector bank established in 1911, with headquarters in Mumbai, Maharashtra. With over 110 years of banking heritage, Central Bank of India is one of India's oldest and most established public sector banks.

The bank operates through a network of 4,600+ branches and 3,800+ ATMs across India. Central Bank of India offers a wide range of banking and financial services including retail banking, corporate banking, agricultural banking, and SME financing. The bank has a strong presence in rural and semi-urban markets and plays a significant role in financial inclusion.

**Post-PCA transformation, quantified:** from PCA exit (April 2021) to Q1 FY27 — quarterly net profit ₹206 cr → ₹1,324 cr; CRAR 13.1% → 18.28%; CET1 11.06% → 16.54%; NNPA 5.09% → 0.49%. June quarter: global business +18.29%, deposits +11.68%, CASA +11.16%, advances +28.58%, RAM +21.38%, PAT +13.26%, GNPA 2.6%, ROE 14.92%, ROA just above 1%.

**Slippages contained despite agri hits:** slippage ratio down to 0.40% (1.47% in March 2025) even after ~₹400 cr of agriculture slippages (Maharashtra + Uttar Pradesh weather hits); annualised ~0.80% — the sub-1% asset-quality guidance holds "with sufficient cushion."

**Profitability levers:** NIM 3.06% (June), guided to stay above 3% for the year; RAM at 68% of advances (guide 65:35 ±5); ~61% of advances EBLR-linked — a repo/benchmark hike would support yields quickly. CD ratio 74% leaves lending headroom toward higher-yield MSME clusters (cluster-based products + quarterly outreach camps).

**Digital lending platform as the cost/underwriting engine:** retail acquisition migrating to DLP journeys, MSME and all government-sponsored schemes covered; digital on-lending initiatives keep CASA above 46%. Cost-to-income 55.4% and declining; fee income via cross-sell flagged as the next fillip.

**New vertical — wealth management by Dec/Jan:** 40+ officers selected from 300 recent marketing-officer hires (already trained in the segment); a senior specialised team being hired to run it full-fledged — a fee-income and HNI-relationship play.

**Management pushes back on the valuation:** "very few public-sector bank stocks trade below 1x P/B — our book value is ₹41, the stock trades at ₹30–31. It is at a rock-bottom level," with 20–22% growth, contained slippages and favourable NPA/credit-cost trends cited as the unjustified-discount case.
