## Comprehensive Overview

Central Bank of India is a public sector bank established in 1911, with headquarters in Mumbai, Maharashtra. With over 110 years of banking heritage, Central Bank of India is one of India's oldest and most established public sector banks.

The bank operates through a network of 4,600+ branches and 3,800+ ATMs across India. Central Bank of India offers a wide range of banking and financial services including retail banking, corporate banking, agricultural banking, and SME financing. The bank has a strong presence in rural and semi-urban markets and plays a significant role in financial inclusion.
