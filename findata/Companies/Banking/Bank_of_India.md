# Bank of India

## Company Overview

Bank of India is a leading public sector bank established in 1906 with over 115 years of banking legacy. Headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, the government-owned bank operates an extensive network of branches and ATMs across India with international presence in select countries. It provides comprehensive banking services including retail banking, corporate banking, treasury operations, international banking, and agricultural banking, with a focus on digital transformation and asset quality improvement.

**Guardrails on unsecured PL:** deliberately slowing low-ticket personal loans / non-salaried segments; concentrating on salaried service-sector accounts with nudge mandates — de-escalating PL volume to protect asset quality.

**Deposit-cost optimisation:** sourcing mid-corporate ₹3-25 cr deposits (finer than bulk ₹500-1,000 cr) to support robust credit growth; CASA and retail-TD mix down, but NIM defended.

> "We have focused on garnering deposits in the 3 crore to 25 crore bucket, where the interest rate is finer than bulk deposits of 500-1,000 crores... we have been taking bulk deposits above 3 crores to support that growth, which has pushed CASA and retail term deposit percentages down. However, we are optimizing costs to keep NPM stable."
