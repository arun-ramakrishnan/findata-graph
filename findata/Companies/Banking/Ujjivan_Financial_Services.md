# Ujjivan Financial Services

## Overview

Ujjivan Financial Services Limited is a leading microfinance-focused financial institution in India, primarily serving the needs of low-income households and micro-entrepreneurs in urban and semi-urban areas. The company operates as a Small Finance Bank and Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), focusing on providing financial services to the underserved sections of society. Ujjivan has played a significant role in promoting financial inclusion and empowering women entrepreneurs through its comprehensive microfinance solutions.
