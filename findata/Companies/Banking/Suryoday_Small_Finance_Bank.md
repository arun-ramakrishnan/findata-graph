## Comprehensive Overview

Suryoday Small Finance Bank Limited is a scheduled small finance bank operating across India, with focus on serving underserved and unbanked populations. The bank was granted small finance bank license by RBI and has been focusing on financial inclusion, microfinance, and small business banking.

Suryoday operates through a network of branches and digital platforms, providing comprehensive banking services with emphasis on micro enterprises, self-help groups, and underserved segments in rural and semi-urban areas. The bank has been demonstrating consistent growth trajectory with strong asset quality metrics.
