## Comprehensive Overview

City Union Bank Limited is a scheduled private sector bank established in 1904, with headquarters in Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu. With over 120 years of banking heritage, City Union Bank has grown from a small regional bank to a pan-India presence with 700+ branches and 1,500+ ATMs.

The bank focuses on retail banking, SME financing, NRI banking, and agricultural banking. City Union Bank has a strong presence in South India, particularly in Tamil Nadu, and has been expanding to other states.
