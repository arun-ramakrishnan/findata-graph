## Comprehensive Overview

Nainital Bank Limited is a scheduled commercial bank operating as a subsidiary of Bank of Baroda since 1975. Headquartered in Nainital, Uttarakhand, the bank has a strong regional presence across North India, particularly in Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, and Rajasthan.

The bank operates through a network of 160+ branches and 140+ ATMs, providing comprehensive banking services with emphasis on personalized customer service. As a regional bank, it has deep local market knowledge and strong community relationships.

Nainital Bank offers traditional banking products including deposits, loans, treasury operations, and digital banking services, with focus on retail and MSME segments.
