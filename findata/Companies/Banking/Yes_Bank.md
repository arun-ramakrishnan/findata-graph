# Yes Bank

## Company Overview
Yes Bank Limited is a full-service private-sector bank (incorporated 2003, HQ Mumbai) offering banking and financial products to Retail, MSME, and corporate clients across Treasury, Corporate/Wholesale, Retail, and Other Banking segments. After a 2020 reconstruction crisis, the bank has staged a turnaround, re-entering major indices and attracting a strategic investor (SMBC).

## The Chatter — Edition #52 (Mar 27, 2026)

- **RIDF runoff on track**: Mandatory NABARD/RIDF deposits (for Priority Sector Lending shortfalls) steadily declining from ~11% in FY24 to ~6.9% in Q3, targeting below 5% of total assets by FY27 — freeing funds for higher-yield lending.
- **Disciplined lending mix**: Intentionally avoiding hyper-competitive, thin-margin loan markets (notably Home Loans and Car Loans) to protect risk-adjusted returns and capital efficiency.
- **Branches as multi-product engines**: Repurposing the physical branch network from deposit-gathering into asset, fee-income, and high-velocity transaction hubs.
- **Funding-cost edge**: Cost of Deposits cut to 5.6% (from 6.1% YoY) via deposit cuts sharper than RBI/peers — signalling brand trust and supporting margins.
- **Asset quality improving**: Fresh slippages contained at ₹1,050 cr in Q3 — the lowest in eight quarters; retail business hit breakeven this quarter and is set to contribute to profitability.
- **Cards health**: Credit card entry rates down from ~20% to ~12%, signalling a healthier, higher-margin portfolio.
