# Bandhan Bank Limited

## Company Overview

Bandhan Bank Limited was founded in **2001** as an NBFC-MFI and converted into a **universal bank in 2015**. Headquartered in **Kolkata**, it retains a microfinance heritage and employs approximately **75,000** people. The bank serves retail, micro-borrower, MSME, and corporate customers across its core segments: Treasury, Retail Banking, Corporate/Wholesale Banking, and Other Banking.
