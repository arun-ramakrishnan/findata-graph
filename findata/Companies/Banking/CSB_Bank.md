## Comprehensive Overview
CSB Bank Limited (formerly Catholic Syrian Bank Limited) is a scheduled private sector bank based in Thrissur, Kerala. Established in 1920, CSB Bank is one of India's oldest private sector banks with over 100 years of heritage. The bank has transformed from a traditional regional bank to a modern private sector bank with nationwide presence.

CSB Bank operates through a network of 500+ branches and 300+ ATMs across India, with significant presence in Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Maharashtra. The bank focuses on retail banking, SME financing, and digital banking while maintaining strong asset quality.
