## Comprehensive Overview
Lakshmi Vilas Bank was a private sector bank with a rich history of serving customers in India, particularly in South Indian markets. Established in 1926, the bank operated for nearly a century before merging with Clix Capital in 2020. The merger marked the end of an era for one of India's oldest private sector banks, creating a new entity focused on retail banking and financial services with enhanced capabilities and broader market reach.
