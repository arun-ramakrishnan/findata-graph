## Comprehensive Overview

Abhyudaya Co-operative Bank is a scheduled co-operative bank operating primarily in Maharashtra, with its headquarters in Mumbai. Established in 1964, the bank has grown from a small co-operative society to one of the leading urban co-operative banks in India. The bank focuses on serving the banking needs of individuals, small businesses, and co-operative societies, with emphasis on retail banking and financial inclusion.

The bank operates through a network of branches and ATMs across Maharashtra and select locations in other states, providing traditional banking products and services while embracing digital transformation to enhance customer experience.
