# Kotak Mahindra Bank | Large Cap | Banking

Kotak Mahindra Bank is a leading private sector bank in India, known for its strong wealth management franchise and consistent performance. Founded by Uday Kotak, it has grown to become one of India's largest banks.

- **Retail Banking**: Deposits, personal loans, home loans, credit cards
- **Corporate Banking**: Corporate loans, trade finance
- **Wealth Management**: Kotak Mahindra Capital, Kotak Securities
