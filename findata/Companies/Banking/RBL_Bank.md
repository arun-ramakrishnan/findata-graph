## Comprehensive Overview

RBL Bank Limited is one of India's fastest-growing private sector banks, established in 1943. Headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, RBL Bank has transformed from a regional bank to a pan-India presence with 500+ branches and 400+ ATMs.

The bank focuses on retail banking, SME financing, corporate banking, and agri-business. RBL Bank has been emphasizing digital banking, customer experience enhancement, and technology-driven solutions to support its growth trajectory.
