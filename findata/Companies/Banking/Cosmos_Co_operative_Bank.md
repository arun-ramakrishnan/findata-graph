## Comprehensive Overview

Cosmos Co-operative Bank Limited is an urban co-operative bank established in 1906, with headquarters in Pune, Maharashtra. With over 115 years of banking heritage, Cosmos has grown from a small co-operative society to one of India's prominent urban co-operative banks.

The bank operates through a network of 130+ branches and 100+ ATMs primarily in Maharashtra, with a strong presence in Pune and surrounding areas. Cosmos focuses on retail banking, SME financing, and community-based banking while maintaining strong co-operative principles and customer relationships.
