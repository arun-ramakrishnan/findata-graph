## Comprehensive Overview
Punjab & Sind Bank is a scheduled public sector bank established in 1908 with headquarters in New Delhi. With over 115 years of banking heritage, P&S Bank has a strong presence in Northern and Western India, particularly in Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, and Rajasthan.

The bank operates through a network of 1,500+ branches and 1,300+ ATMs, focusing on retail banking, SME financing, agricultural banking, and corporate banking. P&S Bank has been emphasizing digital transformation, financial inclusion, and asset quality improvement.
