## Comprehensive Overview

Karur Vysya Bank Limited is a scheduled private sector bank established in 1916, with headquarters in Karur, Tamil Nadu. With over 105 years of banking heritage, Karur Vysya Bank has grown from a small regional bank to a pan-South India presence with 800+ branches and 1,700+ ATMs.

The bank focuses on retail banking, SME financing, agricultural banking, and gold loans. Karur Vysya Bank has a strong presence in South India, particularly in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Kerala.
