## Sector Overview

The Financial Services sector in India encompasses banking, insurance, asset management, broking, and financial technology. With total assets exceeding $2 trillion and growing financialization, this sector plays a critical role in India's economic development and capital formation.

Key characteristics of the Financial Services sector:
- **Underpenetrated Market**: Low financial services penetration compared to global peers
- **Growth Opportunity**: Rising household savings and financialization
- **Technology Adoption**: Rapid digital transformation with UPI, NEFT, and trading platforms
- **Regulatory Evolution**: Progressive reforms and liberalization

The sector is driven by financialization, digital payments, and market development, while facing challenges in regulatory changes, technology disruption, and credit risk.

## The Chatter — TCS, Indian Bank, Amagi, CMR Green & More

**Q1 FY27: CASA +15.43%, RAM +14.80%, MSME +17%, retail +18%** — broad-based growth across segments. FY27 guidance retained: deposits ~11%, advances 11–13%. CEO will revisit upside only after Q2 once macro uncertainties (West Asia, rates) clear.

**Deliberately exiting low-yield advances** — ~₹6,000 cr of low-yielding loans settled in Q1 alone; this depresses headline growth vs. the system but management prioritises NIM and earnings quality over market share.

**Asset quality: sub-1% credit cost and slippage guidance** — credit cost 0.23%, slippage 0.77%, SMA down to 4.69 (from 4.73). MSME stress limited to "one or two" specific accounts, not systemic. No West Asia impact seen so far.

**ECL transition front-loaded** — estimated ₹3,000 cr total ECL impact; ₹1,000 cr already provided in FY26, with a further ₹1,500–2,000 cr to be created this year to ensure a smooth transition when the new rules bite.

**No growth capital needed** — CAR is sufficient for both growth and ECL provisioning despite an unused ₹5,000 cr QIP approval. Branch productivity inflection: 51% of branches hit Q1 target vs. a historical 25–30% — a culture/engagement shift management expects to sustain.
