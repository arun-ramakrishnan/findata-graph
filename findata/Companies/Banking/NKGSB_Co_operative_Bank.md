## Comprehensive Overview

NKGSB Co-operative Bank Limited (Nav Kishanth Sagar Sahakari Bank) is a leading co-operative bank in India, established in 1918 with headquarters in Pune, Maharashtra. With over 105 years of banking heritage, NKGSB has grown from a small co-operative society to one of India's leading urban co-operative banks.

The bank operates through a network of 90+ branches and 80+ ATMs primarily in Maharashtra and Goa. NKGSB focuses on retail banking, SME financing, and community-based banking while maintaining strong co-operative principles and customer relationships.
