## Business Overview
Groww (Billionbrains Garage Ventures; NSE: GROWW), founded in 2017 in Bengaluru, is India's leading digital investment platform offering stocks, mutual funds, ETFs, F&O, IPOs, and credit services. With a user-friendly app and strong technology, it enables millions to invest and build wealth easily.

## Key Insights
- Groww's revenue is primarily driven by derivatives (70%) with a flat fee of INR20 per trade, while cash transactions account for remaining 30%
- The pricing is very simple: they charge INR20 on derivative side and have variable pricing on cash side
- Groww is targeting contribution from derivatives in their overall business to come below 50%
- The contribution from derivatives is beyond 50%, and it can definitely come below 50%, but this will happen as other products like wealth management and commodities start contributing
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) varies quarterly due to fixed branding costs and variable acquisition volumes, tending to normalize on an annual basis
- Part of their growth cost includes branding (fixed cost) and variable cost, and across year, CAC normalizes
- Groww maintains a fixed MTF pricing of 14.95% and believes customer experience, rather than aggressive pricing, is primary competitive differentiator for this secondary product
- Groww's Loan Against Securities (LAS) product is in its nascent stages, currently limited to mutual funds, and is expected to require time to achieve significant scale similar to MTF
- Groww aims to achieve a double-digit market share in MTF within three years, anticipating this growth will come from expanding overall market rather than just capturing existing share
- A significant 80% of Groww's customer acquisition is organic, supplemented by digital performance marketing and a small, non-commercial referral base
- The recently acquired Fisdom business, representing 3% of current revenue, is in early stages of integration with its P&L yet to be fully consolidated
- Fisdom's revenue model is primarily based on distribution income across mutual funds, PMS/AIF, insurance, and unlisted securities
- Groww expects recently launched PMS of mutual funds to accelerate growth and contribute significantly through advisory fees
- Regulatory changes led to a churn of low-quality FNO users, resulting in a smaller, higher-quality and more active user base, which is considered beneficial for Groww and industry
- Groww's personal loan book is approximately INR1250 crores, and lending business, being only three years old, is in a stabilization phase regarding its credit book
- For coming 2-3 years, Groww is focused on products like wealth management, which is going to be a big game for them going forward
