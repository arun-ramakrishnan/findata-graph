# Manipal Payment And Identity Solutions

## Company Overview
Manipal Payment & Identity Solutions (MPI) is a Manipal Group (Pais family) company manufacturing secure payment cards, national IDs and automated banking kiosks — the flagship of the group's legacy BFSI platform (14+ businesses across BFSI, consumer and ancillary platforms). Leading domestic payment-card player with >21% market share (27.4% among public-sector banks), 1 bn+ national ID tags in 12+ languages, presence in 15+ countries through four subsidiaries, and ranked #10 globally in Visa/Mastercard card shipments ex-China. Recently listed — management describes it as "the first of many companies that has now been listed" from the group. (Ticker pending manual resolution — too recent for the resolver.)

## The Chatter — PNB, Central Bank of India, Rentomojo & More

**Manufacture-in-India-for-the-world model:** ~90% of card revenue (the base card) is produced in India; the 10% personalisation layer is local, mandated by data-localisation laws. Nigeria bureau fully operational; exports grew from 7% of revenue (FY23) to ~15% in Q1 (₹64 cr), with a stated 20–25% of revenue target for international business.

**Metal cards = the premium margin engine:** realisation 20–25x a traditional card; global metal-card issuances ~64 mn growing ~15% CAGR, India ~2–2.2 mn growing ~47% CAGR. Few global players manufacture them; MPI holds patents in India and multiple other markets — a pharma-style branded category.

**EBITDA margin 19% → 33% over three years:** driven by mix (digital automation, metal cards, tax stamps), operating leverage (production doubled FY21–FY24) and backward integration — own RuPay chip applet, in-house card inlays, and an internally built IT platform. FY27 EBITDA guided at a clear 30% despite geopolitical/supply-chain noise.

**Technology ladder keeps re-pricing the product:** magstripe (single-digit realisation) → EMV contact (4x) → dual-interface (2x again). Volumes doubled FY21–FY24 on top of the realisation ladder — the compounding behind margin expansion.

**Demand visibility despite UPI:** debit-card issuance seen growing ~13% — transit is migrating from closed-loop to open-loop (re-cardings across all metros), and government welfare/senior-citizen/student passes are moving onto cards. The largest PSU's RFP roughly doubles issuance over the next three years.

**Guidance:** FY27 top line +30–35% vs FY26; 20–25% revenue CAGR over the next three years (lumpy, timing-dependent). ₹238 cr of IPO proceeds earmarked for capacity (₹100–120 cr this FY), including metal-card capacity and personalisation bureaus in developed markets (~₹30 cr each; one more planned this year).

**Tax stamps: regulatory tailwind:** plastic ban (<50 microns) is forcing ~16 polyester-stamp states toward paper-based high-security stamps — MPI is at the forefront with paper + security features; Africa and the Middle East flagged as the global opportunity set.

**Capital allocation:** growth capex first, higher working capital with scale, a dividend policy under Board deliberation ("we definitely want to be a dividend-paying company"), and openness to inorganic deals in-segment. Net-importer FX exposure hedged under a Board-approved policy.
