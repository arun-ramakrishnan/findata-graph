## The Chatter — Q3 FY26 Concall (Tech-Led Consumer Strategy, BNPL Relaunch)

- Strategy reset: building for consumers via technology and products rather than cash burn — "throw technology and products on consumers, not money"
- Business model not dependent on PIDF subsidies; expects to offset lost subsidy income by ~30-40% this quarter via higher subscription revenues and more targeted sales efforts
