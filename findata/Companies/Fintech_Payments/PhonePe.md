## The Chatter — Solar Ind PhonePe

**UPI's $5 bn subsidy era:** The industry invested ~$5 bn of equity capital / balance-sheet losses over a decade to scale UPI to ~500 mn users — all still free; reaching the next 500 mn "requires a pretty serious amount of capital investment" (Founder & CEO Sameer Nigam).

**Zero-MDR was the global anomaly:** 200+ countries make merchants — the beneficiaries of cashlessness — absorb cost via MDR; India was the only market without MDR on its largest network. UPI itself launched with ~0.65% MDR and grew rapidly through 2016–2020; the proposed ~0.4% remains the world's lowest.

**The real cost stack:** 30,000 PhonePe servers and several thousand crore of capex behind 700–800 mn daily network transactions; ongoing chargeback, fraud-mitigation, AI-era cybersecurity and banking-level merchant re-KYC compliance costs "escalating linearly as UPI grows."

**Merchants already absorb payment costs:** ~60 lakh merchants pay 1.7–2.25% interchange on card POS transactions; over 95% of PhonePe's UPI merchants already accept RuPay credit cards on UPI and absorb that cost. Cash isn't free either — pilferage and collection costs keep merchants from pushing customers back.

**Measured regulation:** No MDR on sub-₹2,000 payments (95–96% of transactions), ₹300 cap on large tickets, ₹5 cap on fuel; transaction-splitting to dodge thresholds "won't work at scale" because customers punish it.

**Two independent P&Ls:** The merchant business must run viably on its own — consumer-scale scale will not permanently subsidise it. MDR is the moment digital payments economics start to pay out, which matters with a listing on the horizon.

> "Over the last decade, the industry has invested almost $5 billion of equity capital, or just balance-sheet losses, to grow UPI into the behemoth it is. Almost 500 million Indians have been using UPI, and it continues to be free for all of them."
> — Sameer Nigam, Founder & CEO, PhonePe
