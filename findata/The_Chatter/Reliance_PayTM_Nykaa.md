## The New India Assurance Company Ltd | Large Cap | General Insurance

The New India Assurance Company is India's largest non-life insurer with a significant global footprint across 24 countries and a century-long legacy. The company maintains leadership in the domestic market through a diversified portfoli including health, motor, fire, and marine insurance.

## [Concall]
