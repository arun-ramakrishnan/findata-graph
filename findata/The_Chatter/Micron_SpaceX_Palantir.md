## Micron Technology Semiconductors

Micron Technology is a leading American multinational semiconductor company that designs and manufactures computer memory and data storage products, including DRAM, NAND, and High Bandwidth Memory (HBM).

[Concall]

On demand visibility for High Bandwidth Memory (HBM), management said custo demand remains well ahead of available capacity, even several years out.

"The demand that we have for our HBM products—HBM3E, HBM4 and beyond—is far excess of our ability to supply. Through these Strategic Customer Agreements, we’re discussing demand over a multi-year horizon, even beyond 2027 and into 2028. Our demand for HBM, not just in 2027 but even in 2028, is we above our ability to supply."

Mark Murphy updated Micron's industry outlook following stronger-than-expected demand.

"Today we indicated that we expect market tightness to continue beyond 2027. Part of t reason is that we have increased our estimate for the HBM total addressable market. Previously, we expected it to cross $100 billion in 2028. We now see the HBM TAM eas crossing $100 billion in 2027."

Mark Murphy, Chief Financial Officer

Asked about long-term DRAM and NAND bit demand growth, management explai why it is no longer providing multi-year CAGR forecasts.

"For the foreseeable future, shipment growth for bits is not really determined by demand anymore. It is actually determined by supply. Demand is so much above the industry's ability to supply that supply growth will determine shipment growth far more than demand growth Our expectation is that supply growth will continue to remain short of what is needed to meet demand, and we don't really see when supply will be able to catch up."

- Sumit Sadana, Chief Business Officer

Management explained the structure of the new Strategic Customer Agreements (SCAs), highlighting their long-term nature and financial protection.

"These Strategic Customer Agreements cannot be cancelled. There is no provision that allows a customer to walk away. Outside of automotive, these are generally five-year take or-pay agreements. "The agreements we have already signed aggregate to more than $22 billion in total cash and related financial commitments, of which the cash alone is almost $18 billion. As we expand these agreements toward our target of covering roughly half of the company's revenue, the cash associated with them will increase significantly."

"We continue to expect LPDRAM usage to grow over time as a percentage of DRAM consumption in data centers.
