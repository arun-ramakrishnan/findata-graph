## INDIA SEAFOOD PROCESSING \& EXPORTS INDUSTRY

India's seafood processing industry is projected to reach USD 25.2 billion by 2033, growing at a CAGR of $7.51 \%$, supported by rising demand, aquaculture advances and policy support.

India is the largest shrimp exporter to the US, consistently maintaining a 30-50\% share over the past four years, despite tariff pressures and rising competition from Ecuador and Vietnam.

Vannamei accounts for $\sim 73 \%$ of India's shrimp exports by volume in FY25. India pioneered its commercial farming, achieving high productivity ( $8.5^{\circ} \mathrm{T} / \mathrm{Ha} / \mathrm{Tr}$ ) and widespread global demand.

After a period of decline, Black Tiger shrimp is regaining market share, rising to $7.3 \%$ of exports by value in FY25, driven by shifting farmer preferences and stronger pricing ( $\sim 1449 / \mathrm{kg}$ vs $1367 / \mathrm{kg}$ for Vannamei).

While the US remains the largest market (48\%), India is expanding its presence in China, Japan, EU and Russia. India's role in value-added product (VAP) exports is growing, supported by new capacity and quality advantages.
