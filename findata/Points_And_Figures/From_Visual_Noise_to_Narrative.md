# Government Support on Fertilizers 

- Excessive usage of urea has skewed NPK ratio of India's soil to 7.7:3.1:1. This has led to soil degradation from ideal ratio of 4:2:1 making it $83 \%$ phosphorous deficient.
- Import challenges for DAP in recent years has created shortage and affordability issues. DAP prices have risen sharply due to geopolitical tensions in the Middle East,Tariff War and a halt on China exports.
- To attain self reliance and reduce subsidy burden, government and associations are educating farmers to look at SSP as an alternative.
- Single Super Phosphate (SSP) is a cost-effective indigenous alternative to DAP, especially suitable for oilseeds and pulses. With $16 \%$ P2O5 and $11 \%$ sulfur, SSP addresses micronutrient deficiency in Indian soils. Despite lower subsidy rates and no freight support, SSP has huge potential due to increasing soil awareness and GOI push for fertilizer balance.
